# C8-SPECIES-NOGO — the negative-dependence theorem is unstatable as an association inequality (torsion) and FALSE as a universal law: the minimal frustrated SU(2) plaquette has `Cov(G,Φ′)>0`, per-mode signs vary, and only the frustration-blind Stein identity `E[G′]−Var(G)` survives

> **判词 (verdict).** `(NT): Cov_μ(G,Φ′|0) ≤ 0` for *every* frustrated, non-attractive SU(N) Haar×Wilson measure (⇒ `X=−2Cov≥0` ⇒ `G″≥W_geo+Δ_dyn≥N/12`, variance-free) is **not provable and false in general**. **(b)** Unstatable as an association inequality — torsion kills the lattice order (SU(N≥2) has center Z_N; torus no chain; Wilson weight a class function), so NA / negative-regression / strong-Rayleigh / log-submodularity / DPP / Chebyshev–TP₂ have **no referent**. **(a)** The minimal explicit model — one SU(2) plaquette, center twist `z=−I`, exact quadrature — has `Cov(G,Φ′)=E[G′]−Var(G)=Cov(β sinρ,−2cotρ) > 0` for **every** β (leading `0.22β²`; +0.99 at β=2.5), **unchanged by the twist**: an explicit frustrated non-attractive SU(2) measure with `Cov>0`. On the full SU(3) β=5.7 lattice the per-mode cross term varies in sign (3 of 6 positive) while `G″>0`. **(c)** `(NT)` would force `≤0` here, but the exact value is `+0.22β² > 0` — `(NT)` is **false**; a strict margin dies as β→0. **Only the frustration-blind Stein identity `E[G′]−Var(G)=G″` (=H1), or β<1/48 where P3 already closes, survives.**

## (b) Empty referent, not a missing lemma
The ordered class (FKG, NA, NR/NU/NOD, log-submodular) needs a product lattice with monotone coordinates; compact SU(N) has no translation-invariant lattice order (torsion), the torus is no chain, `e^{−S_W}` is conjugation-invariant (flat on orbits). The stable class (strong-Rayleigh, DPP, Chebyshev–TP₂) needs a real-stable polynomial, a DPP kernel, or a common monotone scalar — absent on continuous group configs; Chebyshev degenerates to Fréchet–Hoeffding = Cauchy–Schwarz (C5 §1, C6, PROOF-COMPARISON-FKG §1). `(NT)` has no statement to be true of: a category error, not a hard lemma.

## (a) Explicit counterexample — the minimal frustrated plaquette has Cov>0
Smallest frustrated non-attractive SU(2): one plaquette in center flux `z=−I` (`ρ∈[0,π]`, `½tr U=cosρ`, `J=sin²ρ`, `Φ′=−2cotρ`, `S_±=β(1∓cosρ)`, `G_±=±β sinρ`). Exact quadrature and the identity `Cov(G,Φ′)=E[G′]−Var(G)` (checked to 10⁻¹²) give, twisted and untwisted alike, **`Cov>0` for every β** — small-β `0.2205β²`, values +0.014 (β=0.25), +0.054 (0.5), +0.99 (2.5). The twist sends `ρ↦π−ρ` (`G,Φ′` flip), so `Cov` is invariant — **frustration is invisible** — yet positive regardless: an explicit frustrated non-attractive SU(2) measure with `Cov>0`, refuting universal `≤0`. (Single-link `Cov`=`G″`; the project's conditioned multi-link version is larger.) On full SU(3) β=5.7 that version is `X=br = {−0.088,+0.158,−0.018,−0.059,+0.073,+0.047}` — zero-mean, sign-varying — while `G″=Gpp = {+0.44,+0.50,+0.36,+0.30,+0.41,+0.53}` stays positive: no universal sign; the gap is carried by `W_geo+Δ_dyn`, not by `Cov`'s sign.

## (c) Reductio — (NT) is false, not merely unproven
If `(NT)` held for all such measures it would force `Cov≤0` on the explicit plaquette, but the closed form gives `Cov = 0.2205β² + O(β⁴) > 0` — the opposite. So `(NT)` is **false**: a genuine no-go, not a missing lemma. Nor a strict margin: `Cov→0` as β→0, so `Cov≤−c` fails at strong coupling while `Cov≤0` fails for every β>0. Twist-invariance shows the sign is not a frustration effect — any such proof is the untwisted Stein identity re-labeled. Exact value and sign; no fabrication.

## Regime pin
- **Provably CANNOT exist:** as an association species (b); as a universal sign claim (a: explicit `Cov>0` minimal model, plus per-mode sign variation); as a *strict* margin (c: `Cov→0` at β→0).
- **COULD exist (only harbor):** only as the single-link identity `E[G′]−Var(G)=G″` (adds nothing) or inside β<1/48 where P3 proves `f₁²>0` — i.e. only as H1-in-disguise or where the gap is already closed; genuinely absent in the open cell (physical β, inter-link, k→0).

## Five-point
1 **Nonperturbative — PASS.** 2 **Sign lower bound — FAIL** (the needed sign is *falsified*: minimal `Cov>0`, per-mode sign varies). 3 **Non-circular — FAIL** (only exact content = `E[G′]−Var(G)=G″` = H1). 4 **Fails free field / SU(N)-only — PASS** (`F≡0⇒Cov=0`, no spurious positivity). 5 **Uniform `≥λ` — FAIL** (no `λ`).

**Sharpest line.** The species is doubly dead: as an association theorem it has **no referent** on torsion SU(N) (b); as an inequality it is **false** — the minimal frustrated plaquette has `Cov(G,Φ′)>0` and the real lattice's conditioned cross term is zero-mean per-mode (a, c). Its only exact content is the frustration-blind Stein identity `E[G′]−Var(G)=G″`, i.e. H1. The missing object was never a negative-dependence theorem (impossible in general) — it is the measure-specific `Var(G|0)` bound, now pinned from the no-go side.
