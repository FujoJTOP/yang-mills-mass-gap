# BS-NUMERICAL-RIGOROUS — the 0⁺⁺ correlator certifies an **upper** bound on `m_{0⁺⁺}`, never a lower one; the lower-bound direction is a structural no-go, not a statistics shortfall

> 2026-11-23 · Target: a **certified (validated-error / interval) lower bound** on the 0⁺⁺ glueball mass from the existing `trF²`/plaquette correlator (`f1sq_b{1.0,2.2}_L6.npz`, `corr/*.npz`; all post-processing only, peak RSS ≈ few MB). **判词 (verdict):** the reflection-positive (RP) correlator gives a **rigorous UPPER bound** `m_{0⁺⁺} ≤ α(t)` for every `t` (the symmetrized effective mass `α(t)` decreases to `m₁` from above). The **lower**-bound direction — the one the mass gap actually needs — is **not available**: (i) *directional no-go* — positivity constrains spectral weights `c_n≥0` but puts no positive floor on the ground overlap `c₁`, so a light state is numerically hidden by heavy modes at small `t`; (ii) *identification no-go* — four noisy points at one `(β,L)` under-determine the truncated moment problem. A lower bound needs a **rigorous uniform decay envelope** `C(t)≤A e^{−μt}` (Simon–Spencer), which finite MC data cannot supply and which, even if supplied, is a finite-`(β,L)` number whose positivity is **automatic** (discrete transfer spectrum) and probes nothing about the continuum. This is a *sign probe*, not a certified bound — and not continuum.

## §1 The direction theorem (rigorous, RP + spectral sum)

For a gauge-invariant `O=trF²`, RP gives the connected spectral representation `C(t)=Σ_{n≥1}c_nλ_n^t`, `c_n=|⟨0|O|n⟩|²≥0`, `λ_n=e^{−E_n}` decreasing, `E₁=a·m_{0⁺⁺}`. Define the symmetrized effective mass `cosh α(t)=(C(t−1)+C(t+1))/(2C(t))`.

> **Theorem (direction).** `α(t) ≥ E₁` for all `t≥1`, and `α(t)↓E₁` monotonically. Hence the correlator canonically **upper-bounds** `m_{0⁺⁺}`; equality iff `O` couples to a single state.

*Proof.* `log C(t)` is convex (`log`-sum-exp); its forward difference `d(t)=log(C(t)/C(t+1))` is increasing with `d(t)↑E₁`. Then `cosh α(t)≥cosh(d(t−1))≥cosh E₁`. ∎ Verified on controlled spectra (every test: `α≥E₁`, decreasing; single-mode equality `α≡E₁`).

**Measured (β=2.2, L=6, SU(2), `f1sq` smearing s=2, N=4000):** `α(t=1,2,3)=1.62, 1.54, 1.47`; cosh-fit `m_G=1.52±0.16`, `f₁²=A=5.24±0.75`. Every smeared level gives `α↓`, log-convex (`C(t+1)²≤C(t)C(t+2)` holds at all `t`); the bare level (s=0) **violates** log-convexity at the `T/2` midpoint — the contact term / statistical noise at `t=3`, itself proof that the check is *statistical*. So `m_{0⁺⁺} ≲ 1.5 a` at this point — an **upper** bound, RP-consistent, agreeing with the known SU(2) 0⁺⁺ glueball.

## §2 The lower-bound no-go (why positivity cannot help)

A lower bound `E₁≥μ` needs the reverse inequality — a **rigorous uniform upper envelope** `C(t)≤A e^{−μt}` for *all* `t` (then `c₁e^{−E₁t}≤C(t)≤Ae^{−μt}` forces `E₁≥μ`; Simon–Spencer). Two hard obstructions, both structural:

1. **Hidden light state.** Positivity fixes `c_n≥0`, not `c₁>0` from below. A state with `E₁=0.3`, `c₁=0.05` plus modes at `E=4` reproduces small-`t` data *identical* to a state with `E₁=1.5`, `c₁=5`: controlled test gives `α(1)=3.51` (light, weak overlap) vs `α(1)=1.61` (heavy, strong overlap) — a 10× mass over-estimate that only relaxes at `t=4`. **The light state is invisible at the `t≤3` the data has.**
2. **Under-determined moment problem.** `C(0..3)` is four constraints; a `(E₁→0, c₁→0)` + heavy-mode solution always fits them. `E₁` is not identifiable from below — a no-go independent of statistics.

**Statistically it is worse still.** Jackknife `σ` is not an interval; a certified `α` needs interval arithmetic **and** a certified sampler (mixing-time/spectral bound), which does not exist at `β=2.2` — the illustrative one-sided LB on the analogous curvature is `≈−0.035` (SU(2), NUMERICS-AUDIT (b)), i.e. zero not excluded. So *neither* direction is "certified" in the interval sense; only the *upper*-bound **architecture** is rigorous, and plugging in `C` needs an enclosure that is exactly the H1 wall.

## §3 What "certifiable positivity of `m_{0⁺⁺}`" does and does not prove

- **`m_{0⁺⁺}(β,L)>0` is automatic and vacuous.** A finite-lattice transfer matrix has discrete spectrum ⇒ `Δ_V>0` trivially; and `Δ_V≥Δ_∞` is the *wrong* direction. Reporting a positive finite-volume lattice mass proves nothing about the gap.
- **The data is a sign probe, not a bound.** At β=2.2, L=6 the 0⁺⁺ operator couples to a light state (`m≈1.5a`), RP-consistent — evidence, not proof. At β=1.0 the correlator is a sub-lattice-spacing contact term (`C(t>0)≈0`, `m·a≳3`, `m_eff` undefined, log-convexity fails on noise) — the state is at the cutoff, un-resolvable (`COMPUTE-F1SQ-SU2`). `corr/` at β=6.0, L=6 (s=8) shows `α≈1.0–1.35a` (monotone only within noise), consistent with the same light state, near-continuum only in `β` (box ≪ 1 fm).
- **Free-field acid test.** RP/log-convexity is satisfied by a **free massive** field (`f₁²=0`, exponential decay) — so correlator positivity is **not** a YM-vs-free discriminator. Only a certified *decay envelope* would exclude the free **massless** field (power-law, `μ=0`) — and that targets the **mass gap** `m₁>0` (Jaffe–Witten), not the residue `f₁²>0` that P3 needs. Even a fully rigorous finite-`(β,L)` positivity would be a lattice-curvature/sign statement, not a gap discriminator (`A5`, `NUMERICS-AUDIT`, `STATE-DENSITY-I2` §6).

## §4 Sharpest line

The 0⁺⁺ correlator is a **one-way device**: `α(t)≥E₁` certifies an **upper** bound on `m_{0⁺⁺}` (measured `≲1.5a`, RP-consistent, log-convex), and the **lower**-bound direction the mass gap requires is blocked twice over — a light state hides under heavy modes when `c₁` is unconstrained from below (positivity is silent), and four points at one volume cannot identify `E₁` from below. No amount of statistics fixes either: the missing input is a **rigorous uniform decay envelope** (equivalently a certified sampler, `=H1` in correlator clothing) or the residue `f₁²>0` itself. The honest object certified by lattice positivity is a **finite-`(β,L)` sign probe** of a light 0⁺⁺ pole — not a continuum gap, not a residue, not a lower bound.
