# IN-1 — fixed-volume UV limit

**1. Statement.** Fix L, RT β=2N_c/g²(a), g²(a)∼1/(2b₀log(1/aΛ)); μ_{L,a} = Wilson measure on T⁴_L. **IN-1:** ∀L ∃ subsequence a_n→0 and probability μ_L on the gauge-invariant algebra Obs with S^{(n)}_{L,a_n}→S^{(n)}_L (weak-*, all n, f∈C_c^k(B(0,L/4))), μ_L satisfying OS0/OS1/OS3 (reflection positivity) and gauge-invariant. Stage 1 only; L→∞ is IN-6.

**2. Status — open; GL3 conditional.** GL3 (`GL3Limits.lean`) proves UVS(L) ⇒ compactness + diagonal subsequence + Minlos ⇒ μ_L; GL4 (`GL4L2.lean`) proves RP is weakly closed, so OS3 passes free. So axioms hold *given existence*; the only content is **UVS(L)** (glue-a (U1)–(U3)): a-uniform Lᵖ bounds on smeared tr F², a⁻⁴ subtraction along the same RT scheme C, factorial growth for Minlos. IN-1 needs only UVS(L); UVS-L (sup_L) is stage 2/IN-6.

**3. Evidence.** Balaban (CMP 109(1987)249; 116(1988)1; 122(1989)175,355) proves UV *stability* (Z bounded, coupling renormalised), not expansion convergence, gauge-invariant correlators, or OS — (U1)–(U3) are folklore, not a citable theorem (T3 audit). MRS (CMP 155(1993)) construct YM₄ with IR cutoff, axial gauge, SU(2), trivial topology: UV-complete but gauge-fixed, no RP — (U2) on Obs unavailable. Gap is existence, not positivity (lattice OS'78 + GL4 give RP once the limit exists).

**4. Advance + exact missing step.** P1-L0 proves the *free* case: gauge-invariant functionals see only F, zero modes are pure gauge, L²(μ)-decay is a-uniform (rate 4π²/L²) — so UVS(L), hence IN-1, holds for free Maxwell after subtraction. Sharpness: free massless GFF fails UVS(L) *before* subtraction (⟨φ²⟩∼log(1/a)), so (U2) is load-bearing. Missing step: a fixed-volume, cutoff-uniform, gauge-covariant renormalisation theorem — the marginal 4D BPHZ bound (W2 §2), the theorem blocking full construction. Fixed volume removes IR (UVS-L, IN-6, IN-9) but not the UV wall: at fixed L, log(L/a)→∞ RG steps and marginality ⇒ infinitely many scales.

**5. Flip + plausibility.** IN-1 ⇔ UVS(L). Flips iff cutoff-uniform gauge-invariant *renormalised* bounds are proven ⇔ gauge-invariant Schwinger functions exist at fixed L along the two-loop RT. Strictly weaker than full construction (implied, not implying). Plausibility: low standalone (G1's UV core, W2's "no known idea"), but the most attackable construct input: Balaban and MRS are each near-UV-complete; the sole bridge is RP-compatible gauge-invariant renormalisation.
