# PROOF-CP-COMBINE — the three pieces do NOT compose: (a) bounds the *wrong* threshold, (b) certifies positivity *almost everywhere* not *at 2.5*, and both quote the gap they would replace

> 2026-11-23 · PROOF-MODE attempt to close the isolation half `I1∧I2` — hence `(CP)` — by *composing* three facts: (a) no-literal-dissolution, atom pinned below its own two-body cut `m₁<θ_{0⁺⁺}`, `θ≥2m_lightest`; (b) analytic transport, `f₁²=a²` real-analytic with `a≢0`, zeros isolated; (c) P3 gives `f₁²≥r₀` at `β_eff`, Kato tracks the simple isolated edge. **Verdict: no closure; circular twice, wrong threshold once.** (a) separates the atom from the *multi-glueball continuum* at `θ≥2m_lightest`, but `I2` and the residue–level-gap lemma need separation from the *next same-channel level* `Λ` — the C/P-blind **excited `0⁺⁺`** at `~1.5m₁<2m₁` (PROOF-ISOLATION §4, PROOF-EDGE-E2). Its margin `Λ−m₁²≈1.25m₁²` is positive only pointwise, `→0` as `m₁→0`: *uniform* `I2` ⟺ `m₁≥μ` = the gap. (b) holds only where Kato's simple-isolated hypothesis holds — which *is* `I2` on the interval; even granted, isolated zeros give only `f₁²(2.5)≥0`, equality possible at an **overlap zero** `a(2.5)=0`. (c)'s tracking stops at the first singular point `β*`, and `β*>2.5` is unproven. NET: no theorem past a restatement of `(CP)`.

**1. The chain as proposed.** (c) ⇒ `f₁²(β_eff)≥r₀>0`, `λ₁` isolated. Kato ⇒ `f₁²` analytic on `[β_eff,β*)`, `β*` the first dissolution. (a) ⇒ no dissolution, so "no `β*`". (b) ⇒ `f₁²>0` off a discrete zero set ⇒ `f₁²(2.5)>0`. Each step is conditional on the next step's conclusion; the break is Step 3 (`a⇒no β*`).

**2. (a) bounds the wrong threshold, and is a tautology for `m₁>0`.** The operative `Λ=inf supp(ν−f₁²δ_{m₁²})` is the *excited* `0⁺⁺` glueball at `m_{0⁺⁺*}≈1.5m₁`, **not** the two-body cut `2m₁`; the `0⁺⁺` tower (C/P-blind to `gg` vs `(gg)(gg)`) resumes there, so "no `0⁺⁺` until `2m₁`" is *false*. The true margin `Λ−m₁²≈(1.5²−1)m₁²` vanishes iff `m₁→0` (PROOF-TRANSPORT-CONTINUITY §3). A *uniform* `I2` therefore needs `m₁≥μ`. And `m₁<2m₁` requires only `m₁>0`: it *is* the pointwise gap, dressed as kinematics — the genuine inputs are confinement (no `(gg)` cut) and `0⁺⁺`-lightest (IN-11), not the inequality itself.

**3. (b) gives `>0` almost everywhere, not at the endpoint.** Real-analytic `a≢0` ⇒ zeros of `f₁²=a²` discrete ⇒ `f₁²>0` off a finite set, `f₁²(2.5)≥0`. This already presumes the ∞-volume/continuum setting: at fixed `L` the residue is a discrete lattice artifact, analytic for free (`λ₂>λ₁`); the physical residue is the `L→∞` limit, where `λ₁` meets the continuum and Kato isolation *is* `m₁≥μ`. Upgrading the value *at 2.5* needs `a(2.5)≠0` — the uniform nonvanishing **K2** (PROOF-TRANSPORT-ANALYTIC §4). Analyticity cannot supply it: a nonnegative analytic function may touch zero quadratically there. Nor does semicontinuity lift "dense positive" to "positive at 2.5": `f₁²=ν({m₁²})` is *upper*-semicontinuous, `f₁²(2.5)≥limsup` (PROOF-F1SQ), which preserves a lower bound only if the approaching values stay `≥r>0` — the residue bound again — and gives just `≥0` when they shrink to `0` along the dense set.

**4. The transport shortcut is the same circle.** The multiplicative law `f₁²(2.5)=Z_O²f₁²(β_eff)>0`, being an *exact* covariance *within one phase*, forces `f₁²=Z_O²r₀>0` everywhere in the phase — it *forbids* overlap zeros and dissolution, i.e. it *is* `(CP)`. Analyticity does not replace that hypothesis: its domain on `[β_eff,2.5]` *is* the isolation `I2`.

**Exact logical status.** The composition reduces `(CP)` to "pointwise gap `m₁>0` on `[β_eff,2.5]` + `a(2.5)≠0`" — the gap and the endpoint residue themselves, no weaker. Missing step, verbatim: a *uniform* `Λ_β−m₁²≥κ` (= `m₁≥μ`) making `[β_eff,2.5]` one analyticity domain, **plus** `a(2.5)≠0` (K2).

**Sharpest line.** The pieces do not compose; each *quotes* the gap. (a) pins the atom below the `2m₁` continuum while `I2` needs the `1.5m₁` excitation, whose margin is uniform only if `m₁≥μ`; (b) certifies `f₁²>0` almost everywhere but leaves `2.5` possibly an overlap zero; (c) tracks the edge only to the first dissolution. The exact residue is `(CP)` unmoved.
