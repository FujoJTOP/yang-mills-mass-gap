# PROOF-CP-COMBINE — the three pieces do NOT compose: (a) bounds the *wrong* threshold, (b) certifies positivity *almost everywhere* not *at 2.5*, and both quote the gap they would replace

> 2026-11-23 · PROOF-MODE attempt to close the isolation half `I1∧I2` — hence `(CP)` — by *composing* three available facts: (a) no-literal-dissolution, the atom pinned below its own two-body cut `m₁<θ_{0⁺⁺}`, `θ≥2m_lightest`; (b) analytic transport, `f₁²=a²` real-analytic with `a≢0` so its zeros are isolated; (c) P3 gives `f₁²≥r₀` at `β_eff`, Kato tracks the simple isolated edge. **Verdict: no closure; the composition is circular twice and targets the wrong threshold once.** (a) separates the atom from the *multi-glueball continuum* at `θ≥2m_lightest`, but `I2` and the residue–level-gap lemma need separation from the *next same-channel level* `Λ` — the C/P-blind **excited `0⁺⁺`** at `~1.5m₁<2m₁` (PROOF-ISOLATION §4, PROOF-EDGE-E2). That margin is `Λ−m₁²≈1.25m₁²`, positive only pointwise and `→0` as `m₁→0`: *uniform* `I2` ⟺ `m₁≥μ` = the gap. (b) holds only on an analyticity domain, i.e. wherever Kato's simple-isolated hypothesis holds — which *is* `I2` on the interval; and even granted, isolated zeros give `f₁²(2.5)≥0`, equality possible at an **overlap zero** `a(2.5)=0` that analyticity cannot forbid. (c)'s tracking stops at the first singular point `β*`; `β*>2.5` is the unproven statement. NET: no theorem past a restatement of `(CP)`.

**1. The chain as proposed, and where it snaps.** (c) ⇒ `f₁²(β_eff)≥r₀>0`, `λ₁` isolated at `β_eff`. Kato ⇒ `f₁²` real-analytic on `[β_eff,β*)`, `β*` the first dissolution. (a) ⇒ no dissolution, so "no `β*`". (b) ⇒ `f₁²>0` off a discrete zero set. Conclusion ⇒ `f₁²(2.5)>0`. Steps 2–4 are each conditional on the others' conclusions; Step 3 is the load-bearing break.

**2. (a) bounds the wrong threshold, and is a tautology for `m₁>0`.** The operative `Λ=inf supp(ν−f₁²δ_{m₁²})` is the *excited* `0⁺⁺` glueball at `m_{0⁺⁺*}≈1.5m₁`, **not** the two-body cut `2m₁`; the `0⁺⁺` tower (C/P-blind to `gg` vs `(gg)(gg)`) resumes there, so "no `0⁺⁺` until `2m₁`" is *false*. The true margin `Λ−m₁²≈(1.5²−1)m₁²` vanishes iff `m₁→0` (PROOF-TRANSPORT-CONTINUITY §3). A *uniform* `I2` therefore needs `m₁≥μ`. And `m₁<2m₁` requires only `m₁>0`: it *is* the pointwise gap, dressed as kinematics — the genuine inputs are confinement (no `(gg)` cut) and `0⁺⁺`-lightest (IN-11), not the inequality itself.

**3. (b) gives `>0` almost everywhere, not at the endpoint.** Real-analytic `a≢0` ⇒ zeros of `f₁²=a²` discrete ⇒ `f₁²>0` off a finite set, `f₁²(2.5)≥0`. Upgrading the value *at 2.5* needs `a(2.5)≠0` — the uniform nonvanishing **K2** (PROOF-TRANSPORT-ANALYTIC §4). Analyticity cannot supply it: a nonnegative analytic function may touch zero quadratically there. Nor does semicontinuity lift "dense positive" to "positive": `f₁²=ν({m₁²})` is *upper*-semicontinuous (PROOF-F1SQ), the direction that bounds the value from *above*.

**4. The transport shortcut is the same circle.** The multiplicative law `f₁²(2.5)=Z_O²f₁²(β_eff)>0`, being an *exact* covariance *within one phase*, already forces `f₁²=Z_O²r₀>0` everywhere in the phase — it therefore *forbids* overlap zeros and dissolution, i.e. it *is* `(CP)`. Invoking (b) to bridge does not replace that hypothesis: the analyticity domain on `[β_eff,2.5]` *is* the isolation `I2`, whose reach (`β*>2.5`) is exactly what is open.

**Exact logical status.** The composition reduces `(CP)` to "pointwise gap `m₁>0` on `[β_eff,2.5]` + `a(2.5)≠0`". Both conjuncts are the gap and the endpoint residue themselves — no weaker, same object renamed. Missing step, verbatim: a *uniform* lower bound `Λ_β−m₁²≥κ` (equivalently `m₁≥μ`, the gap) making `[β_eff,2.5]` one analyticity domain, **plus** `a(2.5)≠0` (K2) exiling the endpoint from the zero set.

**Sharpest line.** The pieces do not compose; each *quotes* the gap. (a) pins the atom below the `2m₁` continuum while `I2` needs the `1.5m₁` excitation, whose margin is uniform only if `m₁≥μ`; (b) certifies `f₁²>0` almost everywhere but leaves `2.5` possibly an overlap zero; (c) tracks the edge only to the first dissolution. The exact residue is `(CP)` unmoved.
