# PROOF-SELFDUAL-BACKGROUND — SD/ASD split, BPS stability, and whether an instanton-ensemble average gives G″(0)>0

> **Verdict.** The **BPS lemma is true**: a single self-dual (instanton) background has a positive-semidefinite Hessian, and the Savvidy negative modes live in the anti-self-dual (`F⁻≠0`) content — the constant chromomagnetic background is exactly an equal SD/ASD mixture, *not* self-dual. But the decomposition **does not control G″**: pointwise the ASD/Savvidy negativity (≈ −1.1β) exceeds the Haar `W` (≳ N/12) by an order of magnitude, so no inequality `(−H_ASD) ⪯ c·W` exists; the operative cancellation is equipartition `Var(G)≈E[H]`, not an ASD-vs-W bound. The **ensemble average is not controllable** at β=2.5: the dilute-instanton-gas measure is invalid there, the measured negativity fraction (22–49%) shows μ is *not* SD-dominated, and even a perfect SD ensemble only gives `E[H]≥0`, leaving `G″ ≥ W−Var(G)`, with BPS *moduli* (zero second variation) making `Var(G)` large exactly along collective directions. Hence **no `G″(0)>0` follows**; the exact missing estimate is (◆_SD) below.

**1. The SD/ASD split and the BPS lemma (true).** In 4D Euclidean, `Λ²(ad)=Λ²₊⊕Λ²₋` (`*²=+1`), `F=F⁺+F⁻`, and `S_W→(1/2g²)(‖F⁺‖²+‖F⁻‖²)`, `Q=(1/8π²)∫Tr F∧F=(1/8π²)(‖F⁺‖²−‖F⁻‖²)` (continuum). Bogomol'nyi `S≥(8π²/g²)|Q|`, equality iff `F⁻=0` (SD) or `F⁺=0` (ASD). A self-dual connection is an **absolute minimum** of `S` in its sector [Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer 1978; Belavin–Polyakov–Schwarz–Tyupkin 1975], so `δ²S ⪰ 0` on *all* transverse fluctuations, with kernel = moduli (collective coordinates) ['t Hooft 1976]. Conversely the Savvidy background (constant chromomagnetic, `F₄ᵢ=0`) has `F⁺,F⁻` both nonzero with `‖F⁺‖=‖F⁻‖`, `Q=0` — **not BPS** — and its fluctuation operator has the tachyonic lowest Landau level `λ_min ∝ −gB` [Savvidy 1977; Nielsen–Olesen 1978; Leutwyler 1981]. So *negativity ⟺ non-self-dual* is correct, **in the continuum**. (On the coarse lattice at β=2.5 the Wilson action is not exactly `‖F‖²`, and the microscopic Hessian on *thermalized* configs is Savvidy-indefinite at every β — even β=40 [BianchiLemma §9 T4] — precisely because those configs are not SD.)

**2. The decomposition cannot bound G″ (false as a tool).** On a general config `H=H₊+H₋+cross`, the needed per-config inequality would be `−H_ASD ⪯ c·W`. It fails on scaling: `W` is pure group geometry, `W ≥ (N/12)‖v‖²` per link, β- and F-independent (PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES Lemma B); the Savvidy floor is `∝β` and grows with `|F|` (W6: min −1.12β at β=2.5; per-link operator bound `−βC_N#□`, PROVE-Gpp §3). At SU(2) β=2.5, `1.1β=2.75` vs `W≈0.17–0.34` — a factor ≳10. No β-uniform bound of ASD by W can exist; `W` cannot absorb the action's negative direction *pointwise*. What the data actually show is a different identity: `E_μ[H]` and `Var(G)` nearly cancel (equipartition, W7: `E[H]≈Var≈2.45`), leaving `W` to carry the sign in `G″=E[H]−Var(G)+W`. That is a **statistical** cancellation, not the **pointwise** statement `ASD ⪯ W`. Only the former is supported.

**3. The ensemble average is not controllable (cannot conclude G″(0)>0).** An "instanton-density-weighted" ensemble is the dilute-instanton-gas measure [Callan–Dashen–Gross 1978/79; Schäfer–Shuryak 1998], controlled only in a weak-coupling, dilute, `ρ≪spacing` regime; at β=2.5 (SU(2) crossover; SU(3) deep strong coupling) that control is absent. The measured negativity fraction — 21.7% (SU2 L=4), 42–49% (SU3) full / per-link — shows the actual μ is **not** SD-dominated (a dilute SD ensemble would be near-PSD by Lemma 1). Even *granting* a perfect SD ensemble: each config has `H⪰0` on the whole transverse space (global-minimum argument), so `E[H]≥0` for every mode `v`, and `G″ ≥ W−Var(G)`. That is **not** positivity — it still needs `Var(G) ≤ E[H]+W`. Worse, on a BPS config the second variation is *zero* along the moduli (the kernel of `H`), so along modulus-adjacent low-k modes `E[H]→0` while `Var(G)` stays O(1) (the action is flat, the force wanders): the marginal can be **negative**, reintroducing exactly the delocalized-collective-mode obstruction (GAP-G2-mass §(c)). The SD ensemble removes the Savvidy *deficit* in `E[H]` but transfers the whole burden to the variance — the same H1 wall, sharpened.

**Sharpest line.** SD stability is a true fact about **exact** instantons (PSD Hessian, kernel = moduli); it is **orthogonal to G″**. Pointwise the ASD negativity dwarfs `W` (≳10×); in the ensemble the sign is carried by `W` beating the *variance*, and BPS moduli make that variance large precisely at k→0. The SD/ASD split relocates the missing estimate from `E[H]` to `Var(G|a=0)`; it does not supply it.

**The exact missing estimate.** For the physical coarse transverse mode `v`, uniformly in `L`:
**(◆_SD)** `Var_μ(⟨v,∇S⟩|a=0) ≤ E_μ[H_vv|a=0] + W_full(v)`,
together with (i) SD-domination of μ (`E[H]≥0`; **false** at β=2.5 by measurement), and (ii) control of `Var(G)` along BPS moduli. If both held, `G″(0) ≥ W−Var(G) > 0` — a genuine k→0 mass. Neither is available; (◆_SD) is the same variance/induced-mass object as PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES (◆) and PROVE-Gpp (★), now with the Savvidy obstacle removed by SD-domination but the localization obstacle intact. **This route does not establish `G″(0)>0`.**
