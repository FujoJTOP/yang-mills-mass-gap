# G1-UNIQUENESS-VACUUM — OS4/unique vacuum is the rung *strictly below* the gap: not G1, not a disguised G2, but "G2⁻ in logic, G2-adjacent in cost"

> **Verdict (判词).** The project already separates **IN-1** (fixed-volume UV limit: `μ_L` subsequential, OS0/OS1/OS3, `⊥` gap — *this is G1*) from **IN-6** (IR limit + unique vacuum: `{μ_L}` has one weak limit `μ`, `μ` satisfies OS4). The task is to place IN-6 exactly. **OS4 and "unique vacuum" are not G1**: G1 is fixed-volume, where clustering is unformulable, and G1-EXISTENCE *explicitly* returns only a subsequence. **They are not G2 either, in logical strength**: a uniform gap is a *sufficient* condition for OS4 (exponential clustering ⇒ ergodicity ⇒ unique vacuum), but OS4 does **not** imply a gap — critical 2D Ising is a unique Gibbs state, clusters, has VAC, and is power-law (uniqueness/REPORT 例 2), and the generalized free field satisfies **all** OS axioms with `Δ>0` yet no atom (mass-spectrum 定理 B′). So in the implication poset `MassGap ⇒ OS4 ⇒ UNI/VAC` is strict, and `OS4 ⊉ MassGap`. **What uniqueness buys:** a named, separable, strictly-weaker milestone — the non-gap half of IN-6 (VAC) is nearly free, and center-vacuum multiplicity dies free under OS4 + center-invariant bc at `T=0`. **What it costs:** the other half (`UNI-lim`) is exactly `W1₀∧W1₁`, no cheaper than MG (no FKG in nonabelian gauge; the PF route is circular — it needs the gap). Honest position: **IN-6 = "G2⁻ in logic, G2-adjacent in proof cost"; the only honest "disguised G2" claim is a *cost* claim, never a *strength* claim.** And `ν` needs only OS0/1/3; OS4 names the vacuum, it does not create the spectral measure.**

**1. The poset (the load-bearing fact).** `MassGap` (`spec(H)∩(0,Δ)=∅`) ⇏ `MG` (hexagonmath E4), `MG` ⇏ atom (定理 B), but `MG` (exponential, bc-uniform) ⇒ OS4/MIX ⇒ `UNI` (L2 of uniqueness/REPORT, Dobrushin–Föllmer) ⇒ `VAC` (L4: Choquet extremality, `L²` fixed space one-dim). None of the arrows reverses. **OS4 is therefore strictly *weaker* than the gap.** This corrects the loose phrase "OS4 is gap-strength" in G1-CONTINUUM-OS §4: the precise relation is *gap ⇒ OS4, but OS4 ⇏ gap*. §4's substance holds — OS4 is **not a free relaxation in the YM proof** (to "name" `ν` presupposes one physical state = [CP-a], entailed by [CP-b]=gap+isolation). The correction is only that "entailed-by-gap" (downstream) ≠ "gap-strength" (equal).

**2. The three distinctions.**
- *(i) Vacuum unique among translation-invariant states* = `VAC`/`UNI`. **Half-free**: given a single limit that satisfies OS4/MIX, VAC follows (L4, no rate needed); the center `Z_N` factor is removed free by OS4+MIX with center-invariant periodic bc at `T=0` (uniqueness/REPORT §5.5.2) — the `Z_N` "vacua" are a finite-`T` artifact. **The real content is `UNI-lim`** (all subsequences → the *same* state), whose minimal sufficient input is per-sector **`W1₀∧W1₁`** (boundary screening, any rate), *not* MG.
- *(ii) Clustering of connected correlators* = OS4/MIX. **Two strengths, never conflated.** *Qualitative* clustering (any decay rate) equals (i) for a fixed state (ergodic ⇔ extremal) — the **weak** object, below the gap. *Exponential* clustering at rate `m` **is** the gap (`MG-inf`). Only the rate upgrades OS4 into G2.
- *(iii) θ-vacua / Gribov / instantons* — an **orthogonal axis**, not an OS4 obstruction. Gribov copies are gauge; Elitzur forces physics onto `𝒜_gi`, so they never multiply *physical* vacua. Topological charge `Q` gives superselection sectors; `θ` is a Bloch label `|θ⟩=Σe^{inθ}|n⟩`, blind to spacetime translations, so OS4 neither creates nor collapses θ. Decisively, the θ-term `e^{iθQ}` is a phase, so **reflection positivity (OS3) fails for generic `θ`** — the OS, RP construction *is* `θ∈{0,π}`. θ is thus not a uniqueness cost: RP already pins it.

**3. Does "the" `ν` need OS4?** No. For a *chosen* state, `ν_O(B)=⟨AΩ,P_{H_L}(B)AΩ⟩` exists by the spectral theorem on any `H_L≥0`; Källén–Lehmann needs only OS0/1/3 (G1-CONTINUUM-OS §1,§3). OS4 is needed solely to call `Ω` *the* physical vacuum (irreducibility/connectedness), not to manufacture `ν`: any OS0/1/3 state already carries its per-state spectral measure — you just cannot call it "the" vacuum's.

**4. Verdict on the G1/G2 split.** IN-6 is a **distinct third rung**: `G1 (IN-1) < IN-6 (UNI/OS4) < G2 (MassGap atom)`. It is **not G1** (G1 is subsequential, finite-volume; OS4 unformulable there) and **not G2 in logic** (`OS4 ⇏ gap`). It is "G2-adjacent" only because its input `W1₀∧W1₁` rides the same nonperturbative RG wall as MG (no FKG ⇒ no monotone shortcut; PF needs the gap). Buy: a separable, strictly-weaker milestone + the free VAC/center half. Cost: string-sector screening `W1₁`, MG-grade in difficulty though MG-sub-strength.
