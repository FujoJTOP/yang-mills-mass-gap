# CONTINUUM-3D-TO-4D — what exactly blocks extending rigorous 3D Yang–Mills quantisation to 4D

> **Verdict.** The wall is **two different walls under one name**. **(1)** The *method* dies on **marginality**: in d=3 the coupling is dimensionful (`[g²]=+½`), so `g²(a)→0`, finitely many trees, and regularity structures gives a **local-in-time** renormalised flow — no measure, no global, no gap. In d=4 `[g²]=0`, `g²(a)~1/(2β₀log)`, the scale-sum `Σg_j²` is **harmonic** and the subcritical degree condition fails, so no gauge-covariant BPHZ theorem exists. **(2)** But that marginal *combinatorial* wall is **not the true obstruction**: BRST leaves one dim-4 class and asymptotic freedom resums `Σg_j²` into the running coupling with `Σg_j⁴<∞` (PROOF-G1-BPHZ), so the log-sum closes. **(3)** What remains is a **new nonperturbative positivity** — the **Savvidy sign** of the second variation, invisible to BPHZ: YM's large field is *concave* (`−D²+2ad F₀` indefinite), Φ⁴'s is *convex*. So 3D→4D is the same marginal wall at the *method* level but a **different wall at the obstruction level**: the marginal-BPHZ wall is resolvable; the Savvidy wall is real and unproven.

**(1) The exact 3D result and method.** Chandra–Chevyrev–Hairer–Shen (CCHS), arXiv:2201.03487, *Invent. Math.* 237 (2024) 541, on `𝕋³`: a nonlinear metric state space `𝒮` of distributional connections, gauge-orbit quotient `𝔒`, and — via **regularity structures** — *local-in-time* solutions of the renormalised stochastic Yang–Mills–Higgs flow, gauge-covariant **in law**, unique counterterm (Chevyrev–Shen 2503.03060): a Markov process on `𝔒` **up to possible finite-time blow-up** — no global flow, no invariant measure, no OS, no gap, and Higgs mass `m>0` required. The state space is non-Banach because Chevyrev's **norm inflation** (*Stoch. PDE Anal. Comp.* 12 (2023) 1745) forbids any Banach space carrying the GFF and preserved by the DeTurck–YM flow — a **3D** statement, *not* a 4D impossibility. The only *complete measure* is 2D: CCHS 2006.04987 (Langevin dynamic; invariance only *conjectural* there) + **Chevyrev–Shen 2302.12160** (the actual invariance) + Bringmann–Cao 2305.07197 — so **even 3D has no pure-YM measure** (Cao–Chatterjee 2111.12813 is conditional on a GFF-like hypothesis).

**(2) The exact 4D obstruction.** *(a) Power counting.* `[g²]=0`; tree weights stop decaying, `Σ_{j≤J}g_j²~β₀⁻¹log J` is harmonic (not absolutely summable), and Hairer's degree condition (*Invent. Math.* 198) fails — regularity structures/paracontrolled require **strict subcriticality**. "One extra dimension" does **not** mean more counterterms: (ghost-#0, dim-4) BRST cohomology leaves only `{Tr F², Tr F F̃}`, a *single* marginal coupling whose log-sum diverges; the failing estimate is the **cutoff-uniform BPHZ/factorial bound**. *(b) The real wall.* After AF resummation the combinatorics closes; what does **not** is the **Savvidy-safe large-field / marginal log-concavity** `(★)`, the sign of `E[H_vv]−Var+W` on marginal modes (PROOF-G1-BPHZ §3). The Hessian `−D²+2ad F₀` is indefinite (Savvidy 1977; Nielsen–Olesen 1978), so large field is *less* convex, unlike Φ⁴'s `−Δ+m²+12λφ²⪰m²` — why the only solved marginal SPDE (4D Φ⁴, **trivial**: Aizenman–Duminil-Copin 1912.07973) is no template. And `∫Tr F²` coerces the curvature but not the connection (orbit/zero mode, P2 §3a): the global-in-time step 3D never takes.

**(3) Partial 4D results; how far the machinery reaches.** Honestly: the 3D machinery extends **essentially zero distance** into the interacting 4D marginal regime as a *construction*. The 4D partial results are *not* stochastic quantisation: Balaban's RG (CMP 95–122, 1984–88; e.g. 109 (1987) 249) gives UV-stability and small-field small-volume effective-action control; Magnen–Rivasseau–Sénéor (*CMP* 155 (1993)) build UV-renormalised Schwinger functions with Slavnov identities but **keep an IR cutoff** — neither yields a measure. There is **no** 4D stochastic-quantisation construct, not even fixed-time well-posedness of the white-noise SQE (marginal uniform-BPHZ unbuilt, W2); even the 2D measure (Bringmann–Cao–Hairer–Zhao 2611.03640) needed *dissipation* with no 4D avatar. The only unconditional 4D gap is lattice strong-coupling: Shen–Zhu–Zhu **2204.12737** (*CMP* 400 (2023) 805), `β<1/48`, via Log-Sobolev — the *opposite* regime to asymptotic freedom.

**Sharpest line.** In d=3 the wall is the marginal coupling and regularity structures stands; pushed up a dimension, the marginal *combinatorics* is resummed by asymptotic freedom rather than blocking — so the 3D→4D failure is **not** the marginal-BPHZ wall W3 first named, but a **new nonperturbative positivity**, the Savvidy sign. Φ⁴'s marginal case is solvable because its interaction is convex where the field is large; YM's is open because it is concave there — and gauge symmetry relocates that concavity rather than removing it.
