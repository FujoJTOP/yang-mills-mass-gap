# PROOF-QUANTUM-GROUP-DEFORM — q-deformed SU_q(N) at a root of unity: real aggregate positivity, but truncation-bound; the Weingarten sign returns at q→1 and no 4D propagating gap exists to transport

> 2026-11-23 · Forge of the 6th species (q-deformation). **Verdict: the positivity at a root of unity is real but *aggregate*, and it lives entirely inside the level truncation that q→1 removes.** Deform to U_q(su(N)) at `q=e^{iπ/(k+N)}`, where the representation category is the modular category SU(N)_k: every quantum dimension `[dim]_q>0` on the integrable range `n<k+N`, and the Turaev–Viro state sum is `Z_TV=|Z_WRT|²≥0` (Turaev 1991), i.e. `Σ[dim]_q²` by 6j orthogonality. That is a genuine positivity the signed SU(N) 6j/Weingarten symbols lack — but **per-6j-symbol nonnegativity, the premise "the q-6j symbols are positive", is NOT a theorem**: the unitary symbol is `Tet/√(positive)` and Tet is signed, with nonnegativity known only in restricted SU(2)/spin-foam regimes. The transport to q=1 fails at two independent joints, and the acid test fails on two horns. Net: a real new positivity, zero transport.

**(1) The model and the precise positivity.** For SU(N)_k the deformation is `q=e^{iπ/(k+N)}`, the category semisimple (WZW), and `[n]_q=0` exactly at `n=k+N` — so `qdim>0` holds *precisely on the truncated spectrum*, with the truncation edge a **zero** of the quantum dimension (qdim Weyl formula; Finkelberg). TV unitarity gives `Z_TV=|WRT|²≥0` (Witten; Reshetikhin–Turaev; Turaev–Viro; BHMV). Both are theorems; **neither is per-vertex sign-definiteness of the surface/6j weights**, which is what a positive Gibbs/FKG measure (PROOF-RANDOM-CLUSTER) would need.

**(2) q→1: the sign returns; the truncation edges are zeros.** `q→1` forces `k→∞`: the truncation lifts, Rep(U_q(su(N))) de-truncates to *all* SU(N) reps, and the Drinfeld classical limit sends the q-6j to the signed Wigner/Weingarten symbols. The classical Weingarten function carries `(−1)^{k+|p∨q|}` with **nonzero** leading coefficient (Collins–Śniady) and is genuinely negative (`Wg((1)(1))=−1/(N(N²−1))`). So the sign one is escaping *is the q→1 limit of the same symbols* — not an accident but the endpoint. The positive region is bounded by `[k+N]_q=0`: the continuation to q=1 meets a **literal zero** of the integrand at the truncation edge — the Fisher-zero/Pringsheim wall of PROOF-FISHER-ZERO-FREE reproduced in the q-plane, now with an explicit zero, so Pringsheim forbids continuing through it.

**(3) Wrong dimension class.** Established q-deformed lattice gauge theories exist only in `d≤3` and are (quasi)topological: Buffenoir–Roche's 2D construction (CMP **170**) reproduces 2D YM *because* 2D YM is quasitopological, and the coordinate-free extension notes the obstruction in `d>2` "where the theory is no longer topological." The `Z_TV`/BF gap is a **topological** gap (finite-dim Hilbert space), *not* a Källén–Lehmann atom `f₁²>0`. There is no 4D non-topological propagating q-deformed YM whose q≠1 gap could even be the target object.

**Acid test (YM-positive, free-field-negative: `f₁²=0`).** The construction is *theory-changing* across q=1, so it fails both ways. **Horn A (vacuous):** if the q≠1 residue transports *uniformly*, it assigns the abelian q-model — a gapped Z_k/TV TQFT — a residue regardless of the target; carried to the free photon (`f₁²=0`) that is a false positive, and the acid test **fails the tool**. **Horn B (inert):** if it correctly spares the free field (residue→0), the *same* group-independent mechanism — truncation-lift + sign-return, `[k+N]_q→0`, `(−1)^{k+|p∨q|}` — also kills the SU(N) residue at q=1. So it is either vacuous (wrong, topological, gap) or refuted by `f₁²(free)=0`. This is the invariant that killed the positivity/analyticity routes: reflection positivity and zero-free `Z` transport the *value*, never the residue *sign* (PROOF-LARGE-N-RIGOROUS (b)(ii); PROOF-FISHER-ZERO-FREE).

**Sharpest line.** The root-of-unity positivity is bought entirely by the truncation `[n]_q>0 (n<k+N)`, and the truncation edge `[k+N]_q=0` is a *zero* on the very path q→1; the Weingarten sign `(−1)^{k+|p∨q|}` you are bypassing *is* the q→1 specialization of the same 6j; and the only convergent q-gauge theories are topological, so the gap is the trivial TQFT gap, not a dynamical atom. The exact missing object: a **per-symbol-positive (positive-measure) 4D non-topological q-deformed gauge theory** whose q→1 limit both lifts the level truncation *and* preserves a modulus-square residue — a q-analog of the open sign problem, not a circumvention of it.
