# GKS-GAUGE — GKS/Griffiths/character-expansion positivity is the only proven route to a **lower** bound on the `trF²` amplitude, and it *is* strong coupling (P3): it lower-bounds the residue `f₁²`, not the spectral edge, and the `β=2.5` sign alternation provably kills the transport

> **判词 (verdict).** **(a)** A proven lower bound on the `0⁺⁺` `trF²` connected-correlator amplitude exists in *exactly one* regime — strong coupling `β<β₁≈1/48`, where the character coefficients `c_r(β)≥0` — and it is precisely PROOF-P3: `f₁²=c₁u^p(1−O(u))≥r₀(β)>0`, `V`- and `L`-uniform. But its engine is the **positive character expansion + KP/OS cluster resummation**, *not* a strict GKS inequality: GKS/FKG on the ferromagnetic (`c_r≥0`) polymer gas gives positivity/association of the **full** correlator (`Cov≥0`, the wrong sign for the H1 cross-term `X=−2Cov`) and never the connected amplitude; the amplitude needs the resummation, which P3 supplies. So no new GKS-type inequality lies beyond P3. **(b)** This lower-bounds the **amplitude/residue** `f₁²=ν({m₁²})`, not the **pole location** `m₁`: an atom-weight bound does not constrain `inf supp ν`. The strong-coupling *edge* bound `m₁≥m>0` comes from the complementary decay-rate **upper** bound `|C(t)|≤Ce^{−mt}` (OS gap), a different inequality; converting amplitude→edge needs the second-level gap `Λ` (residue–level-gap lemma), which is `I2`/isolation, open. **(c)** At `β=2.5` the SU(3) coefficients `c_r(β)` **alternate sign** (the sign problem); with `C8`'s explicit `Cov>0` minimal model and per-mode sign variation, the all-positive/ferromagnetic hypothesis is *provably false*, so GKS cannot be carried across the crossover. **Not 5/5 — score 2/5.** No fabricated closure.

## §1 What a lower bound on the amplitude is, and where positivity sits
Reflection positivity gives `ν≥0`, `f₁²=ν({m₁²})`; every PSD/Cauchy–Schwarz/Christoffel functional bounds `f₁²` from **above** (`f₁²≤1/K_n`, A4; EDGE-f1sq). A *lower* bound is the reversed sign — a Schur-convex coercion `total ≤ C·atom` — absent from anomaly/Ward/RP (EDGE-f1sq, B1). The **only** positivity that reverses the sign is thermodynamic: a configuration measure with **all polymer weights ≥0**, so the leading connected term is positive and the remainder is absolutely bounded. On the lattice this is the character expansion `e^{(β/N)Re tr U_□}=Σ_r c_r(β)χ_r`, `c_r≥0`.

## §2 (a) The one rigorous amplitude lower bound — and it is P3, not GKS
For `β<β₁`, `c_r(β)≥0`, `u:=c_f∈(0,1)`, `u≍β`. The connected-composite (Osterwalder–Seiler) expansion of the `0⁺⁺`-projected `trF²` correlator is a sum over link-connected polymers with **nonnegative** weights; KP convergence (`uK<1`, `K≤CN`, strip-uniform) makes the single fundamental tube the unique leading sector, `a₀=c₁u^p>0` the positive `f⊗f̄→1` recoupling, everything else `O(u)` higher. Hence **`f₁²≥r₀(β)>0` uniformly in `V,L`** (PROOF-P3, unconditional theorem). This is a genuine lower bound on the `trF²` amplitude — not a Wilson loop — but it lives at `β<β₁` and its proof is cluster expansion, *not* a GKS inequality. A strict GKS inequality on the same model returns the **full** correlator `≥0` and `Cov≥0`, which in the H1 split `G″=W_geo+Δ_dyn+X`, `X=−2Cov`, is the **wrong-signed** quantity (C5/C6). No GKS statement adds anything beyond P3.

## §3 (b) Amplitude ≠ edge
`f₁²≥r₀` fixes the atom **weight**, not its **position**: `ν` may carry that weight at any `m≥m₁`. The residue–level-gap lemma `f₁²≥(ΛM₀−M₁)/(Λ−m₁²)` runs amplitude *from* `Λ`, not the reverse; a positive atom does not lower-bound `inf supp` (GAP-G2-edge, PROOF-EDGE-E2). The strong-coupling **edge** bound is the decay-rate upper bound `m₁≥m>0` — a distinct direction, and it is the classically-known OS gap. Carrying *either* to `β=2.5` needs the analyticity wall broken, and amplitude→edge needs `Λ>R̄` = isolation = H1.

## §4 (c) `β=2.5` provably blocks the GKS transport
At `β=2.5`, SU(3) `c_r(β)` alternate in sign — the standard sign problem — so no all-positive (ferromagnetic) representation of the Wilson weight exists. By C8, even the minimal frustrated SU(2) plaquette has `Cov(G,Φ′)=0.22β²>0`, and on the full SU(3) lattice the per-mode cross term is sign-varying (`X∈[−0.09,+0.16]`). There is no order, no real-stability, no positivity structure to host GKS/FKG. The negative-sign regime therefore **provably** removes the hypothesis: positivity-based monotonicity in `β` cannot bridge `β<β₁→2.5`. Scope: this is a no-go for the *tool*, not a proof the amplitude is small at `2.5`. Large-`N` is also not a lower-bound regime — factorization suppresses the connected amplitude as `1/N²`.

## Five-point scorecard
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS.** Exact character expansion, all `β`; P3 bound is measure-level.
2. **Sign-controlling `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL.** Amplitude `≥r₀` is not a curvature bound; and `β<β₁` only.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL at target.** P3 is non-circular but confined to `β<β₁` (OS gap already known); amplitude→edge and transport both invoke `Λ`=H1.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS.** `r₀(β)→0` as `β→0`, `f₁²=0` at the free point; no spurious positivity.
5. **Uniform `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` over `[1/48,2.5]` — FAIL.** Killed at the `β=2.5` sign alternation; `1/N²` non-uniform.

**Sharpest line.** The lower-bound direction on the `trF²` amplitude is reachable, and *only* through the positivity of the character expansion — but that positivity is the strong-coupling fact `c_r(β)≥0`, whose consequence is exactly PROOF-P3's `f₁²≥r₀`, not a GKS inequality and not an edge bound. It pins the **residue**, not the **pole**; the pole lower bound at strong coupling is the complementary decay-rate (upper) bound, and `β=2.5` provably has no ferromagnetic representation to carry either. The missing object is unchanged: amplitude→edge is `Λ`=isolation=H1. **Score 2/5; not 5/5.**
