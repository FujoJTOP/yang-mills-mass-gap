The brief: write the Gaussian reference `e^{−½⟨ξ,C⁻¹ξ⟩}det C` as a boson–Grassmann integral, use its translation invariance `ξ→ξ+εψ` to shuttle weight between measure and potential, and hope the Haar×Wilson measure becomes `Gaussian×(1+positive)` with a mass-gap bound. **判词: the identity is exact, the positivity is not.** On compact Haar there is no Faddeev–Popov determinant to Grassmannize (finite, normalized gauge volume), so the SUSY factor degenerates to the Gaussian normalisation; the exact Ward identity it yields is the divergence/Stein identity, and its only remainder is the non-quadratic Wilson term plus the non-convex Haar `−log J`, whose sign *is* the interaction. It passes #1 (nonperturbative) and #4 (non-SUSY; free field is the trivial `R≡0`, `G″=0` fixed point) and fails #2/#3/#5. **§1 What the translation is, exactly.** Take a reference Gaussian `μ_C`, `C=(−D²+2ad F_B+m²)⁻¹`, and represent `det C=∫e^{−ψ̄C⁻¹ψ}Dψ̄Dψ`. The combined measure on `(ξ,ψ,ψ̄)` is invariant under the BHH translation `δξ=εψ`, `δψ̄=−εC⁻¹ξ`, `δψ=0`; for **any** potential `V`, `∫δF·e^{−V}dμ=0`. Two exact consequences hold at every coupling: the **Stein identity** `E[V′]−E[ξV]=0` and the **covariance identity** `E[ξξ]=C+E[ξ V′]`. This is the whole content — a rewriting, not an estimate.

**§2 Where the "positive remainder" dies.** **(a) The reference covariance needs positivity.** The natural covariance at a Savvidy background is `C_m=(−D²+2ad F_B+m²)⁻¹`; it is a probability only for `m²>g|B|`, since `λ_min(−D²+2ad F_B)=−g|B|<0` (PROOF-SAVVIDY). At `m=0` the Gaussian is **indefinite**, `det C_m` is signed (one negative mode per unstable direction). Any BHH **lower** bound on `G″` is a Gaussian-domination/Brascamp–Lieb bound, which needs `Hess V⪰c>0` — precisely the Savvidy wall, false on the open cell. **(b) No determinant to Grassmannize.** In a gauge-fixed continuum measure the Grassmann factor *is* the Faddeev–Popov determinant and the SUSY translation *is* the BRST/Slavnov–Taylor identity. Compact Haar×Wilson has **no** gauge fixing, so the SUSY factor is trivial and the identity carries no gauge content beyond §1; the BRST it would encode is not exact at finite spacing (Ginsparg–Wilson). The remainder `R_m=(S_W−½ξH_Bξ)+Φ` is non-quadratic, and `Φ=−log J` is non-convex with `Cov(G,Φ′)` sign-varying (C6), minimal plaquette positive (C8) — not sign-definite.

**§3 The gap equation it writes is H1.** The self-consistent mass is `m²=G″_m(0)`, `G″_m(0)=m²+[Δ_m]`, where `Δ_m` is a ratio of Grassmann/determinant correlators. But `G″≈1/Var(a_0)` and `G″=E[H]−Var+W`, so `m_eff²(0)≥λ ⟺ Var(a_0)≤1/λ`, and `Δ_m≥0` **is** H1. The Ward identity is C8's `E[G′]−Var(G)=G″`; BHH is H1 in Grassmann dress. It is non-circular only if `Δ_m` is bounded independently — impossible without `H⪰0`.

**§4 Free-field acid test.** Free massless: `X̄=0`, `S_W` quadratic, `C=(−Δ)⁻¹`, `R≡0`; the translation is a genuine symmetry and the identity gives `G″=0`. So free is the **trivial fixed point** `m²=0`, and structural selectivity lives entirely in the sign of `R`. A uniform `G″≥λ>0` over a class containing free is false; on YM `λ>0` requires `R≥0`, the very sign C6/C8 show is not uniform. The species cannot pass #2 and #4 simultaneously on the actual measure.
