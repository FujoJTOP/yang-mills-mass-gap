# PROOF-SYMPLECTIC-REDUCTION — Hamiltonian reduction (Marsden–Weinstein) and Duistermaat–Heckman localization vs the gap

> 2026-11-23 · 4D pure SU(N) Yang–Mills, Hamiltonian form. Target: Gauss's law is a moment map μ on the symplectic phase space, so the physical space is the quotient μ⁻¹(0)/𝒢; does the *geometry* of the reduced Hamiltonian — its spectrum, Duistermaat–Heckman / equivariant localization — give the gap? **Verdict: the reduction is rigorous and houses the problem exactly (spec H_red = mass spectrum), but it is a reformulation, not a lever.** (i) The quotient is *singular* (stratified symplectic, Gribov/reducible strata) and its *reduced potential is Savvidy-indefinite* — the negatives are orbit-transverse, so the concavity passes to the quotient unchanged. (ii) DH/equivariant localization outputs an *absolutely continuous* classical Liouville density, atom-free by construction; f₁² is an atom of the *quantum* measure, and the energy is the moment map of a *non-compact* ℝ flow, where DH exactness fails (H1-T7). (iii) Acid test: free Maxwell's reduced space is Gaussian and exactly localizable, yet f₁²=0 — the output is zero there *because it is zero everywhere*: vacuous. Wall = H1.

**(1) The reduction and the reduced Hamiltonian.** P=T*𝒜, ω=∫tr δE∧δA; 𝒢 acts and Gauss's law is μ(A,E)=D_A∗E. Physical space = μ⁻¹(0)/𝒢; on the lattice P=T*G^E with μ the summed electric momenta per site. Both YM terms are 𝒢-invariant, so H=(g²/2)ΣE²+(1/g²)Σ(1−Re tr U_p) descends and L²(μ⁻¹(0)/𝒢) is the gauge-invariant space; spec H_red *is* the mass spectrum, exact, no slice. But 0 is not regular with free action: reducibles carry stabilizers and Singer forbids a global slice, so only *singular/stratified* reduction (Sjamaar–Lerman) applies — stratum-wise symplectic, not a manifold. The reduced potential V_red=(1/g²)Σ(1−Re tr U_p) has Hessian = the gauge-transverse Hessian, measured 7.1% negative (min −0.82β) at β=2.5 (W4 Q⊥); those negatives are orbit-orthogonal (PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES: orbit = exact null), hence *physical*, hence inside the reduced tangent space. ω carries no metric, no sign: H_red≥0 is a Kähler fact reduction neither creates nor preserves. It relocates the concavity; it does not cure it.

**(2) Localization → spectrum/gap.** DH: for compact (M,ω) with a Hamiltonian *torus* action, ∫_M e^{i⟨ξ,Φ⟩}ω^n/n! is the fixed-point sum (exact stationary phase) and Φ_*(ω^n/n!) is piecewise-polynomial — *AC, atom-free*. Three obstructions. (a) The energy H_red is the moment map of *time-translation ℝ*, non-compact; DH exactness needs a torus and fails. Compactifying time gives Tr e^{−βĤ} as a one-loop index *only if* Q²=∂_t exists — pure YM has none (H1-T7); the fixed-point formula is then semiclassical, the fluctuation determinant *being* the dynamics. (b) An exact DH pushforward is the **classical** Liouville density; the gap is the bottom of the **quantum** spectral measure, f₁² an atom of it — the classical→quantum passage is the positive Kähler polarization, where the sign/atom information sits. (c) Being AC, the DH measure cannot carry a δ-atom, so f₁² is structurally invisible — the SCHUR-CONVEX cone.

**(3) Acid test — free field.** Free Maxwell (g=0): μ linearizes (∂·E=0), gauge removes longitudinal modes; the reduced space is the cotangent of divergence-free connections, a *Gaussian* symplectic vector space, H_red=Σ_k|k|(a†_k a_k+½) — the ideal DH/Gaussian case with exact Gaussian partition function and density of states. Its 0⁺⁺ (two-transverse-photon) spectrum is the cut [0,∞), f₁²=0; a *gapped* free Proca/scalar composite also has f₁²=0 (PROOF-F1SQ). **Acid-test result: FAIL (vacuous).** Exact reduced geometry + exact localization coexist with f₁²=0; the output is atom-free at every g, avoiding a false free-field gap only because it produces no atom ever — hence nothing on YM either, so it cannot distinguish YM from free field.

**Sharpest line.** The physical space is rigorously the (singular) symplectic quotient and spec H_red is the mass spectrum — the language is right. But reduction relocates, not removes, the Savvidy concavity (negatives orbit-transverse = physical; reduced potential indefinite), and DH/equivariant localization is sign-blind *and* atom-blind: it returns an AC classical Liouville density, whereas the gap/f₁² is the atom at the *metric* edge of the *quantum* reduced space, a Kähler datum neither ω nor its DH pushforward carries. The free Gaussian case is exactly localizable and still f₁²=0; constructing the gap here means quantizing the singular quotient at strong coupling — the edge atom H*(r₀), the H1 wall in orbital coordinates.
