# PROOF-G1-BPHZ — the marginal 4D gauge BPHZ theorem: what closes, the exact residue, why the three routes fail

> **Verdict (判词).** **Not closed.** The marginal *combinatorial* core closes as a theorem: BRST leaves one operator, and asymptotic freedom resums the marginal log into `g_j²`, so the residual scale-sum `Σg_j⁴` converges and the "infinitely many zero-weight trees" never form a wall. What does not close is one nonperturbative **positivity** — the Savvidy-safe large-field / per-shell marginal log-concavity (★). It is not a log, an anomaly, or the zero mode; it is the *sign of a second variation*. None of the three routes (log-critical regularity structures, Langevin smoothing, BRST-BPHZ) removes it: each reduces to (★).

**§1 The exact theorem needed (T_uni).** For every `n`, compact `K`, along the RT `β(a)=2N/g²(a)`, `g²(a)∼1/(2β₀log(1/aΛ))`, there are renormalised moments `S^{(n)}_{L,a}` — counterterms in the ghost-#0, dim-4 BRST class `{Tr F², Tr F F̃}` (θ is a total derivative and drops), finite scheme-C constants fixed by L1, the trap tracking `(g_j, Z_j)` and not a single coupling —
`|S^{(n)}_{L,a}(f_1,…,f_n)| ≤ C_n(L)∏‖f_i‖_{C^k}`, **uniform in `a`**, plus the Minlos factorial bound. This is exactly `UVS(L)`; L2 gives it *order by order*. T_uni is its **a-uniform resummation over all `J∼log(1/a)` scales**. It is what IN-1 needs, and closing it removes only G1's `L3`.

**§2 What closes.** (a) Operator space finite (L2/W3(0)). (b) Scale-sum: `g_j²=1/(2β₀j)+O(j⁻²)`; the naive wall `Σg_j²∼β₀⁻¹log J` *is* the running coupling and is absorbed exactly; every residual power is absolutely summable, `Σ_j g_j⁴=(16β₀⁴)⁻¹ζ(4)<∞`, `δg_J=O(J^{-3/2}log J)→0`. This is the AF analogue of the Bauerschmidt–Brydges–Slade log-resummation and is a theorem given L2; it kills W3's "non-summable log-sum" as the obstruction. (c) The literal **absolute** BPHZ bound `Σ_{trees}|weight|<∞` is nonetheless *false* (the asymptotic renormalon), so T_uni must be proved as a **flow trapping region**, not an order sum — a sharpening: the wrong theorem is easier to refute, the right one is what (★) is about.

**§3 The exact missing estimate.** Uniformly in `j≤J`, for every normalised gauge-invariant marginal collective mode `v`,
```
G″_j(v) := E_{μ_j}[H_{vv}|a=0] − Var_{μ_j}(G_v|a=0) + W(v) ≥ δ_W > 0 ,
```
`H=Hess S_W`, `G=∇S_W`, `W` the Haar/Jacobian curvature (Lemma B: `W≥δ_W`, SU2 ≥½g, SU3 ≥¾g). Equivalently the invariant-region conditional `Var_{μ_j}(Pᵀ∇S|a=0) ≤ E_{μ_j}[PᵀHP|a=0]−δ_W+o(1)`, equivalently the BBS step `‖R_{j+1}‖_X ≤ e^{−c}‖R_j‖_X + Cg_j⁴` with `c,C` independent of `j,a,L`, **provided** `√(2g_j|F_B|)<μ_j` on the trapped region. The rate `e^{−c}` is extracted from the free semigroup `e^{−t(−Δ)}`; the background shift `−Δ→−D²+2ad F_B` carries the Savvidy eigenvalue `−2g_j|F_B|` on the cone `|k|<√(2g_j|F_B|)`, turning the contraction into amplification exactly on the large-field region the LF bound must control.

**§4 Why each route fails.** *(i) Log-critical RS / paracontrolled.* Log-scaling absorbs the marginal weights, but RS's uniform BPHZ bound **is** the subcritical multiplicity estimate; log-scaling replaces it by the exact flow, i.e. by (★). RS is pathwise/local and never meets the background Hessian. *(ii) Langevin.* The YM flow is semilinear parabolic with linear part `−Δ` — **the same** smoothing Φ⁴ already has; the nonlinearity has the identical marginal degree. The only gauge-specific analytic gain is BRST nilpotency ⇒ empty anomaly slot (already L2). Gauge projection does **not** delete Savvidy (W4: 7–8% negative survives). "Trivial like Φ⁴" is a category error: φ⁴'s large-field bound closes because its Hessian is `−Δ+m²+12λφ²⪰m²` — large field is **more** convex; YM's is `⪰−2g|F_B|` — large field is **less** convex. Φ⁴₄ triviality (Aizenman–Duminil-Copin) is an IR/Gaussian-endpoint statement about fixed dimensionless coupling; it does not transfer as an obstruction to a UV limit whose scale Λ is transmuted in the IR. *(iii) BRST-BPHZ.* Ward identities are benign (L2), but they constrain *counterterm structure*, not the Hessian sign.

**§5 Outcome.** IN-1 stays conditional on `L3=(★)∧band-decay`; nothing is removed. (★) is **not** implied by coercivity `∫Tr F²≥0` (bounds the value, not the Hessian), by cone-emptiness `E[H]>0` (`Var≥0` subtracts: `G″≤E[H]`), or by gauge invariance (Savvidy survives projection). Were (★) closed, G1-EXISTENCE-THEOREM becomes unconditional at fixed volume (subsequential `μ_L`, OS0/1/3) with L3 discharged — **not** the gap, **not** `L→∞`.

**Sharpest line.** Φ⁴'s marginal case is solvable because its interaction is convex where the field is large; YM's marginal case is open because its interaction is concave there — and gauge symmetry relocates that concavity rather than removing it.
