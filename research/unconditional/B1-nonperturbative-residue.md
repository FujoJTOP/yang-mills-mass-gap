# B1 — a NONPERTURBATIVE structure (undefined at g=0) as a lower bound on the residue f₁²

> 2026-11-23 · The free-field no-go (EDGE-f1sq; SCHUR-CONVEX) kills every *protected, g-continuous* functional: it is provable at g=0 where f₁²=0, so it cannot lower-bound the residue (`1≤0`). The prescribed escape is a structure *not defined / trivial at g=0*. **Verdict: all four natural candidates genuinely evade the no-go, yet none lower-bounds f₁² — each blocked by an independent wall. The decisive obstruction is not g-continuity; it is the sign forced by ν≥0 together with a channel/operator mismatch, and these survive the relaxation to nonperturbative input.** No fabricated proof.

## What a lower bound must be
`f₁²≥r₀` requires a *same-channel* total bounded **above** by the atom, `T ≤ C·f₁²` (the Schur-convex coercion). The no-go: a `g`-continuous `T` obeying this at g=0 gives `T(0)≤0`, but `T(0)=M₀=1>0`, `f₁²(0)=0` ⇒ `1≤0`. So `T` must fail/trivialize at g=0. Evading the no-go is *necessary*, not sufficient.

## Four candidates — each evades the no-go, none delivers
**1. Z_N center / 't Hooft flux.** The order parameter ('t Hooft loop, 1-form-center phase) is trivial (=1) at g=0 — Gaussian measure, degenerate flux sectors — so it is genuinely nonperturbative. But it is a **nonlocal, static** observable (source confinement / string tension), while f₁² is the residue of a **local** 0⁺⁺ interpolator tr F². Sharper (GAP-IN11 §5): center vortices are adjoint-blind (N-ality-0 ⇒ phase factor 1) and 't Hooft flux yields only *sector/moment* data (`χ_t`, degeneracy) — never an edge mass. Wrong operator *and* wrong type.

**2. Instanton / topological charge.** χ_t→0 at g=0 (essential singularity) — evades. But χ_t is a `M_{-1}` **moment** (same channel ⇒ `f₁²≤χ_t m₁²`, upper, GAP-IN11); it lives in the 0⁻⁺ / θ-vacuum-energy sector, not the 0⁺⁺ residue (THETA-PI: `E(θ)` pins vacuum energy, not `m_{0⁺⁺}`); the ℝ⁴ gas is IR-divergent / non-dilute and χ_t>0 is unproven in continuum (W2). Wrong sign, cross-channel, non-rigorous.

**3. Measure singularity (Gribov horizon).** Nonperturbative (no FP determinant at g=0), but a *field-space / gauge-fixing* object; the gauge-invariant residue is insensitive — one deforms the measure near the horizon without moving an atom to m₁².

**4. Dimensional transmutation.** `Λ=μe^{−8π²/b₀g²}` (→0 at g=0) is the one **same-channel** nonperturbative structure. But "Λ lower-bounds the 0⁺⁺ residue" *is* the gap: it needs a stable glueball pole below the two-gluon threshold. Not an independent lever (W2).

## The real obstruction — it survives dropping g-continuity
The sign wall is **ν≥0** (reflection positivity), not g-continuity: every positivity/anomaly total is `∫φ dν` with `φ≥0`, hence `T ≥` atom ⇒ an **upper** bound on f₁² (GAP-IN11 moment lemma). Nonperturbative input does not make ν indefinite, so it cannot flip the sign; and center / instanton / horizon are cross-channel or moment data (GAP-IN11 §5, THETA-PI). The missing object is unchanged: a **same-channel, nonperturbative** coercion `T ≤ C·f₁²` — verbatim the stable-0⁺⁺-glueball statement, i.e. `H*(r₀)`, the gap.

**Bottom line.** No nonperturbative order parameter lower-bounds the residue from present structures: center/'t Hooft (nonlocal, moment data), instanton (wrong sign, cross-channel, IR-divergent), horizon (measure-insensitive), dimensional transmutation (the gap renamed). Evading the free-field no-go is necessary but not sufficient — the sign-plus-channel obstruction is *deeper* than the no-go, and `f₁²>0` *is* the bound-state-pole / gap statement.
