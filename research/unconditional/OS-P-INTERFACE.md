## §1. The load-bearing fact: the OS inner product is not `L²(μ)`, but the plane slice is special

A generic `L²(μ)`-orthogonal projection is **not** OS-orthogonal. The OS inner product weights by `θ`; an `L²(μ)` projection `P` is OS-orthogonal only if it commutes with the `θ`-twisted structure. The whole question turns on whether the **spatial** coarse projection escapes this generic failure. It does, and here is the exact reason.

**Lemma 1 (plane restriction of the OS form).** `[真定理]` For `F, G ∈ A_0`, `⟨F,G⟩_OS = ⟨F,G⟩_{L²(μ_0)}`.

*Proof.* MP site reflection fixes every time-0 spatial link **pointwise** (and does **not** reverse it — `os3` §1.2 table, row "`(i,j)` faces, `k=0` fixed, not reversed"; spatial links at the plane are `θ`-invariant). Hence `F∘θ = F` for `F ∈ A_0`, and
`⟨F,G⟩_OS = ∫ \overline{F(θω)}\,G(ω)dμ = ∫ \overline{F(ω_0)}\,G(ω_0)dμ = ∫ \overline{F}\,G\,dμ_0`. ∎

So on the plane slice, the OS form **coincides** with the honest `L²(μ_0)` form, and `A_0 ↪ H_phys` is an **isometric** embedding.

**Lemma 2 (`E[·|A_0]` is the OS-orthogonal projection onto the plane).** `[真定理]` `P_0 := E[·\,|\,A_0]` is the `⟨·,·⟩_OS`-orthogonal projection of `H_phys` onto `\overline{A_0}`.

*Proof.* `P_0` is idempotent and (by Lemma 1, symmetric display) self-adjoint for `⟨·,·⟩_OS`. For `F ∈ A_+`, `G ∈ A_0`:
```
⟨F − P_0F, G⟩_OS = ∫ \overline{(F − P_0F)(θω)}\,G(ω)dμ = ∫ G(φ_0)\big[\overline{E[F∘θ|A_0]} − \overline{P_0F}\big]dμ_0 .
```
Now `θ` is measure-preserving and preserves `A_0`, so `E[F∘θ|A_0] = E[F|A_0] = P_0F`; the bracket vanishes. Hence `F − P_0F ⊥ A_0`, so `P_0` is the orthogonal projection. ∎

*(Numerically verified, §7 table: the OS-tangent defect `‖⟨φ_1 − E[φ_1|φ_0], φ_0⟩_OS‖` is `≤ 3.6×10^{−16}` across three parameter sets — machine zero.)*

**Hazard (why "spatial" is essential).** For a coarse variable on a **generic** time slice `t ≠ 0`, `θ V_t = V_{−t}` (or `V_{−1−t}`) is a **different** slice, so `σ(V_t)` is not `θ`-invariant; `E[·|V_t]` does **not** commute with `θ` and is **not** OS-orthogonal — it need not descend. This is the precise content of "the time-symmetric projection behaves better": the spatial block on the **reflection plane** is the good case; a generic `L²(μ)` projection is the bad case.
