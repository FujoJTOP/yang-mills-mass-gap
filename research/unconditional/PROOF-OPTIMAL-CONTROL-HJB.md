# PROOF-OPTIMAL-CONTROL-HJB — transfer matrix as a controlled diffusion: the HJB returns the vacuum, the gap is the min-max, and the control is pinned on Savvidy

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict. Optimal control / HJB is a faithful *re-presentation* of the gap, not a proof.** **(i) Ground-state HJB** (quadratic-cost control; Donsker–Varadhan): the value function is the **principal** eigenvalue `E₀` — in physical time, the Langevin *fictitious-time* rate — an **object mismatch** (H1-T1), bridged to the mass gap only by LSI⇒clustering via Bakry–Émery, dead at β=2.5 (W6). **(ii) Gap as min-max.** `Δ=a⁻¹(E₁−E₀)≥m` is a **lower** bound; a single feedback yields only the **Rayleigh upper** bound `Δ≤R(ψ)/a` — exactly LATTICE-TM-GAP's `≈1/a` (P4 §2). A lower bound needs the inequality **uniform over all controls** = Poincaré/LSI = H1; its optimizer is `ψ₁`, the first-excited eigenfunction — **the controller is the eigenvector: circular.** **(iii) Convexifying control** (Bakry–Émery HJB). `λ₁≥ρ` needs `Hess(S_W+W)⪰ρ`; the control must beat the Savvidy negative `−D²+2adF`, depth `−g|B|` and count `∝∫|F|³` both **unbounded in the field**, so the HJB has **no finite value function at β=2.5** — control is forced pointwise onto the unstable mode and diverges. **Acid test.** The min-max form returns `λ₁(free)=0`, `G″=S−D=0` *exactly* (`f₁²=0`) — passing only by **being** the gap inequality; the quadratic-cost HJB returns `0` for free but `+∞` for YM; the vacuum HJB solves the wrong eigenvalue. **Net: the HJB reduces to LSI/H1; the optimal controller is the Savvidy mode.**

**(1) The control formulation.** `T=e^{−aH}` on `L²(𝒜/𝒢,μ̄)`, `H` the loop-time Schrödinger operator; `Δ` its gap. Gauging by `ψ>0` (Doob/h-transform, Hopf–Cole) gives the conservative generator `L_ψ f=ψ⁻¹H(ψf)−(Hψ/ψ)f`; for `ψ₀` the PF ground state `H−E₀=ψ₀L_{ψ₀}ψ₀⁻¹`, so `gap(L_{ψ₀})=gap(H)`. The controller **is** the gauge `ψ`.

**(2) HJB and its viscosity bound.** Ergodic/additive-eigenvalue HJB: `ℋ(x,∇V)=λ`, `ℋ(x,p)=sup_u{p·b(x,u)−½|u|²}`; the comparison principle (Crandall–Lions) brackets `λ` by sub/supersolutions. This is rigorous and pins the **ground state** `E₀`. The gap is `λ₁=inf_{f⊥ψ₀}R(f)`; the constraint makes the HJB **non-convex**, the comparison principle fails, and the optimizer is `ψ₁` itself — so a constructive single-feedback bound returns `R(f)≥E₁`, an **upper** bound.

**(3) Where the control is forced — Savvidy.** `λ₁≥ρ` needs a control Lyapunov function with `Hess(S_W+W)⪰ρ`, i.e. `Hess W` exceeding each unstable rate of `−D²+2adF`. Those are the Savvidy ladder `λ_min=−g|B|`, with `N_neg∝(gB)²V` and weight `∝∫|F|³` (PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN) — **unbounded depth and count**; `W_geo≈+0.22–0.31` is ≳10× short. No finite HJB value function; the Riccati equation with indefinite `Hess S_W` (no `Q⪰0`) has no stabilizing solution. **The control is pinned to the wall.**

**(4) Acid test — mandatory.** Free massless: `λ₁=0` (marginal `k→0`, `λ_min=(4/a²)sin²(πa/L)`), `G″=S−D=0` (INVENT-VARIANCE-INEQ §4), `f₁²=0` (no atom). *Min-max/Poincaré:* `inf_{f⊥1}R(f)=0` at the zero controller ⇒ returns `0`: **passes exactly**, but the returned inequality **is** LSI=H1. *Quadratic-cost HJB:* Gaussian at the free point ⇒ `0`; at β=2.5 indefinite ⇒ `+∞`: **vacuous on the YM side.** *Vacuum HJB:* wrong eigenvalue. Only min-max passes, and it passes by **being** the gap inequality.

**(5) Exact missing step.** Uniform in controls and `(a,L)`: `Hess(S_W+W)⪰ρ>0` beating Savvidy — provable with `ρ=N/12` at β=0 and (SZZ) for `β<1/48`, open at β=2.5, and **equal to H1**. No slack added.

**Sharpest line.** Optimal control names the gap correctly — the controller `u*=∇log ψ₁` is the first-excited eigenvector — and returns, in cleaner words, the two facts already banked: a constructive feedback bounds the gap only from above (Rayleigh, `≈1/a`), while a *lower* bound is uniform over all controls and **is** LSI/H1; the convexifying control's curvature demand `∑g|B|` grows without bound on the Savvidy modes, so it cannot exist at β=2.5. The optimal controller is the Savvidy-unstable mode, and taming it uniformly is the wall.
