# T3 — Rigorous 4D Yang–Mills construction: the true frontier

**Verdict: OPEN for a complete theory; PARTIAL for existence.** No peer-reviewed complete route exists. The 2026 Lean claim (OpenAI; Gross committee) asserts existence only — no unique infinite-volume limit, no mass gap — and is unrefereed with full proof unreleased; our audit reduces it to 16 inputs (`IN-0`).

**Balaban** (CMP 109 (1987) 249; 116 (1988) 1; 122 (1989) 175, 355). Proved UV stability of 4D lattice gauge partition functions at small coupling: block-spin RG, small-field effective actions, coupling-constant renormalization, large-field R-operation bounds. Did **not** prove convergence of the expansion, gauge-invariant local Schwinger functions, the infinite-volume limit, OS axioms, or a gap. It is a UV-control machine, not a construction.

**Magnen–Rivasseau–Sénéor** (CMP 155 (1993) 325). SU(2) Schwinger functions with an IR cutoff and no UV cutoff, axial gauge, trivial topological sector; Slavnov identities verified. Gauge-fixed, not gauge-invariant; no OS reconstruction, no gap.

**Stochastic quantization / regularity structures.** Rigorous in 2D YM (arXiv:2006.04987), 2D paracontrolled (arXiv:2305.07197), and local 3D YM–Higgs dynamics (arXiv:2201.03487); survey arXiv:2202.13359. Full 3D pure YM and all of 4D remain open. In 4D marginality forces infinitely many counterterms; no gauge-covariant BPHZ/BRST renormalization or state space is known. No pathwise 4D gauge result.

**OS for gauge.** Lattice reflection positivity is proved (Osterwalder–Seiler, Ann. Phys. 110 (1978) 440; Menotti–Pelissetto, CMP 113 (1987) 369), but continuum OS reconstruction needs a gauge-invariant algebra; gauge-fixed limits do not supply it.

**Strong coupling.** Lattice mass gap in any d (arXiv:2204.12737); random-surface expansions (arXiv:2307.06790). Neither reaches the continuum.

**Claims.** arXiv:2307.00788 is unrefereed; arXiv:2506.00284 was withdrawn; neither is accepted.

**Exact missing piece.** A non-perturbative gauge-invariant RG proving, uniformly in cutoff and volume: (i) coercivity/large-field control (Balaban's missing uniform bound); (ii) convergence to a unique vacuum; (iii) OS0–OS4 with reflection positivity/ergodicity; (iv) volume-uniform exponential clustering (= gap, `IN-3/4`). Core obstruction: the marginal 4D flow with infinitely many counterterms. **No current method is believed sufficient without a genuinely new idea;** gauge-covariant regularity structures is the most active hope but has no 4D renormalization theory.
