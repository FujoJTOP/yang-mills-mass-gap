# PROOF-IR-H1 — does the global contraction close the gap, or rename H1?

> **Verdict. NO — proving the global contraction does not close the mass gap.** The repaired machinery (block-local H2 **proven** + H3′ **free** + the inter-block contraction) is *entirely the fiber-side (UV) half* of an exact orthogonal decomposition. The theorem's coarse-side hypothesis **H1 is absent from that list because no repaired step discharges it — and none can: H1 ⟺ `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` ⟺ the gap** (PROVE-Gpp §5; W6-induced-mass). The route is **not formally circular** (H1 is strictly stronger than the conclusion; `H1∧H2-global ⇒ Δ≥m` is valid), but as a path to the Clay gap it is a **RENAME, not a closure** — its terminal obligation is the gap. **PROOF-H2-GLOBAL can never close it** — and at β=2.5 the contraction fails anyway (`C(β)≈15≫1`).

## 1. The split the route never crosses
Disintegration is exact: `Var_μ(F)=E_ν[Var_{μ(·|U')}(F)] + Var_ν(E_μ[F|U'])`, with gradients splitting **orthogonally**, `|∇F|²=|∇_∥F|²+|∇_⊥F|²` (CONDITIONAL Setup; PROVE-H3(a)). The fiber term is bounded by `1/Λ_UV` (H2), the coarse term by `1/λ` (H1). Block-local H2 bounds `Var_ξ` at fixed `U'` (`∇_⊥` only); H3′ is structural; the contraction globalizes the **fiber** LSI. **All three act on `∇_⊥`.** H1 acts on `∇_∥` (the coarse marginal over the fiber). No inequality converts a `∇_⊥` bound into a `∇_∥` bound: the list omits H1 because the machinery *is* the wrong half.

## 2. Does the contraction close it? No.
Even granting `C(β)<1`, it yields only H2-global — a **UV** statement at fixed `U'`, the high-band fiber LSI (PROOF-H2-GLOBAL; H2-TIEBREAKER). The system gap still needs the coarse IR Poincaré, which is untouched; `m=min(λ/2,…)≤0` because H1 fails at the soft mode. And `C(β)≳15` at β=2.5 (one shared face gives `2β=5>1`), so it does not even discharge H2-global.

## 3. Circular or rename? — adjudication
**Not logically circular.** SLC ⇒ Poincaré, not conversely; H1 is *strictly stronger* than `Δ≥m`, and no hypothesis is the conclusion. But it is a **rename with zero slack**: nothing sits strictly between H1 and the gap. The minimal hypothesis that closes the coarse term — a coarse Poincaré constant `1/λ` — *is* "the mass gap in measure language" (P4 §4). Weakening H1 to "coarse Poincaré" replaces it by the gap; strengthening does not help. The only exit is an **independent** proof.

## 4. Can the block-local gap *supply* H1? — the attempt and its failure
No, structurally. The fiber gap is a **fast-mode** gap; the coarse mode is **slow** — a gapped fast mode does not gap the slow mode (adiabatic elimination controls the memory kernel, not the slow-mode stiffness). H2 gives a **variance** bound `Λ_UV=e^{−2βN_p}/C_G` (≲`e^{−5N_p}`, astronomically loose); H1 needs the **curvature** `Hess(−log ν)`, fine information a variance bound lacks. Iterating to `k→0` *is* the RG flow: one step leaves `β_eff≈β≫1`; Nachtergaele's per-scale `γ_j` at growing blocks equals the `k→0` transverse mass = H1 (IDEA-finite-size), while the block gap supplies only the base `γ_0`; driving `β_eff<1` is the flow to the area law = the gap (IN-9). **H1 is not supplied; the induced mass stays an independent assumption.**

## 5. The exact additional input — and it is not provable
One needs, from *outside* the spectral route, a uniform-in-`(V,a)` proof of `G″(0)>0` at the softest mode (`m_eff²(0)≥λ`): a gauge-covariant nonperturbative RG with `m_eff²` flowing positive (BREAK-IN-1 / GAP-G2-mass route 1), or a constructive spectral-edge theorem (route 2). Neither exists. The one concrete handle is the numeric sign of `G″(0)` (W6 foothold; VAR-FINAL's "only clean decider"), currently `+0.043−0.208=−0.165` (DEAD-lean) — evidence, not theorem, and a *uniform* sign **is** G2 itself.

**Sharpest line.** A correct implication `[H1∧H2-global]⇒Δ≥m` whose every proved or provable piece (block H2, H3′, any global contraction) lies on the fiber side of an exact orthogonal split; the coarse side is H1, and the coarse Poincaré that would close it *is* the mass gap in measure language. Proving the global contraction relocates the gap by zero distance: a RENAME, not a PROOF, and gap #7 stands.
