# FORGE5-BOOTSTRAP-EXCLUDE — a rigorous conformal SDP cannot exclude the gapless YM point, and cannot meet the five

> 2026-11-23 · FORGE5 direction probe. Target: run a **rigorous** (dual / interval-arithmetic, *not* numerical-island) conformal bootstrap on the pure-YM `0⁺⁺` (`tr F²`) correlator and **exclude every interacting 4D CFT matching the YM data**, hence a gap. **VERDICT: cannot satisfy all 5.** **PASS #1 only.** **FAIL #2** (positivity is Schur-concave — no cone sign), **FAIL #3** (`¬(iii) ≡ no-interacting-FP` = the wall; free field is an exact crossing solution), **FAIL #4** (vacuous — "fails at `f₁²=0`" *is* the sharpness clause of `¬(iii)`), **FAIL #5** (no uniform `λ`; wrong coordinate and direction). ## The attempt — what a rigorous exclusion would need
To make a scan *global* rather than per-point one needs either an **analytic extremal functional** or an **a-priori compactness reduction + rigorous finite scan** — neither exists in 4D (`III-UNIQUENESS`: SDPB gives certified islands, never uniqueness; "more points" is not a route). The setup is at least definable: `tr F²` and `T_μν` are pinned at `Δ=4` (anomaly/Ward), and pure-gauge field content forces a scalar gap `Δ_scalar≥4` (L2). The crux is a single lemma.

**Lemma (free field is crossing-exact).** Free Maxwell is a 4D CFT: its `0⁺⁺` data satisfy crossing + unitarity to all orders, so every admissible dual functional `Λ` (with `Λ(crossing)=0`) is `≥0` on it. Hence **no crossing+unitarity datum can exclude the free gapless point**; excluding "the gapless point" is impossible on its free component, and on its interacting component it is verbatim `¬(iii)`.

## Five-point verdict
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS.** Exact crossing + unitarity, all couplings, no expansion. The one genuine merit.
2. **Controls SIGN — FAIL.** The bootstrap is class P (`SCHUR-CONVEX`; `INEQ-T3`): every positivity functional is Schur-concave — it gives *upper* bounds on totals and **cannot lower-bound the edge atom nor sign the Hessian**. The sign lives in the curvature coordinate `G″=Δ_dyn+W_geo+X` (`PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES`), reached only through the unproven gluon→glueball bridge. (Read literally, a *lower* bound on `Var` is the wrong sign — `G″≈1/Var` pushes it toward 0.)
3. **Non-circular — FAIL.** By the `TOOL-not-iii` §0 Lemma, "no interacting pure-gauge IR CFT" ⟺ "no interacting IR fixed point" = statement **(b)**, the wall. To feed "YM data" one needs a datum distinguishing it from free; at a fixed point the trace anomaly vanishes (`β=0`), leaving no conformal handle — the residue is the gap.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — FAIL (vacuous).** Free Maxwell is the conformal *boundary* solution; "the tool fails at `f₁²=0`" is exactly "`(iii)` is sharp at the free point" = the content of `¬(iii)`, not an independent check. Any datum strong enough to exclude the interacting CFT is a free-vs-interacting discriminator = Schur-convex coercivity, which class P forbids.
5. **Uniform `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL.** A global "no CFT" is qualitative; rigorous SDPB/interval bounds return a one-sided `Δ`-island in the **conformal coordinate** (wrong object, `GLUEBALL-BOOTSTRAP` §b). "Not conformal ⇒ gapped with uniform `λ`" needs compactness of the flow / a scale `Λ` = the gap.

## Exact failure — two compounding holes
**(A) Global-exclusion hole:** no 4D uniqueness theorem; a certified interval bound is per-point, and the free gapless CFT is crossing-exact, so it can never be excluded. **(B) Sign/class hole:** even a *granted* global exclusion of interacting CFTs lives in the `Δ` coordinate, while `#2` and `#5` are one statement in the `k→0` curvature coordinate (`REDTEAM-SPEC`: `(2)≡(5)`), and positivity is Schur-concave. The dilemma is sharp: **keep free feasible ⇒ cannot exclude interacting; exclude interacting ⇒ needs the gap.** `#1` is all that survives.

## Sharpest line
The gapless free CFT *is* the crossing solution, so "exclude the gapless point" excludes everything or nothing: **exclude-all wrongly kills the free field (violates #4); exclude-interacting is `¬(iii)` (circular, #3), and yields neither a cone sign (#2) nor a uniform `λ` (#5).** A bootstrap is a positivity method; positivity was already proven unable to force a positive-mass atom (`GOLDS-POS-MASS`). `#1` only.
