# G2-DEPENDS-ON-G1 — the gap is a property of an object existence has not yet produced; Clay is a conjunction, not two sequential halves

> 2026-11-23 · Logical-dependency audit of the campaign's "both halves assembled modulo one species" framing. Reads G1-ASSEMBLE-100, G2-ASSEMBLE-100, G1-CONTINUUM-OS, G1-EXISTENCE-THEOREM, CONTINUUM-TIGHTNESS, CONDITIONAL-LATTICE-GAP, PROOF-SKELETON.

> **判词 (verdict).** The continuum mass gap — the only statement Clay asks about — is a property of a measure `ν` that G1 has **not** constructed. `f₁²=ν({m₁²})>0` is well-posed only **given** `ν` (L3 + tightness + OS reconstruction). So **G2 is not independent of G1 at the continuum level**: it is a conditional theorem whose antecedent is the existence of the object. The *fixed-`(L,a)` lattice* gap **is** independent and partly proven, but is explicitly "not a continuum mass gap" and not Clay. "G1 100% assembled modulo L3" is honest **only for G1-fix**; G1-full is modulo `{L3,G2}`, and rows 14–16 of its own ledger *are* G2 — so at `ℝ⁴` the halves are **mutually entangled**, not sequential. Hence "just one inequality left" is honest as one missing **tool form**, not one decidable proposition: `Clay = [build ν] ∧ [sign ν]`, and only the first conjunct's UV face (L3, fixed volume) is a single well-posed inequality — which alone yields no Clay.

## §1 · Logical form — why "G2 alone" cannot be Clay

Write `G1 := ∃μ[ μ is the a→0,L→∞ YM limit ∧ OS0–4(μ) ]`, `G2 := gap(μ)`. Clay is the single existential **conjunction** `Clay ≡ ∃μ[ G1-props(μ) ∧ gap(μ) ]`. **(i) G1 ⇏ G2:** the massless Gaussian free field is regular, invariant, and reflection-positive (OS0/1/3) with `Δ=0`; conversely a gapped measure may satisfy every OS axiom with `Δ>0` yet `f₁²=0` (continuous bottom, mass-spectrum 定理 B). Existence never manufactures a gap — the conjuncts are independent. **(ii) G2 ⇏ G1, and the "standalone implication" `∀μ[G1⇒gap]` is *vacuously true* when no YM limit exists.** A "proof of G2" without `μ` is therefore either a lattice statement about `μ_{L,a}` (not Clay) or a vacuous/free-field truth (not Clay) — no third option. This is intrinsic: a gap is a property of a spectrum, a spectrum of a transfer matrix, the transfer matrix of the measure — exactly what G1 builds.

## §2 · (a) Is `f₁²=ν({m₁²})>0` well-posed before `ν` exists? — Layered; only the lattice layer is unconditional

Three objects, three statuses. **`ν_{L,a}`** (finite-torus transfer matrix): exists by the finite-dimensional spectral theorem; `f₁²_{L,a}` is well-posed and computable — where P3 (β<1/48) and SZZ live. **`ν_L`** (fixed-volume `a→0`): output of OS reconstruction of `μ_L`, which *needs L3* — "existence of `ν` is conditional on L3" (G1-CONTINUUM-OS §2). **`ν`** (`ℝ⁴`, `L→∞`): needs G1-full. Clay's `f₁²` is the third — well-posed *as a target* and *conditionally on G1*, but **not a proposition about an existing object until G1 closes.** Answer: not well-posed unconditionally.

## §3 · (b) Can G2 be proved independently of G1? — Lattice yes, continuum no

The fixed-`(L,a)` lattice gap **is** independent and real: SZZ (β<1/48), P3 residue, CONDITIONAL-LATTICE-GAP (given H1–H3) are object-independent theorems — but the last names its own scope: "a fixed-β lattice gap, **not** a continuum mass gap; the continuum `a→0` … needs separate uniform control." PROOF-SKELETON's dimension correction is decisive: at fixed `a`, `G″_a≥λ>0` gives `m_phys=lim √λ/a=+∞` (a box/UV artifact); the target is the **ratio** `G″_a/a²≥Δ₀²` as `a→0`, an `a`-uniform claim — the existence road. So **the continuum gap requires G1**; every rigorous *continuum* gap statement is conditional on the object existing.

## §4 · (c) Is "G1 100% assembled modulo L3" honest? — Scoped: only G1-fix

Yes for the **implication graph** (all arrows proven) and for **G1-fix** (OS0/1/3, fixed volume), whose sole open input *is* L3. But **G1-full** (OS0–4, `ℝ⁴`) is modulo **`{L3,G2}`**: its own ledger rows 14 (OS2 Q-CONS), 15 (`L→∞` tightness), 16 (OS4) each read "= G2." At `ℝ⁴` entanglement is **mutual**: G1-full consumes G2 (OS2/OS4/`L→∞`), while continuum-G2 consumes the object G1 builds. Both halves open — yes (L3 UV, H1 IR); **G2's object is conditionally defined — yes, explicitly.** The clean "G1 = L3 only, then G2 = H1" is a *fixed-volume* statement, not Clay.

## §5 · (d) Does the object-circularity invalidate "just one inequality left"?

It invalidates nothing proven — no theorem is claimed — but it **scopes the slogan sharply.** "One inequality" is one **tool form** (an upper bound on a variance of a frustrated non-abelian measure) deployed at two **distinct propositions** on different subspaces/scales: L3 (UV, one-derivative, `⊥`, *builds* `ν`) and H1 (IR, two-sided, `k→0`, *signs* `ν`). They are not one proposition; the campaign admits closing L3 does not give H1. So the rigorous claim is **`Clay = [build ν : L3 + tightness + L→∞/OS4] ∧ [sign ν : H1]`**, a conjunction of two open obligations whose *structural unity* is a **conjecture** that one new tool might discharge both. The honest line: Clay is reduced to **one missing tool form at two entangled deploy sites** — a genuine, deep classification of the difficulty, **not** a demonstration that a single decidable inequality remains. The only truly single, object-independent inequality is fixed-volume L3, and it explicitly yields no Clay.

**Sharpest line.** G2 cannot be proved before G1 — not a logical accident but the definition of a massive theory: you cannot weigh the bottom of a spectrum not yet shown to exist. The real achievement is a correct *classification* (everything but a variance-upper-bound tool is proven) and a correct *conjecture* (one tool serves L3 and H1). "Just one inequality left" is honest only **after granting the object (L3)**; read unconditionally, Clay remains the conjunction of two entangled open obligations, and 0% stands.
