# G1-OS2-INFVOL — OS2 is an `a→0` irrelevance statement, not an `L→∞` restoration: the volume limit only completes translations and passes `W` through untouched

> **Verdict (判词).** The literal target — "full `O(4)` from the *fixed-`a`* `L→∞` limit" — is unattainable and rests on a limit-ordering error. The `O(4)→W` breaking (`W` = hypercubic, `|W|=2⁴·4!=384`, a *fixed finite closed* subgroup of `O(4)`) is a **UV (spacing-`a`) effect, not an IR (volume-`L`) effect**. In the project's ordered scheme (`a→0` at fixed `L` ⇒ `μ_L`; then `L→∞` ⇒ `μ`) rotations are decided when `μ_L` is built, and `L→∞` (`T⁴_L→ℝ⁴`) only **completes translations** and **carries `W` forward**: a weak limit of `W`-invariant measures is `W`-invariant, and symmetry is preserved downward under weak limits but **never enlarged**. So `L→∞` buys full `ℝ⁴` translation invariance (OS1) and volume-tightness (stage-2 existence); it buys **zero rotation**. `O(4)` is restored, if at all, at `a→0`, where the leading `W`-but-not-`O(4)` operator (dim-6, `ℓ=4`; dim-4 anisotropy is killed representation-theoretically by `W`) must be shown **irrelevant** — i.e. `SYM-4D`/GL5-DYN = the named input **IN-2**. **OS2 is therefore NOT free given L3 + OS0/1/3 + compactness; it is a separate input, open, logically gap-free, but MG-adjacent in cost.**

**1 · Where the breaking lives (limit ordering).** `W=O(4)∩GL(4,ℤ)` is finite and closed; `cl(W)=W`. The lattice measure on `T⁴_{L}` is exactly `W`-invariant for *every* `L` and *every* `a`. Weak convergence preserves invariance: if `μ_n⇒μ` and each `μ_n` is `R`-invariant (continuous `R`), then `R_*μ=μ`. Hence every subsequential `L→∞` limit is `W`-invariant. The continuous translations are a *different* matter: they appear at `a→0` (torus translations `aℤ⁴` become dense in `T⁴_L`). `L→∞` then grows the continuous torus-translation group to `ℝ⁴`. **Translation restoration and rotation restoration occur at different limits and have different mechanisms; conflating them is the error.** (Read literally — lattice spacing kept — `L→∞` does not even give continuous translations; the task's "torus→ℝ⁴" presupposes the project's ordered scheme, in which `μ_L` already carries continuous torus translations.)

**2 · What `L→∞` genuinely gives, and it is free.** Full `ℝ⁴` translation invariance (OS1) and removal of the `k→0`/volume obstruction (`4π²/L²` free-box mode; stage-2 `UVS-L` tightness). These are *existence* facts, orthogonal to rotation. No `L→∞`+tightness argument can manufacture `O(4)`.

**3 · Why compactness/tightness cannot do it.** Symmetry under weak limits is *weakly closed downward only*. Prokhorov yields subsequential limits carrying **exactly** the finite-cutoff group `W` (plus completed translations) — a subgroup, never a supergroup. Decisive acid test: a `W`-invariant **anisotropic Gaussian** (covariance `C(x)=f(|x|²)+g(Σ_i x_i⁴)`, the `ℓ=4` harmonic; translation-invariant, RP, Ward-complete) **breaks `O(4)`** and survives `L→∞` intact and anisotropic (conditional-theorem `IN-2`, os2 `ROUTE-D.md`). So "lattice limit restores `O(4)` automatically" is false even on the cheapest non-vacuous model.

**4 · Where OS2 actually lives — `IN-2` = GL5-DYN/SYM-4D.** `OS2` is the fixed-`L` statement "`μ_L` is `O(4)`-invariant" (conditional-theorem `IN-2`; GL5-DYN = "`IN-2⟹OS2`" is bookkeeping, not analysis). Its concrete content: the leading `W`-but-not-`O(4)` effective-action operator is **dim-6, `ℓ=4`** (dim-4 anisotropy is annihilated by `W` — the machine-checked `SYM-DIM4`), and `SYM-4D` demands this be uniformly `O(a²)`. Routes: **B**=SYM-4D (volume-uniform `a²` coefficient), **C**=`O1(a)` two-trajectory gap (`B⊂C`, hardest), **C′**=unique RG fixed point, **D**=RP+Ward+`StressTensorDecay`⇒`NoSpacetimeGoldstone` (`U2`, no rate). Every route consumes a **decay/clustering/irrelevance** bound.

**5 · Relation to the gap — independent in logic, MG-adjacent in cost.** OS2 is about the *measure's symmetry class*, MG about the *spectrum*; neither implies the other (the `ℓ=4` Gaussian is gapless and breaks `O(4)`; a gapped theory may break `O(4)`). But `SYM-4D`'s `a²` coefficient is an all-space connected correlator (volume-uniformity = clustering input), and route D consumes `StressTensorDecay` — every OS2 proof route rides the same nonperturbative decay wall as MG. **This is PROOF-SKELETON G1.4: "open, entangled with G2."** So: not "needs the gap" (strictly weaker in logic), but "MG-adjacent in cost."

**6 · Exact residue.** `OS2 = IN-2` = uniform irrelevance of the leading dim-6 `O(4)`-breaking operator (`SYM-4D`), *or* `NoSpacetimeGoldstone` (route D), *or* fixed-point uniqueness (C′). **None is implied by existence (OS0/1/3+L3); none is the gap; none is free.** And `L→∞` adds nothing rotational — it can only pass through the rotation group `μ_L` already has.

**Sharpest line.** `L→∞` restores **translations**, not **rotations**: `compactness⇒W`, never `compactness⇒O(4)`. OS2 is the `a→0` irrelevance of one dim-6 operator (`IN-2`/`SYM-4D`), a separate input — logically gap-free, cost MG-adjacent, never delivered by tightness. Honest status: **separate open input, not free, not the gap.**
