# HIGGS-DIM-TRANS — is there a rigorous "Higgs-like" forcing of the 0⁺⁺ atom from dimensional transmutation?

**Verdict. No.** The analogy fails at its load-bearing premise. A Higgs theory forces a radial mode `m_H² = 2λv² ∝ v²` because it has a broken symmetry with an explicit modulus `|φ|` **and an independent quartic λ**, so `v` and `λ` decouple and `m²∝v²` is sharp. Pure YM has neither: scale symmetry is **anomalous, not spontaneously broken**; there is **no local order parameter** and **no independent λ**; Λ is the *only* dimensional input. The anomaly fixes Λ (IN-7's N2) but never an isolated 0⁺⁺ pole — which is the gap. This is a rephrasing of the GAP-G2/ATOM-FRG wall, not a new mechanism.

**The construction (strongest honest version).** Quantum scale breaking is exact and operatorial: `T^μ_μ = (β(g)/2g) Gᵃ_{μν}G^{aμν} =: θ` (Nielsen 1977; Collins–Duncan–Joglekar 1977), with `β=−b₀g³+…`, `b₀=11N/3`. Transmutation gives `Λ=μ exp(−∫dg/β)`, the sole scale. The EFT that *saturates* the resulting infinite trace-anomaly Ward identities with a single scalar σ — the dilaton ≡ the 0⁺⁺ glueball, interpolated by `θ ∝ G²` — is **Migdal–Shifman (1982)**; its couplings are anomaly-fixed (`⟨θ⟩ ∼ Λ⁴`, the gluon condensate, SVZ). This is the concrete "Higgs-like" object, and *inside* the EFT it yields `m_σ² ∝ Λ²` relations. That is all it is.

**Why it is not a mechanism.** (i) **No spontaneous breaking.** By Coleman–Jackiw the dilatation charge does not generate dilatations quantum-mechanically — the symmetry is *explicitly* (anomalously) broken. An anomalous symmetry has no order parameter, no `⟨φ⟩=v`. (ii) **No independent λ.** With Λ the only scale, "`m²∝Λ²`" is *dimensional analysis true of every state* and carries no information about which state exists or is isolated. (iii) **Not parametrically light.** A Goldstone/dilaton EFT needs `m ≪ Λ`; the glueball sits at `m_{0⁺⁺} ≈ 1.7 GeV ≈ 3.5–4√σ` (Morningstar–Peardon) — comparable to every scale. No small parameter ⇒ "dilaton dominance" (which identifies `θ` with one pole; Gomm–Jain–Johnson–**Schechter**) is an *assumption*, not a theorem. (iv) **`θ` interpolates the whole tower**: its Källén–Lehmann density is a multi-glueball continuum above threshold, not a single delta.

**Why scale-setting ⇒ continuum, not an isolated atom.** The anomaly is a *sum rule* — a global moment constraint (`∫ds ρ(s)/s² ∝ Λ⁴`, …). A pure continuum down to `s=0` saturates it exactly as well as one delta, and the operator identity `θ=(β/2g)G²` holds identically in a gapped *and* a gapless (free-like) measure. So the anomaly **cannot exclude** a massless/continuum spectrum — it is compatible with gap = 0. Forcing `ρ(s)` to host an isolated atom at `s=m²>0` with `f₁²>0` is precisely GAP-G2-mass's edge/localization statement (pole residue `c>0`; `f₁²>0`) — untouched.

**Bottom line.** The dilaton picture supplies Λ (IN-7 N2: the limit is not the free trajectory), consistent with our earlier results — and nothing more. "The 0⁺⁺ is the dilaton, forced massive by the anomaly" is **Migdal–Shifman dilaton-dominance EFT**, not a rigorous forcing of an isolated residue. The rigorous statement stays conditional: `c>0 ⇒ m²(0)>0`. The Higgs route, like dual-superconductivity and FRG, terminates at the same UV→IR edge / 离域 wall.
