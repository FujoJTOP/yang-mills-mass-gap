# PROOF-THERMODYNAMIC-FORMALISM — does the transfer-operator pressure (Ruelle/Mayer) give the gap?

> 2026-11-23 · Ruelle thermodynamic-formalism forge. **Verdict (判词).** **No — the reduction is exact on both halves; it is not a sixth species.** The pressure `p(β) := lim_V (1/V)log Z_Λ = −(1/a)log λ₀` is the *dominant*-eigenvalue log, i.e. the free energy. (1) Its analyticity domain is fixed by the complex zeros of `Z_Λ` (Ruelle/Lee–Yang/Fisher) — *literally* the strong-coupling cluster/Mayer disk `|β|<1/48` (`PROOF-FISHER-ZERO-FREE`), Pringsheim-maximal and un-crossable by resummation; *granting* it through `β=2.5` is the same zero-freeness wall (~10²× the proven radius). (2) Decisively, analyticity of `p` **cannot deliver the gap**: `Δ=(1/a)log(λ₀/λ₁)` is a *second-eigenvalue / correlation-decay* object, and `λ₁` has no pressure — it is a resonance of the Fredholm determinant `det(1−zT)` (`CONTINUUM-FREDHOLM`), not a bulk `log Z`. The acid test kills it (massless free field: zero-free `Z`, analytic `p`, `Δ=0`, `f₁²=0`). It re-proves Osterwalder–Seiler, reduces to Pringsheim, and adds nothing past `PROOF-P3`.

**1. The pressure and its analyticity domain.** `Z_Λ(β)=tr T^{N_t}=Σᵢλᵢ^{N_t}`, so `p=−(1/a)log λ₀` as `N_t→∞`: analyticity of `p` ⇔ a branch of `log λ₀`. Each `Z_Λ` is entire in `β` (pointwise-positive Wilson integrand), so the sole obstacle is complex zeros accumulating on `ℝ`; `p` is holomorphic on `Ω` iff `Z_Λ` is zero-free there with `p_Λ→p` on compacts (Vitali; Ruelle 1969). The rigorous zero-free domain for SU(N)₄ is the cluster/Mayer expansion, real reach `β<1/48` (Osterwalder–Seiler 1978; SZZ 2023). "Analyticity of the pressure" **is** the Fisher-zero statement, Pringsheim-capped — the halves coincide.

**2. The gap vs a pressure singularity.** A phase transition = non-analyticity of `p` — a statement about `λ₀` only. But `Δ=(1/a)log(λ₀/λ₁)>0` is the exponential-decay rate, carried by `λ₁`, which is exponentially subdominant (`λ₁/λ₀=e^{−aΔ}`) and has **no bulk pressure**: its log is read off `D_O(z)=det(1−zT_O)=∏(1−z e^{−aE_n})` (`CONTINUUM-FREDHOLM`), equivalently a difference of twisted-sector pressures (a surface, not bulk, free energy). The prompt's "pressure minus second eigenvalue" is a category slip — there is no pressure for `λ₁`. Analyticity of `p` moves `λ₀` and is blind to `λ₁`; a gap closing (`λ₁→λ₀`) leaves `p` smooth. Analytic pressure = *no bulk transition*, strictly weaker than exponential clustering. The object whose analyticity *would* see `λ₁` is the correlation/dynamical-zeta function — and *its* analyticity is exactly the cluster-expansion clustering theorem, already the strong-coupling result.

**3. Acid test (FAILS).** The massless Gaussian (free) field: `Z_Λ` is zero-free in `β` (a Gaussian determinant, no transition), so `p` is analytic; the `k=2π/L` mode `→` the vacuum as `L→∞`, so `Δ=0` and `f₁²=0` (a `0⁺⁺` two-particle cut, no atom) — precisely `G″=0`, `f₁²=0`. Any "analytic pressure ⇒ gap" tool applies to it and outputs `Δ>0` — **false, hence vacuous.** The free field erases exactly the second-eigenvalue content the pressure cannot carry; the residual (`G″(0)>0`, cutoff-uniform) lives in `λ₁`, invisible to `p`.

**Sharpest line.** The pressure is the log of the *top* eigenvalue; its analyticity is the free-energy/Fisher-zero statement — the `β<1/48` wall, verbatim — and it controls `λ₀` alone. The gap is `log(λ₀/λ₁)`, a resonance of the Fredholm determinant the partition-function pressure never sees; granting analyticity through `2.5` buys one analytic free-energy branch, not a second eigenvalue. The acid test seals it: an analytic-pressure, `Δ=0`, `f₁²=0` free field is "gapped" by any such argument. The formalism is a sixth species only in name.
