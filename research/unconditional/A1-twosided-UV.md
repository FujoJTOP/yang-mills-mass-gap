# A1 — attempting (c) at weak UV coupling: E[H]−Var is the wrong object; the obstruction is exact, and it is sign

**Verdict (判词).** The proposed route does **not** prove (c), and (c) *as stated* — `E[H]−Var ≥ δ>0` — is **false**, not merely unproven. The one-loop-exact Hessian forces **equipartition** `Var(G_v)=E[H_vv]` on the cone-free typical region, so `E[H]−Var=0` there; the Gaussian tail reduces the Savvidy cone's contribution to `O(e^{−c/g_j²})`. The sharp bound the three tools yield is `E[H]−Var ≥ −C e^{−c/g_j²}` (a `0⁻` bound), never `+δ`. The genuine positive curvature is the Jacobian/marginal term `W≈1/Var(a)`, which (c)'s formula omits; closing `G″≥δ` needs `Var(a)` bounded **above** — the induced-mass/H1 object. So (c) is empty in the UV as written; its non-empty form is G2.

**1 · What each tool actually gives.** (i) *Cone-empty* (GAP-G1 i): with `|F_j|=O(g_j)` the negative cone `|k|<√(2g_j|F_j|)∼g_j` sits below `Λ_{j+1}`, so `H_vv⪰0` on the typical region and `E[H]≥0`. (ii) *Gaussian tail* (`e^{−c/g_j²}`): the large-field region where H can turn negative carries measure `≤e^{−c/g_j²}`, bounding its contribution by `C e^{−c/g_j²}`. This *certifies* `E[H]−Var=0`; it does not break it.

**2 · Exact failure — sign, not size.** On the typical region (i)+(iii) give `E[H]−Var=0` identically; the cone (ii) adds `−O(e^{−c/g_j²})`. Hence `E[H]−Var ≥ −C e^{−c/g_j²} → 0` — the negation of a positive δ. **3 · Uniformity fails independently.** `E[H]−Var` is a sub-leading non-Gaussian residual (≈1 % of `E[H]`; `→0` as `g_j→0`). No scale-uniform δ can come from it. (What is scale-uniform is W, below.)

`G″ = E[H]−Var+W ≥ δ_W·μ(typ) − C e^{−c/g_j²} > 0`, uniform in scale.
