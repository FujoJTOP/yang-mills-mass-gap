# PROOF-POLYA-FREQUENCY — is the KL measure PF/TP₂, and can total positivity force the edge atom `f₁²>0`?

> 2026-11-23 · The Karlin / Pólya-frequency / variation-diminishing route, tested as a "second invariant cone." **Verdict: the TP property is automatic — total positivity of the Källén–Lehmann (RP) kernel is EQUIVALENT to `ν≥0` (Bernstein–Schoenberg), i.e. to KL/unitarity positivity itself. It is free-field-blind and cannot force `f₁²>0`: the free-field test returns YES (PF/TP holds with `f₁²=0`), so there is no separator.** At field-space level reflection positivity gives only `K_T≥0`, not TP; even granted interacting TP, its variation-diminishing property bounds sign-change COUNT, not the vanishing of `⟨G|O|0⟩`, and its cone flag is exactly the "second cone" INEQ-R1 named as missing — yet never supplies the atom. No fabricated proof.

**Setup.** `ν≥0` on `[m₁²,∞)` (KL), `f₁²=ν({m₁²})`, `C(t)=∫e^{−ts}dν(s)`. The reflection-positive (Osterwalder–Schrader) kernel on a time slab is the **additive** one `K_RP(x,y)=C(x+y)=⟨φ(θx)φ(y)⟩` (reflection `θ:t↦−t` pairs the two times); its positivity *is* `ν≥0`.

**(i) Is the KL measure PF? Yes — tautologically.** Bernstein–Widder: `C` is a Laplace transform of a positive measure ⟺ `C` is completely monotone. Karlin–Schoenberg: `C` completely monotone ⟺ the additive kernel `C(x+y)` is **TP_∞**, i.e. `C` is a Pólya-frequency function (bilateral-Laplace / Lévy form, positive Lévy measure). Both sides are *equivalent to `ν≥0`*. Transfer-matrix reading: `ν` is the spectral measure of `H` (`C(t)=⟨0|Oe^{−tH}O|0⟩`); PF_∞/TP is an *order* statement about the weights alone — `H` self-adjoint and bounded below (the measure is positive) — blind to whether `m₁²` carries an atom or merely bottoms a continuum. Interacting atom and free cut are *both* PF_∞ with the *same* `ν≥0`. So "ν is PF" ≡ "`ν≥0`": zero dynamical content.

**(ii) Does TP supply R1's second cone?** In form, yes. Karlin: a TP_∞ kernel preserves the **flag** of "≤k sign-change" cones `Π₀⊂Π₁⊂⋯` (Tchebycheff-system cones), with VD `S⁻(Kf)≤S⁻(f)`. That flag *is* the second invariant cone of INEQ-R1 (`Π₁` on the `ψ₁`-sector). It under-delivers on the sign twice. (a) The excited vector `ψ₁⊥ℓ_Ω` is sign-changing (R1); `Π₁` is single-crossing functions, whose *interior* holds both signs, so "`O|0⟩∈int Π₁`" gives no definite sign for `⟨G|O|0⟩`. (b) VD bounds the *count* of sign changes of a transformed *function*, not the *amplitude* of a single spectral coefficient; `f₁` may be zero while `C` stays TP_∞ (free field). TP's best output remains Perron/vacuum dominance + a gap (R1); the atom needs `m₁` isolated below the 2-body cut — strictly stronger than the gap, not implied by any VD count.

**(iii) Free-field test — the separator fails.** The massless free field has `ν` absolutely continuous on `[0,∞)` (two-gluon cut), `f₁²=ν({0})=0`, yet `ν≥0`, so `C` is completely monotone and `K_RP=C(x+y)` is TP_∞. **PF/TP holds with `f₁²=0`** → PF/TP cannot force the atom — the same free-field no-go as SCHUR-CONVEX: any implied `f₁²>0` must fail at `g=0`, and its restoration *is* the gap. At the field-space kernel `K_T(φ,φ')=⟨φ|e^{−TH}|φ'⟩` it is worse: RP gives only `K_T≥0` (positivity improving), NOT TP; TP of the interacting 4D YM heat kernel is unproven and not a consequence of RP (the free Gaussian kernel is TP_∞, a generic potential heat kernel is not). TP of the transfer kernel is an independent axiom — precisely `H*(r₀)`.

**Sharpest line.** The Pólya-frequency/total-positivity structure the target hoped to exploit is *not* an extra dynamical fact: by Bernstein–Schoenberg, "`ν` is PF / its RP kernel is TP_∞" is *equivalent* to "`ν≥0`," i.e. to KL/unitarity positivity, which the interacting theory and the free field (`f₁²=0`) satisfy identically. TP's VD cone-flag is exactly R1's missing second cone, but VD bounds sign-change counts, not the vanishing of `⟨G|O|0⟩`, and the free field witnesses PF_∞ coexisting with `f₁²=0`. **The free-field test returns YES → no separator.** Pinning `⟨G|O|0⟩` away from zero with a TP transfer kernel is once again edge dominance `H*(r₀)` — the gap, renamed.
