# PROOF-MULTISCALE-4D — the 4D marginal multiscale construction: scales, trap, and the Savvidy sign as (★), not the log

> **Verdict (判词). NOT closed.** The multiscale data are fixed and the *marginal log closes*: asymptotic freedom resums it into the running coupling, `Σ_j g_j⁴ = ζ(4)/(16β₀⁴) < ∞`, and a Polchinski/BBS trapping region absorbs the whole `J∼log(1/a)` tower. The lone residue is the **same (★)** — the a-uniform two-sided large-field marginal log-concavity, i.e. the *variance-vs-Hessian sign* `G″_j = E[H]−Var(G)+W ≥ δ`. A trapping-region argument genuinely **tames** the UV Savvidy cone (invariant region `√(2g_j|F_B|)<Λ_j`, typical cone empty at `g_j≤g*`, tail `e^{−c|F|²/g_j²}`-thin), but it controls the action's *value* and the norm `‖R_j‖_X`, never the sign of the second variation against its own force variance. The CPS/CNS `λ<1/24` area law does **not** extend toward β=2.5 by a multiscale step: `λ(β=2.5)=g²N≈3.2` is ~75× away (≈500 IR e-folds), and the *blocked* measure at the next scale is signed, so the step loses the small-λ hypothesis and lands on the same (★). Same wall, not a new one.

**§1 Scales, coupling, effective action, marginal entry.** Index `j=0,…,J` upward toward the UV with block scale `Λ_j=Λe^{j}` (Λ the fixed-volume IR reference), shell `[Λ_{j−1},Λ_j]`, `J=⌊log(1/aΛ)⌋`. One-loop AF: `g_j²=1/(2β₀j)+O(j^{−2})`, `1/g_j²=1/g_0²+2β₀j`, `g_{j+1}²=g_j²−2β₀g_j⁴+O(g_j⁶)`, `β₀=11N/48π²`. Effective action on `P(𝒜/𝒢)`: `K_j=(1/4g_j²)∫Tr F²+R_j`, `R_j` the dim>4 remainder, Polchinski flow `∂_jK=½(δK/δA)Ċ_j(δK/δA)−δ²K/δA²:Ċ_j`; the trap tracks `(g_j,Z_j)` (Tr F² is kinetic+cubic+quartic, not a monomial). The marginal divergence enters at three points: (i) the *running coupling* — `[g²]=0` weights every shell equally, `Σ_j g_j²∼(2β₀)^{-1}log J`, absorbed exactly by `1/g_j²=2β₀j`; residual powers `Σ_j g_j⁴=ζ(4)/(16β₀⁴)<∞`, `δg_J=O(J^{−3/2}log J)→0`; (ii) `Z_j`; (iii) the `a^{−4}` vacuum energy (asymptotic renormalon, only order-by-order, L2). The *combinatorial* problem is trivial: BRST cohomology (ghost#0, dim 4) leaves only `Tr F²` (θ a total derivative, drops), so there is no infinite counterterm tower — one coupling, `Z_j`, one vacuum constant. The open marginal problem is analytic, not combinatorial.

**§2 Attempted closure — can the trap sign Savvidy?** The trap `0<g_j≤g*`, `‖R_j‖_X≤rj^{−θ}`, `(T2)‖R_{j+1}‖_X≤e^{−c}‖R_j‖_X+Cg_j⁴` closes the log (§1). The contraction `e^{−c}` comes from the free semigroup `e^{−tL_0}` on dim>4. About a background `A_B` the Hessian is `L=−D_B²+2 ad F_B`, lowest eigenvalue `−2g_j|F_B|` on the cone `|k|<√(2g_j|F_B|)` (Savvidy): there `e^{−c}→e^{+|c|}`. *Attempt.* Impose the invariant region `√(2g_j|F_B|)<Λ_j`; then on the retained band `|k|≥Λ_j` the operator is positive and contraction survives. Make it invariant: (a) coercivity `∫Tr F²≥0` plus the trap norm give `E|F_j|≤Cg_j`; (b) `g_{j+1}²<g_j²` and `Λ_{j+1}=eΛ_j`, so the cone shrinks while the shell edge grows — preservation is *easier* at `j+1` (one-loop box, L1); (c) on the large-field tail `|F_j|≫g_j` the weight is `e^{−c|F_j|²/g_j²}` but the cone *re-enters*. So the trap empties the *typical* cone and thins the tail. It fails on `G″_j=E[H]−Var(G)+W≥δ`: on the cone-empty region `E[H]≥0`, but `Var≥0` *subtracts* (`G″≤E[H]`); Cauchy–Schwarz `Var≤E|G|²∼β_j²` misses the needed `E[H]∼β_j` by a factor β_j, and the bridge is one-loop equipartition `Var=E[H]`, exact only as `g_j→0`. A value/norm contraction cannot sign `Var−E[H]`. **Dies: coercivity bounds the value, not the Hessian** (G1-fixed-volume (c)).

**§3 The CPS/CNS anchor and one multiscale step.** With `λ₀=1/(8(d−1))=1/24` and β=2.5 (`λ=g²N=2N²/β≈3.2`, ~75×). Each IR e-fold moves `1/λ` by `+11/24π²≈0.046`, so a blocking step extends `λ` by `O(λ²)≲0.2%`; reaching `λ≈3.2` needs `Δ(1/λ)/0.046≈500` IR e-folds. But the step is *qualitatively* blocked: the theorem lives at small λ (UV/weak), β=2.5 is the strong-coupling IR corner, and the IR flow *increases* λ, degrading the hypothesis; the *blocked* measure at the next scale is *signed* (Weingarten=Savvidy), so the positive-weight/bounded-surface hypothesis is lost, not transported. One step does not extend the anchor — it meets the same sign one block in.

**§4 The exact missing estimate — (★), not new.** Uniformly in `j≤J`, conditioning `U′`, and `a,L`, for every normalised gauge-invariant marginal collective mode `v`:
```
(★)  Var_{μ_j(·|U′)}(⟨Pᵀ∇S_j,v⟩) ≤ E_{μ_j(·|U′)}⟨PᵀH_jP v,v⟩ + W_j(v) − δ_W ,
```
equivalently `G″_j(v)=E[H_vv|a=0]−Var(G_v|a=0)+W(v)≥δ_W`, equivalently the BBS step `‖R_{j+1}‖_X≤e^{−c}‖R_j‖_X+Cg_j⁴` with `c,C` independent of `j,a,L`, *given* `√(2g_j|F_B|)<Λ_j` on the trapped set. This is the **same (★)**. The area law needs only *positivity* of the blocked surface sum (a sign-cancelling regrouping); a *uniform* multiscale transport needs *log-concavity* (★), strictly stronger — the escalation lands exactly on (★). Both are the one non-abelian sign (Weingarten, Savvidy, `6j`, `H_ff` are one object's faces). Not the log (it closes), not the anomaly (empty), not a new estimate.

**Sharpest line.** Φ⁴'s marginal trap closes because its large field is *more* convex (`−Δ+m²+12λφ²⪰m²`); YM's is open because its large field is *less* convex (`−D²+2ad F_B⪰−2g|F_B|`). The trapping region absorbs the log and empties the *typical* Savvidy cone, but the sign that survives is the variance's, and no value/positivity bound signs it — so the multiscale step, like the area law, stops at the same one-signed inequality (★).
