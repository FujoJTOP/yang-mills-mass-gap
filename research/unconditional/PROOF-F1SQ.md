# PROOF-F1SQ — three constructive attacks on `f₁²=|⟨0|tr F²|G⟩|²>0`, and the exact missing inequality

> 2026-11-23 · Constructive follow-up to EDGE-f1sq / SCHUR-CONVEX / LEVER-nonconvex-edge: stop extracting the atom from positivity, *build* the dynamics. Three constructions — (i) strong-coupling SZZ gap + transport via H2; (ii) a from-below variational overlap bound; (iii) an explicit second (Gantmacher–Krein) cone for `tr F²`. **Verdict: none closes the CORE gap.** Each fails on a distinct wall, but the walls are one object stated three ways: not the *mass gap*, but an **isolation** bound. Sharpening (new): a *gapped* admissible OS theory — massive free scalar, `O=:φ²:` — already has `f₁²=0`, so even a proved strong-coupling gap cannot imply the residue. The one place a genuine β<1/48 theorem is plausibly reachable is flagged as program, not claimed.

**The object.** `ν≥0`, `supp ν⊂[m₁²,∞)`, `f₁²=ν({m₁²})`, `M_k=∫sᵏdν`. The residue–level-gap lemma gives the sharpest total-based handle: with `Λ:=inf supp(ν−f₁²δ_{m₁²})`, `f₁² ≥ (ΛM₀−M₁)/(Λ−m₁²) = M₀(Λ−R̄)/(Λ−m₁²)`, `R̄:=M₁/M₀`. The RHS is `>0` **iff** `Λ>R̄`, and `Λ>R̄` **suffices**: if `f₁²=0` then `Λ=m₁²` and `R̄≥Λ` (a measure's mean lies above its support infimum). The converse **fails** (a positive atom with a heavy tail can have `R̄>Λ`). So this is a *self-certifying* sufficient certificate, not an equivalence — and any total-based positivity proof must establish `Λ>R̄`.

**Route (i) — strong coupling; the gap is the wrong lever.** SZZ (`β<1/48`, `λ>96`) prove LSI + Poincaré + clustering, i.e. a transfer-matrix gap `Δ>0`, but the residue is an atom *weight*, not a decay rate. The gapped counterexample settles it: massive free scalar (`m>0`, RP, gap `m`), `O=:φ²:` is a genuine two-particle `0⁺⁺` interpolator with `ν` the cut on `[4m²,∞)` — `f₁²=0` — while the same theory's elementary `O=φ` has `f₁²=Z>0`. Gap does not manufacture a residue; **binding does**. Closing the strong-coupling sub-theorem needs an **isolation lemma** (L1): at fixed `a`, `Λ−m₁²≥δ>0` uniform in `L`, lightest glueball below the two-glueball threshold, the leading single-polymer term of `C(t)` positive and all others `e^{−δt}` smaller. L1 is the Glimm–Jaffe–Spencer particle-structure mechanism, plausibly reachable by the polymer expansion (Bałaban–Jaffe / Schor), but **not** SZZ and not claimed — and even it lives at `β<1/48`, fixed `a`.

**Transport.** Blocked. (a) `f₁²` is *upper*-semicontinuous (`ν({x})` is usc): an atom can only *fall* toward `β_c≈2.5`; one dissolving at an interior threshold (`m_{glue}→2m_g`) drops to 0 invisibly to every protected total — no continuity. (b) H2 is *UV fiber* (PROVE-H2-uvgap: block-local, `∇_⊥` annihilates `k→0`), the wrong end; H2-global fails (`C≈15`), H1 fails at the soft mode (`G″(0)≈−0.165<0`). (c) The bridge is the analyticity wall (ATOM-strong-coupling-extend). Exact demand: L1 *uniform along the window*, `a→0`.

**Route (ii) — variational from-below.** A lower bound on `|⟨G|O|0⟩|²` is a lower bound on a matrix element; positivity is Cauchy–Schwarz/PSD ⇒ *upper* only. Every total-based bound is the lemma above, positive only once `Λ>R̄`, and moments cannot supply it (`δ₀+δ₂` vs `2δ₁` share `M₀,M₁`). Restriction/GEVP raises energies. No from-below overlap exists without a cone — which is (iii).

**Route (iii) — the second cone.** R1's Karlin cone *is* constructible in a 1-D collective-coordinate reduction: a TP₂ kernel makes the mean-zero cone `K₁={f−⟨f⟩ : f nondecreasing in φ}` `T`-invariant, and `φ−⟨φ⟩` (one sign change, Gantmacher–Krein) is interior ⇒ `f₁²>0` for the toy. But (a) TP/oscillatory kernels require an **order** on configuration space; `G^{E}/gauge` has none, so no Gantmacher–Krein cone exists for the full kernel; (b) a truncation computes a toy law, and projection cannot *lower*-bound the full `f₁²` (the lightest state may sit in the complementary directions). Constructing the full `K₁` = edge dominance = `H*(r₀)`.

**Exact missing inequality.** Uniform in `(β∈[β_SC,2.5], a,V)`:
  **`Λ_β − M₁/M₀ ≥ κ > 0`** (or the stronger isolation gap `Λ_β−m₁²≥κ`), whence `f₁² ≥ r₀(κ)>0` by the lemma. This is verbatim `H*(r₀)`+isolation, strictly above the mass gap. The one genuine sub-theorem — strong-coupling L1 — is real at `β<1/48`, fixed `a`, does not transport, and is not proved here.

**Sharpest line.** The three attacks collapse onto one missing object — an **isolation** (bound-state) bound `Λ>R̄` — not a positivity or gap bound. The gapped free field shows the gap is insufficient; usc shows it is not upgradable by continuity; the second cone needs an order the gauge-quotient lacks. To force `f₁²>0` one must prove the lightest `0⁺⁺` glueball is a stable, isolated bound state below threshold with a residue bounded away from zero *uniformly to the continuum* — which is the dynamics.
