## §1 · The classification (D≥3, unitary, relativistic, stress tensor, RG endpoint)

1. **CFT** — scale + conformal invariant. Includes **free massless** (IR-free) and **interacting fixed points** (Banks–Zaks). The generic gapless phase.
2. **Dilaton / spontaneously-broken scale invariance** — a massless dilaton along a **flat direction** (an exactly-marginal operator = a modulus). The dilaton is **not** a standard Goldstone (scale/conformal is a spacetime symmetry); its EFT carries the Wess–Zumino anomaly terms. Requires a flat direction / modulus.
3. **Excluded by the hypothesis** — non-relativistic / Lorentz-violating vacua; non-isolated or non-QFT (non-UV-completable) spectra.

**Claim [C]:** no gapless, interacting, relativistic, unitary **RG-endpoint** vacuum is *neither* a CFT *nor* a dilaton vacuum. The a-theorem forbids a third class at an endpoint.

## §2 · The theorems behind it

- **[T] Scale ⟹ conformal** (D=4, unitary, stress tensor exists, discrete spectrum, technical convergence): Polchinski, *Nucl. Phys.* B303 (1988) 226; Dymarsky–Komargodski–Schwimmer–Terning, *JHEP* 08 (2015) 142. A scale-invariant unitary 4D QFT is conformal.
- **[T] a-theorem** (Komargodski–Schwimmer, *JHEP* 12 (2011) 099): the IR endpoint of a unitary 4D QFT flow is **gapped** (`a_IR=0`) **or** a **CFT** (`a_IR>0`); `a` decreases monotonically along the flow.
