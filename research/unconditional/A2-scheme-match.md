# A2 — same-scheme-C lattice↔RT match (U2): a three-layer split, not one wall

**Verdict (判词).** (U2) is not one statement. It splits into three layers of *different* status: **(L1)** fixing scheme C is a finite reparametrisation — bookkeeping, provable/definitional; **(L2)** the *perturbative all-orders* lattice↔RT match is a real theorem (the lattice is a bona fide regulator; BRST cohomology ⇒ finite marginal operator space; Symanzik/Reisz lattice BPHZ) — **provable now**; **(L3)** the *nonperturbative a-uniformity* (UVS(L)'s `C_{n,k}(L)<∞` and SPEC-RES's `Z_a` bound) is **open** — it is the marginal/Savvidy wall in its *one-derivative* (generator) form, and it is ⊥ the gap. The clean `Z_a` formulation below makes SPEC-RES *equivalent* to (L2) + a UV Poincaré inequality + one open uniformity (L3): it **reduces**, it does not prove.

## L1 — scheme C is a choice, not an obstruction
The RT is fixed by one constant C (gluing §1.4): `1/g²(a)=2b₀log(1/aΛ)+(b₁/b₀)loglog(1/aΛ)+C+o(1)`. A scheme change `g'²=g²+cg⁴` keeps `b₀`, shifts `Λ'=Λe^{c/2b₀}`, leaving `m/Λ, √σ/Λ, q=a/ξ` invariant. The additive `a⁻⁴` of `tr F²` is likewise a *finite* local constant (in practice the *measured* tadpole — one number per `a`). A mismatch `C'≠C`, or a different finite subtraction, shifts the renormalised functions by a finite local term; it does **not** turn an a-uniform bound into a divergent one. "Same scheme C" is a *consistency condition on the statement*, not its analytic content — provable, not the wall. The additive `a⁻⁴` is benign **provided** the fluctuation is controlled — exactly L3.

## L2 — perturbative all-orders match: a theorem
The marginal operator space is finite: BRST cohomology (ghost #0, dim 4) leaves only `Tr F²` (coupling) and `Tr F F̃` (`θ`, total derivative) — W3(0). The Wilson lattice is a bona fide regulator; lattice BPHZ power counting (Reisz) plus Symanzik's `a`-expansion give, *order by order in perturbation theory*, convergence of the renormalised Green's functions with the **same** counterterm structure as the continuum, up to the finite matching constants of C. A genuine lemma, proven. The `a⁻⁴` coefficient `c(g)=Σ_ℓ c_ℓ g^{2ℓ}` is asymptotic (vacuum-energy renormalon — BREAK-IN-7(b)), so "all-orders" cannot be *resummed*; L2 needs only the finite scheme constants per order, which exist. **Provable now.**

## L3 — nonperturbative a-uniformity: the wall
UVS(L) needs `|S^{(n)}_{L,a}|≤C_{n,k}(L)∏‖f_i‖`, uniform in `a`, on the *renormalised* moments; SPEC-RES needs `sup_a Z_a^{-1}‖H_a‖_D<∞` on a common core. Neither follows from L2: the renormalon makes the bare divergence non-summable, and IN-9's `T_a=diag(1,e^{-c₁a},e^{-c₂a})` shows the bound is *first-order in a*, invisible to C⁰/Schwinger convergence.

**Clean `Z_a`.** Take common core `D` = smooth smeared gauge-invariant field-strength polynomials, graph norm `‖ψ‖_D=‖ψ‖+‖Fψ‖`; define `Z_a` := the UV-block fluctuation variance (block second moment of `δ_a tr F²`), the normalisation making the block field unit-variance. Then (SPEC-RES′) = `sup_a ‖Ĥ_a|_D‖<∞`, `Ĥ_a:=Z_a^{-1}ι_aH_aι_a^{-1}`, is *equivalent* to: **(i)** `Z_a` matches scheme C (L2, provable); **(ii)** a UV Poincaré inequality on the block, constant `Λ_UV>0` (block-local Holley–Stroock — REPAIR-2SCALE H2 / PROVE-H2; global promotion a standard martingale); **(iii)** `Λ_UV` (the effective Hessian's lower bound) uniform across the `J~log(1/a)` scale tower. Trotter–Kato (Reed–Simon I, VIII.25) closes given (i)–(iii). **(iii) is open.** It is the Savvidy large-field step (BREAK-IN-1; G1(c)) at *one lower derivative* — a gradient/Dirichlet bound, not the two-sided Hessian sign of (c). Hence **strictly milder** than (c), not implied by it without the matching. For free Maxwell it holds after subtraction (P1-L0).

## Bottom line
Provable now: L1 (definitional), L2 (a theorem). Open: L3 = an a-uniform UV Sobolev/operator bound — the *one-derivative* face of the marginal wall; mild but real, and ⊥ the gap. (U2) does not hide a *new* obstruction: it bundles a provable perturbative matching (L2) with the *same* Savvidy wall at reduced order (L3). The gap never enters.
