# k0-HOMOGENIZATION — the k→0 slow mode as a diffusive/homogenized mode: the effective diffusion coefficient is not the mass

> **2026-11-23 · Verdict.** The DIRECTION has a real foothold and a fatal type error. *Foothold:* the standard stochastic-homogenization closure — cell problem `∇·(A(∇χ_ξ+ξ))=0`, effective coefficient `A_hom ξ·ξ = E[ξ·Aξ] − E[∇χ_ξ·A∇χ_ξ]` — is term-for-term the corpus's exact identity **`G″ = S − D`** (INVENT-VARIANCE): the "homogenized stiffness" is the bare averaged coarse Hessian `S` minus the corrector energy `D`. So homogenization is **no new leverage** — `A_hom` *is* `G″`, and `A_hom>0 ⟺ D<S` *is* H1. *Fatal error:* a homogenized **diffusion** operator is order-2 (transport); the constants lie in its kernel, so its slow eigenvalue at `k→0` is `A_hom k₁² → 0` — **massless by construction**. The gap is the order-0 (reaction) coefficient `m²`; the order-0 homogenized coefficient is trivially `E[m²_bare]=0` for the massless bare theory. "Effective diffusion coefficient = gap" conflates the k² transport coefficient with the k⁰ mass. Homogenizing the **energy** (Γ-convergence, or marginalizing the fiber) instead reproduces `G″ = S − D`, whose Schur/cell step needs `H_ff⪰0` — false (Savvidy). Same wrong half as Mori–Zwanzig/BO/flow: a fiber-side **rate**, not the coarse-side **stiffness**.

## Five-point verdict
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS (vacuous).** The cell problem is exact, no loop expansion. But its hypotheses — uniform ellipticity and infinite-volume ergodicity — hold only in the weak-coupling/gapless regime; the ergodic measure's existence *is* G1, open.
2. **Sign-controlling — FAIL.** `A_hom≻0` needs `A` pointwise PD. YM's `A = Hess S_W` is Savvidy-indefinite (`K` indefinite, 90–97% fiber negatives, W6-BE); then `D = E[∇χ·A∇χ]` is not `≥0` and the sign of `S−D` is uncontrolled. The cell problem may even have no `L²` corrector.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL at closing.** `A_hom = S − D = G″` is H1 verbatim (`G″(0)>0 ⟺ m_eff²(0)≥λ`); the corrector energy `D` *is* the fiber-Savvidy object, and solving the cell problem *is* controlling `K^{−1}`.
4. **Non-SUSY / free field — FAIL (false positive).** Free massless: `A≈I ⇒ A_hom≈I>0`. Read as the gap, this **certifies a gap on a gapless field** — the acid test fails in the worst direction. Read rightly as transport, `k=0` has `Var=∞`, `m²=E[m²_bare]=0`: zero mass everywhere, no YM positivity either.
5. **Uniform lower bound — FAIL.** A transport coefficient bounds the k² term, not the k⁰ mass: `Var_μ(a_0) ≤ 1/(A_hom k₁²) → ∞` as `L→∞`. The only available lower bound `A_hom ≥ λ_-` inherits the Savvidy `λ_-≤0`. No `λ>0`.

**Exact failure.** `G″ = A_hom = S − D`, closing iff `D<S` = H1. The diffusion coefficient is `≈I` at the free field (no gap); the mass term is `E[m²_bare]=0`. Homogenizing a generator with a zero mode at `k=0` returns that same zero mode — pure transport cannot manufacture reaction. The only reaction present is the dynamically generated `S−D`, unprovable because `K` is indefinite. The "rigorous integration of fast modes" (the corrector/cell solve) is precisely the step that is undefined on the indefinite fiber; a cell problem is only as rigorous as the `H_ff` it inverts.

**Sharpest line.** Homogenization is not new leverage — it is `G″ = S − D` rewritten, with the cell problem's corrector energy *being* the Savvidy defect that has to be beaten. And it is the wrong type: a homogenized diffusion is transport (k²), the gap is reaction (k⁰), so any homogenized generator is massless at `k=0` (`A_hom k²→0`, `m²=E[m²_bare]=0`). Read as the gap it falsely certifies a free field; read honestly it certifies none anywhere. It hands you the mobility of a mode with no rest mass; the rest mass is H1.
