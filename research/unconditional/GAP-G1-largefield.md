# GAP-G1 — taming the Savvidy large-field non-concavity

**Verdict (判词).** Not closed, but **displaced**. The bare Savvidy non-concavity is real and *not* pure-gauge (W4-blocked-positivity: the gauge-projected transverse Hessian keeps 7% negative at the SU(2) crossover) — yet it does **not block the UV trap**, because the unstable cone sits *below every RG shell at weak coupling*; the correct substitute is **marginal log-concavity of gauge-invariant collective modes**, not pointwise SLC. What remains is a bootstrap verification plus a k→0 residue that is exactly G2.

**1. Where the trap needs convexity — and doesn't.** (T2)'s e^{−c} acts on the *retained* (blocked, low-momentum) field; each shell's high-momentum part is integrated out into R_j and contracts at e^{−cΔ} regardless of Hessian sign. So the trap needs convexity only on the retained band. Two facts apply:

(i) **Cone empty at weak coupling.** On a smooth background |F|=b the fluctuation eigenvalues are k²±2gb; the negative cone is |k|<√(2g_j|F_j|). In the UV g_j→0 and, under the marginal measure, |F_j|=O(g_j) (the positive marginal term ∫Tr F²≥0 gives the coercive bound), so the cone radius ~g_j lies *parametrically below the shell's IR cutoff* Λ_{j+1}. "Negative at every scale" describes the *operator's presence*, not the *cone's non-emptiness per shell*; the cone fills only toward the crossover/IR.

(ii) **Marginal curvature.** The right substitute is the exact constrained-free-energy curvature G''(a)=E[H_vv|a]−Var(G_v|a) of a gauge-invariant collective mode v (the W7 object). G''>0 *is* log-concavity of the scale-j **marginal** measure — "damping the negative cone on gauge-invariant observables." The sign is the *hard* one: Var(G)≥0 *subtracts*, so G''≤E[H] (MARGINAL.md); marginal log-concavity is strictly harder than E[H]>0, not free. Data: collective plane-wave E[H] strictly positive at β=2.5/2.6/3.0 (0% negative, REPORT 11-slc-blocked); the blocked gauge-orthogonal Hessian is positive at the crossover (min +0.81β SU2, +0.44β SU3@5.7; ~8% negative density in-band) — the variance does not overcome E[H] there.

**2. Why background-split and pure-gauge tames fail.** A=Ā+a reproduces det(−D²+2ad F̄)^{−1/2}: it *localizes*, not removes, the negative mode (MARGINAL.md: −2.7→−0.3 after one block, still negative); the one-loop marginal stays ill-defined. Pure-gauge is falsified by the data. A curvature-weighted norm carries coercivity but is non-local, breaking Kotecký–Preiss factorization.

**3. Exact remaining obstruction.** Fold (i) into the BBS trap as an **invariant-region inequality** √(2g_j|F_j|)<Λ_{j+1}, replacing the external "Savvidy-safe" hypothesis (P2 §4). Closure needs: (a) an a priori field-moment bound E[|F_j|]≤Cg_j from the trap's ‖R_j‖ bound + marginal positivity; (b) preservation of the inequality under one flow step — the one-loop box (W4 L1); (c) G''(a)≥δ>0 uniformly on the retained band, i.e. a Brascamp–Lieb-type variance bound Var(G)≤E[H]−δ. (a)–(c) are plausible precisely because the trap lives at weak coupling (g_j≤g*). **Irreducible residue = the k→0 constant mode**, where Λ_{j+1}→0 makes the cone condition marginal, Var(G)→0 makes G''→E[H]= the bare long-distance curvature, and positivity requires an **induced gluon mass m²>0** — that is G2, not Savvidy.

**4. What a proof needs.** Uniform in a,V: the trapping-region bootstrap with (i) as an invariant inequality; the marginal-curvature bound (c) as the analytic core (the "one-loop box"); and a separate G2 input m²>0 for the zero mode. **Honest boundary:** the O(g_j) cone radius / blocked-SLC is a *weak-coupling (UV) statement* — at SU(3) deep strong coupling one block still leaves 12% negative (W4-blocked-positivity), so no global claim survives. Savvidy tames on the UV side; the residue re-points to induced mass.
