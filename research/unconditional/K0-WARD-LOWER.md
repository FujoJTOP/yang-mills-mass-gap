## 2 · Steelman: one IbP generates the whole tower — and it is a *point* statement
`∫(v·∇)[a^n f e^{−V}]=0` gives, for every `n`, `nE[a^{n−1}f]+E[a^n(H−f²)]=0`; using `E[a^{n−1}f]=(n−1)E[a^{n−2}]`,
> **`E[a^{2j}(H−f²)] = −2j(2j−1)E[a^{2j−2}]`,  `j≥0`  (n=0 ⇒ SD `E[H]=E[f²]`; n=2 ⇒ first Ward moment `E[a²(H−f²)]=−2`).**
This is a genuine new *tower*, but it is **not a set of independent constraints**: matching `E[a^{2j}(H−f²)]=−∫a^{2j}p″` for all even `j` forces the distributional
> **`G̃(a)=E[H−f²|a]=−p″(a)/p(a)`** pointwise, hence `G″(0)=(−p″/p)(0)+m(0)²=−p″(0)/p(0)`.
So *the entire Ward+SD content is "the log-density peaks at the mode."* This is the sharpest possible form of the identity route — and it shows why it cannot manufacture sign: the tower never sees `p″(0)` except through this one identity, whose sign is the open datum.
