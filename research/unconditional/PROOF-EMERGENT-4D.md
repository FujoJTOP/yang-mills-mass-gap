# PROOF-EMERGENT-4D — the closest framework: large-N strong-coupling lattice gauge; a proven 0⁺⁺ gap that cannot cross the cutoff

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict. The strongest honest framework is 't Hooft-scaling lattice SU(N) Wilson theory in its strong-coupling window: it yields a genuine proven 4D 0⁺⁺ mass gap — the physical channel — by a rigorous spectral inequality, at fixed spacing, uniform in volume, for every finite N.** The finite-N transfer that killed large-N-as-master-field is *free*: the theorem is finite-N, so SU(3) needs no N→∞ limit, and the 0⁺⁺ residue (P3) sits in the lightest channel. What does *not* transfer is the **cutoff**: the proven window is small β (strong coupling), asymptotic freedom puts the continuum at large β (weak coupling), and they are joined only across the non-abelian-sign/Savvidy crossover (no β-monotonicity for SU(N)) and the marginal-BPHZ a→0 limit. The framework proves a true 0⁺⁺ gap yet, at the physical point, reduces to the same wall — G1's scale-uniformity (L3) plus the crossover sign. Not Clay; a proof of everything short of the continuum.

**1. The model, and why its cousins fail.** Lattice SU(N) Wilson gauge on T⁴_L, spacing a, 't Hooft prefactor S_YM = Nβ Σ_p Re Tr Q_p (CNS convention: β = 2/λ_tHooft, so *small β is strong coupling*). Only this keeps Euclidean time, reflection positivity, and a thermodynamic spectral edge. Eguchi–Kawai one-site reduction is a 0D matrix integral — no time, no Källén–Lehmann (KL) measure, no spectral gap (EGUCHI-KAWAI). S³/fuzzy-sphere's certified *finite-mode* gap is an upper bound on Δ_{ℝ⁴}, and its fuzzy UV limit adds UV/IR mixing (PROOF-SPHERE-S3). BFSS/BMN gaps are an input mass or a wrong-channel SUSY atom (BPS-SUSY-PROTECTION).

**2. The transfer theorem — what genuinely reaches 0⁺⁺.** Fix 0<β<β₀(d). (i) Osterwalder–Seiler: an all-observable cluster expansion gives |⟨O(x)O(0)⟩_c| ≤ C e^{−m(β)|x|}, m(β)>0 uniform in L, for every compact gauge group (a fast decay *upper* bound is a *lower* bound on the lightest mass in every channel). (ii) SZZ / Cao–Nissim–Sheffield verify the equivalent Durhuus–Fröhlich slab σ-model mass gap at the improved threshold β₀ = 1/8(d−1). (iii) The P3 residue theorem gives f₁² > 0 in the 0⁺⁺ (tr F²) channel at β<1/48. KL then sets m_GI = inf supp ρ_{tr F²} = m_{0⁺⁺} = m₁ > 0 — the physical 0⁺⁺ gap **at that (N,β,a)**, volume-uniform; holding at each finite N, the N→∞→3 transfer is unnecessary.

**3. The exact obstruction — the cutoff, in two steps.** *Step A (β).* Asymptotic freedom puts the continuum at β→∞; the proven window is β<β₀(d), a fixed small constant. The physical SU(3) point is β = 2/λ ≈ 0.63 (β_W = 5.7), O(1) and outside it; CNS name "treating small N and large β … is of particular interest" as open. Crossing the Savvidy strong→weak crossover needs a sign-definite or FKG monotonicity of the gap in β, which **fails** for non-abelian SU(N) (alternating character coefficients; even U(1) loses area law at large β, Fröhlich–Spencer). *Step B (a).* A continuum mass m>0 needs Δ(β(a)) ≈ a·m with m fixed in (0,∞): exactly G1's marginal scale-uniformity (L3) with IN-b. Both steps are the corpus's single wall in lattice attire: uniform-in-(a,β) control of a sign-indefinite crossover.

**Sharpest line.** The most promising emergent framework proves a true 4D 0⁺⁺ mass gap for all N and all volumes — it even frees the finite-N transfer that blocked large-N — but it lives at strong bare coupling while the continuum lives at weak bare coupling, and the only bridge (a β-monotone, sign-definite inequality) does not exist for non-abelian SU(N); the residual is G1-L3 plus the Savvidy sign — the mass gap itself in lattice coordinates.
