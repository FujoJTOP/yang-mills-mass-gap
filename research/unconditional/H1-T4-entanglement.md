# H1-T4 — Entanglement / area-law route to a YM mass-gap floor

**判词：未闭合，且不新。** The area-law route neither yields a gap floor nor is genuinely distinct from P4. The proven implication runs *gap ⇒ area law*, not the reverse; the entropy's leading area term is UV/gap-blind; and for the transverse residue it hits the same aggregate-vs-edge-atom wall.

## 1. The implication runs the wrong way
The CM slogan "1D area law ⇒ gap" inverts the theorem. Hastings (JSTAT 2007, P08024) proves *gapped* 1D systems obey an area law; the converse — area law ⇒ gap — is **not a theorem** and fails in known constructions. The actual gap-proving engines are finite-block / martingale criteria (Nachtergaele, CMP 175 (1996); Nachtergaele–Sims, CMP 265 (2006); Bachmann–Michalakis–Nachtergaele–Sims, CMP 309 (2012)), which **take a uniform block gap as input** — exactly P4's L\*. The area law is *downstream* of the gap, not a source of it.

## 2. Provability in 4D
A rigorous area law for an interacting 4D YM ground state is unproven. In d≥2 it exists only for free fields (Bombelli–Koul–Lee–Sorkin 1986; Srednicki 1993; Casini–Huerta 2009) and special/topological models; interacting d≥2 area laws are open (Eisert–Cramer–Plenio, RMP 2010). Lattice YM (Kogut–Susskind) is a finite-range local Hamiltonian, so *Lieb–Robinson bounds* are generic — but LR gives clustering **from** a gap (Nachtergaele–Sims 2006), one-way; a uniform LR velocity is the gap itself (P4 §3). A rigorous area law at the physical coupling is the `09-area-law` wall (Borgs–Seiler β<0.706 vs β≈2.5).

## 3. Does an area law give a floor? No.
The leading area term S ∼ Area/ε² is fixed by UV boundary-mode count and is **identical for gapped and gapless** theories at short distance; the gap enters only the O(1) subleading finite piece, and no theorem converts subleading entropy data into a gap *lower* bound. (The vacuum modular Hamiltonian of a wedge is the interaction-blind boost — Bisognano–Wichmann 1975 — so even modular data carries no mass scale.) "Area law holds" is therefore gap-blind: granting it yields no m>0.

## 4. Versus P4; the residue
LR/finite-block ≡ P4 exactly (not new); the area-law ingredient is strictly weaker than a gap and sits on the consequence side. For the transverse residue f₁²: entropies / Rényi / entanglement spectrum are **aggregate (concave) functionals of ρ_A**, while f₁² is an operator-specific spectral *edge atom*. Aggregate data cannot lower-bound a single atom — the GAP-G2-edge / RESIDUE-CONDITIONAL sign wall, in entanglement language. Gauge-theory ρ_A also carries Gauss-law edge modes (Donnelly, PRD 85 (2012); Casini–Huerta–Rosabal, PRD 89 (2014); Ghosh–Soni–Trivedi, JHEP 2015), further from the glueball residue. The Li–Haldane entanglement gap (PRL 101 (2008)) is heuristic, tracks the *entanglement* Hamiltonian, and has no physical-gap theorem.

**Sharpest line.** Entanglement is a consequence of the gap and an aggregate of the spectrum; a gap *floor* and a residue *lower bound* are both individual-edge data it cannot reach. Same wall, new vocabulary — no new door.
