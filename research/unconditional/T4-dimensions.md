# T4 — Why 3D is doable, 4D not (yet)

## Proven

**2D** exactly solvable — Migdal recursion (JETP 69, 810, 1975), Witten (CMP 141, 153, 1991), Gross–Witten large-N (PRD 21, 446, 1980) — and rigorously constructed with OS axioms: Osterwalder–Seiler (Ann. Phys. 110, 440, 1978), Driver (CMP 123, 575, 1989), Sengupta (Mem. AMS 600, 1997), Lévy (arXiv:1112.2452). The spectral gap ∝ g²C₂ is degenerate (no propagating gluon): not a template.

**3D.** Lattice strong-coupling gap: Osterwalder–Seiler 1978; Seiler, LNP 159 (1982). Balaban's low-temperature expansion proves exponential decay/gap for 3D classical N-vector models (CMP 167, 1995, series); UV-stability RG in CMP 109, 249 (1987). Continuum YM-**Higgs**: Chandra–Chevyrev–Hairer–Shen (arXiv:2201.03487, Invent. Math. 2024). A full continuum OS+gap proof for *pure* 3D YM is unpublished; no structural obstruction is known.

## The obstruction

D=3: [g²]=1 — **relevant/super-renormalizable**: finitely many counterterms, Gaussian UV at finite dimensionless coupling (g²a→0), geometric RG errors, and g² itself is the mass scale ⇒ classical gap ∼ g².

D=4: [g²]=0 — **marginal**: (g²log)ˡ log-divergences at ℓ-loop give an asymptotic, Borel-nonsummable series; IR renormalon lies on the positive Borel axis (resurgence cannot supply it — see cpb-attack/07). The Gaussian UV fixed point sits at the **boundary** g=0, reached only logarithmically, g²(a)∼1/log(1/a). Scale invariance breaks only by **dimensional transmutation**, Λ=μe^{−1/(2b₀g²)} (b₀>0); the gap Δ∼Λ is **exponentially small in 1/g²**, invisible to any finite-order β-expansion.

## Is 4D special? Yes

4D is the **upper-critical/marginal** dimension: D<4 super-renormalizable, D>4 non-renormalizable (trivial), D=4 carries asymptotic freedom, transmutation, and the exponentially small gap. Asymptotic freedom (β₀>0) alone is **not** the obstruction — 3D is also AF; what blocks is AF *at a marginal coupling*, forcing a logarithmic approach to the Gaussian point with a transmuted scale. The obstruction is **technical-structural, not a proven impossibility**.

**Sharpest form.** 3D works because g² is a relevant coupling that is *simultaneously* the mass scale and a super-renormalizable UV regulator. 4D has neither: its gap is a transmuted quantum scale that the β-expansion reproduces only as an asymptotic series.
