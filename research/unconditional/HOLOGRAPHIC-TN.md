## What is actually new vs FORGE5-CERTIFIED-TN

## Five-point verdict
**(1) Nonperturbative — PASS.** Contractions carry no `g`/`1/N` expansion, all β.
**(2) Sign / lower bound — FAIL.** The causal cone bounds *degrees of freedom per scale* (legs across a cut × `log D`), **not** the correlation length. One bond-dimension-D tensor yields a *critical* MERA with ξ=∞ **or** a finite-depth gapped MERA with finite ξ — **the same D supports both**. ξ is a fixed-point datum, not a bond-dimension datum, so no D-uniform gap follows. Further, RP (genuine; PROOF-GAUSS-INVARIANT-PF) makes T positivity-improving: PF pins the *top* of a channel and gives a finite-box gap = an **upper** bound on Δ_∞. Every contraction is positivity ⇒ bounds |⟨OO⟩| above, ξ above, hence `m=1/ξ` **below** — the wrong side.
**(3) Non-circular — FAIL.** The bottom boundary of a 4D gauge MERA, and its causal-cone transfer operator, **is** the Euclidean transfer matrix; its gap **is** the mass gap (KLPG, CMP 366,895; H1-T3). A certified bond-dimension-D MERA needs a 4D sub-volume law (open; downstream of the gap, H1-T4) **and** a transverse-uniform boundary gap = the gap in disguise. The cone makes the disguise *cleaner*, not smaller.
**(4) Acid test — VACUOUS.** A gapped *free* field is the easiest MERA (small D, finite depth, gap = m by hand): the tool succeeds most cleanly where it must fail. The massless free field is scale-invariant, D∼poly(L), no gap — but the theorem is then merely *silent* (hypothesis violated), not refusing. It emits no residue: `f₁²=ν({m₁²})` is a spectral edge-atom an entanglement/TN aggregate cannot see (H1-T4 §4; EDGE-f1sq-attack). Non-SUSY ✓.
**(5) Uniform — FAIL.** (a) Direction: finite-box `Δ_L≥λ` with `Δ_∞=inf_L Δ_L≤Δ_L` ⇒ a lower bound on `Δ_L` is an **upper** bound on `Δ_∞` (PROOF-FSS). (b) Volume: `L²(G^{E_V})` is infinite-dimensional, so a finite-D MERA must truncate the Peter–Weyl irrep sum (electric cutoff); that UV error compounds over the log L layers of the cone while the gap is IR — no volume-uniform ε(D).
