# PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY — can the Z_N 1-form 't Hooft anomaly force a 0⁺⁺ mass gap?

> **Verdict: No.** 1-form anomaly matching constrains only the **1-form / line (topological) sector** — the defect Hilbert space, the confining string, the vacuum's topological order. The neutral, N-ality-0 **local** interpolator `tr F²` is *provably blind* to it (a point cannot carry a 1-form charge), so nothing about the 0⁺⁺ residue `f₁²` or a glueball pole follows. Two premises are also wrong: by GKSW the confining phase has the electric `Z_N^{(1)}` **unbroken** (area law = unbroken), consistent with a *unique*, trivially gapped vacuum — not "N-fold degenerate"; and at θ=0 the electric symmetry carries *no* 't Hooft anomaly, so nothing is forced. At θ=π the mixed anomaly forces at most a TQFT — still gapped — never a 0⁺⁺ mass.

**The dictionary — read first.** GKSW (1412.5148 §5), verbatim: a perimeter law "corresponds to the symmetry being spontaneously broken"; "an area law for a charged loop" reflects "the corresponding one-form symmetry is unbroken." For pure SU(N) with an area-law Wilson loop: "the electric one-form symmetry is unbroken." So confinement is the electric `Z_N^{(1)}` **symmetric** phase; the *magnetic* `Z_N^{(1)}` is the broken one ('t Hooft loop perimeter law). Neither gives N-fold degeneracy on ℝ⁴: discrete 1-form breaking yields topological order, with ground-state degeneracy only on nontrivial spatial topology (T³), never on ℝ⁴. The "N vacua from center breaking" picture is the *compactified / high-T* **0-form** center in the *deconfined* phase — opposite form degree, opposite phase.

**What anomaly matching actually forces** (GKSW §5; Cordova–Ohmori 1910.04962; CDI 1802.04790). A `Z_N^{(1)}` 't Hooft anomaly forbids a *symmetric, trivially gapped* vacuum; the IR is then one of {1-form SSB, nontrivial TQFT, CFT}. Its content is about **lines / defects** — charged-loop screening vs. confinement, the projective 't Hooft algebra, the defect crossing kernel (cf. ANOMALY.md). At θ=0 the electric anomaly is trivial ⇒ *no* obstruction, a trivially gapped symmetric vacuum is allowed. At θ=π the mixed electric–magnetic anomaly forbids a trivial vacuum; the ℝ⁴-unique vacuum excludes 0-form SSB, leaving TQFT (gapped) or CFT. No local-channel mass is ever fixed.

**Key question — is `tr F²` sensitive to the anomaly?** No, structurally. A q-form symmetry charges q-manifolds and is measured by symmetry operators on (d−q−1)-manifolds; for `Z_N^{(1)}`, d=4, these are **1-lines and 2-surfaces**. A 2-surface in 4D links a 1-line, not a 0-point — so a **local operator cannot carry 1-form charge**, and `tr F²(x)` is `Z_N^{(1)}`-neutral by construction. The anomaly lives in the line / defect Hilbert space; the 0⁺⁺ spectral density, the residue `f₁²=⟨0|¼trF²|G⟩²`, and the existence of a glueball pole are **0-form / local** data. The anomaly is matched by the confining *string* (the area law), not by any local glueball — the cross-sector wall, the same physical content as the BPS / dual-Meissner split: confinement is a flux-tube (line) statement, blind to the local 0⁺⁺ interpolator (T1-INb; B1 §1; GAP-IN11 §4–5).

**Does "GKSW SSB ⇒ N vacua" rescue a 0⁺⁺ gap? No.** Even granting the SSB branch: the electric sector is the *unbroken* one, and discrete 1-form SSB gives topological order with degeneracy only on nontrivial topology, not a ℝ⁴ vacuum manifold — so no "N vacua" to begin with. The SSB/TQFT dichotomy is a ground-state / topological statement; it fixes no local mass.

**Sharpest line.** *The anomalous 1-form symmetry, when it bites, fixes the vacuum's topological order and the line spectrum; it is provably invisible to the N-ality-0 `tr F²` residue, so it can neither force nor forbid a gap in the 0⁺⁺ channel.*
