# BREAK-IN-2 — Q-CONS ⟹ Q_gΩ=0: fails; the boundary flux carries an R⁴ UV self-fluctuation, not a vanishing surface term

**Exact target.** With `Q_g = lim_{R→∞} ∫_{|x|<R} x_{[i}T_{j]4}`, the split `[H,Q_g(R)] = i B_g(R)`, `B_g(R) = ∮_{S_R} x_{[i}T_{j]k}n_k`, plus Theorem S (Q anti-self-adjoint, [H,Q]=0, `ker H=ℂΩ` ⟹ QΩ=0) is airtight. It closes iff `H(Q_gΩ)=0`. But `H Q_gΩ = Q_gHΩ + [H,Q_g]Ω = 0 + i B_gΩ`, so this needs **`B_gΩ=0` as a vector, `‖B_gΩ‖=0`** — not as a form.

**The needed vector is false.** `‖B_g(R)Ω‖² = ⟨Ω,B_g(R)²Ω⟩ = ∫∫_{S_R²}(x·n)(x'·n)⟨T(x)T(x')⟩_c dSdS.` The disconnected piece `⟨T⟩⟨T⟩` vanishes **identically**: W-invariance forces `⟨T_{μν}⟩=cδ_{μν}` and `x_{[i}δ_{j]k}n_k = c(x_i n_j−x_j n_i)/R = 0` (n=x/R) — the trace-anomaly VEV gives **zero mean flux**, `⟨B_g(R)⟩_Ω=0`. But the connected part is *not* small. With `s=|x−x'|`, `(x·n)(x'·n)~R²`, `dSdS~R⁴`: `‖B_g(R)Ω‖² ~ R⁶∫∫_{S²}F(R(ω−ω'))dωdω'`. The large-R mass is near the diagonal. The collar `|x−x'|≲1/m` has measure `~R²/m²`, where `F=⟨TT⟩_c ~ m⁸` (T has dim 4). So `‖B_g(R)Ω‖² ~ R²·(R²/m²)·m⁸ = R⁴m⁶ → ∞` (free massive scalar: `~R⁴ln R`). Mean flux 0, but **variance ~R⁴**.

**Why clustering misses it.** Clustering gives `⟨ψ,T(x)φ⟩_c→0` for local ψ,φ — the **form** `⟨ψ,B_g(R)φ⟩→0` on the dense local domain. That is one T-insertion decay. But `B_gΩ` is a **vector**; its norm is the **two-insertion** forward correlator `⟨B_g(R)Ω,B_g(R)Ω⟩`, dominated by `|x−x'|~1/m` self-contractions — the short-distance region where clustering gives no decay and the OPE singularity controls. Form-vanishing on D×D does **not** upgrade to the operator/vector statement Theorem S needs.

**Consistency, not counterexample.** Free massive scalar has `Q_gΩ=0` and `[H,Q_g]=0` — but `Q_g` is the Fock rotation generator, **not** `lim∫_{|x|<R}xT`: the hard-cutoff first moment has the same R⁴-divergent self-energy piece and never converges to the generator. So `Q_g=lim Q_g(R)` fails; `[H,Q_g]=0` is carried by the symmetry structure, never by `B_g→0`.

**Verdict.** Q-CONS⟹Q_gΩ=0 does **not** close. The failure is not anisotropy: it survives in the maximally symmetric, gapped, clustered free theory (where Q_gΩ=0 is true for unrelated reasons). The boundary angular flux carries a nonzero (R⁴-divergent) surface term = stress-tensor UV self-fluctuation on the shell; killing the mean is not killing the fluctuation. Hence IN-2 does **not** fold into IN-3/4/MG; inputs stay **6**. The only repair — define Q_g as the O(4)-net automorphism generator, where `[H,Q_g]=0` is structural — presupposes the symmetric net, i.e. U2: **circular**.
