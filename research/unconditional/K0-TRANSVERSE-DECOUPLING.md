# K0-TRANSVERSE-DECOUPLING — is the 0⁺⁺ scalar in a sector disjoint from the transverse-magnetic Savvidy tachyon?

> 2026-11-23 · 4D pure SU(N) YM · angle: transverse/longitudinal + J^PC decoupling at k=0.
> **判词 (Verdict): NOT 5/5.** The *classical* quadratic form does split exactly into a transverse-magnetic (J=1, possibly negative) block and a longitudinal+temporal (≥0) block — but the hoped-for identification runs **backwards**: the negative sits in the **physical transverse** sector and the positive block is the **gauge/constraint** sector (`a₀` Gauss, `a₃` longitudinal), which carries no 0⁺⁺. The physical 0⁺⁺ is a two-gluon `J=1⊗J=1⊃0` composite fed by the very transverse modes, and the uniform k=0 lattice mode is the **holonomy/centre modulus**, not the 0⁺⁺. Exact stuck step = two-gluon binding positivity = the gap. The sector-isolation lemma is free-field-blind and fails the acid test.

## 1. The exact classical split (what is TRUE)
Around a constant abelian magnetic background `F₁₂=B` on a fixed Cartan charge, the spin-1 fluctuation operator `M_{μν}=−D²δ_{μν}+2(adF)_{μν}` is block-diagonal under the little group `SO(2)₁₂×SO(2)₃₄`, because a pure magnetic `F` has `F_{0i}=F_{3i}=F_{4i}=0` ⇒ the `a₀,a₃,a₄` rows of `adF` vanish:
- **transverse-magnetic** `a₁∓i a₂` (anti-self-dual): `λ=k₃₄²+(2n+1)gB−2gB`, bottom **−gB** (tachyon);
- **longitudinal `a₃` + temporal `a₀`**: `λ=k₃₄²+(2n+1)gB ≥ gB > 0`.
No cross terms for pure magnetic `F`. So "the tachyon is transverse-magnetic" is exact (Savvidy; Nielsen–Olesen; Leutwyler). This is the genuine content of asks (a)–(b): there *is* a clean transverse/longitudinal block split, with the only negativity in the transverse-magnetic block.

## 2. Three identifications that break the 0⁺⁺ inference
**(i) The positive block is the gauge/constraint sector, not the scalar.** `a₀` is the Gauss multiplier and `a₃` the longitudinal gauge direction; both are removed in the physical theory — pure-gauge is the **exact nullspace** of `Hess S_W`, and the longitudinal/constraint directions are deleted (PROOF-PHYSICAL-SUBSPACE-CONVEXITY §1). The **negative** transverse-magnetic block is the **physical** polarization (it survives the gauge quotient: 7.1% negative, min −0.82β at the SU(2) crossover — W4-blocked-positivity). So the split puts the sign on the *physical* side and the "positive Hessian" on the *unphysical* side — the reverse of what step (c) needs. A positive `a₀` Hessian is just trivial kinetic `−D²>0` on a constraint; it contains no 0⁺⁺.

**(ii) A single gluon is 1⁻⁻, not 0⁺⁺.** Under `P`: `a₀` even, `a_i` odd; under `C` (adjoint): the gluon is C-odd ⇒ the physical gluon is `J^{PC}=1⁻⁻`. So a "0⁺⁺ single-gluon block" **does not exist**; the 0⁺⁺ is reached only as `1⁻⁻⊗1⁻⁻ ⊃ 0⁺⁺` (P,C both even) — a two-gluon (and higher) composite. The tachyon pair therefore feeds the 0⁺⁺ channel at second order, *through the transverse modes the classical split separates*. Quantum-number decoupling protects the (nonexistent) single-gluon 0⁺⁺, not the composite.

**(iii) The k=0 mode is the holonomy/centre modulus, not the glueball.** On `T⁴_L` the uniform mode is gauge-invariantly the centre/holonomy order parameter; the tree potential is quartic with `V₀″(0)=0` on the flat Cartan valley, and `[H_B,P₀]≠0` (the plaquette is a momentum convolution, so `P₀TP₀` is no sub-operator) — k0-CONSTANT-MODE-TRANSFER. Reading a 0⁺⁺ gap off "the k=0 block" is thus ill-posed; that block is orthogonal to the flux sector carrying the gap.

## 3. Gauge coupling and coarse-graining re-mix
The cubic vertex `A·[A,A]` is *simultaneously* the mass generator and the `J=0` binder — it is exactly what turns two `J=1` modes into a 0⁺⁺ and what maps `k=0` into every `k` (k0-CONSTANT-MODE-TRANSFER (A)). Coarse-graining averages each vector component equally: it preserves vector structure, projects onto no `J^{PC}` sector, and its curvature `G″` is a vector-mode object, rescued only by the additive Jacobian `W_geo≥N/12` (PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES), with the Savvidy fiber entering the defect `D` of `G″=S−D` (INVENT-VARIANCE-INEQ, `D/S≈2%`). The measured `G″>0` (W7: +0.27…+0.51) is for the **soft collective transverse** mode — a soft/hard, collective/fiber split (negatives 90–97% in the fiber, W6-BE), **not** a scalar/vector split.

## 4. Acid test — the lemma is free-field-blind
"Sector isolation" asserts only that the 0⁺⁺ is multi-gluon and the single tachyon is absent from the 0⁺⁺ single-particle spectrum. The **massless free field satisfies this trivially with 0⁺⁺ gap = 0** (two massless gluons reach zero; `V_eff″=0`, k0-CONSTRAINT-EFFPOT). A tool that returns the same "composite, tachyon-absent" verdict for free and interacting theory does not control the **sign** — it fails the charter's acid test (must be positive for YM, fail for free). It is sign-blind, like the near-orthogonality diagnosis (G2-CORRELATION-GEOMETRY).

## 5. Five-point verdict and exact stuck step
- (a) classical block split: **TRUE**; proves nothing about the 0⁺⁺.
- (b) "0⁺⁺ disconnected from the tachyon": **FALSE** — the 0⁺⁺ is a transverse two-gluon composite that *contains* the tachyon pair.
- (c) "0⁺⁺ k=0 block positive Hessian": **ill-posed** — that block is the holonomy/centre modulus (k=0) and the constraint `a₀`, not the 0⁺⁺.

**Exact stuck step.** A lower bound on the `J=0` two-gluon Bethe–Salpeter binding, `m²_{0⁺⁺}>0`, given only the transverse single-gluon spectral measure. That *is* the gap; the symmetry split relocates it (the tachyon is genuinely `1⁻⁻`) without supplying it. Transversality makes the tachyon a vector and isolates it from the **single-gluon** 0⁺⁺ (which does not exist) — not from the **composite** 0⁺⁺ (which is the target). The negativity staying in a clean transverse-magnetic block is real geometry, but it is the *physical* block; the hoped-for "the negative is in a harmless vector sector" is the one place the symmetry story fails.
