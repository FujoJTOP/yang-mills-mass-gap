## §1 · Two densities the corpus conflates
(α) **Window count** — discrete `0⁺⁺` (zero-momentum A1g) levels in a *fixed* physical window `(m₁,Λ)`. (β) **Weyl bulk** — continuum density `~L³`. LOC-DETERMINANT's `S₂=Σ_{n≥2}λ_n` is `(β)`-flavoured: it sums the *whole* spectrum above `m₁`, so `S₂/λ₁→∞` as `L→∞` because `~L³` pair states sit at energy `≥2m₁`. Those never enter the window count `(α)`. This conflation is the entire "density grows with `L`" worry.

## §2 · The exact trace is a total, not a split
`Tr T_O=Σ_nλ_n=a₀u+O(u²)` (PROOF-P3) is **one scalar**: it fixes the total trace mass, not the distribution `N(E)`. The split — how trace mass spreads over levels — *is* the state density, and a self-adjoint operator constrains nothing at second order beyond its trace. `H` gives the bottom `z₁=e^{am₁}` and the total; **not** the counting above `m₁` (LOC-DETERMINANT §1). The trace is a *gauge* for the total, blind to density.

## §3 · Weyl law — bulk asymptote, blind to a fixed window; it kills the all-state Rouché *benignly*
`N_spatial(E)~ω₃L³E^{3/2}/(2π)³`. *(i)* It is an `E→∞` asymptote — it controls the *bulk*, not a fixed low window; **no** Weyl-type theorem certifies *zero* states in a *specific* window above the bottom. *(ii)* Below `2m₁` there is no `0⁺⁺` Weyl continuum at all: pairs have `2√(m₁²+k²)≥2m₁`, reaching `2m₁` only *from above* as `k=2πn/L→0`, so they never accumulate below threshold; the `(m₁,2m₁)` count is a **fixed integer** (radial single-glueball excitations), `L`-independent for large `L`. *(iii)* Hence the all-state Rouché `S₂=o(λ₁)` is **provably false uniformly in `L`** (`S₂/λ₁~L³u`) — but only because it counts the `≥2m₁` bulk, *benign*: the correct probe is the **window-restricted** `S₂^{win}=Σ_{m₁<E<Λ}λ_n`. At strong coupling the only state there is the excited `0⁺⁺`, weight `u^{1.5}=o(u)=o(λ₁)`; the windowed Rouché closes at fixed `(β<1/48,a,L)` **for free**. The `L`-wall was never about the window.

## §4 · RP, and `2m₁` vs `1.5m₁`
RP gives `ν≥0`, log-convex `C(t)`, `m_eff↓m₁` — but the **free field satisfies every RP consequence** (`f₁²=0`): it carries **no** threshold lower bound (PROOF-EDGE-E2; G2D §4). The kinematic `2m₁` (margin `m₁` under the moving/two-particle cut) sits **above** the operative cut: `0⁺⁺` is C/P-blind, so the excited tower (physical `m_{0⁺⁺*}≈1.5m₁`, PROOF-ISOLATION §4.4) fills below `2m₁`, and it is *this* bound state the uniform window must sit below — `2m₁` excludes nothing the window cares about.

## §5 · Verdict, SSC reduction, exact residual
Uniform `(m₁,Λ)` emptiness with a *fixed* margin `κ` cannot be free: by **single-scale covariance** (G2D §2) every pure-YM mass is `c·√(scale)`, so `Λ−m₁²=(ρ²−1)m₁²` and `Λ−m₁²≥κ ⟺ m₁≥√(κ/(ρ²−1))` — **uniform I2 ⟺ the gap**. This is exactly `(MS)` (G2H §5: uniform-in-`(a,L)` gap / `inf supp ν≥Δ²`) and its isolation form `H*=H1` (G2G §4, λ₁−λ₂). **Exact residual:** a `β`- and `a`-uniform excited-`0⁺⁺` binding, `ρ=m_{0⁺⁺*}/m₁∈[1+c,\,2−c]`, `c>0` — the sub-leading `O(u)` correction pushing the leading integer-cost `2m₁` state down to `1.5m₁` (SUB2 `Cu` vs `Be^{−δt}`). No tool among `{H, RP, trace, Weyl, 2m₁}` supplies it: `H` gives bottom+trace, RP is free-field-vacuous, the trace is a total, Weyl is bulk-blind, `2m₁` is above the operative cut.
