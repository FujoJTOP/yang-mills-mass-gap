# PROOF-ESI-SU-N — can ESI L3 (single-ℒ reduction) or L1 (force a′=0) be proven for non-abelian pure YM?

**Verdict.** Neither closes, and **L3 cannot close by any Furry-type argument**: ESI's own eq. (4.10) *exhibits* the nonzero, gauge- and conformally-invariant 3-point family that the abelian proof annihilates, so ⟨FFF⟩≠0 is a theorem, not a gap to fill. The surviving content of L3 reduces rigorously to **one dynamical hypothesis — no emergent gauge-invariant scalar of dimension 2 or 4 beyond tr F² (and its parity-odd partner tr F F̃)** — which is *the same object as (iii) written in the scalar sector*, not a separable lemma. L1 is a finite, uncomputed partial-wave problem, **not decisive** without L3; the no-emergent-SUSY step is blocked by the identical emergent-operator wall. No step is fabricated.

## What (4.1) is
**(4.1) is `a₀ = 0 = 2b₁ + b₂`**, from "the absence of scalar fields of dimension 2 and 4 in the OPE of two ℒ's" (ℒ = tr F², dim 4). `a₀` weights a dimension-2 scalar exchange; `b₁,b₂` the dimension-4 scalar coefficients. Positivity of the twist-4,6 partial waves with (4.1) gives (4.5) `−3a′ ≤ b ≤ a′/3`, so `a′=0 ⇒ b=0`, and the ℒ 4-point function is free Maxwell. The "single-ℒ reduction" **is** `2b₁+b₂=0`: the only dimension-4 scalar in ℒ·ℒ is ℒ itself, with its self-coupling fixed by the free-fixed 2-point function (eq. 4.8), and nothing induced.

## Why the abelian proof fails
Its two pillars are abelian-only. (a) *Furry / local commutativity*: the unique conformally-invariant 3-point function of a Maxwell field violates local commutativity of Bose fields, so ⟨FFF⟩=0. (b) The clean ℒ·ℒ OPE then forces (4.1). For non-abelian ESI state "This last argument fails for a non-abelian gauge field," and *construct* the survivors: **(4.10)** `W^{abc}=f^{abc}(N₁W^{(1)}+N₂W^{(2)})` is a 2-parameter family of local, gauge- and conformally-invariant 3-point functions for any `f^{abc}≠0`. The color antisymmetry `f^{abc}` carries the antisymmetry that in the abelian case Bose symmetry forced onto the spacetime tensor (making it acausal) — exactly the loophole restoring local commutativity. Hence ⟨FFF⟩≠0 is **forced**, and (4.8)+(4.10) require an ℐ in an indecomposable rep of the conformal group in the F⊗F OPE (their **4.13**). Route (i) is therefore **false**, not unproven: no exact symmetry sets `N₁=N₂=0`.

## (ii)–(iii) status
- **(iii) no-emergent-SUSY.** N=4 carries *protected* gauge-invariant scalars of **dim 2** (tr φ^Iφ^J, the 20′) and **dim 3** (tr φ³, the 50); pure YM has neither — its lightest gauge-invariant scalar is tr F² at dim 4 (any gauge-invariant scalar needs ≥2 F's, dim ≥4), and it has no dim-3 scalar. "Pure YM ↛ N=4" fails only if such operators can *emerge*: the wall of L3.
- **(ii) L1.** The bilocal reorganization (hep-th/0504146) collapses the 31,990 structures to a finite PSD problem; the scalar structure is computed and reproduces (4.5). The four non-scalar structures are uncomputed. Even closed, L1 gives only ℒ-sector freeness, not ¬(iii).

## Conditional L3 and the exact missing lemma
Grant **NEO**: no emergent gauge-invariant scalar of dim 2 or 4 (nor dim-3 current) in the would-be IR CFT beyond {tr F², tr F F̃}. Then L2 kills `a₀`; at θ=0 parity kills tr F F̃ in ℒ·ℒ; the free-fixed ⟨ℒℒ⟩ pins ℒ's coefficient. Hence `2b₁+b₂=0`, i.e. (4.1), hence (4.5): `a′=0 ⇒ b=0 ⇒` free. NEO is **not** implied by locality + conformal invariance (ESI say so) and is equivalent to excluding the interacting branch. **No known 4D theorem supplies NEO.**

**Remaining hypotheses.** (i) L2 `a₀=0` — holds in the UV by operator content, conditional on the IR identification. (ii) **(4.1)/L3 ⇔ NEO** — does *not* follow; the decisive gap. (iii) L1 — an open computation. (iv) no-emergent-SUSY ⇔ NEO for N=4 — open. So ¬(iii) ⟹ free holds **only** as [ESI hypotheses] ∧ L1 ∧ NEO; NEO is irreducible.

**Sharpest line.** L3 is not a computation away from closing — it *is* (iii) in the scalar sector: "ℒ·ℒ has only ℒ at dim 4" ⇔ "no emergent low-dimension operator" ⇔ "no interacting IR CFT." The abelian proof fails because `f^{abc}≠0` spares ⟨FFF⟩ (eq. 4.10); the N=4 escape hatch needs the same NEO lemma, so it is no independent step.
