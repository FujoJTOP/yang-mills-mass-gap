# FORGE5-CONFINING-MONOPOLE — can a rigorous *confining-phase* monopole / fractional-instanton gas resummation lower-bound the 0⁺⁺ gap?

> 2026-11-23 · Fifth-constraint forge of a **direct confining-phase** monopole-instanton / fractional-instanton gas (exact holonomy-space Coulomb gas, or center-symmetry-preserving deformed potential) — **distinct** from the Coulomb-phase gas of `FORGE5-DUAL-SUPERCONDUCTOR` and the killed exact-duality route `FORGE5-DUALITY`. Question: is a gas resummed *in the confining regime itself* non-circular where the dilute Coulomb gas was not?
> **Verdict (判词): 2 of 5 — passes 1, 4; fails 2, 3, 5.** Fatal and coupled: **#3 (circularity)**, sharpened — the gas is rigorous only in the **wrong phase**; **#5** the mass it yields is the 1-form `√σ` scale, never the neutral 0⁺⁺; **#2** fails on the wrong object. The confining-phase framing does **not** rescue the dual-superconductor route — it *worsens* the circularity and preserves the channel failure.

**The forged tool (strongest form).** On ℝ³×S¹ add a center-symmetric double-trace deformation keeping `Z_N` intact (Ünsal–Yaffe 0803.0344); the effective IR theory is compact `U(1)^{N−1}` with a gas of monopole-instantons / fractional instantons of fugacity `ζ ∼ e^{−8π²/g²N}`. Or, on ℝ⁴, a dense monopole plasma in the confining phase. **Resum the gas**: Debye/dual-Meissner screening `m_D>0`, a magnetic-bion condensate (Ünsal 0709.3269; Poppitz–Ünsal 0906.5156; Anber–Poppitz 1105.0940), hence `σ>0` and a mass gap. Hope: this gas *is* the object whose convergence gives `σ>0` directly (Tomboulis–Yaffe-style), non-circularly, and — unlike the isomorphism route — the nonperturbative defect exists.

**1. Nonperturbative — PASS.** Resums an infinite topological-defect class (`e^{−S}`, fractional charge `±1/N`); no finite-order UV loop expansion. The nonperturbative object genuinely exists (contrast `FORGE5-DUALITY` #1).

**2. Sign — FAIL (wrong object).** `m_D²>0` is the **magnetic / dual-photon** screening mass. The Savvidy cone `{2g|F|>μ²}` is a 4D **large-field UV** object whose Hessian is `−D²+2ad F`, bottom `−g|B|`, negative count `∝(gB)²V`, negative weight `≲∫|F|³` — depth and count both **grow** with the field (`PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN`). An IR collective mass `m_D` cannot upper-bound `Var(G|0)` on a UV-dominated cone; transporting magnetic mass to the electric `G″=S−D` needs an exact 4D electric–magnetic duality, absent in non-abelian YM (`GAP-G2-magnetic` §4). Wrong object.

**3. Non-circular — FAIL (fatal, sharpened).** Rigorous cluster expansions (Mayer; Kotecký–Preiss; Fröhlich–Spencer) require **diluteness**. The only rigorous monopole/gas expansion is therefore the **Coulomb** phase (4D `U(1)`: Guth; Fröhlich–Spencer) or **3D** compact `U(1)` (Polyakov; Göpfert–Mack) — massless, no gap. The **confining** phase is dense/strong-coupling: no rigorous expansion exists there, and "the gas converges / condenses in the confining regime" ⟺ 't Hooft-loop perimeter law ⟺ electric area law ⟺ confinement (`GAP-G2-magnetic` §3): **input = answer.** The one controlled "confining" bion gas is the *deformed, small-S¹, semiclassical* one (Ünsal 0709.3269; Ünsal–Yaffe 0803.0344; Poppitz–Ünsal 0906.5156; Anber–Poppitz 1105.0940): it needs a **double-trace deformation** (not pure YM), **small L** (large L crosses deconfinement, GPY), and is a **leading-order semiclassical** expansion, not a convergent cluster-expansion theorem. **Rigor ⟺ wrong phase; right phase ⟺ no rigor.** The dichotomy is structural, as in the vortex route (`PROOF-CENTER-VORTEX` §3).

**4. Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS (nominal, vacuous).** Pure YM is non-SUSY; the free field has zero monopole density, `m_D=0`, returns `0` correctly (`f₁²=0`). Caveat: the *rigorous* deformed variant is not pure YM, and the pass does not transfer — `m_D>0` needs the circular condensation of (3).

**5. Lower bound `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` uniform — FAIL.** `m_D` lives in the 1-form / `√σ` channel; `tr F²` is N-ality-0 and provably blind to the 1-form center symmetry (`PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY`). The `k→0` soft mode is exactly the conditioned `G″=E[H]−Var+W` quantity = H1 = the gap. Even inside the rigorous deformed regime, the gap obtained is the **effective 3D** gap, not the 4D 0⁺⁺.

**Constructive ≠ non-circular — and confining ≠ easier.** The Coulomb gas at least *had* rigor (wrong phase). Demanding convergence *in the confining phase* demands confinement itself. There is no free lunch: rigor forces the Coulomb phase (no gap); the gap phase forbids rigor.

**Sharpest line.** A rigorous monopole gas exists only where it confines nothing (Coulomb) or in 3D; where it confines (`m_D>0`) it is non-rigorous *and* its positivity is the dual string's (1-form, `√σ`), blind to the neutral `tr F²`. The only *controlled* confining gas — the deformed small-S¹ magnetic bion — is a semiclassical computation of a deformed theory whose 4D limit crosses deconfinement, not a theorem about pure 4D YM. **5 条过不齐：确切断于 #3（禁闭相气体收敛=禁闭，循环；且严格气体只存在于库仑相）与 #5（错道，1-form `√σ` 非 `0⁺⁺`），并 #2（磁屏蔽质量≠Savvidy 电 Hessian 号）；通过的 #1、#4（非微扰、自由场正确返零）都不触及正界。**
