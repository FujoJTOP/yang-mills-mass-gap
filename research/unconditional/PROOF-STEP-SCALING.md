# PROOF-STEP-SCALING — Lüscher step-scaling: a rigorous UV running, the wrong half for the gap

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict. Step-scaling transports the *coupling*, never the *gap*.** Via the gradient flow the finite-volume coupling `g²_GF(μ)=t²⟨E(t)⟩` (normalized), `μ=c/√t`, is a rigorously renormalized observable with a finite continuum limit **to all orders in perturbation theory** at every fixed physical scale (Lüscher–Weisz; A2-L2). That is the *entire* rigorous content: an **asymptotic** step-scaling function `σ(s,g²)=lim_{a→0}g²(sL²)∣_{g²(L)=g}`, i.e. a one-loop-universal `β`-function expansion about the **Gaussian** fixed point `g*=0` — where the theory is free and the gap is zero. A genuine nonperturbative fixed point would be scale-invariant; pure YM has none in the IR (it confines), and its only exact one, `g*=0`, is gapless. **A gap/lower bound cannot be transported.** `σ(s,g)` is the running of the *marginal coupling* — the whole object lives on the **UV/high-band (`∇_⊥`, `Λ_UV`, H2) fiber side** of PROOF-IR-H1's exact orthogonal split, and is blind to the `k→0` coarse mode (`∇_∥`, H1) that *is* the gap. **Wrong half, sharply — and it adds its own wrong-half obligation (the nonperturbative `a→0` of `σ` is L3/G1, open) while never touching the gap half.**

## 1. The one rigorous theorem — and where it stops
The GF definition is banked (PROOF-GRADIENT-FLOW §1): for `t>0`, `E(t)=¼G^a_{μν}G^a_{μν}(t)` is a renormalized local operator with finite correlators. A.2-L2 (Reisz lattice BPHZ + Symanzik) then gives, *order by order in `g`*, convergence of the renormalized coupling at fixed physical `μ`, with the universal one-loop coefficient — so `σ(s,·)` exists as a **formal power series** around `g=0`. Stop there. The nonperturbative continuum limit at fixed `L`, and *a fortiori* uniformly across the `s`-tower, is exactly **L3/G1** (`sup_a Z_a⁻¹‖H_a‖_D<∞`; A2-L3; G1-EXISTENCE) — the one-derivative marginal wall, **open** and `⊥` gap.

## 2. The fixed point is free — hence gap-free
Asymptotic freedom says `g=0` is the IR-repulsive (`s>1` ⇒ `σ(s,g²)>g²` for `g²>0`) fixed point of `σ`. The equation `σ(s,g)=g` has, for pure YM, only the trivial solution; the IR is confining, not conformal, so scale invariance is **broken**, not fixed. At `g=0` the measure is Gaussian, the transfer matrix is gapless. So any *exact* "step-scaling invariance" is invariance of a gapless theory; the interacting content of `σ` is its `g>0` tail, known only as an asymptotic series. **The provable running and the physical gap sit at opposite ends of the trajectory:** `σ` is controlled at small `g` (fine/UV), the SZZ gap at `β<1/48` (large `g`, coarse); between them lies the crossover, where both stop (ATOM-strong-coupling; W2-transmuted).

## 3. Why no gap transport (the steelman and its failure)
Suppose `m/μ_GF` invariant under `σ`; then a gap at one scale would give it at another. It fails three ways. **(i)** `σ` is a function of the coupling only — it maps the *argument* `g`, not the gap function `m(g)`, which is the unknown; it carries no spectral datum. **(ii)** The AF invariant is the transmutation scale `Λ=μ·e^{−∫dg/β}`; "`m` invariant in units of `μ_GF`" means `m=c·Λ`, `c` a pure number — and proving `c>0` *is* the gap (GAP-G2-mass, route 1: `μ∂_μ m²>0`, `m²(0)>0` uniform). `σ` produces `Λ`; it cannot bound `m/Λ`. **(iii)** Even the *existence* of the physical gap as an RG-invariant observable is IN-9's `SPEC-RES` (open, G1-type), distinct from the gap (G2). Transporting SZZ's `β<1/48` gap to physical scale is crossing the analyticity wall — the Clay problem.

## 4. Which half — precise
Exact split `Var_μ=E_ν Var_fiber + Var_ν(coarse)`. `σ`'s derivable content is the anomalous dimension of `tr F²` — marginal, one-derivative, `∇_⊥`/`Λ_UV`/H2 (A2-L2/L3; PROOF-IR-H1 §1). It is the `β`-function twin of the flow multiplier `e^{−tλ_a(p)}`: on the gap mode `k₁=2π/L`, rescaling `L→sL` sends `k₁→k₁/s` and injects no stiffness — the identity on the mode that sets the mass (PROOF-GRADIENT-FLOW §4). The coarse `∇_∥` side is H1: `G″(0)≥λ>0` uniform, untouched, currently disfavoured (`≈−0.165`).

**Exact missing steps.** For *transport*: H1 — uniform `m_eff²(k→0)>0` — unchanged; no inequality in `σ` implies it. For `σ` itself to be nonperturbative: L3 — a-uniform marginal bound — also unchanged, UV, `⊥` gap. Step-scaling closes neither; it is not even adjacent to the second.

**Sharpest line.** Step-scaling is the cleanest provable *running* in the corpus — a one-loop-universal `β`-function rigorously extractable through the gradient flow — and it is orthogonal to the number it is asked to move: its exact fixed point is the free, gapless theory; its controlled regime is where there is no gap to transport; and the invariant it does yield is the transmutation scale `Λ`, whose ratio to the gap is a pure nonperturbative number that *is* the gap. It hands you the argument of `m(g)` and withholds the function.
