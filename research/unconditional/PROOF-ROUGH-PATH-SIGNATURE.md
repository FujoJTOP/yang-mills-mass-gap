# PROOF-ROUGH-PATH-SIGNATURE — the gauge field as a rough path: does the Chen signature tame the marginal / Savvidy wall?

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict (判词).** **No — a new language for the same wall, and it cannot even see the wall.** The signature (Chen iterated integrals) `S(A)_{s,t}=(1,∫dA,∫∫dA⊗dA,…)` is the universal feature map; on a loop the path-ordered exponential `Pexp∮A` *is* its first summands, so `S(A)` is the collection of all Wilson/nested-Wilson observables — gauge-covariant and a true *sufficient statistic* for the field's action on any flow (Lyons' universal limit theorem). But (1) the only renormalisation it admits is the Hopf algebra of iterated integrals = Connes–Kreimer = **BPHZ**, the route PROOF-G1-BPHZ already shows closes combinatorially and leaves (★) as sole residue; (2) rough paths is the subcritical branch of regularity structures (Hairer 2014), so marginally the Chen series does not truncate — all levels `n→∞` need scale-uniform bounds, the failing uniform-BPHZ estimate in tensor-algebra dress. **It hits the same marginal wall, is positivity-blind, and zero-mode-blind.** Acid test **fails**: the free field is a Gaussian rough path with a canonical signature, so every signature bound holds at `g=0`, where `f₁²=0`.

**§1 The formulation.** Lift the connection (along the flow, or along Wilson lines) to a rough path; `S` lands in the group-like Chen tensor algebra, gauge transformations acting by conjugation, so it is the natural covariant observable. "Signature renormalisation" subtracts the divergent *expected signature* — Hopf-algebra Birkhoff factorisation: BPHZ reorganised.

**§2 Two failures.** *(a) Marginal.* RS and rough paths coincide subcritically (W2); marginally infinitely many lift levels diverge. The signature's infinite dimensionality is the infinite tree/tensor sum, not a truncation — no subcritical degree, same wall (W3). *(b) Sign.* Iterated integrals are multilinear, positive-degree functionals; a signature *bound* `‖S‖` is a two-sided magnitude estimate, sign-blind like every Cauchy–Schwarz/RS bound (EDGE-f₁²), which run the wrong way (atom ⇒ total larger). Savvidy `(★)` is the *sign* of a second variation `E[H]−Var+W`, `Var` competing with `E[H]`; no norm bound supplies the Schur-convex coercivity (total ≤ atom). One may bound the signature of the indefinite Hessian `−D²+2ad F` and learn nothing of its sign.

**§3 Acid test — fails at `g=0`.** The free/Gaussian field is the *prototype* rough path (canonical Lévy-area lift). Thus `S(A_free)` exists and every signature bound is met with room to spare where `f₁²=0`. A bound the free theory already satisfies cannot force the nonperturbative residue.

**§4 Zero-mode blindness.** The signature is invariant to the constant (level-0) shift and records increments only; loop signatures record curvature-holonomy, i.e. `∫Tr F²`-type data, which coerces curvature but not the connection (CONTINUUM-3D-TO-4D §2b). The gap's marginal mode is `k→0` — the constant mode the signature discards. Like the gradient flow (PROOF-FLOW-CONVEXITY-TRANSFER §3), it controls where the gap is *not*.

**Sharpest line.** The signature is lossless where it is defined (Lyons) and sign-blind where it is decisive: it renormalises by the BPHZ Hopf algebra, is the subcritical branch of RS, and is invariant to the very zero mode the gap lives on. Honest sliver — it could give clean bookkeeping of T_uni's `a`-uniform moment bound, but that is downstream of `(★)`, not around it. The signature reformulates the wall; it does not move it.
