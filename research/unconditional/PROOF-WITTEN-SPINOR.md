# PROOF-WITTEN-SPINOR — the positive-mass-theorem template vs. the YM gap: the identity exists, it is the wrong inequality, and its curvature term *is* Savvidy

> **Verdict.** Witten's 1981 proof is **not a device for producing curvature positivity—it consumes it.** The spinor solves `Dψ=0`; Schrödinger–Lichnerowicz `D²=∇*∇+R/4` converts the *hypothesis* `R≥0` into the *boundary* identity `m_ADM|ψ_∞|²=∫(|∇ψ|²+(R/4)|ψ|²)≥0`. The YM analog **does exist**—the Weitzenböck/Jacobi decomposition `G″(a,a)=∫(|∇_A a|²+⟨a,ℛ_A a⟩)`, `ℛ_A` the curvature endomorphism from `F`—but `ℛ_A` is **sign-indefinite, its negative directions being exactly the Savvidy unstable modes.** The identity returns `G″≥0` only if `ℛ_A≳0` dominates `∇_A*∇_A`, which is false. **No spinor identity gives `G″≥0`.** Three breaks kill the analogy: pure YM has no fermion / gauge spin-connection for the spinor to solve against; gravity's mass is a *boundary datum at spatial infinity* while the YM gap is a *bulk spectral-edge datum* at `k→0`; and "mass≥0" maps to "vacuum `E₀≥0`" (free by OS positivity), **not** to the gap `E₁>E₀`. Right *kind* of object (a positivity **proof device**, not a BPS state), wrong quantity, wrong sign, no lever on the open datum. No fabrication.

## The gravity template
Witten (CMP **80** (1981) 381): on an asymptotically flat spin manifold with `R≥0`, a harmonic spinor `ψ→ψ_∞` (const) exists (Fredholm; a compactly supported metric perturbation, mass unchanged, restores surjectivity). The work is done by `R≥0` as *input*, mass = the *boundary* term at `∞`, and a bulk integrand nonnegative given `R≥0`. Schoen–Yau (CMP **65** (1979) 45; **79** (1981) 231) prove the same theorem **without** spinors—the spinor is a sly device, not the source of positivity.

## (1) A YM Witten/Bogomolny object? — exists, wrong inequality
**(a) Instanton/Bogomolny** `∫|F|²≥8π²|k|` bounds energy below by a *topological/charge* datum—the BPS charge channel, not the lightest excitation above vacuum (= BPS-SUSY-PROTECTION's wrong channel). **(b) Supercharge:** positivity needs `{Q,Q̄}∼H`; pure YM has only nilpotent BRST `Q` (cohomology = observables, no energy positivity). **(c) Gauge-fixed Lichnerowicz:** exists, and is indefinite (§3).

## (2) Witten spinor ≠ BPS — right object, no purchase
**Different and correctly so:** the Witten spinor is an **auxiliary elliptic variable used to prove a bound** (existence by Fredholm/index, not a protected in-spectrum state, no SUSY), whereas BPS protection (GOLDS-POS-MASS) is a *protected state* pinned by a central charge. So a Witten-type object genuinely is "a positivity *proof*, not a protected state." The sterility is structural: its PDE is tied to the **metric/Levi-Civita** connection, not the dynamical gauge field; pure YM has no fermion for it to solve against, and added adjoint fermions give N=1 SYM (gaugino-condensation—an EFT-anomaly gap, not a positivity identity) or a determinant, not a positive square.

## (3) The Lichnerowicz identity for the YM Hessian — and it *is* Savvidy
At a critical point `D_A*F=0` the second variation is (Bourguignon–Lawson, CMP **79** (1981) 189; cf. Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer)

On flat space `R=0`, so the only geometric term is `ℛ_A`; a slowly varying mode with `ℛ_A<0` gives `G″<0`—realized by the Savvidy background (PLB **71** (1977) 133; Nielsen–Olesen, NPB **144** (1978) 376). These negative directions **are** the gravity-analog of negative scalar curvature—but *dynamical*, not a fixed assumption. ## Exact obstruction
A Witten route needs a spinor solving `D_Aψ=0` for the *gauge* connection with a Lichnerowicz formula whose bulk term is `≥0` **without** assuming `ℛ_A≳0`, and whose boundary/flat-space piece yields `m_gap>0`. None exists: (i) flat space has `R=0`, so geometric positivity vanishes and only indefinite `ℛ_A` survives; (ii) the "mass" analog is a **boundary flux at spatial infinity**, but the YM gap is a **bulk** spectral edge at `k→0`, where the box boundary carries no ADM-like charge; (iii) `E₀≥0` is already OS-trivial, while the gap `E₁>E₀` is a spectral-edge/atom datum positivity provably cannot reach (SCHUR-CONVEX; GOLDS-POS-MASS). The spinor lands on the wrong side of the same wall.

**Sharpest line.** Witten's spinor proves `R≥0 ⇒ mass≥0`; the YM project needs `G″≥0 ⇒ gap>0` *with `G″≥0` unproven*. The YM Lichnerowicz identity reads `G″≥0 ⟸ ℛ_A≳0 dominating ∇_A*∇_A`, and `ℛ_A`'s negative directions **are** the Savvidy modes—so the identity consumes the very positivity that is H1/G2, and its conclusion (`mass≥0`) corresponds to the vacuum `E₀≥0` (free), **not** the gap. Right kind of object, wrong quantity, wrong side of the sign, no fermion to carry it.
