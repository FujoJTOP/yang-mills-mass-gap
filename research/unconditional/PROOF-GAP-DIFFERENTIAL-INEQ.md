# PROOF-GAP-DIFFERENTIAL-INEQ — no β-ODE governs `m(β)`; the only Grönwall transport is extensive and dies in the volume

> 2026-11-23 · Test whether a first-order differential inequality `dM/dβ ≷ F(M,β)` — from a Ward identity or an RG/β-function equation — lets ODE comparison (Grönwall) carry `m(1/48)>0` to `m(2.5)>0`. **Verdict: no such scalar inequality exists.** With `m₁(β)=−(1/a)ln λ₁(β)`, the eigenvalue is a *spectral-edge* datum, not a flow: its β-derivative is `⟨1|∂_β T_β|1⟩` (Kato), needing the eigenvector and the full matrix derivation, not `λ₁` alone — there is **no** `F(λ₁,β)`. `β` is a *bare* parameter of a fixed lattice measure, not a scale: the rigorous gradient-flow β-function governs the *coupling* and fixes the *argument* of `m(g)`, never its value (fixed point `g=0`, gapless); the `a`-theorem is a scale-flow between CFTs, mass-blind, not a flow along bare `β`. The only rigorous β-inequalities that exist — Weyl/Lipschitz and Holley–Stroock — *do* give Grönwall transport, but with constants scaling like the volume `V`; the bound is positive on a finite lattice and `→0` as `V→∞, a→0`. Uniform transport is exactly `H1`/the gap.**

**1. No scalar ODE — the derivative is not a function of the edge.** At a simple isolated `λ₁`, Kato gives `dλ₁/dβ=⟨1|∂_β T_β|1⟩`, a matrix element depending on the eigenvector and the *whole* `∂_β T_β`, not on `λ₁`; no one-dimensional vector field `F(λ₁,β)` exists. At the measure level `∂_β⟨O⟩=−Cov_β(O,A)`, `A=Σ_p(1−Re tr U_p/N)`, sign-indefinite because SU(N) carries **no FKG** (PROOF-COMPARISON-FKG). A spectral edge has no β-variational principle. (A genuine scale-flow gives `μ dm/dμ=−γ_m m` for a *renormalized* mass — not the bare-`β` axis.)

**2. The rigorous β-function and the `a`-theorem miss on type.** The Lüscher–Weisz flow coupling runs by `μ dḡ/dμ=β(ḡ)`, rigorous to all orders — but `σ(s,g)` maps the *argument* `g`, not the unknown `m(g)`; its exact fixed point `g*=0` is free and gapless; its invariant is the transmutation scale `Λ`, with `m/Λ` the gap itself (PROOF-STEP-SCALING, PROOF-GRADIENT-FLOW). The `a`-theorem (`a_UV≥a_IR`) is monotone along *scale*, *mass-blind* (a massive free field also flows `a→0`), rateless, and absent at fixed cutoff `a` along `β`. Neither bounds `m(β)`.

**3. The Grönwall inequalities that *do* exist, and their transport.** (i) **Weyl/Lipschitz:** eigenvalues are `1`-Lipschitz in operator norm, so with `L=sup‖∂_βT_β‖<∞`, `λ₁(β)≥λ₁(β₀)−L·Δβ`. (ii) **Holley–Stroock:** `dμ_β/dμ_{β₀}∝e^{−(β−β₀)A}` lies within `e^{±M}`, `M≍|β−β₀|·osc A≲|β−β₀|N_p`, so the Poincaré constant (= `Δ_Λ`) obeys `Δ_Λ(β)≥Δ_Λ(β₀)e^{−M}`. Both **transport positivity at finite `L`** — but `L,N_p≍V`, so (i) reaches `β₀+λ₁(β₀)/L` (window `∼1/V`), (ii) gives `e^{−cVΔβ}`; both `→0` under `V→∞, a→0`. No uniform transport to `2.5`.

**4. The genuine constant-physics ODE, and why it is circular.** Along a line of constant physics `m_lat(β)=a(β)·m_phys`, so `d ln m_lat/dβ=d ln a/dβ` — an exact ODE with the (nonperturbative) lattice β-function as RHS. But `m_lat>0 ⟺ m_phys>0`: it transports the *prefactor* `a(β)>0`, never the sign; `m_phys` is the very unknown, and the crossover RHS is `L3/G1`, open. Real, and vacuous for the sign.

**5. Exact missing inequality.** A cutoff- and volume-uniform bound `dm₁/dβ ≥ −g(β)`, `g∈L¹[1/48,2.5]`, uniformly in `(L,a)` — equivalently the isolation margin `Λ_β−m₁²(β)≥κ>0`, i.e. `H*(r₀)` one rung above the gap. Structural obstruction: every `β`-probe is **extensive** (`osc A∼V`; `∂_β⟨·⟩`=covariance with the extensive action), while the gap is an **intensive** localized `k→0` edge (GAP-G2-mass §c); an extensive derivative cannot certify an intensive edge uniformly.

**Sharpest line.** Grönwall needs a *closed* one-variable inequality and a *uniform* constant; the edge supplies neither — its β-derivative is a matrix element over the full, extensive action, positive on a finite box but collapsing like `e^{−cVΔβ}`. The one exact `β`-ODE (constant physics) moves only the prefactor `a(β)` and leaves `m_phys>0` — the gap — untouched. Transport is the gap, restated in derivative form.
