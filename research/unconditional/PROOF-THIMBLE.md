# PROOF-THIMBLE — Picard–Lefschetz thimbles for the 4D lattice sign problem

> 2026-11-23 · Complex-analysis attack (thimbles / Stokes), distinct from the W2 positive-expansion route. **Verdict: no — and the premise is misidentified.** At real β≈2.5 the bare Wilson measure is *already positive* (and reflection-positive); there is no complex weight for PL to remove. On the genuinely signed object — the blocked/Savvidy action — the thimble decomposition is unproven to equal the physical measure (T5). On any single thimble the measure is positive only *up to a residual Jacobian phase*; inter-thimble phases and gauge-orbit (Morse–Bott / Gribov) degeneracy defeat "single-thimble positivity"; and by Cauchy invariance any valid thimble contour reproduces the *same* positive-RP correlators, hence the *same* β<β₀ gap and nothing at 2.5. Positivity is the wrong resource: the wall is activity/convergence, not sign.

**Premise check — there is no bare sign problem.** On $G^E=\mathrm{SU}(N)^{|E|}$ with Haar $dU\ge0$ and $S_W=-\tfrac{\beta}{N}\sum_p\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{tr}\,U_p\in\mathbb R$, the weight $e^{-S_W}=\exp[\tfrac{\beta}{N}\sum_p\mathrm{Re}\,\mathrm{tr}\,U_p]>0$ pointwise for real β. So $d\mu_\beta\propto e^{-S_W}dU$ is a genuine *positive probability measure*, and it is reflection-positive (Osterwalder–Seiler, Ann. Phys. 110 (1978) 440). PL is machinery for *complex/oscillatory* weights; at real β≈2.5 there is nothing to deform. The "signed gas" is an artifact of the *blocked* action (Savvidy-negative Hessian, T5/W2) or of complex β — not of the physical contour. This answers (1): the bare measure needs no thimbling.

**A thimble is positive only up to residual phase.** On the stable manifold $J_\sigma$ of a complex saddle $\varphi_\sigma$, $\mathrm{Im}\,S\equiv\mathrm{Im}\,S(\varphi_\sigma)$ (the flow conserves it), so $|e^{-S}|=e^{-\mathrm{Re}\,S}>0$. But the pullback to real coordinates carries the complex Jacobian:
$$Z=\textstyle\sum_\sigma e^{-i\,\mathrm{Im}\,S(\varphi_\sigma)}\int_{J_\sigma}e^{-\mathrm{Re}\,S}\,e^{i\omega_\sigma}\,d\mu_\sigma,\qquad \omega_\sigma=\arg\det J_\sigma .$$
Positivity of $e^{-\mathrm{Re}\,S}$ does *not* make the integrand positive: the per-thimble *residual Jacobian phase* $\omega_\sigma$ and the *relative phases* $e^{-i\,\mathrm{Im}\,S(\varphi_\sigma)}$ are precisely a leftover sign problem (arXiv:1205.3996, 1303.7204; Witten, arXiv:1001.2933). So (2) is **no**: thimbles do not *recover* positivity — they *relocate* it into $\omega_\sigma$ and inter-thimble phases, which must be bounded independently.

**Single-thimble dominance — ill-posed and disfavoured (3).** $S_W$ is gauge-invariant, so its gradient vanishes along (complexified) gauge orbits: critical points are not isolated but **critical manifolds** (Morse–Bott). "A single thimble" needs a gauge slice; distinct slice↔orbit intersections are **Gribov copies** — exactly the saddle multiplicity one wants suppressed. The slice inserts the Faddeev–Popov determinant, whose sign flips across the Gribov horizon (a residual sign *before* complexification), and near the β≈2.5 crossover the saddle density proliferates across Stokes walls. Dominance is unestablished and structurally disfavoured; even a dominant thimble would be tainted by $\omega_{\sigma^*}$.

**Cauchy invariance — same theory, same gap.** When the flow gives a valid cobordism $G^E\rightsquigarrow\sum_\sigma J_\sigma$ (holomorphic integrand, no poles/Stokes crossing), $\langle O\rangle_{\rm thimble}=\langle O\rangle_{\rm real}$ for every gauge-invariant $O$. The thimble contour reproduces the *identical* positive-RP correlators, whose gap is a theorem only for β<β₀ (Shen–Zhu–Zhu, arXiv:2304.13917) and open at β≈2.5. A contour deformation cannot add convergence the physical theory lacks.

**Sharpest line.** Positivity on a thimble $\ne$ a gap: a positive measure still needs log-concavity/clustering, and $-\mathrm{Re}\,S$ restricted to a thimble is exactly as Savvidy-indefinite as the dynamics. Thimbles give a (phase-tainted) *representation* of the same integral, never a *convexification* of it.

**Exact missing lemma (would yield the gap; is not implied by thimble positivity).** *Thimble–SLC.* With $S_W$ complexified on $\mathrm{SL}(N,\mathbb C)^{|E|}$ and critical sets modulo complex gauge, there is a gauge slice and a critical manifold $\sigma^*$ such that, uniformly in $L$:
- **(T1) phase triviality.** $\mathrm{Im}\,S(\varphi_\sigma)=\mathrm{Im}\,S(\varphi_{\sigma^*})$ for every $\sigma$ of non-negligible weight, the rest $o(1)$ relative, and $\omega_{\sigma^*}\equiv0$ on $J_{\sigma^*}$ *(including a constant sign of the real FP determinant)*;
- **(T2) thimble SLC.** $\mathrm{Hess}_{J_{\sigma^*}}(-\mathrm{Re}\,S)\ge\lambda\,\mathrm{Id}$ uniformly in $L$ on the gauge-reduced thimble.

Then (T2) $\Rightarrow$ Brascamp–Lieb/Poincaré $\Rightarrow$ gap at β≈2.5. It is **not** a consequence of thimble positivity: (T1)'s phase clause fails generically (residual phase unbounded; FP sign flips at the Gribov horizon; Morse–Bott non-isolation), and (T2) **is** IDEA's H1 — unproven and numerically disfavoured (VAR-FINAL softest mode $+0.043-0.208<0$). Restricting to a thimble can only *enlarge* the indefinite directions. The lemma is the gap hypothesis relabelled, plus two extra obstructions; it reduces nothing.
