# H1-T3 — Tensor-network renormalization (PEPS/MPS/MERA) as a gap route

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh technique: rigorous TN contraction. Verdict: real theorems **1D only**; in 2D+ (hence 4D gauge) the terminal lemma is the mass gap re-expressed.

**Verdict.** Rigorous TN *gap* theorems exist **only in 1D**. In 2D+ — hence for 3D/4D gauge — every rigorous route ends at a "boundary transfer-matrix gap" lemma that is the physical mass gap re-expressed. The machinery is genuinely different (entanglement/area-law, transfer-matrix spectral theory), but the terminal step is the gap in disguise: it relocates the wall exactly as the finite-size criterion does (`IDEA-finite-size.md`), and adds no leverage.

**Proven / not proven.**
- **1D (real):** injective ("normal") MPS are the unique ground states of *gapped* parent Hamiltonians — Fannes–Nachtergaele–Werner, CMP **144**, 443 (1992); martingale proofs in Kastoryano–Lucia, JSTAT (2018).
- **2D+ (open):** whether a 2D PEPS parent Hamiltonian is gapped is "an important open problem" (Kastoryano–Lucia–Pérez-García, CMP **366**, 895 (2019), arXiv:1709.07691). *Injectivity does not help*: an injective 2D PEPS can have power-law correlations, so injectivity ⇒ unique ground state, **not** a gap — no 1D analogue to borrow.
- **No generic theorem can exist:** spectral gap is undecidable even restricted to PEPS parent Hamiltonians (Cubitt–Pérez-García–Wolf, Nature **528**, 207 (2015)).
- **3D/4D gauge: none.** Gapped solvable models (toric code, quantum doubles, 3D Walker–Wang string-nets) are gapped by *commuting-projector* structure, not TN contraction, and are not continuum YM. Gauge-TN work (Cataldi et al., PRR **6**, 033057 (2024), 2+1D SU(2)) is numerical. No 3D/4D gauge gap has ever been proven via a tensor network.

**Can 4D gauge be a rigorous PEPS with a gap floor?** Lattice gauge *can* be written as gauge-invariant PEPS (Zohar–Burrello–Wahl–Cirac, Ann. Phys. **363**, 385 (2015)). But representation ≠ theorem. The only sufficient condition on record is KLPG's: if every subregion's boundary state is the Gibbs state of a *quasi-local 1D boundary Hamiltonian*, the bulk gap is bounded below — exactly one direction of the Schuch–Cirac–Pérez-García conjecture "bulk gap ⟺ short-range boundary Hamiltonian." A 4D gauge PEPS's boundary transfer matrix *is* the Euclidean transfer operator; its spectral gap **is** the mass gap. The decisive lemma is the gap in disguise.

**Recent (2020–26) transferables.** Kennedy–Rychkov rigorous tensor-RG — J. Stat. Phys. **187**, 33 (2022); Ann. H. Poincaré **25**, 773 (2024) — is rigorous but for 2D *classical Ising near fixed points* (high-T / low-T discontinuity), not at criticality, not 4D gauge. The 2D area law remains open in general (best: frustration-free/gapped and 2D Gibbs states, Anshu–Arad–Gosset). None applies to frustrated 4D YM.

**Different from cluster/RG/RP? What would a proof require?** The area-law input is genuinely different from cluster/RP. But KLPG's method is a **martingale/conditional-expectation** argument — the *same toolbox* as the project's finite-size criterion, so it is not independent of the route already on record. A rigorous 4D gauge PEPS proof needs: (i) a 4D sub-volume law (open; 2D already open); (ii) normal/injective PEPS with a volume-uniform injectivity radius (the 2D+ "fundamental theorem of PEPS" is not established); (iii) a boundary-transfer-matrix gap uniform in transverse size = **the mass gap**. Step (iii) is unavoidable and circular; (i)–(ii) are open. The project's own `tm_gap_*` GEVP runs are precisely small-system instances of step (iii), confirming the equivalence numerically. Verdict: relocates the wall, no new leverage.
