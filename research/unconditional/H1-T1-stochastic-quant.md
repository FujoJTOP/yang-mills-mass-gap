# H1-T1 — Rigorous stochastic quantization as a gap route: the generator gap is fictitious time, Bakry–Émery still dies at β=2.5

**Verdict: NOT a fresh route, and not a gap route. Real Parisi–Wu (real β, positive measure) is the project's A5 / cpb 05+12 on the *measure* side and the NA-1 v2 *object-mismatch* on the *gap* side: the Langevin generator's gap is the fictitious-time gap, not the mass gap. Its only bridge — LSI ⇒ Zegarlinski clustering — needs Bakry–Émery, which W6 killed at β=2.5. The SPDE presentation leaves the Savvidy negative Hessian untouched: no new positivity.**

**Not the tried thing.** Not complex Langevin (T5). At real β the Wilson measure is positive, but that positivity (W2) only reaches β<1/48, never the crossover.

**(1) Measure side — known, stops at 4D marginality.** Constructive SQ via singular SPDE is rigorous in 2D YM (CCHS, arXiv:2006.04987) and 3D YM–Higgs (CCHS, Invent. Math. 237 (2024)); the invariant measure *is* the achievement. 4D is marginal ([g]=0): Hairer's subcritical degree condition fails, no gauge-covariant BPHZ, no well-posedness. 4D Φ⁴, the sole solved marginal case, is trivial (Aizenman–Duminil-Copin), so well-posedness would not even give a non-trivial measure. This is cpb 05/12: NEEDS-X. Nothing new.

**(2) Gap side — object mismatch.** L=Δ−∇S·∇ is self-adjoint in L²(e^{−S}); its gap λ₁ is the fictitious-time L² relaxation rate, **not** the transfer-matrix gap e^{−aH}. NA-1 v2 corrected this: "Poincaré constant of Langevin on A/G = mass gap" is an object mismatch. Smoking gun: NA-3 measured simulation-time mixing τ_P≈0.5 (finite-volume λ₁=O(1)) while the mass gap stays open — decoupled. The only bridge is LSI(μ) ⇒ Euclidean clustering (Zegarlinski); its three supplies are closed — (a) Bakry–Émery (3), (b) cluster = BL-6, β<1/48 (W2), (c) entropy/transport = **OB-1** circular (needs χ<∞ ≈ the gap). Gauge covariance adds nothing: full-space LSI restricts to gauge-invariants without Gribov fixing (gauge-invariance L5.1), but one must have it first.

**(3) Attempt — BE at β=2.5 fails; exactly W6.** Γ₂ = Ric + Hess S. On the full link space Hess S carries the gauge-orbit kernel ⇒ ρ≤0; on A/G the O'Neill curvature ≥0 helps but Hess S reaches ≈−1.1β in Savvidy directions (SU(2) L=4 β=2.5: min −2.81, 21.7% negative; min(Ric+Hess S)≈−2.3). So Γ₂≥ρ>0 is **false**; BE yields no LSI, hence no gap. Non-pointwise escapes (integrated Γ₂, Lyapunov + local-Poincaré, weak transport-entropy) all bottom out at the Savvidy region being *typical* at β=2.5, or at OB-1; GOV coarse-grained convexity fails on −[F,F]. Continuum: BE on the 4D SPDE is not even posed, and any well-posed presentation keeps the leading negative Hessian.

**What it gives / stops / new.** Gives: rigorous gap at β<1/48 (SZZ, arXiv:2204.12737) — already banked; a clean Gribov-free formulation; trivial finite-volume simulation-time gaps. Stops: at β=2.5, same curvature wall, plus fake-time ≠ physical-time. **Genuinely new? No** — a presentation change. Honest sliver: W6 refutes the BE *certificate*, not the spectral-gap *statement* (negative pointwise curvature can coexist with a gap); but no known tool exploits that at β=2.5 without re-importing the wall.
