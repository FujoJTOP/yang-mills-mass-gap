L3 is the **Osterwalder–Schrader regularity/temperedness axiom (OS0)** in its `a`-uniform form: the renormalised moment bounds plus Minlos factorial bound that yield precompactness and the limiting measure itself. Equivalently it is the one-derivative generator bound `sup_a Z_a⁻¹‖H_a‖_D<∞`. ## 1 · What L3 is, exactly
The fixed-volume theorem (G1-EXISTENCE) assembles: L1 (scheme C, definitional) + L2 (perturbative all-orders lattice↔RT match, a theorem) give the `a⁻⁴` subtraction; a **single-block** `G″≥δ_W` (Lemma B Haar curvature `W≥δ_W`, free-box gap `4π²/L²` a-uniform, weak coupling) gives `UVS(L)`; GL3 turns `UVS(L)` into relative compactness + Minlos ⇒ `μ_L`; GL4 passes RP. The **one open input** is L3, used solely to extend the single-block bound across the `J≈log(1/a)` shell tower — i.e. to make **OS0 hold uniformly in `a`**. Concretely: `UVS(L)` = `|S^{(n)}_{L,a}(f_1,…,f_n)|≤C_n(L)∏‖f_i‖`, uniform in `a`, + factorial growth; equivalently `sup_a Z_a⁻¹‖H_a‖_D<∞` on the smeared gauge-invariant core `D`.
