# FORGE5-TWISTED — 't Hooft magnetic flux / twisted BC: an exact identity, a real BPS saddle, and the transfer that is kinematically false

> 2026-11-23 · FORGE5 attempt: non-trivial **'t Hooft flux sector** (unique vacuum, no center degeneracy, "easier" Hessian) + an exact twisted-trace / spectral comparison to the physical `0⁺⁺`. **Verdict: cannot satisfy all 5.** Constructed: the center-Fourier identity `Z_0=N⁻¹Σ_θ Z_θ` and the `(anti-)self-dual` flux saddle (van Baal) with PSD Hessian. But **#2 fails** (a flux sector fixes a *quantum number*, not the local Hessian sign), **#3 fails** (the positive transfer inputs `σ>0`), **#4 fails** (free Maxwell is a counterexample), **#5 fails** (twisted lowest = flux/torelon, heavier, `L`-dependent). **#1 passes only for the identity part**; the useful sign input is semiclassical.

## 1. What is exactly true
- **Center Fourier, all β:** `Z_0=N⁻¹Σ_θ Z_θ`, with `Z_θ/Z_0=⟨V_θ⟩` the 't Hooft-loop expectation (GKSW 1412.5148; 't Hooft NPB B153). Twisted traces are observables of the *same* theory.
- **BPS saddle:** a stable constant-field background on `T⁴` with 't Hooft flux is necessarily `(anti-)self-dual` (van Baal CMP **94** (1984) 397); there `Hess(−D²+2ad F_B)⪰0` (BPS lemma, PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN §3). This is small-volume/semiclassical, weight `e^{−8π²|Q|/g²}`.

## 2. Why a unique flux vacuum says nothing about the cone (#2)
"Unique vacuum at fixed flux" means the 1-form/center charge is **fixed** — a superselection label, true in *every* regime including the free field and the deconfined phase. It is orthogonal to the Savvidy question, the *local* curvature of `S_W`: non-BPS backgrounds of nonzero excess exist in **every** sector, with `λ_min=−g|B|` there. PSD holds only at the single self-dual saddle of measure `e^{−8π²/g²}`; the typical large-volume field is non-BPS, where W6 measures `−1.12β`, 22–49% negative. Flux quantizes `N_neg`, never signs it. By the exact `G″≈1/Var`, a *lower* bound on the flux/variance energy pushes `G″` toward `0⁻` — the wrong direction (REDTEAM-SPEC).

## 3. The transfer inequality is kinematically false (#3, #4, #5)
Suppose an `L`-uniform sector comparison `m_phys≥Φ(m_tw)`, `Φ>0`, interaction-independent. **Free Maxwell on `T³` refutes it:** the trivial sector has the `k=0` transverse zero mode, `m_phys=0`; a twist lifts it, `m_tw>0`, so `0≥Φ(>0)` is false. A sector comparison is pure transfer-operator kinematics, identical for free and interacting gauge theory, so it cannot hold for YM and fail for free Maxwell. Rescuing `Φ>0` forces `σ>0`/confinement — **circular (#3)**, and no longer a transfer. Structurally, local operators are blind to flux sectors (PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY): the exact Fourier identity gives only the *first*-eigenvalue bound `E_0^{(0)}≤N⁻¹Σ_θ E_0^{(θ)}` (vacuum below average), while the gap is a *second* eigenvalue, invisible. In confined YM the twisted ground state is the flux/torelon at `E∼σL`, **above** the physical vacuum and growing with `L` — the inequality runs backward (#5). The one clean twisted-sector bound, semiclassical `∼1/L` (Lüscher NPB **219** (1983) 233 is a one-loop expansion), vanishes as `L→∞`: no uniform `λ`.

## 4. Exact failure
`Z_θ=Tr(T U_θ)` encodes the **first** eigenvalue of each flux block; the `0⁺⁺` gap is the **second** eigenvalue of the trivial block. No exact inequality maps one to the other: free Maxwell (`m_phys=0<m_tw`) is a strict counterexample, and any YM-specific version must assume `σ>0` (GAP-G2-magnetic). **Fails #2, #3, #4, #5; #1 only for the Fourier identity.** Salvageable: center-Fourier, the BPS-PSD saddle, twisted-EK / Lüscher volume-independence (González-Arroyo–Okawa PRD **27** (1983) 2397) — all about center symmetry / finite-volume corrections, never the nonperturbative sign or a uniform gap.

**Sharpest line.** *A 't Hooft flux sector fixes a quantum number, not the sign of a Hessian.* The unique vacuum and PSD saddle are true at small volume / measure zero; the transfer is a second-eigenvalue statement the twisted trace cannot carry, and free Maxwell's `m_phys=0<m_tw` is the exact counterexample.
