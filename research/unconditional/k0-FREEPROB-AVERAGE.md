# k0-FREEPROB-AVERAGE — free probability pins the volume-average's ESD, not the k→0 gap

> 2026-11-23 · Target: the zero-momentum coarse mode `a_0`, `Var_μ(a_0)≤1/λ`, covariance norm `=1/m_eff²=gap`; hypothesis: the volume average `a_0=(1/V)∫A` is a random-matrix / free-probability object (ESD of a sum of weakly-dependent local matrices), so Voiculescu free CLT + concentration give a deterministic limit whose curvature is the gap. **判词: cannot satisfy all 5.** Free CLT *does* control the normalized average — and produces exactly the `1/V`-vanishing fluctuation that the gap's `V`-uniform `1/λ` forbids, on the wrong measure (equal-time ESD, not the Euclidean-time Källén–Lehmann atom). **#1 in principle only; #2–#5 fail.**

**The normalization trap — the whole verdict in one line.** The k=0 Fourier coefficient and the volume average are the same field up to one factor `V`: for a free massive field `Var((1/V)∫A)=1/(V m_eff²)` while the gap's covariance is the *unnormalized* coefficient `⟨Â(0)Â(0)⟩=1/m_eff²`, `V`-independent. Free probability computes the former: the `√V`-normalized sum converges (free CLT) to a semicircle of the single-site variance, and the volume average `(1/V)Σ` concentrates with variance `σ²/V→0`. Both are `V`-scaling statements about the *normalized* sum. The gap is a `V`-uniform bound on the *unnormalized* zero mode. The two differ by exactly the factor `V` and by measure — this is the "aggregate vs gap" fork, and it is fatal.

**#1 nonperturbative — PASS in principle, unbuilt in 4D.** Free CLT / free additivity are exact `N=∞` statements, not loop expansions (Voiculescu). But the object they presuppose — the 4D master field — is the unbuilt planar-loop solution (H1-T8 §3); Chatterjee's construction is small-β only (CMP 366 (2019) 203).

**#2 sign-controlling — FAIL.** The ESD and its variance are positive second moments: `(1/N)tr a_0²≥0` whether `Hess V` is convex or Savvidy-indefinite. Free-probability concentration is Hessian-sign-blind — the identical defect diagnosed for bounded-oscillation concentration (PROOF-CONCENTRATION §3). It cannot prefer `G″>0` to `G″<0`.

**#3 non-circular — FAIL.** Reading a mass off the deterministic ESD needs (a) identifying the ESD with the Källén–Lehmann measure, but `τ` is equal-time and the gap is the transfer/KMS generator — trace static, gap dynamical (H1-T8 §2; PROOF-DISSOLUTION-SPECTRAL-DENSITY §3); and (b) a `V`-uniform `k→0` stiffness, which *is* H1, the gap renamed (PROOF-COARSE-POINCARE). The free-CLT hypothesis — the average is a sum of asymptotically free variables — is precisely the planar closure 4D lacks (Makeenko–Migdal does not close). Circular.

**#4 non-SUSY + fails free field — FAIL (vacuous).** Non-SUSY ✓; but in the normalization where free probability applies the bound *cannot fail* the free field: the free massive average has `Var=1/(Vm²)→0`, the massless field (zero mode removed) has `Var→0` — both satisfy `≤1/λ`, extracting no positivity. In the normalization where the free field *does* fail (`Var(Â(0))=∞`), free probability does not compute the object. Both horns are vacuous.

**#5 uniform LOWER bound — FAIL.** The free-CLT `1/V` is exactly the `V`-dependence a uniform gap forbids: it is an upper bound on the aggregate spread that `→0`, not a `V`-uniform lower bound on `m_eff²(0)`. Any mass read at the ESD *edge* is Tracy–Widom-random at scale `V^{-2/3}`, so the deterministic limit free probability advertises fails precisely where the mass would sit (GAP-G2-edge: moments are upper-only; the edge is the non-moment datum).

**Sharpest line.** Free probability and the gap are the same kind of statement at opposite normalizations: free CLT *derives* the `1/V` vanishing of the volume-average's fluctuations that trivializes `Var≤1/λ` for any mass; the gap demands a `V`-uniform `1/λ` on the *unnormalized* zero mode — a trace-static statement free additivity never sees, whose atom the free-CLT limit (an absolutely continuous semicircle with soft edges) does not even contain. **#1 in principle; #2, #3, #4, #5 fail. The fence is the factor `V` and the ESD-vs-KL measure — not a technical gap but a category error.**
