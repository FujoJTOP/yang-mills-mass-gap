# PROOF-COMPARISON-FKG — can a monotone / FKG comparison carry the gap from strong coupling to β≈2.5?

> **Verdict: the route is closed, for two independent reasons, and the second is fatal even if the first were repaired.** (1) Lattice SU(N) Yang–Mills carries **no FKG / positive-association structure**: a lattice-ordered group is torsion-free, but SU(N) has torsion, and U(1)=circle has only a cyclic order — so the single-site space admits no group-compatible lattice order; the Wilson interaction is a *class function* of `tr U_p`, flat along non-ordered orbits. Positive character coefficients are *basis* positivity, not association. FKG holds only for finite gauge groups (Z_N / Ising gauge is an FK random-cluster) and ordered abelian spins, and does not transfer. (2) Even granting any stochastic comparison `μ_{β≈2.5} ≼ ν_strong`, FKG's inequality has the **wrong sign**: it lower-bounds connected correlations (`E[fg]−E[f]E[g] ≥ 0`, anti-decay), while a gap is an *upper* bound on `|E[fg]−E[f]E[g]|` with a uniform rate; a monotone coupling orders means, not covariances. The exact missing inequality is the **reverse-FKG / clustering upper bound**, which *is* the gap. No fabrication.

**(1) No order to be monotone in.** FKG needs (a) single-site spaces that are lattices under a product order and (b) a supermodular (attractive) density. Both fail. SU(N), N≥2, is compact, hence has torsion, and a lattice-ordered group is torsion-free — so no translation-invariant lattice order exists; the maximal torus U(1)^{N−1} is a torus, not a chain, and even U(1) has only a *circular* order. The Wilson weight `e^{βΣ_p Re tr U_p}` is real and positive (W2), but its exponent is conjugation-invariant and cannot be supermodular in any link order. The character expansion `e^{β Re tr U}=Σ_λ c_λ χ_λ(U)`, `c_λ≥0` (the positivity OS'78 exploit), is positivity of *coefficients*; the Wilson-loop algebra is a commutative `*`-algebra, not a measure on a lattice. RP gives a *cone* (INEQ-R1), not a lattice order. Hence the comparison inequality itself is unavailable — the corpus's standing "SU(N) 无 FKG/正关联."

**(2) Wrong inequality type — and the coupling does not contract.** If `μ ≼ ν` in the FKG order, domination orders expectations of *increasing* `f`; it does **not** order covariances — FKG measures have arbitrarily slow decay (critical Ising is FKG with `ξ=∞`). FKG's native output `0 ≤ E_μ[fg]−E_μ[f]E_μ[g]` pushes correlations *up*; a gap `m` is the *upper* bound `|E_μ[fg]−E_μ[f]E_μ[g]| ≤ e^{−m·d(A,B)}‖f‖‖g‖`, false in general exactly where the gap fails. The literal monotone coupling of the heat-bath/SW dynamics fares no better: its Dobrushin coefficient is `C(β)≈2β(d−1)·O(1)≈15≫1` at β=2.5 (each link sits in six plaquettes), so `1−C<0` — no contraction, no transfer. The strong-coupling gap (OS'78; SZZ β<1/48, ≈10²× below β_c; SU(2) β≈2.5, SU(3) β≈5.7) is real but proven *directly* by cluster expansion, never by FKG, and shrinks toward β₀.

**(3) Exact missing inequality.** For all monotone local `f,g` and all β∈[β_c,2.5]:
`0 ≤ E_{μ_β}[fg]−E[f]E[g] ≤ C‖f‖‖g‖ e^{−m_β d(A,B)},  inf_β m_β>0.`
The left line is FKG — absent on SU(N). The right line, uniform in β, **is** the gap. FKG could ever supply only the left; constructing the right is proving the gap. This is distinct from the cluster (analyticity radius), RG (uniform control) and positivity-of-measure (sign) walls: this route fails by **direction** overlaid on **order**. The available substitutes — Ginibre/OS inequalities, the positive character/polymer expansion — are exactly what OS'78 already use for the strong-coupling gap, and give no monotone handle across the crossover.

**Sharpest line.** The program asks FKG to prove its own reversal: FKG drives correlations *up*, the gap needs them bounded *down*. Even with an order on SU(N) supplied, a monotone comparison is a lower-bound machine aimed at an upper-bound target, and the coupling itself fails to contract at β=2.5.
