# W2 — Extracting the transmuted gap

**Verdict.** Each route proves a gap only in a controlled regime (small β, small S¹, or D<4). None reaches ℝ⁴ at strong coupling; the missing input is always a continuity/RG theorem across the crossover.

**(i) Instanton/caloron gas.** Proves a gap only if diluteness and an IR cutoff are assumed. ℝ⁴ fails: the instanton size ρ is a modulus, large instantons cost no action, so the gas is non-dilute and IR-divergent. Calorons on ℝ³×S¹ have maximal size and are controlled (Schäfer–Shuryak hep-ph/9610451; Ünsal 0708.1772). No rigorous ℝ⁴ gas.

**(ii) Resurgence/trans-series.** Proves Borel–Écalle resummation and a trans-series; in 2D CP^{N−1} it reproduces the gap (Argyres–Ünsal 1206.1890; Dunne–Ünsal 1210.2423). ℝ⁴ fails: IR renormalons lie on the positive Borel axis and the transseries parameter is undetermined — ℝ⁴ has no semiclassical bion sector (Maiezza–Vasquez 2405.01639). Proves consistency, not a gap.

**(iii) Lattice strong coupling.** Rigorously proves area law and mass gap for β<β₀, with area law extended in β (Osterwalder–Seiler 1978; Shen–Zhu–Zhu 2204.12737; Cao–Park–Sheffield 2307.06790; Cao–Nissim–Sheffield 2505.16585). ℝ⁴ fails: finite convergence radius; no rigorous resummation to β→∞, where the continuum is an essential singularity (transmutation).

**(iv) 't Hooft-flux torus / semiclassical confinement.** On ℝ³×S¹ with center stabilization, monopole-instantons and magnetic bions generate dual-photon mass, area law, and gap in the small-L semiclassical regime (Ünsal 0708.1772, 0709.3269; Shifman–Ünsal 0802.1232; Ünsal–Yaffe 0803.0344; review Poppitz 2111.10423). ℝ⁴ fails: L→∞ is strong coupling; adiabatic continuity is conjectural; bions exist only on ℝ³×S¹. Small-L control is plausibly rigorizable; the ℝ⁴ limit is the open problem.

**(v) Variational/operator/sum rules.** QCD inequalities rigorously order meson/baryon masses given positivity and chiral breaking, but give no pure-glue gap and vanish with m_π (Weingarten PRL 51 1830; Vafa–Witten PRL 53 535). SVZ sum rules (Shifman–Vainshtein–Zakharov, NPB 147, 1979) are heuristic. Variational methods give only upper bounds; lower bounds need Temple/Weinstein a priori gaps, unavailable. Rigorous spectral bounds exist only at strong coupling/tiny volume.

**Plausibly rigorous?** Not now. (iv) is closest: a proof of adiabatic continuity (or a new nonperturbative RG) from small S¹/strong coupling to ℝ⁴ would close it — that is the target itself.
