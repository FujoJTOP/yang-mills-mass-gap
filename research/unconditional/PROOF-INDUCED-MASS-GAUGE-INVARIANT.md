# PROOF-INDUCED-MASS-GAUGE-INVARIANT — is there a gauge-invariant Schwinger mechanism that forces m_GI>0 without the gap?

> **Verdict. No. A gauge-invariant Schwinger mechanism cannot be made logically weaker than the edge atom, because in the physical channel it *is* the edge atom — and the edge atom is the gap.** Two structural facts force this. **(A) Factorization.** On `H_phys` the Källén–Lehmann theorem makes every singularity of a physical correlator come from physical states: at a common intermediate state a 1PI `s`-vertex factorizes as `f_s·(vertex)·f_s`, so a pole *implies* the spectral atom `f_s²=ν({m²})`, and conversely. "Gauge-invariant vertex pole" and "edge atom" are one datum. **(B) The engine is unphysical.** The gauge-fixed mechanism beats the gap *only* because its `1/k²` pole is the unphysical gluon (`ρ_A` sign-indefinite, `A∉H_phys`); positivity deletes it — a connected `1/k²` pole in a physical scalar channel would be a massless physical state (a Goldstone), which gapped pure YM lacks (GOLDS-POS-MASS). Neither the STI nor the trace anomaly forces a pole, and a *forced* pole would sit at `m²=0` (Goldstone-locked), giving `m_GI=0`. The gauge-invariant induced mass is **not rigorously forceable; it is the gap renamed.**

## 1. (1) The gauge-invariant formulation collapses onto the edge

The mechanism (Schwinger 1962; Cornwall 1982) is a zero-momentum pole `Π(k²)~c/k²` feeding `m²>0` into `D⁻¹=k²+Π`. Its carrier is the residue `c` of the transverse three-gluon vertex's IR massless pole — the unprotected order parameter `H*(r₀)` (RESIDUE-CONDITIONAL; ATOM-FRG-mass). Replace the gluon by `s=trF²`: on the OS-positive, BRST-closed `H_phys` every pole of `⟨ss⟩_c` is a physical-state atom, and every pole of `⟨sss⟩` factorizes through it. The "pole" and the "edge atom" are the same object; the mechanism *names* the edge rather than producing it. Moreover the `c/k²` engine is absent: gapped physical channels carry no connected massless pole, and the free-field (`g→0`) `trF²` correlator is a two-photon cut from threshold `0` — a branch point, no atom, `m_GI=0` (acid test).

## 2. (2) Not forced by a Ward identity or the trace anomaly

The STI is linear/longitudinal; the transverse vertex carrying the pole has no anomalous slot (ghost#1 dim-5 empty, W3-marginal-BPHZ), so `c` is unprotected. The trace anomaly `T^μ_μ=(β/2g)O_S` is exact and the 0⁺⁺ channel is its channel — but it is an **aggregate/UV/moment** datum: it fixes the forward charge and the cubic coupling `g_GGG`, while the off-diagonal residue `f_s²` **cancels out of every Ward identity at the pole** (INEQ-T4; `F_T` is `f_s²` up to `(β/2g)²`, INEQ-R7), and its protected short-distance coefficient upper-bounds — the wrong direction (GAP-G2-edge). Anomalies do make "anomaly poles" (WZW, dilaton contact), but in longitudinal form factors, never a two-point atom. The only positivity-anchored *forced* atom is the Goldstone, at `m²=0` ⇒ `m_GI=0`; the `m²>0` pole is unprotected dynamics (W6 §ii; GAP-G2-mass).

## 3. (3) The transfer is the assumption

Grant `c>0`. The conditional theorem `c>0 ⇒ m²>0` (W6; ATOM-FRG-mass) concerns a *gauge-fixed* propagator mass that does not enter `H_phys` (PROOF-LANDAU-GAUGE-IR); its gauge-invariant shadow is the statement that `ν` carries an atom at the edge, i.e. `m_GI=inf supp ρ_s>0`. The "transfer" therefore presupposes exactly that atom: it reduces to the edge-atom hypothesis `H*(r₀)` = the gap (RESIDUE-CONDITIONAL; INVENT-GAUGE-INVARIANT-MASS).

**Sharpest line.** The Schwinger mechanism beats the gap only in the gauge-fixed sector, where its massless pole is the *unphysical* gluon; move to the OS-positive `trF²` channel and the pole factorizes *into* the edge atom while the massless pole evaporates — so a gauge-invariant induced mass is not a mechanism beneath the gap but the edge atom, hence the gap, renamed. No Ward identity or anomaly can supply it: any identity that fixed the pole would fix it at `m²=0`. **A gauge-invariant induced mass cannot be rigorously forced; it is not a shortcut to the gap.**
