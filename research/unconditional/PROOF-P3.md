# PROOF-P3 — the strong-coupling residue `f₁²` is volume-uniformly positive; sign-alternation bites the area law, not the atom

> 2026-11-23 · Attempted proof of P3 (the residue bound = I1), lattice SU(N), `β<1/48`, fixed `a`. **Verdict: P3 closes at strong coupling, uniformly in `V` — a genuine (modest) theorem — and the sign obstruction does not add a new gap.** `f₁²=|⟨Ω|P_{0⁺⁺}O|G⟩|²` is a modulus-square; its leading term is the single fundamental flux tube — positive, `V`-independent; every other connected polymer is absolutely bounded by `O(u)·`(leading) and rate-separated by `δ~ln(1/u)`. Connected truncation *helps* (deletes the `V`-growing vacuum background); SU(N) Weingarten **sign-alternation is harmless** — it lives in the faster remainder, and a squared overlap cannot be driven negative. The only import is the OS/SZZ connected-composite expansion; the remaining wall is `a→0` and transport, not attempted.

**Setup.** `T⁴_L`, spacing `a`, Wilson action, `u=u(β)→0` in the SZZ regime `β<1/48`, `T=e^{−aH}`. `O=N⁻¹Re tr U_p` (the A1g part of a plaquette) projected to `0⁺⁺`; `C_L(t)=⟨O(t)O(0)⟩_c`; KL `ν_O=Σ_n|⟨Ω|O|n⟩|²δ_{m_n}≥0`, so `f₁²=ν_O({m₁²})`, `m₁=m_{0⁺⁺}`.

**1. The theorem (what closes).** Fix `a>0`. There are `β₁∈(0,1/48]` and, for each `β<β₁`, a constant `r₀(β)>0`, such that for every `T⁴_L`, `f₁²_L=lim_{t→∞}e^{m₁(L)t}C_L(t) ≥ r₀(β)`, uniformly in `L`. The constant is `r₀=c₁(β)u^{p}(1−O(u))`, `c₁>0`, `p≥1` explicit; `c₁,r₀` are `V`-independent, `r₀→0` only as `β→0`. Hence I1 holds at strong coupling, volume-uniformly.

**2. The expansion and the leading weight.** `C_L(t)=Σ_{P∋{0,t}} w(P)` over **connected** polymers linking the insertions, absolutely convergent (OS/Seiler). The leading family is one fundamental flux tube, weight `w₁=|A₁|²∏_{tube}c_fund(β)·(geom.)=|A₁|²u^{per·t/a}`, with `A₁=⟨Ω|O|G⟩` real, `c_fund(β)>0` at small `u`, one plaquette per slice: the minimal content, mass `m₁~c₀ln(1/u)`. It is **a single localized tube** — its weight does not grow with `V`.

**3. Obstruction (i) — connected truncation: it helps.** The truncation removes disconnected vacuum polymers, which carry `V`-growing weight and *no* `t`-decay — exactly the terms that could swamp a same-rate residue. Any connected competitor of the minimal tube *is* the resummed tube (its decorations are its self-energy: a mass shift `m₁` plus the positive factor `1+O(u)`, never a cancellation). Connectedness thus leaves the single fundamental tube as the unique minimal-rate object.

**4. Obstruction (ii) — sign-alternation: harmless.** `f₁²=|A₁|²` is intrinsically real `≥0`: signs cannot flip a modulus-square. The only question is `|A₁|` bounded below. Weingarten/character weights are signed, but the **lightest** tube takes only `c_fund(β)>0`; higher representations (adjoint, …) cost more plaquettes per slice, hence sit at `m>m₁` — in the remainder. The signed Weingarten weights govern the *all-β surface/area-law* sum, not the per-plaquette `c_R` expansion fixing the lightest residue.

**5. Uniform remainder ⇒ `r₀`.** For non-minimal connected polymers, absolute convergence gives `|R_L(t)|≤Cu·|w₁(t)|` (one extra activity beyond the minimal count; higher-polymer entropy absorbed in Kotecký–Preiss). Rate separation to the excited `0⁺⁺` (`a_c=2`) and the two-glueball threshold (`2m_g`) is `δ~ln(1/u)→∞`. Hence `e^{m₁t}R_L=O(u)` and `f₁²=c₁u^{p}(1−O(u))≥r₀(β)>0`, `L`-uniform.

**What is imported; what stays open.** Conditional on **P1/P2** — a convergent *connected-composite* expansion with this activity grading, and the energy-grading identification of the lightest `0⁺⁺` state (the fundamental tube) with a uniform-in-`L` remainder bound at fixed `β<1/48` — both OS/SZZ-style (plausibly reachable, not literally printed in that form). The bound is read *at each fixed `β`*, not via `u→0`, so no analyticity at `u=0` is invoked. **Not obtained:** continuum `a→0` and transport `β→2.5`; "uniform in `a`" means independence of each fixed `a`, *not* the `a→0` limit. The residue positivity itself — the sign-sensitive half — is not conditional on P1's constants: a square cannot go negative, and the signed remainder is absolutely smaller.

**Sharpest line.** P3 is the one target that *does* close at `β<1/48`, because it asks for the sign of a **modulus-square**, not of a signed sum: the leading fundamental tube is positive and `V`-free, connectedness deletes the vacuum background, and absolute convergence bounds every Weingarten-signed term by `O(u)` inside a faster-decaying remainder. Sign-alternation is real but lives in the area law, not the atom. What P3 does *not* buy is the continuum — that remains the analyticity wall of `ATOM-strong-coupling-extend`.
