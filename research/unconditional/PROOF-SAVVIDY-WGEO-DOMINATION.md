# PROOF-SAVVIDY-WGEO-DOMINATION — no gauge-invariant norm makes W_geo dominate H_ff pointwise; the surplus convexity is chart-local and anti-correlated with the field

> **Verdict.** **No.** For no gauge-invariant norm does `−H_ff^{−} ⪯ c(W_geo+S)` hold with a `c<1` **uniform** over large-field configs. (1) The Savvidy negative fiber is *physical*: every negative mode has `δF=d_AδA≠0`, so its gauge-invariant curl norm is O(1), and even the "best" norm `⟨·,(W_geo+S)·⟩` leaves the spectral ratio `ρ≳2.8–16≫1` on the very β=2.5 configs. (2) W_geo's gauge-invariant content is exactly its **constant floor `N/12`**; the convexity *beyond* it (`g″→∞`) sits only at the log cut (links near the center element, `F≈0`) — **anti-correlated** with the Savvidy driver `∝|F|`. (3) Prékopa is already a measure-level tool (`W_geo=E[Φ″]−Var(Φ′)≥0`), so it cannot be "pushed beyond N/12" to a per-config operator bound — a category mismatch, not a missing estimate. The measured `D/S≈2%` is a measure-average (equipartition) fact with a ~50× margin, already numerically beaten, but it is *not* the Loewner object and cannot be upgraded to one. The β-vs-β-independent scaling is a **genuine obstruction no norm choice can fix**.

## (1) The norm is innocent — the obstruction is physical
Gauge-invariant norm on the physical tangent space: `‖δA‖²_GI := Σ_□|d_AδA|²`, the quadratic form of the Wilson kinetic operator (depends on `δF` only, annihilates `d_Aω`). Every Savvidy/Nielsen–Olesen negative mode is transverse-magnetic, `δF≠0`, so its GI norm is `O(1)‖δA‖²`; the fiber negativity survives the quotient intact. Even the *sharpest* norm — take `‖·‖²=⟨·,(W_geo+S)·⟩` itself — only reduces the target to the spectral ratio `ρ:=sup_v(−H_ff)_{vv}/(W_geo+S)_{vv}`. With `−H_ff ≈ −1.12β = −2.8` (W6), `W_geo≈0.17–0.34`, `S≈1/Var(a)≈0.3`: `ρ≈3–4` against `W_geo+S`, `ρ≈8–16` against `W_geo`. Both exceed 1: `c<1` fails on the configs that define the measure, and the only β admitting pointwise `c<1` is `β < N/(12·1.1) ≈ 0.15` (SU2) — SZZ's strong-coupling window, not 2.5.

## (2) Full convexity lives in the wrong corner
Exact `g(d)=−2log(sin(d/2)/(d/2))`: `g″→1/6` as `d→0` (an eigenvalue **collision**, genuine — this is the floor), but `g″→∞` only as `d→2π` (the **log cut**, links approaching the center element z∈Z_N), diverging like `1/(2π−d)²`. There the surplus is confined. At a link `≈z`: `U_□=zzz^{−1}z^{−1}=1`, so **F≈0**; conversely a large field needs only per-link phases `O(π)<2π`, away from the cut, giving `Φ″=O(1)=N/12`. The enhancement is **anti-correlated with |F|** — large exactly where Savvidy is small — and cannot furnish a bound growing with the field.

## (3) Prékopa cannot be "pushed" to a pointwise bound
`W_geo≥0` is `E[Φ″]−Var(Φ′)≥0`, a statement about the **pushed coarse marginal** (global convexity of Φ ⇒ log-concavity ⇒ convex `V_eff`). It is structurally an *average/variance* identity; its only pointwise content is `Φ″⪰0` (Lemma B's `Φ″≥N/12`). Asking Prékopa for a per-config Loewner bound on `H_ff` is asking a marginal-sign theorem for a spectral-operator one — a category error.

## (4) The honest target that does hold
The viable inequality is the SCBL measure-level one: `G″=S−D`, `D/S≈2%` (50× margin), carried by equipartition `Var(G)≈E[H]`, **not** a pointwise `H_ff⪯W_geo`. Proving it uniformly is H1 (`Var(G|0)` bound) — a variance statement, not a gauge-norm statement. So "beat the 2% threshold" is already met on average and unmeetable pointwise.

**Sharpest line.** Gauge invariance gives no leverage: the negative fiber is physical and W_geo's invariant floor `N/12` is |F|-free, while the extra convexity is chart-local (log-cut, F≈0) and anti-correlated with the field — so `−H_ff^{−}⪯c(W_geo+S)`, `c<1` uniform, is **false**, and the β-vs-β-independent scaling is a genuine wall. What survives is the measure-averaged `D<S` (2%, = H1); the uniform pointwise domination is not makeable rigorous.
