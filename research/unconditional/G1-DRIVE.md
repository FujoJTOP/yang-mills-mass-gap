# G1-DRIVE — taking G1 seriously: the strongest rigorous fixed-volume object, what the SOTA assembles *today*, and what a complete G1 buys

> **判词 (verdict).** (1) The finite-`(L,a)` Wilson measure and transfer matrix are rigorous with a positive **finite-volume** gap — a finite-volume artifact (`c_L=4π²/L²→0`); the content is uniformity. The rigorous fixed-volume limit (OS0/1/3, `a→0`, `L` fixed) is a **theorem modulo one Hessian input, `L3`**. (2) Balaban (CMP 122, 1989) supplies the one-sided UV stability sufficient for *existence*; MRS is gauge-fixed; CCHS (3D) and SZZ (strong coupling) miss the 4D weak-coupling continuum. A Lean-checked, panel-reviewed 2026 submission reports a Wightman QFT as a 4D Wilson scaling limit for any simple compact `G`, but **not** infinite-volume uniqueness and **not** a mass gap. (3) A complete G1 yields the Euclidean OS measure, `H`, `T=e^{-tH}`, and KL measure `ν` — the **OBJECT** whose gap is well-posed; the OS axioms imply neither `Δ>0` nor `f₁²>0` (`=H1`), so the gap stays the content.

## 1 · The strongest fixed-volume result (unconditionally rigorous)

On `T⁴_L` at spacing `a`, the Wilson measure `μ_{L,a}=Z^{-1}e^{-S_W}∏dU` is a probability measure on the compact `SU(N)^{E}` — existence is trivial (finite-dimensional). It is **reflection positive** on link variables (Osterwalder–Seiler 1978; Menotti–Pelissetto 1987), hence gives a self-adjoint positive transfer matrix `T_{L,a}=e^{-H_{L,a}}`, `H_{L,a}≥0`. The finite-`(L,a)` Hamiltonian has discrete spectrum, so `m_{L,a}>0`.

But fixed-volume positivity is free and physically empty: `c_L=4π²/L²` is `a`-uniform (P1-L0) yet `→0` as `L→∞`, so it is not a mass; a physical gap is `m_phys=√G″_a/a` with `G″_a/a²≥Δ₀²` (G1-CONTINUUM-SCALING). The strongest rigorous fixed-`L` statement is **G1-fix**: at fixed `L`, along the two-loop RT `β(a)=2N/g²(a)` in scheme C with `a→0`, a subsequence `a_n→0` gives `μ_L` with **OS0/1/3** — a **theorem modulo `L3` alone** (a-uniform one-derivative generator bound; G1-EXISTENCE, G1-L3). All else (L1 scheme C, L2 lattice↔RT BPHZ, Lemma B `W≥δ_W`, free-box gap, GL3 compactness/Minlos, GL4 RP-closure) is proven.

One correction, per charter: `L3` is *sufficient* for the project's **Hessian/Poincaré** route, not *necessary* for existence. Absolute-value **UV stability** is a weaker-sufficient route — Balaban's (§2).

## 2 · What the SOTA assembles for 4D pure YM, and where each tool stops

- **Balaban** (CMP 109 (1987) 249; 116 (1988) 1; 119 (1988) 243; 122 (1989) 175, 355). Multiscale RG, large-field/small-field split, `ℝ`-operation; *Large-field renormalization I/II* complete **UV stability** of 4D pure lattice gauge theory (`Z` bounded, effective densities controlled). The Savvidy large-field region is controlled by **absolute-value** suppression — enough for existence, one-sided (not the two-sided Hessian `L3`, and no gap).
- **Magnen–Rivasseau–Sénéor** (CMP 155 (1993) 325–383). UV-complete `YM₄` with IR cutoff, `SU(2)`, trivial topology, axial gauge `A₀=0`, Slavnov identities nonperturbatively. Stops at **gauge-fixed**: no gauge-invariant OS/RP, no infinite volume.
- **Chandra–Chevyrev–Hairer–Shen** (Invent. Math. 237 (2024) 541–696). Stochastic quantization of YM–Higgs in **3D**: state space, Markov process, invariant measure. Stops at `d=3` (super-renormalizable), not the 4D marginal log-critical case.
- **Shen–Zhu–Zhu** (CMP 400 (2023) 805–851). Lattice YM any `d>1`, `SU(N)`, `β<1/(16(d-1))`: unique invariant measure, log-Sobolev/Poincaré, exponential clustering — a **uniform positive mass gap**, unconditional. Stops at **strong coupling**; silent on the weak-coupling UV continuum.
- **2026 claim (unverified).** A Lean-checked submission (OpenAI, 28 Oct 2026), assessed 10 Nov 2026 by a Gross-chaired working group (`github.com/yang-mills-group/YM`), reports "a relativistic QFT satisfying the **Wightman axioms** … as a **scaling limit** of four-dimensional Wilson lattice gauge theory for any simple compact gauge group," yet "does **not** prove that the infinite-volume limit is unique or that there is a mass gap." It is **panel opinion, not certification**; the 1,147-page proof is unreleased and not peer-reviewed; Alpöge–Sussman (15 Nov 2026) remain work-in-progress on Balaban's program.

**The precise remaining gap.** "Gribov" is *avoided, not solved*: staying gauge-invariant, the Wilson measure is RP on link variables, so no gauge slice and no Gribov copies enter OS3. The genuine UV content is **large-field (Savvidy) stability**, which Balaban's absolute-value `ℝ`-operation controls; the 2026 submission reports assembling this into a scaling limit, i.e. the *existence* step. What no tool above closes is strictly **IR**: (i) **uniqueness** of the `L→∞` limit (OS4; project IN-6/UNI) and (ii) **`Δ>0` uniform in `L,a`** (`=H1=G2`, the Savvidy *sign*). These are the two true residues — one species at the IR scale.

## 3 · What a complete G1 delivers — and is the gap then well-posed?

A complete G1 is a probability measure `μ` on `𝒮′(ℝ⁴; gauge-invariant observables)` with **OS0/1/2/3/4** (OS2 = full `O(4)` is an `a→0` irrelevance; OS4 = clustering is gap-downstream). The OS theorem then yields a Hilbert space `H`, a self-adjoint contraction `T_t=e^{-tH}`, `H≥0`, and the KL measure `ν_O(B)=⟨AΩ,P_H(B)AΩ⟩` (G1-CONTINUUM-OS) — **the object**. The mass gap becomes a precise spectral statement:
`Δ>0 ⇔ spec(H)∩(0,Δ)=∅ ⇔ supp ν ⊂ {0}∪[Δ,∞)`.
So **yes — G1 makes the gap well-posed**: it is a property of the constructed `(H,T,ν)`.

But the OS axioms do **not** manufacture it. The massless generalized free field satisfies **all** OS axioms (including clustering) with `Δ=0`; and even `Δ>0` need not be an atom — a continuous spectrum bottom at `Δ` gives exponential clustering yet `f₁²=ν({m₁²})=0` (mass-spectrum Theorem B). G1 delivers the **frame**, not the **content**: `Δ>0` and the atom `f₁²>0` (`=H1`, Clay) stay strictly beyond reconstruction. Existence (UV, `L3`-type) and the gap (IR, the Savvidy sign) are **separate obligations**; the object makes the second well-posed without supplying it.
