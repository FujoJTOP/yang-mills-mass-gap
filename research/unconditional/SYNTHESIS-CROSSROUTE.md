# SYNTHESIS-CROSSROUTE — does any combination of the partial results close the gap?

> **Verdict (判词). The sum is NOT greater than its parts.** Four cross-pairs tested; none closes. The "positive + variance" sum (a) is *degenerate*: by one-loop equipartition (`A1`; W7 measures `E[H]=2.46`, `Var=2.44`, `E[H]−Var=+0.02`) the two "complementary" pieces are **the same number to ≈1%**, so they cancel and the sum collapses to the Haar term `W` — the Stein/score variance bound is both unusable (it routes through `Λ_UV=e^{−2β_jN_p}→0`) and unnecessary. The strip (b): `I2` and the isolation lemma are *literally the same datum* (Theorem A). The strong-coupling combo (c) yields one genuine **small theorem** — a-uniform marginal log-concavity at `β<1/48`, because there `Λ_UV=e^{−C_B}/C_G=O(1)` — but it produces the *gap* (already SZZ), never the *residue*. Every pair reduces to the same one or two unprotected inequalities. Corpus tension flagged: L3 banks `E[H]≥free gap` as a positive term; PROVE-Gpp §3 and A1 deny it.

## (a) Log-concavity sum — degenerate, not complementary
Target `G″_j=E[H_vv]−Var(G_v)+W≥δ` (L3 (★)). Ingredients: **(i)** identity (PROVE-Gpp); **(ii)** Lemma B `W≥δ_W>0` on `B_{π/2}`, scale-uniform; **(iii)** free gap `c_L=4π²/L²` a-uniform at fixed `L` (P1-L0); **(iv)** Stein/score `|Cov(F,s_B)|²≤(Var s_B/Λ_UV)E|∇_⊥F|²`, `m=min(λ/2,Λ_UV/(1+2S/λ))` (REPAIR-2SCALE, VERIFY-FINAL(2)); **(v)** `Λ_UV=e^{−2β_jN_p}/C_G` (PROVE-H2). **Test:** (iv) does *not* bound `Var(G_v)` below `E[H]`; and it cannot, because (iii)+(W7) give `E[H]−Var=0` on the cone-free typical region, the cone adding only `−O(e^{−c/g_j²})` (A1). So `G″=W+O(e^{−c/g²})`: the positive `E[H]` and the variance are the **same quantity**, mutually cancelling — the sum is `W`, not `E[H]+(small Var)`. (iv) further dies two ways: `m` is `a`-uniform only if `Λ_UV` is, and `Λ_UV→0` doubly-exponentially; literally `m∼λΛ_UV/(2N_{E'}S)`, needing `(★dec)` for volume-uniformity. **Does not close.** Single missing inequality, *not* a variance bound: the large-field tail/concentration `μ_j({|x|≥π/2})≤e^{−c/g_j²}` uniformly in `j` (equivalently the non-Gaussian residual `|E[H]−Var|<δ_W`, equivalently `Var_μ(a)≤1/δ`, the induced-mass form) — the same large-field negative mode GAP-G1 names, localized to a tail bound.

## (b) EDGE-E2 `I2` + F1SQ isolation — the same object
EDGE-E2's exact missing estimate *is* the two-mass asymptotic `C(t)=Ae^{−m₁t}(1+η)`, `|η|≤Be^{−δt}`, `A≥a₀>0`; F1SQ's `L1` ("glueball below 2-glueball threshold, leading single-polymer term positive, rest `e^{−δt}`") is verbatim the same. EDGE-E2 supplies the *equivalence* machinery (Theorem A: `I1∧I2⟺` sharp decay; residue–level-gap lemma `Λ⟺f₁²`); F1SQ supplies the only *mechanism* (GJS/polymer). They are complementary in *type*, not content: `I2` alone gives no atom without `I1` (the *visibility/overlap* `⟨Ω|tr F²|G⟩≠0`, O3), which F1SQ bundles. **Does not close:** `L1` is unproven, lives at `β<1/48` fixed `a`, and does not transport (`f₁²` is usc; analyticity wall). Single missing inequality: uniform-in-`(a,V)` `Λ_β−M₁/M₀≥κ>0` with `a₀≥κ` — i.e. `H*(r₀)` itself.

## (c) Strong-coupling combo (isolation + H2 + `Z_a`) — a real theorem, the wrong half
At `β<1/48`, `C_B=2βN_p(B)` is `O(10⁻¹)`, so `Λ_UV=e^{−C_B}/C_G=O(1)`, `a`-uniform — L3's killer evaporates. With `Var≤β²N_□/Λ_UV=O(β²)` and `W≥δ_W=O(g)` (large at strong coupling), `G″≥δ` holds `a`-uniformly. **This is a genuine small theorem** — strong-coupling marginal log-concavity — but it is the *gap* (SZZ already supplies it), **not** `f₁²>0`: the gapped massive free scalar (`:φ²:`, `f₁²=0`) is the counterexample. **Does not close.** Missing: `L1` isolation/visibility, which H2 (variance) and `Z_a` (generator norm) are structurally unable to supply.

## (d) Other pairs — all rename the same wall
`Y3` `W1₀`+H2: globalized H2 gives *at most* a neutral gap ⇒ `W1₀`, but globalization fails (`C≈15`), and even granted it is strictly below the gap (Y3). `ESI` NEO+trace-anomaly: the anomaly is UV/total and does not forbid an emergent IR scalar; NEO `⟺¬(iii)⟺` no interacting IR CFT — irreducible, not supplied by `T^μ_μ=(β/2g)tr F²`. IN11 membership+H2: the `0⁺⁺/2⁺⁺` leading degeneracy (`a_c=1`) is a subleading `O(1)` splitting no symmetry fixes; membership never orders the pair.

**Sharpest line.** The routes do not sum because they are not independent: equipartition makes the "positive mean" and "variance" one object (their sum is `W`), isolation and `I2` are one theorem, and the strong-coupling trio reaches the gap not the atom. No combination beats the single route; each collapses onto one unprotected inequality — `Var_μ(a)≤1/δ` (a-fixing, ≙H1) for L3, and `Λ_β−M₁/M₀≥κ` (≙`H*`) for the residue. *Sum = max of parts, not their sum.*
