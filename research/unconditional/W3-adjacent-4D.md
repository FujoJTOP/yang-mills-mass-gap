# W3 — Adjacent 4D gauge theories: does any rigor transfer?

**Verdict: No.** Every rigorously constructed gauge QFT lives in d≤3 (super-renormalizable or solvable). No 4D gauge theory — Abelian, non-Abelian, NC, or SUSY — has an OS/Hilbert-space construction with a proven gap. Each "success" survives only because of a regulator or extra scale that pure 4D YM lacks.

- **4D Abelian Higgs.** Continuum built only in 2D (Brydges–Fröhlich–Seiler, CMP 79 (1981) 353) and 3D (King, CMP 102 (1986) 649; CMP 103 (1986) 323; foundation Bałaban–Imbrie–Jaffe CMP 97 (1985) 299). Mass gap proven only on a *unit* lattice (Bałaban–Brydges–Imbrie–Jaffe, Ann. Phys. 158 (1984) 281). 4D continuum: open. **Fails at v→0:** the gap *is* the Higgs mechanism — v fixes gauge mass m=gv and an IR scale; pure YM has no Higgs and must *dynamically* transmute. Decoupling (m_H→∞) returns pure YM at the marginal wall. 3D works only because the coupling is super-renormalizable.

- **Noncommutative YM.** Scalar NC φ⁴₄ is renormalizable and non-perturbatively solvable (Grosse–Wulkenhaar hep-th/0401128; all-orders hep-th/0403232; solved arXiv:1205.0465) — but **reflection positivity fails** (arXiv:1406.7755). NC *gauge* is only shown renormalizable (harmonically-extended action; review hep-th/0409312), never constructed. **Fails at θ→0:** the harmonic term / θ>0 supplies the IR cutoff and gauge mass that make the model tractable — it vanishes in the commutative target.

- **Lattice → 4D continuum.** Balaban's RG (CMP 109–122, 1987–89) gives UV stability only — no Schwinger functions, infinite volume, OS, or gap. Strong-coupling gap is finite-β (Osterwalder–Seiler; arXiv:2204.12737). **Fails at β→∞:** reaching the continuum is an essential singularity = the target.

- **SUSY.** Constructive SUSY exists only in 2D (Jaffe–Lesniewski–Weitsman, CMP 114 (1988) 147). N=2 Seiberg–Witten (hep-th/9407087, hep-th/9408099) is exact *IR/moduli* data, not an OS/Hilbert construction; 4D SUSY gauge has none. **Fails:** breaking SUSY discards the non-renormalization theorems the exact results rest on.

- **Proven 4D "gauge" with OS+gap?** Only Gaussian: free Proca massive vector (OS ✓, gap m>0) — but gauge symmetry is *explicitly broken*; massless Maxwell is gauge-invariant yet gapless. No interacting example.

**Common wall.** All rigor sits where the coupling is super-renormalizable (d≤3) or an external scale (v, θ, lattice, supercharge) supplies the mass/cutoff. 4D pure YM is marginal, scale-free, and must generate its own gap — no adjacent technique does that. (Consistent with T3: the missing piece is a gauge-covariant non-perturbative RG at a marginal fixed point.)
