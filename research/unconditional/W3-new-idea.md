# W3 — New idea: a Wasserstein–Otto contraction of the Yang–Mills measure

**The structure.** Equip P(𝒜/𝒢), the probability measures on the gauge-orbit space, with the L²-Wasserstein metric W₂ induced by the natural Riemannian metric on connections. Treat one Wilsonian RG step ℛ_τ (finite block-spin/coarse-graining) as a map of measures. Claim to prove: ℛ_τ is a strict W₂-contraction with factor e^{−mτ}, m>0 independent of cutoff and volume. Then uniqueness of the invariant measure = continuum vacuum (G1), and exponential decay of gauge-invariant correlations at rate m = mass gap (G2). Such a contraction is equivalent to a Bakry–Émery curvature-dimension bound CD(m,∞): geodesic displacement-convexity of the YM action on orbit space. This is metric-measure (Otto/JKO) geometry, not an expansion, SDP, or signed weight.

**(a) Not ruled out.** The ledger's wall is two *expansion* obstructions: the real-β Pringsheim radius 1/48 (W2-1) and the Savvidy negative-mode breakdown of the *perturbative effective action*. Both forbid Taylor/positivity *series*; neither forbids a global inequality on measures. G2 explicitly demands "a new RG crossing Savvidy" and marks it open, not impossible — a series-free CD(m,∞) bound is precisely such an object, so no ledger entry contradicts it.

**(b) What it needs.** (i) A gauge-invariant W₂ on orbit-space measures (well-defined pushforward of the quotient metric — the technical crux after gauge-fixing); (ii) displacement convexity of the YM action along W₂-geodesics with m>0; (iii) uniformity in cutoff *and* volume (m must not degenerate in either limit); (iv) the quantitative chain W₂-contraction ⇒ Poincaré ⇒ exponential clustering of Wilson loops.

**(c) Most likely failure.** Displacement convexity fails exactly along the Savvidy directions: the negative-Hessian modes are where the action is concave along W₂-geodesics, i.e. negative "Ricci curvature." In d=4 marginality then forces effective curvature m(μ) ≲ 1/|log μ| → 0 toward the IR, so even a scale-dependent bound degenerates to "no gap." The idea dies unless Savvidy concavity is neutralized by a Harris/Lyapunov-minorization substitute for strict CD.
