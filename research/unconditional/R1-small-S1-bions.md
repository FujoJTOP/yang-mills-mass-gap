# R1 — Small-S¹ semiclassical confinement: rigorization status

**Verdict.** No nonabelian small-L confinement is rigorous. The only rigorous "monopole gas ⇒ area law" is abelian (Göpfert–Mack, 3D U(1) lattice, all couplings). The SU(N) bion programme (Ünsal 0709.3269) is controlled semiclassics — a small parameter, not a proof.

**Rigorous vs heuristic.**
- Center symmetry, the Polyakov and 't Hooft loop algebras, and the monopole-instanton / twisted-sector classification are rigorous operator/lattice statements.
- Dilute monopole/bion gas ⇒ dual-photon mass, area law, gap is heuristic. Diluteness is `e^{−S₀}`, `S₀=8π²/(g²N)≫1` at small L; determinants and moduli are *computed*, not bounded. The bion is a marginal `1/r` monopole–antimonopole "molecule" — a threshold object, not a cleanly separated particle.
- Center stabilization by double-trace deformation (Shifman–Ünsal 0802.1232; Ünsal–Yaffe 0803.0344) or 't Hooft flux is center-symmetric *by construction*, but that it dynamically fixes the vacuum, and is "irrelevant at large L" (adiabatic continuity), is conjectural — lattice even finds four phases for QCD(adj) (Cossu–D'Elia 0904.1353). The programme's own 2025 statement calls the YM part a conjecture (Nguyen–Ünsal 2509.09625; review Poppitz 2111.10423).

**Can the dilute gas be made rigorous?** Ingredients exist — Göpfert–Mack's iterated-Mayer expansion (RG for Coulomb gases, CMP 81 (1981) 97) is exactly the monopole-plasma tool; Balaban (CMP 109 (1987) 249) is the UV template — but never assembled for nonabelian YM. Obstructions: (i) no rigorous continuum small-L SU(N) measure; (ii) no controlled expansion for the marginal bion; (iii) abelianization to a 3D dual photon is an unproven all-orders reduction.

**Precise unproven step.** Construct the reflection-positive continuum SU(N) measure on ℝ³×S¹ at small L with a center-stabilizing deformation / 't Hooft flux, and prove a convergent cluster expansion over the interacting monopole–bion gas giving area law and exponential clustering.

**Pieces already available:** Göpfert–Mack (abelian, fully rigorous); Balaban (UV machine); Lüscher T³ small-volume expansion (NPB 219 (1983) 233; Koller–van Baal — *formal*, not rigorous); Adhikari–Cao 2202.10375 (rigorous weak-coupling lattice mass gap, finite groups, perturbative regime — *not* the confining gap). Strong-coupling lattice YM (Osterwalder–Seiler, Ann. Phys. 110 (1978) 440) is rigorous but not semiclassical.
