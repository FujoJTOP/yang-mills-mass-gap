# MINISUPERSPACE — the Wheeler–DeWitt / Hamiltonian-constraint reduction is a mis-transplanted constraint on the wrong sector: an upper bound where a lower is needed

> **判词 (verdict).** "Reduce YM to a minisuperspace (constant/coarse mode), solve the quantum Hamiltonian constraint, read the gap off the spectrum" is **not a new species**: it restates `k0-CONSTRAINT-EFFPOT` (`V_eff″=G″`) + `k0-CONSTANT-MODE-TRANSFER` (constant mode no sub-operator; tree curvature zero on a flat valley) + `PROOF-HAMILTONIAN-VARIATIONAL` (spectrum bounds from above) in quantum-cosmology vocabulary. Three facts: **(i) mis-transplanted** — `ĤΨ=0` is GR's time-reparametrization constraint, absent in YM, whose constraint is Gauss's law; **(ii) wrong sector** — minisuperspace on the correct constraint truncates to the holonomy/centre modulus, orthogonal to the flux carrying the gap; **(iii) drops the sign-setter** — even the exact one-mode spectrum gives `gap_red=S ≥ S−D=gap_true`, the **upper** bound where a **lower** is required. Passes #1(β=0)/#4 cleanly; fails #2, #3, #5.

## (1) Mis-transplanted constraint
`Ĥ⊥Ψ=0` is GR's Hamiltonian constraint, generated by time diffeomorphisms (reparametrization makes `t` a gauge direction). Pure YM has a genuine background time; its evolution is Schrödinger, `i∂_tΨ=ĤΨ`, **not** `ĤΨ=0` (Jackiw 1980; Kogut–Susskind 1975) — imposing the latter declares the theory static with zero energy. The actual constraint is Gauss's law `D_iE^i=0`; its kernel is the physical Hilbert space, and the gap is the lightest gauge-invariant excitation. "Solve the quantum Hamiltonian constraint" has no YM referent as stated.

## (2) Minisuperspace = holonomy sector, orthogonal to the gap
Take minisuperspace = constant mode `a₀`. It is the holonomy/centre modulus (PROOF-GAUSS-INVARIANT-PF); the glueball gap lives in the transverse *flux* sector, **orthogonal** to it (k0-CONSTANT-MODE-TRANSFER (5)(ii)). The reduction is also not exact: the plaquette is a momentum convolution, `[T,P₀]≠0` at cubic order, so `P₀TP₀` is no sub-operator — solving on `a₀` means eliminating `Θ(N_□)` modes, i.e. the full theory. The Savvidy-indefinite fiber `H_ff=−D²+2adF_B` sits **on** the `k→0` slice; minisuperspace deletes exactly the sign-setter.

## (3) The spectrum is `G″` respelled — or zero
`Ĥ_red=−½Δ_{a₀}+V_eff(a₀)`; its low spectrum is pinned by `V_eff″(0)=G″(0)=m_eff²(k→0)` (k0-CONSTRAINT-EFFPOT). Tree level: `V₀=(V/4g²)Σtr[a_μ,a_ν]²` is quartic, exactly zero on the `4(N−1)`-dim flat Cartan valley, so `V₀″(0)=0` — flat valleys are not harmonically gapped; only the compact Haar term `≥N/12` survives. Eliminating the fiber (Schur/BO) makes `V_eff″=G″` *exactly* the target, one-loop-indefinite (Schur undefined). The one genuinely-WDW ingredient — the superspace/DeWitt measure curvature (operator-ordering `∼R`) — *is* the compact Haar term `Φ″≥N/12`, i.e. the already-banked `W_geo`; it cannot beat the Savvidy `Δ_dyn`. Any peak `p≤e^{−ψ}` is the variance bound itself (F1 §2); WKB `Ψ=e^{−W}` reproduces `S`. No independent spectral datum appears.

## (4) Direction — the exact answer is an upper bound
The one exactly solvable minisuperspace is the Gaussian `D=0` slice (k0-EXACT-ONEMODE): it computes `S`, so `Var_red=1/S`, while `Var_true=1/(S−D)`, `D≥0` the fiber Brascamp–Lieb defect. Hence
```
Var_red = 1/S  ≤  1/(S−D) = Var_true   ⇒   gap_red = S  ≥  S−D = gap_true ,
```
the **upper** bound the five tests forbid; deficit `=D=H1`. Hamiltonian-variational agrees: Rayleigh–Ritz is from above; Temple/Weinstein need the second level (second-level regress, PROOF-HAMILTONIAN-VARIATIONAL).

**Sharpest line.** The Wheeler–DeWitt minisuperspace is the constraint-effective-potential cluster in gravitational clothing: its "quantum Hamiltonian constraint" is GR's time-reparametrization constraint, which YM lacks (it has time and a Gauss constraint); where the constraint *does* refer, minisuperspace truncates to the holonomy/centre modulus provably orthogonal to the flux carrying the gap; and even the exact minisuperspace spectrum is `G″` respelled (circle) or `V₀″=0` (flat valley), bounding the gap from **above**. It passes the acid test only because it *is* `G″` — the same clean pass as k0-CONSTRAINT-EFFPOT, buying nothing. **Two no-gos to bank: (a) WDW constraint mis-transplanted; (b) minisuperspace = holonomy sector ⊥ the flux. Fails #2, #3, #5 — a rename, not a new species.**
