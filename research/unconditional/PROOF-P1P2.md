# PROOF-P1P2 — the connected-`tr F²` expansion and the strong-coupling residue grading close (I1); the isolation I2 does NOT

> 2026-11-23 · Attempted proof of **P1** (convergent connected-`tr F²` cluster expansion) and **P2** (grading) to discharge the condition in PROOF-P3. **Verdict: P1 closes outright; the cost grading closes the positive overlap (I1) and the `O(u)` remainder — but does NOT close the isolation (I2).** `tr F²` is a finite sum of local plaquette observables, so its connected correlator is an absolutely convergent sum of connected polymers (Osterwalder–Seiler / Kotecký–Preiss) below an explicit activity radius; the integer *cost* grading makes the minimal single flux tube the unique leading polymer, the leading amplitude positive. **Composition gives the unconditional residue theorem `f₁²≥c₁(β)u^p(1−O(u))>0`, volume- and `L`-uniform, `β<β₁`, fixed `a` — I1 only.** I2 is **not** obtained: the integer cost grading is a statement about polymer *amplitudes*, not about transfer-matrix eigenvalue spacing, and cannot be converted into a uniform isolation gap (correction, §3; cf. PROOF-P3-UNCONDITIONAL §5, REDTEAM-FORGE). The one non-printed ingredient is a graded-strip KP lemma, standard in form: assembly, not new analysis, and no transport across the analyticity wall. Strong-coupling *binding* manufactures the residue; the free-scalar `f₁²=0` is the unbound case and is exactly excluded.

**1. The theorem (unconditional).** Fix `a>0`. There are `β₁>0` (the KP radius, `O(1/N)`, same order as SZZ's `1/48`) and `c₁(β)>0` such that for every `β<β₁` and every `T⁴_L`: `f₁²_L=lim_{t→∞}e^{m₁t}C_L(t) ≥ c₁(β)u^p` (`p≥1` an explicit finite integer, `c₁,r₀` `V`-independent). This is the **residue (I1)** statement: the `0⁺⁺` channel has a single-particle atom of weight `≥r₀(β)>0` at all `L`. The **isolation (I2)** — `inf supp(ν−f₁²δ_{m₁²})−m₁² ≥ δ>0` — is **not** obtained here (see §3); hence the theorem is a residue theorem, not a gap theorem.

**2. P1 — the connected-composite expansion.** Write `O=Σ_p φ_p`, the finite A1g sum of one cell's plaquettes; `C(t)=Σ_{p∈0,q∈t}Σ^{conn}_{P∋p,q}w(P)` with each `c(p,q)=Σ_{P conn}w(P)` the Ursell sum of bare character weights (positive for `β>0`). Absolute convergence is the KP condition `Σ_{P∋x}|w(P)|e^{a|P|}≤a`, i.e. `u(β)·K<1`, `K` the lattice-animal growth × `SU(N)` link multiplicity (`K≤CN`). KP is **uniform in volume**, hence uniform in strip length, so the bound on `C(t)` holds for **all `t`** — exactly what the `t→∞` KL extraction requires. P1 closes.

**3. P2 — the grading gives I1, NOT I2 (correction).** Grade polymers by plaquette cost. The leading tube is the *unique* minimal polymer through the origin; its amplitude `A₁=⟨Ω|O|G⟩` is a product of positive fundamental character coefficients along one connected chain (`⟨O⟩²` is lower order for `t/a>2/p`), so `f₁²=|A₁|²≥c₁u^p` — **I1**, `L`-uniform (local). **The grading does *not* give I2.** The naive order-claim `λ₁=c₁u^p+O(u^{p+1})`, `λ₂=c₂u^{p'}` (`p'≥p+1`) treats an *asymptotic* strong-coupling series as if it had a uniform remainder; but the `O(u^{p+1})` correction to `λ₁` is the *same order* as `λ₂` when `p'=p+1`, so `λ₂` is never resolved — the 'two-gap' statement `δ=(p'−p)ln(1/u)/a` is the one-gap (`m₁`) statement renamed. The bridge between polymer amplitudes and transfer-matrix eigenvalues **is** the second-level gap, so the cost grading cannot supply it (PROOF-EDGE-E2 bottom-functional blindness; PROOF-ISOLATION §4). Placement: the physical `0⁺⁺*` sits at `≈1.5m₁`, *sub-leading*, while the leading integer cost (a boxy `2×1` tube) sits at `2m₁`, on/above the continuum — no isolation gap. I2 remains exactly the open step.

**4. The remainder.** Decorating the minimal tube shifts its mass (self-energy) and adds one activity — a relative `O(u)`, **no rate change**; excited/wider tubes and two-tube states are a whole factor `u` higher in amplitude. KP gives `|R_L(t)|≤C·u·|w₁(t)|` uniformly in `V,t`, so `e^{m₁t}R_L=O(u)` and `f₁²=c₁u^p(1−O(u))≥r₀(β)`. (The `O(u)` is a `t`-*uniform* multiplicative bound, satisfied by any `λ₂/λ₁∈[1−Cu,1)` — hence it certifies the residue I1, not the isolation I2.)

**5. Assembly vs open.** Imports are standard: OS/Seiler/KP (expansion), SZZ (uniform gap, used only for the analytic-perturbation identification of the atom weight with the resummed tube). The single step *not literally printed* is the graded-strip KP lemma — "the minimal-cost tube is the unique leading connected polymer, with coefficient `a₀(β)>0` and entropy absorbed in `K`"; criterion `uK<1`, explicit. **Not obtained:** the isolation `I2` (§3), continuum `a→0`, and transport to `β≈2.5` — the analyticity wall is untouched.

**Sharpest line.** P1 closes and P2 gives I1 because at strong coupling *confinement is binding*: the minimal connected `tr F²` correlator is one flux tube = one particle, so `f₁²=|⟨Ω|O|G⟩|²` is a positive sum of squares of a nonvanishing, computable overlap. SU(N) Weingarten sign-alternation lives in the area-law surface sum, not in the per-tube character expansion (positive for `β>0`). The gapped free scalar has `f₁²=0` precisely because it binds no tube; strong coupling does, and this manufactures the positive residue. What the cost grading does *not* buy is the isolation `I2` (the grading is about amplitudes, not eigenvalue spacing) or the continuum. This is a genuine, volume-uniform, strong-coupling **residue** theorem (I1).
