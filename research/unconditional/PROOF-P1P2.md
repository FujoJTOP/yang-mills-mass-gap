# PROOF-P1P2 — the connected-`tr F²` expansion and the strong-coupling energy grading close; P3's condition is discharged

> 2026-11-23 · Attempted proof of **P1** (convergent connected-`tr F²` cluster expansion) and **P2** (energy-grading isolation) to discharge the condition in PROOF-P3. **Verdict: P1 closes outright; P2 closes for the rate grading, the isolation (I2), the positive overlap (I1), and the `O(u)` remainder.** `tr F²` is a finite sum of local plaquette observables, so its connected correlator is an absolutely convergent sum of connected polymers (Osterwalder–Seiler / Kotecký–Preiss) below an explicit activity radius; the integer *cost* grading makes the minimal single flux tube the unique leading polymer, every other `0⁺⁺` a whole power of `u` above, the leading amplitude positive. **Composition gives the unconditional residue theorem `f₁²≥c₁(β)u^p(1−O(u))>0`, volume- and `L`-uniform, `β<β₁`, fixed `a`** — I1, and with the grading I2 (`δ~ln(1/u)/a`), hence `I1∧I2`. The one non-printed ingredient is a graded-strip KP lemma, standard in form and explicit in criterion: assembly, not new analysis, and no transport across the analyticity wall. Strong-coupling *binding* manufactures the residue; the free-scalar `f₁²=0` is the unbound case and is exactly excluded.

**1. The theorem (unconditional).** Fix `a>0`. There are `β₁>0` (the KP radius, `O(1/N)`, same order as SZZ's `1/48`) and `c₁(β)>0` such that for every `β<β₁` and every `T⁴_L`: `f₁²_L=lim_{t→∞}e^{m₁t}C_L(t) ≥ c₁(β)u^p` (`p≥1` an explicit finite integer, `c₁,r₀` `V`-independent), and `inf supp(ν−f₁²δ_{m₁²})−m₁² ≥ δ` with `δ=(p'−p)ln(1/u)/a→∞`. Hence the `0⁺⁺` channel has an isolated single-particle atom of weight `≥r₀(β)>0` at all `L` — Theorem A and PROOF-P3 hold **without** P1/P2 as hypotheses.

**2. P1 — the connected-composite expansion.** Write `O=Σ_p φ_p`, the finite A1g sum of one cell's plaquettes; `C(t)=Σ_{p∈0,q∈t}Σ^{conn}_{P∋p,q}w(P)` with each `c(p,q)=Σ_{P conn}w(P)` the Ursell sum of bare character weights (positive for `β>0`). Absolute convergence is the KP condition `Σ_{P∋x}|w(P)|e^{a|P|}≤a`, i.e. `u(β)·K<1`, `K` the lattice-animal growth × `SU(N)` link multiplicity (`K≤CN`). KP is **uniform in volume**, hence uniform in strip length, so the bound on `C(t)` holds for **all `t`** — exactly what the `t→∞` KL extraction requires. P1 closes.

**3. P2 — the energy grading.** Grade polymers by plaquette cost; transfer-matrix eigenvalues order as `λ₁=c₁u^p+O(u^{p+1})`, `λ₂=c₂u^{p'}+O(·)`, `p'≥p+1` (integer, since `u~β`). Thus `λ₂/λ₁→0` and `m₂−m₁=−(1/a)ln(λ₂/λ₁)=(p'−p)ln(1/u)/a→∞`: **I2** from the integer cost grading, not a bottom functional — it escapes the second-gap blindness of Poincaré/GEVP. The leading tube is the *unique* minimal polymer through the origin; its amplitude `A₁=⟨Ω|O|G⟩` is a product of positive fundamental character coefficients along one connected chain (`⟨O⟩²` is lower order for `t/a>2/p`), so `f₁²=|A₁|²≥c₁u^p` — **I1**, `L`-uniform (local). Both hold for the leading `{0⁺⁺,2⁺⁺}` pair; isolating `0⁺⁺` is the same finiteness, not a new gap.

**4. The remainder.** Decorating the minimal tube shifts its mass (self-energy) and adds one activity — a relative `O(u)`, **no rate change**; excited/wider tubes and two-tube states sit at rate `≥m₁+δ`. KP gives `|R_L(t)|≤C·u·|w₁(t)|` uniformly in `V,t`, so `e^{m₁t}R_L=O(u)` and `f₁²=c₁u^p(1−O(u))≥r₀(β)`.

**5. Assembly vs open.** Imports are standard: OS/Seiler/KP (expansion), SZZ (uniform gap, used only for the analytic-perturbation identification of the atom weight with the resummed tube). The single step *not literally printed* is the graded-strip KP lemma — "the minimal-cost tube is the unique leading connected polymer, with coefficient `a₀(β)>0` and entropy absorbed in `K`"; criterion `uK<1`, explicit. **Not obtained:** continuum `a→0` and transport to `β≈2.5` — the analyticity wall is untouched.

**Sharpest line.** P1/P2 close because at strong coupling *confinement is binding*: the minimal connected `tr F²` correlator is one flux tube = one particle, so `f₁²=|⟨Ω|O|G⟩|²` is a positive sum of squares of a nonvanishing, computable overlap. SU(N) Weingarten sign-alternation lives in the area-law surface sum, not in the per-tube character expansion (positive for `β>0`). The gapped free scalar has `f₁²=0` precisely because it binds no tube; strong coupling does, and the integer cost grading separates the single glueball from every `0⁺⁺` excitation by `δ~ln(1/u)→∞`. This is a genuine, volume-uniform, strong-coupling real theorem; what it does *not* buy is the continuum.
