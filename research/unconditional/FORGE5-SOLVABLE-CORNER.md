# FORGE5-SOLVABLE-CORNER — solve `G″` exactly in a solvable corner, then transfer the sign to 4D by an exact inequality?

> 2026-11-23 · FORGE5 direction: compute the gap/Hessian sign **exactly** in a mean-field-controlled corner (large spatial `d→∞`, strongly anisotropic reduction, or the 2D cross-section) where the sign is provable, then move it to the physical 4D point by an **exact monotonicity/comparison** inequality. **Verdict: 2 of 5 — (1) and (4) pass; (2), (3), (5) fail and are ONE obstruction.** The only exact transfer that is genuinely a theorem — min–max on an invariant subspace — **runs the wrong way**: it UPPER-bounds the 4D gap, never lower-bounds it. Every lower-bounding comparison either needs FKG (false for SU(N)) or *is* H1 = the gap.

## The forged attempt (strongest form)
**(A) Large `d→∞` mean-field.** At leading order the lattice measure is the saddle (Gaussian) measure; the transverse curvature `G″_∞` is explicit and provably `>0`, and the perfect-Gaussian free massless field is handled *exactly* (`G″=0`, acid-clean). Transfer attempt: if `m(d)` were monotone, `m(4) ≥ m(∞) > 0`. **(B) Strong coupling** `β<1/48`: the *only rigorous* gapped corner (Osterwalder–Seiler + P3 `f₁²≥r₀`); transfer `β₀→2.5`. **(C) Anisotropic** small circle `S¹`: reduces 4D→3D; **(D) 2D** Migdal: exact. Below, why none transfers.

## Why every exact-transfer candidate fails
- **Min–max restriction — WRONG DIRECTION (rigorous).** For self-adjoint `T` and an invariant subspace `P H`, `spec(T|_P)⊂spec(T)` ⇒ `gap(T|_P) ≥ gap(T)`. Restricting 4D to frozen extra directions (a solvable subsector) therefore **upper-bounds** `gap(4D)`, useless for a lower bound; to lower-bound 4D we must embed it in a *larger* theory (5D, 6D …), which is strictly harder and gapless-uncertain. The "restricted = 2D" picture also fails (freezing `A_{μ⊥}` yields 2D YM + adjoint scalars, not gapless 2D YM).
- **`d`-monotonicity — ill-posed + unproven.** The gap is dimensionful; comparing `m(d)` requires fixing units, and the only canonical fixing is through the `d`-dependent `β`-function (`Λ_QCD(d)`) = circular. Even granting a normalization, no min–max monotonicity of the gap under *adding spatial directions* exists; for the exactly solvable Gaussian corner `m(d)=m` for **all** `d` (no `d`-leverage at all), so all leverage would have to come from the interaction's `1/d` corrections.
- **`1/d` asymptotic — uncontrolled at `d=4`.** `m(d)=m_∞+c₁/d+…` is an asymptotic series with uncontrolled remainder at `d=4` and unknown sign of `c₁` — the exact transplant of PROOF-LARGE-N-RIGOROUS (`m_∞+c/N²`, no bound at `N=3`). Also, no rigorous gapped `d→∞` lattice-gauge limit at physical coupling exists (rigorous mean-field is for spin systems / strong coupling).
- **Strong→weak coupling — no FKG.** `∂_β` of any expectation is a sign-indefinite covariance; SU(N) has no lattice FKG (PROOF-COMPARISON-FKG), so the gap is not `β`-monotone (PROOF-TRANSPORT-MONOTONE).
- **Comparison form — circular.** Any form gapped on all of `L²(μ)` whose kernel includes the coarse scale *is* H1 (`Var_ν φ≤λ⁻¹∫|∇φ|²` ⇔ `m_eff²(0)≥λ`); the rigorously gapped references (fiber H2, coarse H1) have the wrong scale in their kernel (PROOF-DIRICHLET-COMPARISON, PROOF-IR-H1).
- **Anisotropic / 2D — wrong corner.** 3D itself has no proven weak-coupling measure/gap (CONTINUUM-3D-TO-4D) and the small-circle transfer lands on KK scale `g²/R→∞`, not the exponentially small 4D gap; 2D is exactly solved but **spectrally gapless** (Migdal), so `G″=0` — a zero cannot transfer a positive sign.

## Five-point verdict
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS.** A rigorous nonperturbative gapped corner exists (strong coupling, `β<1/48`); mean-field/2D are nonperturbative methods.
2. **Controls SIGN (lower bound on cone variance/Hessian) — FAIL.** The exact transfer (min–max restriction) upper-bounds; the mean-field defect Gaussianizes, `D_∞ ≤ D_4D` (wrong sign); no lower bound on `G″` reaches 4D.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL.** Each transfer is (i) wrong-direction, (ii) ill-posed/asymptotic-uncontrolled, or (iii) FKG-dependent/circular = H1.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS (with caveat).** Genuine via large-`d` mean-field (exact for the Gaussian free field ⇒ `G″=0`); under the strong-coupling corner it is *vacuous* (free field lives at `β→∞`).
5. **Uniform lower bound `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL.** No valid sign-preserving transfer ⇒ no uniform `λ`; equivalently = H1 = the gap.

## Exact failure — the missing inequality
A functional `Ψ` on the corner that is (a) `≥ r₀>0` there (needs SCHUR-**CONVEX**, atom-coercive), **and** (b) related to 4D by an exact inequality `G″_{4D} ≥ c·Ψ`, `c>0`, **pointing downward**. No such `c` exists: the one exact downward transfer (min–max on invariant subspaces, reflection-positive restriction) yields the *opposite* inequality `G″_{4D} ≥ G″_{larger}` requiring a larger gapped theory; the atom-coercive functionals (residue, `Δ`) do not transport (`Δ` not monotone; PROOF-TRANSPORT-MONOTONE), and the monotone ones (free energy, `a`-charge) are Schur-**concave** totals that upper-bound the edge and are free-field-blind.

## Sharpest line
The program needs a *downward* exact comparison. The unique downward theorem — restriction to an invariant subspace raises the gap — pushes the wrong way (it bounds 4D from above); obtaining a lower bound requires embedding 4D in a *harder* larger theory. Every other downward route is either the asymptotic `1/d`/`1/N` remainder (no bound at `d=4`,`N=3`), the absent FKG, or literally H1. **The solvable corner proves its own sign; the exact transfer that would carry that sign to 4D does not exist — #2, #3, #5 are the single missing inequality, and it is the gap.**
