# G1-L3-UV-FORCE-BOUND — the one-sided UV force-variance bound: four routes, the exact failure, the verdict

> **判词 (verdict).** **L3 does not close.** But the blockage is **not** the brute "`Var≥0` subtracts, and no positivity supplies an upper bound." In the deep UV the physical inequality (★) is **true** — the cone empties (`√(2g_j|F_j|)∼g_j≪Λ_{j+1}`), the shell fiber is log-concave with gap `∼Λ_j²`, and the Haar margin `W≥δ_W` (Lemma B) carries `G″_j≥δ_W−O(g_j²)` — so the *per-shell inequality is bankable*. What is missing is a **theorem**: (i) a gauge-invariant, `a`-uniform field-moment/cone bootstrap `E|F_j|≤Cg_j`; (ii) a bound on the non-Gaussian remainder `Var(G)−E[H]` (the *equipartition defect*), which is a `U'`-boundary/divergence term controlled by `(★dec)`, not a free variance; (iii) the head/crossover clustering at `β≈2.5`. All three are UV, one-derivative, `⊥` gap. The tower "collapse" of the only provable constant (Holley–Stroock `e^{−2β_jN_p}`) is a **bound artifact**, not geometry (`G1-L3-TOWER-POINCARE`). L3 stays open; not a no-go.

**§1 · Why "the sign issue" is the wrong framing.** The exact identity is `G″=E[H]−Var+W`. Coercivity and cone-emptiness give `E[H]⪰0` — a **lower** edge — and `Var≥0` subtracts, so no *sign* tool bounds `G″` below (`PROOF-L3 §2`, `A1`). But for **any** smooth density on flat space, `∂²logZ=0` gives `Var(G_v)=E[H_vv]` **identically**, i.e. `E[H]−Var≡0`. On the compact group the only surviving piece is the **divergence of the collective (gauge-projected) mode's vector field** — the Haar/connection curvature `W` — plus a **boundary term from conditioning on `U'`** (the RG split). Hence `E[H]−Var` is **not an unconstrained variance** but a *defect*: a divergence plus a boundary covariance. The measured `E[H]−Var≈+0.02` vs `W≈0.25` (`W7`) is exactly this bookkeeping. So (★) is equivalent to *"boundary/divergence defect `≤W−δ_W`"* — which is why positivity (wrong edge) fails and why the true target is `W` (proven) plus `(★dec)` (clustering), not a variance concentration.

**§2 · Route A — Balaban small/large-field.** He supplies the *right frame*: multiscale UV stability, `‖R_j‖` small in small-field regions, `E|F_j|≤Cg_j` with large-field weight `e^{−cQ/g_j²}`. Three exact failures: (a) **gauge-fixed** — his log-concavity is in Landau/axial variables; the gauge-invariant `Pᵀ` re-admits the Savvidy negative cone (7–8% survives, `W4`); (b) large-field control is **value** suppression, not the two-sided variance; (c) no `a`-uniform resummation over `J∼log(1/a)` (the `a⁻⁴` renormalon). He stops *at* (★), as cited (`CMP 109/116/122`).

**§3 · Route B — exact UV cone (`g_j→0`, AF).** The strongest route, and the one showing (★) is *physically true*. Deep UV: `|F_j|=O(g_j)`, cone radius `∼g_j≪Λ_{j+1}`, so the shell band sits **outside** the cone and `Hess S_j⪰cΛ_j²` uniformly; with `W≥δ_W` scale-free and the Savvidy tail `O(e^{−c/g_j²})`, `G″_j≥δ_W−ε`. To make it a theorem needs precisely (i) the moment bootstrap — `|F_j|=O(g_j)` is unproven *unless* Balaban's UV stability is made gauge-invariant and `a`-uniform (circular with the flow), and (ii) the `O(g_j²)` remainder `Var−E[H]` (Lemma A / `λ_f`, `(★dec)`); the renormalon makes the bridge `Var≲E[H]` *asymptotic*, not uniform (`CONTINUUM-ASYMPTOTIC-BEYOND`).

**§4 · Route C — lattice-RG flow.** The BBS trap `‖R_{j+1}‖≤e^{−c}‖R_j‖+Cg_j⁴`, with `Σ_jg_j⁴<∞`, closes on the **small-field half**; the large-field/cone half **is** `(★_LF)=(★)` (`PROOF-TRAPPING-REGION §3`). It reduces (★) to *preservation* of `2g_j|F_j|<μ_j²` under one step — the bootstrap again.

**§5 · Route D — Reisz/Symanzik.** They yield the **power counting** (which operators, what degree = L2, proven) and the per-order `a`-expansion — *not* a nonperturbative `a`-uniform moment bound; the all-orders sum is the renormalon.

**§6 · Adjudication.** No route proves the `a`-uniform one-sided bound. Exact missing steps: **(1)** gauge-invariant `a`-uniform field-moment/cone bootstrap (Balaban-caliber, unproven in the right variables); **(2)** the equipartition-defect/`(★dec)` bound on `Var−E[H]` uniformly across the tower; **(3)** head/crossover clustering at `β≈2.5` (Zegarlinski/Dobrushin fail). The tower is *not* geometrically degrading — `c_L=4π²/L²` is `a`-uniform and rising into the UV; `(★)` is the inequality that replaces the collapsing HS constant by this floor.

**Sharpest line.** L3 is the **UV instance of one species**: an upper bound on the force variance — the same species as H1 at `k→0`, one derivative milder, `⊥` gap. The deep-UV inequality is essentially true; what is unbuilt is the gauge-covariant flow that certifies it uniformly and the clustering that closes the head. Not the `Var≥0` wall; the *certification*.
