# PROOF-REGGE-FROISSART — does S-matrix analyticity / Froissart / Regge constrain the lightest glueball mass?

> 2026-11-23 · The high-energy S-matrix program audited against the one open object. **Verdict: no non-circular lever, and the circularity is sharper than stated.** Mandelstam analyticity, the Froissart bound and Regge trajectories are statements about a scattering amplitude, hence about asymptotic one-particle states. In confining pure YM those states are glueballs whose existence as *isolated atoms below threshold* is exactly `I1∧I2` (`f₁²>0` plus isolation `Λ−m₁²>0`) — the open `H*`, strictly **stronger** than the Clay gap `Δ>0`. The formalism presupposes the atom it would prove. Froissart consumes the lightest exchanged mass (its Martin–Lehmann ellipse is sized by it) and `σ_total` is undefined without an S-matrix; the Regge intercept gives only the *upper* bound `α(0)≤1`; a consistent trajectory allows `m₀²=0` (critical Pomeron). `m₀²>0` is an **input**, never an output. No non-circular OS+clustering route exists. Nothing fabricated.

**(1) Analyticity vs `m_{0⁺⁺}`: circular, and stronger than the gap.** An S-matrix needs asymptotic states: LSZ and Haag–Ruelle both require an **isolated one-particle mass shell** — an atom `f₁²>0` separated from the continuum (`Λ−m₁²>0`) — plus a unique clustering vacuum, i.e. `L1=I1∧I2` (PROOF-ISOLATION, Theorem A). The Clay gap `Δ>0` is *weaker*: a continuous `supp ν=[m,∞)` gives `Δ>0` with **no particles, no S-matrix** (mass-spectrum Theorem B/B′: gapped generalized free field, atomless spectrum). So analyticity is downstream not of the gap but of `H*`; using it to bound `m_{0⁺⁺}` *assumes* `H*`. (Pure YM has no asymptotic gluon, so no `(gg)` cut; PROOF-NO-DISSOLUTION-KINEMATIC.)

**(2) Froissart/Regge: assume a gap, not force one.** Martin's rigorous `σ_total≤C ln²(s/s₀)` uses unitarity `|S_l|≤1`, fixed-`t` analyticity and polynomial boundedness — all S-matrix data, the analyticity ellipse sized by the lightest **exchanged** mass (a crossed-channel gap); `σ_total=(1/s)\,Im\,T(s,0)` is undefined without asymptotic states. The arrow is *gap ⇒ S-matrix ⇒ Froissart*; it constrains high-energy growth in a world that already has particles and produces none. Mandelstam analyticity is not a Wightman/OS theorem either (proven only order-by-order, Steinmann) — an extra input even granted the gap. Regge consistency gives the one-sided `α(0)≤1`, satisfied by the massless critical Pomeron `α(0)=1` (Gribov Reggeon calculus) and by the perturbative BFKL Pomeron whose intercept `→1` as `g→0` — never a mass lower bound.

**(3) `m₀²>0`: forced or input? Input.** `m²(t)=m₀²+α't`; `m₀²` is the mass² at `t=0`, an observed dynamical scale. A *physical* trajectory (a tower `J=0,2,4,…` at integer `α(m²)`) already presupposes the poles — the atoms `f₁²>0`, `H*` again. Regge consistency (Froissart–Gribov continuation, residue positivity) is sign/**upper**, fixing nothing below `m₀²`; a critical Pomeron is consistent, so consistency does **not** force `m₀²>0`. Supplying it is exactly dimensional transmutation (GAP-G2-mass, ATOM-FRG-mass). The Chew–Frautschi/Veneziano bootstrap fit trajectories *to* the spectrum — masses read off, never derived.

**Any non-circular use (Froissart from OS + clustering, no asymptotic states)? None.** OS + clustering does not force a gap: the free massless scalar satisfies every OS axiom with power-law clustering and `Δ=0` (B2). A cross-section needs an amplitude, which needs asymptotic states; there is no amplitude-free `σ_total`. Amplitude-free analyticity (tube/Källén–Lehmann) gives only the spectral representation `ρ≥0` — a sign/positivity statement yielding *upper* bounds, never a gap (same wall as RP: INEQ-T2, EDGE-f₁²). The 4D S-matrix bootstrap likewise must assume the gap and returns only allowed regions / upper bounds on couplings, never a proof (GLUEBALL-BOOTSTRAP).

**Sharpest line.** Every object here is a functional of the amplitude, and the amplitude exists only because glueballs are isolated atoms — `f₁²>0` and isolation, the open `H*`. Froissart is a rigorous *consequence* of that structure (`α(0)≤1`, an upper bound), Regge consistency a *consistency* (`m₀²` free, critical Pomeron allowed), analyticity itself partly an assumption (Steinmann). The arrow is one-way and points away from the proof: `gap+atoms ⇒ S-matrix ⇒ Froissart/Regge`. The circularity is not merely fatal for using analyticity to force the gap — the formalism *consumes the residue*, the exact object still missing.
