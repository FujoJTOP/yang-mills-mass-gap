# PROOF-PHYSICAL-SUBSPACE-CONVEXITY — are the Savvidy negatives pure-gauge, and does the Gauss-restricted physical Hessian turn positive?

> 2026-11-23 · 4D pure SU(N) Yang–Mills, Wilson lattice (β=2N/g²). Target: split the Savvidy negative modes (Hess S_W ≤ −1.1β, W6-BE) into gauge vs physical, and test whether the Gauss-constrained physical Hessian is positive although indefinite on the full space. **Verdict: NO — the negatives are not pure-gauge.** Gauge-invariance makes the pure-gauge orbit an *exact nullspace* of Hess S_W, so every negative eigenvalue is orbit-orthogonal — already a tangent vector of the physical quotient 𝒜/G; the direct projection (W4 Q⊥) leaves 7.1% negative (min −0.82β) at the SU(2) crossover. The action Hessian on the physical subspace is **genuinely indefinite**, and the gauge-invariant PF retreat that retires Gribov/FP does **not** retire Savvidy — a slice artifact vs a gauge-invariant feature. Gauge invariance *adds* one provable positivity, the Haar-pushforward Jacobian W_geo ≥ 0 (Prékopa), but does not delete the negatives: pointwise curvature at β=2.5 stays negative, and the marginal rescue (+0.51 on the softest mode) is one variance bound — H1/G2 — short. No fabrication.

**(1) Pure-gauge is exact null, never negative — a theorem.** S_W is gauge-invariant, so along any orbit direction ξ (timelike/longitudinal/pure-gauge) its second variation vanishes identically: the orbit, dim (N²−1)·#sites, is the **exact nullspace** of Hess S_W (PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES). Hence no negative eigenvalue is pure-gauge: every negative direction is metric-orthogonal to the orbit — a tangent vector of 𝒜/G. The continuum analog agrees: around a constant chromomagnetic F₀, −D²+2i ad F₀ has its negative mode in the lowest Landau level, spin-aligned to B — a **transverse magnetic** mode, the Nielsen–Olesen tachyon; longitudinal/FP-ghost directions are removed separately.

**(2) The physical Hessian is not positive — measured.** W4-blocked-positivity already did the split: it projected out the orbit (Q⊥) and diagonalised the gauge-transverse Hessian. Result: **7.1% negative, min −0.82β** at the SU(2) β=2.5 crossover; 12% negative (min −0.14β) in one blocked band at SU(3) deep strong coupling. MARGINAL's coarse restriction PᵀHP is min ≈ −0.3 (still negative after blocking), and Var ≥ 0 only subtracts, so the marginal is *more* negative than E[PᵀHP]. The broad negativity (04: 49% of links) is orbit-transverse; the W6 "fluctuation block" is a *momentum* split, not the orbit. The idea "positive on H_phys because the negatives are unphysical" is **false at the action level**.

**(3) Why gauge-invariance does not retire Savvidy.** PROOF-GAUSS-INVARIANT-PF defeats Gribov/FP because *that* obstruction lives in the gauge **slice**: gauge-fixing replaces positive Haar by sign(Δ_FP)·(signed), killing the PF cone, and the invariant pushforward μ̄ restores it. Savvidy is not on the slice — a gauge-invariant feature of the action landscape — so the invariant restriction *inherits* it; gauge invariance cannot help by deletion. What it supplies is the quotient volume form: on 𝒜/G the log-density is −(S_W+Φ), Φ=−log J convex with Φ″ ≥ (N/12)‖v‖² pointwise (SU2 1/6, SU3 1/4; measured 0.34, 0.46), and W_geo = E[Φ″]−Var(Φ′) ≥ 0 by Prékopa on **every** mode including k→0, uniformly. Genuine and provable — but **additive**, not subtractive.

**Pointwise vs marginal.** Pointwise at β=2.5 the Jacobian floor (+0.25–0.46) is below the Savvidy deficit (−1.1β), so Hess S_W+Φ″ is still negative in the worst directions: *pointwise* physical convexity is FALSE. The marginal/coarse G″ = Δ_dyn+W_geo+X is positive on the softest blocked mode (+0.51, W7; +0.22–0.31 finite k), but certifying it needs an upper bound on Var(G|0) — exactly H1 ⟺ m_eff²(k→0) ≥ λ.

**Sharpest line.** By gauge-invariance the pure-gauge directions are the *exact zero* of Hess S_W, so the negatives are unavoidably physical — orbit-transverse and surviving Q⊥ (7.1% at the crossover); the Gribov-killing PF retreat cannot touch them because Savvidy was never on the slice. Gauge invariance relocates the concavity to 𝒜/G and adds a provably-nonnegative Jacobian W_geo, but the physical **action** Hessian stays indefinite; only the **measure** marginal, if the Jacobian dominates the Savvidy floor, turns positive — and that is exactly H1/G2. The wall is real, not a gauge artifact.
