## §3 · The structural obstruction (why no 4D analogue exists)

The 3D specimen's readability rests on one **accidental coincidence**: in 3D, the `'t Hooft` loop (electric–magnetic
dual of the Wilson loop) is a **0-form** object dual to the Ising **spin**. This is special to 3D (and to
`Z₂`). In 4D:

- the confining order parameter is a **1-form** (center / monopole-condensation) order parameter; the
  Wilson–'t Hooft algebra is a **1-form** algebra;
- the `0⁺⁺` channel (the `tr F²` scalar / its Z₂ analog `∑_p U_p`) is a **0-form** object;
- these two are **Schur-orthogonal** (`BASE-ORDER-PARAMETER`): the 1-form condensate does *not* act as an order
  parameter for the `0⁺⁺` scalar.

So whereas the 3D roof is **one object read two ways** (`'t Hooft` loop = dual spin), the 4D roof is **two
decoupled objects**: confinement (1-form, "readable" in principle but off-target) and the `0⁺⁺` gap (0-form, **not
readable** off any dual magnetization). **The 3D transfer fails exactly at this decoupling** — not at a tool gap, a
knowledge gap, or a small-parameter gap alone. The 4D roof must therefore be **built directly as a 0-form object**.
