## 1. The flow and the covariance process
Flat chart `X=log U`, `S=S_W+Φ` (Haar Jacobian inside `Φ`, PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES). Localize by a noisy observation `Z_t=√t\,X+g`, `g∼N(0,I)`; then `μ_t=law(X|Z_t)` has density
`p_t(x) ∝ exp(−S(x)+⟨c_t,x⟩−(t/2)|x|²)`, `c_t=√t\,Z_t`, score `∇log p_t=−∇S+c_t−t x`.
Because the tilt is affine in `x`, its Hessian is **exactly** `tI`:
`Hess(−log p_t)(x) = Hess S(x) + tI`.
The covariance process `A_t=Cov(μ_t)` tracks the martingale mean `a_t=E_μ[x|F_t]`, `a_t→X`, `A_t→0`; for Gaussian `μ`, `A_t=(Hess S+tI)^{−1}` exactly, so `A_0=Cov(μ)`.

## 2. Energy / martingale bound — what a gap actually needs
A slice `μ_t` that is log-concave satisfies Brascamp–Lieb, `Var_{μ_t}(f)≤E_{\mu_t}⟨Hess S_t^{−1}∇f,∇f⟩`; i.e. the usable "energy" is the matrix comparison `A_t ⪯ E_{\mu_t}[Hess S_t^{−1}]`. On the coarse mode `v` (with fiber `K`, `Π₀`, `c`, `h_n` as in INVENT-VARIANCE) the `t`-sliced right side is `S_t(v)−D_t(v)`; at `t→0` it is `S(v)−D(v)=G″_0`, and `A_0`'s reciprocal is `1/Var_\mu(a)`. Hence *bounding the SL energy at its terminal slice ⟺ `Var_\mu(a)≤1/λ` ⟺ `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ`* — H1 verbatim (PROOF-COARSE-POINCARE). Backward transfer of the `t>2.8` slice to `t=0` reweights by `e^{−⟨c_t,x⟩+(t/2)|x|²}`; Holley–Stroock charges `e^{osc}` with `osc ≳ tR²` (`R` the chart radius), the same `e^{ct/a²}` disease as the flow — the convexification window is real but sits at the **wrong end**.

## 3. Acid test — mandatory

Every slice `A+tI≻0` (`t>0`) is log-concave, `A_t=(A+tI)^{−1}`; the zero mode has variance `1/t`, whose reciprocal → `0` as `t→0`, and `∫_0 1/t\,dt` diverges *exactly there*. The recovered gap is `λ=0`, `G″=0` (`f₁²=0`): **acid test passed** — no false positivity, and the divergence pins the free zero mode, so the machinery is non-vacuous. **Massive** free field: `A≻0 ⇒ A_0` finite, `λ=λ_min(A)>0`. **Pure Haar (β=0):** `S=Φ` convex, `Φ″⪰c_Φ`, already log-concave at `t=0`; BL gives `λ≥c_Φ=N/12` (the PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES anchor). All three calibrations exact.
