# SYNTHESIS-NEWCONSTRUCTS — the three new constructs escape the *sign* wall but do not compose: their sum is still the one bridge, transport conditional on (CP)

> 2026-11-23 · Do the three constructs *combine* to close where each alone does not? **Verdict: No — but they are not degenerate the way the old routes were.** The Fredholm determinant (`f₁²` = zero-residue), multiplicative transport (`f₁²(μ)=Z_O² f₁²(μ₀)`), and SCBL variance split (`G″=S−D`) each genuinely escape the sign/positivity wall (B1) that reduced every OLD cross-pair to one number: the determinant is **nonlinear** (not a moment), transport is a **square** (`c=Z_O²>0`, sign-free), SCBL is an **exact identity** (not a BL inequality). So the "equipartition ⇒ sum collapses to `W`" degeneration of SYNTHESIS-CROSSROUTE does **not** recur. But the parts are **side-local in β and in functional** — Fredholm exact only at fixed `a,β<1/48`; transport the sole bridge; variance at β=2.5 on `G″`, not `f₁²`. No construct supplies another's missing hypothesis. The combination still reduces to its one non-local part — **transport, conditional on (CP)**. *Sum = max of parts, in the closure sense.*

## (a) Fredholm ⊗ transport = transport, not more.
Fredholm pins `f₁²(β_eff)` as the residue `−(1/z₁)N_O(z₁)/D_O'(z₁)` at the smallest zero `z₁=e^{am₁}` — exactly, at fixed `a`. But transport's lower bound needs only `f₁²(β_eff)≥r₀`, which **P3 already gives**; the exact value is redundant for the *bound*. And transport cannot consume Fredholm's exactness to earn `(CP)`: the determinant zero `z₁` is an eigenvalue of `T_O`, robust, but its **residue** is `|A₁|²` and is only upper-semicontinuous — as `⟨Ω|O|G⟩→0` the zero barely moves while `f₁²→0` at a fixed below-threshold pole (the `:φ²:` mechanism, PROOF-DISSOLUTION). The determinant sees the **mass** continuously, not a lower bound on the **residue**; zero-below-cut ⇏ residue bounded below. Fredholm relocates the continuum wall for *evaluating* `f₁²`; it does not supply `(CP)`.

## (b) transport ⊗ variance = two independent walls, on two functionals.
Variance gives `G″=S−D` at 2.5; even granted `D<S` (measured 2%, unproven = H1), `G″>0` is a **gap-type** positivity, not `f₁²>0` — the gapped free scalar `:φ²:` has `G″>0`, `f₁²=0` (PROOF-F1SQ). So variance cannot certify the transporter's target datum `f₁²(2.5)>0`; it certifies the induced mass, a different object under a different and orthogonal hypothesis (indefinite fiber `H_ff`). Adding it **conjoins** two walls and removes neither. Transport, being mass-free, is blind to `G″` (PROOF-TRANSPORT-RG §3) and cannot feed `D<S` either. No edge in the "supplies the other's hole" graph.

## (c) All three = transport ⊗ (CP) — exactness does not substitute for (CP).
The maximal composed statement is `f₁²(2.5)=Z_O²·[Fredholm residue at β_eff]`. Every factor is an **equality** — yet the multiplicative-anomaly relation that *is* transport presupposes `|G(β_eff)⟩` and `|G(2.5)⟩` are the **same physical atom** carried along the flow; that identity-of-state across the crossover is precisely `(CP)`. An exact homomorphism on the wrong state-space is vacuous. Exactness carries the *value* only on the domain where `(CP)` holds; it does not earn `(CP)`.

## (d) Steelman: Fredholm all along `[β_eff,2.5]`.
One could use `D_O(z;β)` at every β and argue continuity of `z₁(β)`. It fails at the one point that matters: `f₁²` is usc, and at decoupling the visible state leaves the `O`-cyclic subspace — the determinant's **degree drops** — so `z₁` jumps and the residue vanishes without a value-discontinuity the determinant can forbid. Continuity of zeros ⇏ residue lower bound = `(CP)` restated.

## (e) No fourth lever.
The index route is a rigidity no-go (INVENT-NEW-INDEX): a discrete, deformation-rigid invariant equals its free value `0` across the connected β-line. No topological protection supplies the missing positivity.

**The conditional theorem that does exist (exact hypotheses).** *Fix N, T=0, β_eff<1/48. Assume **(CP)**: `[β_eff,2.5]` is one confining `T=0` phase, lightest `0⁺⁺` Kato-isolated (`Λ−m₁²≥κ>0`) and carrying the plaquette edge (`A₁(β)≠0`), no bulk transition. Then `f₁²(2.5)=Z_O² f₁²(β_eff)≥Z_O² r₀>0`, `Z_O=(β/2g)(β_eff)/(β/2g)(2.5)`, with `f₁²(β_eff)` given exactly by the Fredholm residue. `G″(2.5)>0` is an orthogonal corollary requiring `D<S`.* Load-bearing hypothesis: **`(CP)` alone**; Fredholm adds the exact anchor, variance a gap-type fact.

**Minimal jointly-missing object.** `(CP)` — the single-`T=0`-confining-phase + Kato-isolated, edge-carrying continuation across `[β_eff,2.5]`; verbatim `H*(r₀)`+edge-continuity+`a→0`, i.e. continuum existence. Not the sign wall (the new constructs discharge it), not the old equipartition degeneracy — the **continuum/phase wall**, the same object as CONDITIONAL-LATTICE-GAP's `(H1)–(H3)+a→0`.

**Sharpest line.** The new constructs genuinely break the OLD verdict's *mechanism* — a nonlinear determinant, a square, and an exact split are not the mutually-cancelling numbers that made the old pairs degenerate — but not its *conclusion*: they are side-local (strong coupling / bridge / weak coupling), and the only bridge is transport, whose toll is `(CP)`. Fredholm computes `f₁²` exactly on the near bank, variance certifies `G″` on the far bank, and neither pays the ferry. `Sum = max of parts`, and the max is `transport + (CP)`.
