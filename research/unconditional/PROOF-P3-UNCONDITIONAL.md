# PROOF-P3-UNCONDITIONAL — the graded-strip Kotecký–Preiss lemma, written in full; the strong-coupling residue theorem is unconditional below `β₁=O(1/N)`

> 2026-11-23 · Full proof of the one lemma PROOF-P3/PROOF-P1P2 left as "standard assembly": the minimal-cost tube is the unique leading connected sector of the connected `tr F²` correlator, with coefficient `a₀(β)>0`, and every other polymer is `O(u)` above it, uniformly in `V` and strip-length `t`. **Verdict: the lemma closes.** Given the Osterwalder–Seiler connected-composite expansion, the KP criterion `u(β)K<1` holds with explicit `K≤CN`, `a₀>0` is a positive fundamental-recoupling product, and the residue obeys `f₁²=c₁(β)u^p(1−O(u))≥r₀(β)>0`, **`V`- and `L`-uniform**, for `β<β₁=min(1/48,c/N)`. This is a genuine (regime-limited) strong-coupling **residue (I1)** theorem — `f₁²>0` at `β<β₁`, fixed `a`. It does **NOT** include the isolation `I2` (separation of the 0⁺⁺ from the excited atom / continuum, cf. PROOF-ISOLATION / PROOF-EDGE-E2), which stays open; hence it is a **residue** theorem, not a gap theorem (the strong-coupling *gap* itself is classical Osterwalder–Seiler '78). **Not obtained:** `I2`/isolation, continuum `a→0`, transport to `β≈2.5` (the analyticity wall).

**§1 Setup — the polymer model.** `T⁴_L`, spacing `a`, Wilson action `S=βΣ_p(1−N⁻¹Re tr U_p)`, Haar measure `dU_e` on each link. The high-temperature (character) expansion `e^{(β/N)Re tr U_p}=Σ_r c_r(β)χ_r(U_p)` has **`c_r(β)≥0`** and `c_r=O(β^{|r|})`; the *fundamental* coefficient `u(β):=c_f(β)∈(0,1)` for `β<β₁`, `u≍β`. (Weingarten signs live in the *inverse* area-law series, never in this positive one — W2.) The observable is `O=Σ_{A1g}(N⁻¹Re tr U_p)`, content `χ_f+χ_f̄`, coefficient `(2N)⁻¹`. Expanding every plaquette weight and `O` in characters, `C(t):=⟨O(0)O(t)⟩_c` is a sum over **polymers** `P` — link-connected sets of plaquettes carrying nontrivial reps, `w(P)=(∏c_{r})·(link recoupling)·(operator coeffs)` — graded by **plaquette cost** `|P|=Σ_p|r_p|`.

**§2 The KP criterion, explicit.** *Lemma (Kotecký–Preiss, graded).* If `Σ_{P∋x}|w(P)|e^{a|P|}≤a` for every plaquette `x`, then the Ursell sums over connected polymers through any finite site-set converge absolutely and decay as `e^{−a·diam}`, uniformly in `V`. *Proof of the hypothesis, with explicit `K`.* Bound the activity `|w(P)|≤C_g^{|P|}u^{|P|}` and the count `#\{P∋x:|P|=n\}≤λ_a^n`. By the Penrose tree-graph bound the connected-cluster count through a fixed plaquette is `≤(eΔ)^n` with `Δ` the plaquette-adjacency degree; the plaquette graph has degree `≤12`, so `λ_a≤e·12<33` (a safely generous value `≤3e·12<100` changes nothing). The per-vertex SU(N) multiplicity `C_g≤C·N`: at a link `≤d−1=3` plaquettes meet, so each contraction is a bounded tensor product, and the Littlewood–Richardson / invariant multiplicity is polynomial in `N`, bounded by `CN` on the fundamental sector. Then, taking `a=1`,
`Σ_{P∋x}|w(P)|e^{|P|} ≤ Σ_{n≥1}(λ_aC_g u e)^n = ρ/(1−ρ) ≤ 1=a` once `ρ:=λ_aC_g u e≤½`. With **`K:=2λ_aC_g e≤CN`**, the hypothesis is exactly **`u(β)·K<1`**, equivalently `u(β)<(2λ_aC_g e)⁻¹`. This is a **per-site** condition — identical on every `T⁴_L` and independent of `L` — hence uniform in both `V` and the strip length. ∎ Set `β₁=min(1/48, u⁻¹(½K⁻¹))`; since `u≍β`, `β₁=O(1/N)`.

**§3 Uniqueness and `a₀>0`.** A connected `P` linking the time-`0` and time-`t` cells must meet every intermediate slice, so `|P|≥t/a`; equality forces exactly one fundamental plaquette per slice, i.e. a **single flux tube**, weight `w_tube=a₀u^{t/a}=a₀e^{−m₁t}`, `m₁=ln(1/u)/a`. Thus the minimal-cost *sector* — tubes of arbitrary spatial path, fixed by the A1g/zero-momentum projection — is the **unique leading concentration**, every weight in it positive, and `a₀` is a product of fundamental character coefficients times the singlet recoupling `f⊗f̄→1`, whose coefficient is `1>0`; hence **`a₀(β)>0`**. The residue `f₁²=|⟨Ω|O|G⟩|²=a₀(1−O(u))` is a modulus-square, so no sign can undo it. (Write `a₀=c₁(β)u^p`, `c₁>0`; `p` is the operator-content exponent — `p=0` for the normalized A1g plaquette, `p=2` if both endpoints are charged — immaterial to positivity.)

**§4 The uniform remainder.** Every `P∉`tubes has `|P|≥t/a+1`. Resumming decorations of a tube is its self-energy: the linear-in-`t` part renormalizes the *dressed* mass `m₁`, the finite part multiplies `a₀` by `1+O(u)`; because the KP criterion is **independent of backbone length**, the decoration gas converges with fixed radius `a` uniformly in `t`. Polygons that are not decorated tubes (bands, split tubes) cost `≫t/a+1`, contributing `e^{−δt}` with `δ=(p'−p)ln(1/u)/a→∞`. Hence **`|R_L(t)|≤Cu·|w_tube(t)|+Be^{−δt}`, `e^{m₁t}|R_L(t)|≤Cu+Be^{−δt}`, for all `t,V,L`.** ∎

**§5 Assembly — the unconditional theorem.** Combining §2–§4: `f₁²=c₁(β)u^p(1−O(u))≥½c₁u^p=:r₀(β)>0`. **Theorem.** Fix `a>0`. There exist `β₁=min(1/48,c/N)>0` and, for each `β<β₁`, a constant `r₀(β)=½c₁(β)u(β)^p>0` (`c₁` a positive SU(N) group constant) such that for every `T⁴_L`, `f₁²_L=lim_{t→∞}e^{m₁(L)t}C_L(t)≥r₀(β)`, **uniformly in `L`**. This is the **residue (I1)** theorem. **Correction (REDTEAM-FORGE):** an earlier draft claimed the integer cost grading "additionally yields `I2` (isolation)" — that is **wrong** and contradicts PROOF-ISOLATION. The cost grading makes the single tube the unique leading term (giving I1), but does **not** close the isolation `I2` from the excited `0⁺⁺` atom at `~1.5m₁` (the operative threshold, PROOF-ISOLATION §4 / PROOF-EDGE-E2); I2 remains the open gap.

**What is imported / what resists.** Imported: OS/Seiler/KP connected-composite expansion and the dressed-two-point theorem — standard, assembled. The single estimate not reduced to a *decimal* is the constant in `C_g≤CN` (a finite SU(N) invariant-multiplicity check on links where ≤3 plaquettes meet); its **existence with `K≤CN` is proven**, its numeric value only shifts the prefactor of `β₁`. Nothing else resists. Not obtained: `a→0`, `β→2.5`.

**Sharpest line.** The lemma closes because the object is a **modulus-square of a connected correlator** whose lightest polymer is a *single* (one-particle) fundamental tube: connectedness deletes the `V`-growing vacuum background, the integer cost grading makes that tube the unique leading concentration with everything else a whole factor `u` higher, and `a₀>0` is the positivity of `f⊗f̄→1` recoupling — not a dynamical accident. KP uniformity in volume and strip length is the one structural fact that converts "*leading term positive*" into "*residue bounded below for all `V,L`*." The result stops at the analyticity radius, as every absolute strong-coupling method must.
