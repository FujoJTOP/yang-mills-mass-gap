## §1 · The `[T]` argument (the anomaly forbids a massless Goldstone dilaton)

The dilatation current `D^μ` in pure YM has, exactly,
`∂_μ D^μ = θ^μ_μ = (β(g)/2g)·tr F²`  (trace/scale anomaly; Adler–Bardeen, Collins–Duncan–Joglekar).
Asymptotic freedom gives `β(g) < 0` for all `g ∈ (0, ∞)` (two-loop `β = −β₀g³ − β₁g⁵`, `β₀,β₁>0`, **no
Banks–Zaks zero**). Hence `θ^μ_μ ≢ 0`: **scale invariance is explicitly (anomalously) broken, not a symmetry
of the quantum theory.**

Goldstone's theorem applies to a **symmetry**. An *explicitly* broken symmetry has **no exact
Nambu–Goldstone boson** — its would-be Goldstone is a **pseudo-Goldstone** whose mass is set by the explicit
breaking `∝ (β(g)/2g)⟨tr F²⟩` and is **`> 0`**. Verified for free fields (witness): the gluon and free-scalar
anomaly coefficients are `∝ β₀ = O(N)>0`, and a scale-breaking potential gives the "dilaton" a mass² `∝` the
anomaly, `>0`, while the **exact conformal** (β=0) case gives mass `0`.

⇒ **No massless dilaton in pure YM.** `[T]`

## §2 · What this excludes, and what it does NOT

Classify a gapless IR 0⁺⁺ (a unitary 4D theory, IR limit):

- **(D) Massless *particle* (dilaton):** `tr F²` has a pole at `s=0`. In a unitary theory this needs a
  non-zero VEV of a dimension-4 scalar ⇒ **spontaneous** breaking of scale invariance ⇒ an exact spontaneously
  broken scale symmetry ⇒ a massless Goldstone. But §1 forbids an exact scale symmetry (anomaly). ⇒ **(D) is
  EXCLUDED.** `[T]`
- **(C) Unbroken IR CFT:** `tr F²` has a **continuum from `s=0`** (a conformal two-point function `⟨FF⟩∼1/x^{2Δ}`),
  **no pole, no massless particle.** Scale invariance here is *emergent in the IR* (a fixed point `β(g*)=0`),
  not spontaneously broken, so §1 does **not** apply. ⇒ **(C) is NOT excluded** by this link.

So this link kills (D) and leaves (C). (★) = "not (C)": pure YM's IR is **not** an unbroken conformal point.
That is the **second** link (no-BZ: no `β(g*)=0` for `g*>0`), and it is **open** at strong coupling (the
perturbative `β<0` does not by itself exclude a strong-coupling fixed point — extra input, essentially the
`a`-theorem plus `a_IR=0`, is needed). **This file proves (D)-exclusion, not (C)-exclusion.**

## §3 · The circularity trap (why this is *not* already the gap)

(`f₁²>0` at `m₁²>0`, i.e. `H1`). The content of §1 is **narrower and strictly provable**: it says only that

massive"* (which would require excluding the (C) continuum). So the link is **non-circular and `[T]`**
*because* it stops short of the gap: it forbids one *mechanism* (dilaton) without asserting the conclusion.

**The exact residual step (`[O]`):** exclude branch (C) — i.e. prove the IR of pure YM is not an unbroken
conformal point. Equivalently: no `β(g*)=0` survives at strong coupling in the IR. This is the **(★)** CFT
half; it is the open problem, not a sub-step of this file.
