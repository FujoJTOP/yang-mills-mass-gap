# H1-T8 — Free probability / random-matrix / master field: a rigorous *language* for N=∞, not a gap route

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh-framework probe on H1. **Verdict: genuinely different *mathematics* — an exact N=∞ limit, not an asymptotic 1/N series — but the same large-N axis and the same wall.** Freeness builds the master field and computes its spectrum exactly **only where the planar loop closes**: 0D (semicircle) and 2D YM (Gopakumar–Gross). In 4D it is unconstructed; even granted, its trace carries *equal-time* loop moments, not the *Euclidean-time* atom that is a glueball mass. It re-encodes G1+G2, adds no edge bound, transfers to N=2,3 by no theorem.

**1. What is rigorous.** Freeness, free CLT, semicircle: Voiculescu–Dykema–Nica (1992). Factorization into a deterministic master field: 't Hooft (NPB 72 (1974)); concentration (Chatterjee arXiv:2201.01838). **Constructed master field with computed spectrum — two cases only:** 0D (semicircle) and **2D YM**, where Gopakumar–Gross (hep-th/9411021) realise the master field and Wilson loops as **Cuntz-algebra** elements, solvable because the e.o.m. close. Eguchi–Kawai (PRL 48 (1982)) reduces large-N to one site — a theorem only with unbroken centre/twisting/quenching, formal otherwise. 4D: no explicit master field; the strongest rigorous anchor is small-β — Nissim (arXiv:2510.22788) proves mass gap + unique infinite-volume limit + large-N for U(N) there, not free-probabilistic, not physical coupling.

**2. "Spectrum = moment spectrum" conflates two measures.** τ fixes equal-time moments ⟨W(C)⟩ (R-transforms compute these). A mass is an **atom** m₁² of the Källén–Lehmann measure ν of a *two-point function* (f₁²=ν({m₁²})), read from Euclidean-time decay (mass-spectrum; GAP-G2-edge). τ's spectral measure ≠ ν; no freeness theorem bridges them. The bridge is OS reconstruction + the transfer operator, whose gap is the time-translation generator, not a functional of τ. *Attempted "master-field spectral gap":* put {W(C)} in a tracial W*-probability space; the gap becomes its KMS generator's gap. Dies: (i) no 4D master field (G1); (ii) modular flow is mass-blind — the free massless field has full-circle spectrum yet Δ=0 (T2/AQFT); (iii) reading a gap off τ is OS+gap disguised.

**3. Different from the tried 1/N?** As *mathematics*, yes — an exact limit, not a renormalon-divergent series. But same large-N axis; the construction is *equivalent to solving the planar loop equation* — what 4D fails. Freeness presupposes the N=∞ object exists; it does not supply it (10-dse). It rigorises IN-b/DSE/fixed-g²; moves no wall.

**4. Transfer to SU(2)/SU(3): no theorem.** Nothing links m(N=∞) to m(3); "ratios ~N-independent" is empirical (SURVEY NP-6), not a bound. At physical λ_G=h∨g², w=h∨/λ_G→∞, so strong-coupling expansions fail; rigorous large-N lives at fixed g²/small β — disjoint (gluing §2.6). The 1/N series is asymptotic with uncontrolled error (C1, GAP-IN11); Eguchi–Kawai independence fails at finite N.

**5. What it gives / first test.** Gives a rigorous deterministic N=∞ limit (tracial W*-probability space), factorization, exact semicircle (0D), a constructed 2D master field, and the sharpest *statement*: gap = spectral gap of its KMS generator. Does **not** give the 4D master field, its low spectrum, the gap, or transfer to N=3. **Milestone:** prove the large-N lattice-YM limit exists *at fixed λ_G*, free-probabilistic, with a transfer-matrix gap; failure kills the route. **Different microscope, same wall** — "master field = planar loop solution" (G1) ∧ "trace static, gap dynamical" (G2). No new tool; two objects banked.
