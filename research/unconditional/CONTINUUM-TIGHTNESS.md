# CONTINUUM-TIGHTNESS — tightness on the physical class *is* the marginal BPHZ; it yields existence, never the gap

> **Verdict (判词).** **No separable tightness shortcut exists; tightness *is* the marginal BPHZ, and it does not give the gap.** Tightness splits by observable class, and the two halves have opposite status. On the *bounded* Wilson-loop algebra it is free and **vacuous**: `|Φ|≤1` puts every moment in `[-1,1]`, so `∏[-1,1]` is compact (Tychonoff/Prokhorov) with constant `1` (GL3-B, R30) — no analytic content, and it cannot even recover the smeared `tr F²` moments. On the physically relevant class — smeared gauge-invariant field strength, the minimal class carrying OS0 regularity and a nontrivial theory — tightness of `{μ_{L,a}}` under `a→0` **is** `UVS(L)`: `(U1)` a-uniform `L^p` bounds, `(U2)` scheme-C subtraction, `(U3)` Minlos equicontinuity. Its whole non-vacuous content is the *nonperturbative a-uniform resummation* over the `J∼log(1/a)` shell tower that order-by-order BPHZ (L2, *proven*) does not supply — and driving it through forces the Savvidy large-field sign `(★)`. So tightness is **not weaker** than BPHZ; it is that wall in compactness language. And even granted, it yields only fixed-volume existence (subsequential `μ_L`, OS0/1/3 = G1) — **not** `0⁺⁺` existence, **not** the gap.

**[1] Two tightness classes.** Relative compactness of `{μ_{L,a}}` on `(C_c^k)′` is *exactly* a-uniform boundedness of the renormalized moments: Banach–Alaoglu + countable diagonal (GL3). A uniform moment bound and Prokhorov tightness are the same datum; the bounded Wilson-loop version, being constant `1`, is the degenerate case that carries nothing. The substance lives entirely in the unbounded `(U1)–(U3)`.

**[2] Physical tightness = UVS(L), not separable; base-pocket is not a bypass.** `(U2)` — the same-scheme `a⁻⁴` subtraction — *is* the marginal counterterm. L2 gives it order-by-order, but the `a⁻⁴` coefficient is an asymptotic renormalon, non-resummable (BREAK-IN-7), so the a-uniform *bound* cannot be summed from the series; it must be a flow trapping region. The only technology for uniform base-pocket/moment bounds in 4D is Balaban's small-field/large-field RG: the right frame, but gauge-fixed, giving neither gauge-invariant correlator convergence nor OS, and its scale-uniformity reduces to `(★)`. Base-pocket *value* bounds from coercivity `∫Tr F²≥0` control the action (the tail = part of `(U1)`), not the *Hessian norm* across scales. No route here is BPHZ-free — and "bypassing order-by-order BPHZ" is a red herring: the order-by-order half is the *easy, proven* part, so tightness asks strictly more.

**[3] Tightness ⇒ a measure, not the gap.** From `UVS(L)`, Prokhorov/Minlos give a subsequence and `μ_L`; GL4 passes reflection positivity, giving OS0/1/3 — this *is* G1-EXISTENCE. The gap is a spectral property of the transfer matrix, and no compactness statement produces a spectral gap. The in-corpus witness is decisive: massive Gaussian with `m_n→0` is tight, RP, OS-complete, and converges to the *massless* GFF — no exponential clustering, no gap (R28; GL4 §3.3). `0⁺⁺` *existence* (`f₁²>0`) fares no better: the free field, itself a legitimate tightness limit, has `f₁²=0` (SCHUR-CONVEX). Tightness bounds the *size* of `μ`, never the position or weight of an atom.

**Sharpest line.** Tightness is compactness of the measure family, not a lower bound on the mass. It can be bought only by solving the same nonperturbative a-uniform marginal-BPHZ bound that is G1's `L3` (hence G1, not `⊥` the BPHZ wall in content — only in *language*); and once bought, it certifies that a continuum theory *exists*, never that it is massive — the `m→0` Gaussian is the counterexample and the free field the null case.
