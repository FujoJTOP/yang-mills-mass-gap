# CONTINUUM-ASYMPTOTIC-BEYOND — can renormalon resummation cross the asymptotic wall (★)?

> **Verdict.** **No. It re-expresses the wall; it does not remove it.** The a-uniformity (★/L3) fails at the bridge `Var(G_v)≲E[H]`, and the *defect* of that bridge is the factorial growth of its coefficient series — the IR renormalon. The *known* singularity structure says exactly what the defect **is**: IR renormalons at `u=+2n/β₀` on the positive Borel axis, each factorising into the matrix element of a dimension-`4n` operator (n=1: the gluon condensate `⟨Tr F²⟩`). The two lateral Borel sums exist but differ by **precisely that condensate**; on ℝ⁴ the trans-series Stokes parameter is undetermined (no semiclassical bion sector, Maiezza–Vasquez). Hence the Borel ambiguity *is* the nonperturbative data — the residue's sign, the Savvidy defect, the transmuted `Λ`. Resurgence converts "the series diverges" into "the series is fixed only up to exactly the physics it omits." The one exact handle, `Z_O=(β/2g)(μ₀)/(β/2g)(μ)`, is already a *covariance* (INVENT-TRANSPORT), needs no resummation, and cannot lower-bound the nonperturbative base point.

**1. Positions and factorisation.** With `β₀=11N/48π²>0` the one-loop slope (AF), the Borel variable `u` conjugate to `g²` carries singularities at `u=+2n/β₀` (IR, `n≥1`) and `u=−2n/β₀` (UV) — in the project's `z=−2n/β₁` convention (`β₁<0`) the IR set lands on the **positive** real axis. The **UV** branch points sit on the *negative* axis: no obstruction to Borel–Laplace, and their resummation is exactly the log-sum `Σ_j g_j⁴=(16β₀⁴)⁻¹ζ(4)<∞` already proved given L2 (BBS; PROOF-G1 §2b). The **IR** points are the obstruction. Near `u=2n/β₀` the Borel transform factorises, `B(u)∼(1−u/(2n/β₀))^{−1−γ_n}·C_n` with `C_n∝γ_n·⟨O_{4n}⟩`: the renormalon residue is the **anomalous dimension × condensate** of a higher-dimension operator. For the dim-4 `O_S=¼tr F²` sourcing `f₁²`, `n=1` ties the ambiguity to `⟨Tr F²⟩` itself.

**2. Existence and definite sign?** The series in `g` has zero radius; only the **lateral** Borel sums exist, differing by `ΔS=2πi·Σ_n C_n e^{−2n/(β₀g²)}(…)`, a purely imaginary trans-series amount whose real completion needs the Stokes constant `σ`. On ℝ⁴ `σ` is *free* — no monopole/bion sector fixes it (07-resurgence; Maiezza–Vasquez 2405.01639). So the Borel sum has **no definite sign** — only a *determined magnitude, undetermined sign* ambiguity of order `(Λ/μ)^{2n}`. Worse for a **lower** bound: the median sum is a `g`-continuous function vanishing at `g=0` (free field, `f₁²≡0`, EDGE-f₁²), and Borel summation is a homomorphism, preserving `0↦0`; it cannot manufacture `f₁²≥r₀>0` from a `g`-trivial start (INVENT-TRANSPORT's acid test is this `0↦0`). The needed positivity is nonperturbative by construction.

**3. Trace anomaly and a-uniform f₁².** The anomaly `T^μ_μ=(β/2g)O_S` makes `f₁²(μ)=Z_O²f₁²(μ₀)` **exact** — a ratio of running couplings, positive as a *square*, `O(1)` in `V` (INVENT-TRANSPORT). This is the one place the β-function controls the running, and it needs **no** Borel sum: `Z_O` is defined nonperturbatively as the exact running. Resumming the perturbative series for `Z_O` would reimport an IR-renormalon ambiguity at `u=2/β₀`, i.e. at `O(Λ²)` — exactly the order the whole problem turns on. And `Z_O²` *multiplies* a base value `f₁²(μ₀)` that is the spectral-edge atom (RESIDUE-CONDITIONAL `H*`): nonperturbative, untouched by series methods. The renormalon–OPE relation `t₀=−a_IR/β₀` is itself proved only in 2D large-N; in 4D it is an assumption (07-resurgence).

**4. What Borel-ness actually buys.** Nothing a-uniform in the *positive* direction. The ambiguity `∼(Λ/μ)^{2n}` is **asymmetric in scale**: *small deep in the UV* (the easy side of the trap), *largest at the IR edge / Savvidy crossover* (`k→0`; GAP-G1 §3; GAP-G2) where the bound is hard. A band of undetermined sign and width `ε>0` cannot certify `G″_j≥δ_W` unless `δ_W>ε` with a *located* centre — which needs `σ`. The trap's convergence edge and the ambiguity's maximum coincide: Borel-ness is most useless exactly where (★) bites, sharpening the diagnosis (the wall is a trans-series parameter, a condensate) but supplying no sign.

**Sharpest line.** The renormalon ambiguity is not a residual error to be resummed away; it **is** the nonperturbative piece — numerically the gluon condensate, physically the residue/Savvidy/`Λ` datum. Borel summation makes the missing input explicit; it cannot replace it.
