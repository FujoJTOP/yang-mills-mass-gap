# G1-OS4-GIVEN-GAP — G2 ⇒ OS4: clustering is free from *any* gap; vacuum *uniqueness across sectors* is free iff the gap is volume- and bc-uniform

> **Verdict (判词).** "OS4" hides two objects: **(C) clustering** of the state (⇔ its *extremality*) and **(U) uniqueness of the infinite-volume vacuum**/DLR state (`UNI-lim`). Given a gap `Δ>0`, **(C) is free and unconditional** — exponential decay at rate `Δ` *is* the projection `T=e^{−aH}=P_0+R`, `‖R‖≤e^{−aΔ}`. If the gap is further **uniform in volume `L` and across centre/boundary sectors**, **(U) is free too** (Dobrushin uniqueness: uniform influence-decay ⇒ all limits coincide). If it is *not* volume/bc-uniform — only "each pure phase is gapped" — (C) stays free but (U) is **not**: it costs per-sector screening `W1₀∧W1₁` (2D Ising: two gapped, exponentially-clustering phases; uniqueness/REPORT Ex. 1). So **strong-OS4 is free exactly when G2 is the uniform (L- and sector-independent) gap** — as SZZ/Osterwalder–Seiler and CONDITIONAL-LATTICE-GAP state it. Only the word *uniform* is non-free; the atom (H1) is never needed.

**§1. Ladder.** `G2` ⇔ gap `Δ>0` of `H≥0`. *G2-weak*: `spec(H)∩(0,Δ)=∅` (no atom); *G2-strong*: + bottom atom. `OS4` = clustering/ergodicity; `VAC` = `ker(H)=ℂΩ`; `UNI-lim` = unique translation-invariant DLR state. Poset: `G2-strong ⇒ G2-weak ⇒ (C ⇔ extremal)`; `(C + uniformity) ⇒ UNI-lim ⇒ VAC`. No arrow reverses (`OS4 ⇏` gap: critical 2D Ising; gapped-GFF of 定理 B′ satisfies every OS axiom with `Δ>0` and no atom).

**§2. Lattice implication.** Let `T_Λ=e^{−aH_Λ}`, `0≤T≤1`, `H_Λ≥0`, and suppose **(U-gap)** `λ₁(T_Λ)≤e^{−aΔ}`, `Δ>0` independent of `Λ` *and* of the boundary condition `ξ`. Then:
1. *Clustering.* `T_Λ=P_0+R_Λ`, `P_0=|Ω_Λ⟩⟨Ω_Λ|`, `‖R_Λ‖=λ₁(T_Λ)≤e^{−aΔ}`. For local `A,B` on slices separated by `s`: `|ω_{Λ,ξ}(Aτ_sB)−ω(A)ω(B)|=|⟨AΩ,R^{s/a}BΩ⟩_c|≤‖A‖‖B‖e^{−Δs}` — rate `Δ`, **uniform in `Λ,ξ`**.
2. *Limit extremal.* A uniform bound passes to any weak-`*` limit `μ=lim μ_{Λ_n}`; `μ` clusters at rate `Δ` ⇒ trivial tail σ-field ⇒ `μ` extremal ⇒ unique vacuum of its GNS space (`ker H=ℂΩ`, VAC). *(C) done.*
3. *Uniqueness.* Because the bound is uniform over `ξ`, boundary influence at distance `r` is `≤C e^{−Δr}`; Dobrushin uniqueness (Georgii ch. 8) ⇒ every bc gives the same limit, `UNI-lim`. The unique state is `μ`, so OS4 holds for *the* vacuum. ∎

*Failure mode.* Drop "uniform in `Λ`": a fixed-`L` PBC gap does not pass to `L→∞` — coexistence **is** tunneling splitting `~e^{−cL}→0`, so `λ₁(T_Λ)` collapses. This is 2D Ising: each phase gapped, yet two phases coexist (the PBC state's gap→0). Hence the load-bearing word: **volume-uniform**.

**§3. Continuum.** One level up, the same steps run on the OS-reconstructed `(H,T_t,Ω)` (G1-CONTINUUM-OS): `Δ_phys>0 ⇒ T_t=P_0+R`, `‖R‖≤e^{−Δ_phys t}` ⇒ Euclidean-time clustering; the torus's four transfer matrices share `Δ` (hypercubic) ⇒ clustering along `ℤ⁴` ⇒ OS4. OS2 (full `O(4)`) is not an OS4 cost — it only makes the rate isotropic (G1/UV side).

**§4. Centre `Z_N`.** At `T=0` on `ℝ⁴` the centre is not a bulk order parameter (no Polyakov loop; on a finite `T⁴_L` the centre-twisted / `'t Hooft`-flux sectors label boundary conditions). Requiring the gap uniform **across all centre sectors** ⇒ no sector carries a lower ground energy ⇒ the sectors are superselection labels of **one** vacuum, not distinct vacua: the `Z_N` multiplicity dies free (uniqueness/REPORT §5.5.2: "OS4 + centre-invariant PBC at `T=0`"). A *sector-dependent* gap (one sector gapless) would be spontaneous centre breaking — forbidden by uniform `Δ`. The `Z_N` "vacua" are the finite-`T` deconfinement artifact (`β_c(N_t)→∞`), a different phase.

**§5. Exponential vs qualitative.** OS4 literally demands only *qualitative* (any-rate) clustering. G2 overshoots: it delivers **exponential at rate `Δ`**, the stronger (MIX) form. Conversely qualitative `C` does **not** upgrade to a gap (critical 2D Ising; gapped-GFF of 定理 B′). The arrow is strict and one-way; "exponential" is free *because* it rides the gap.

**§6. Honest verdict.** *Is OS4 free given G2?* **Clustering/extremality: yes, unconditionally.** **Unique vacuum: yes iff G2 is the volume- and sector-uniform gap** — which the project's G2 *is* (SZZ/Osterwalder–Seiler "uniform in `L,a`"; CONDITIONAL-LATTICE-GAP `Δ_Λ≥m`). The only "more" is *uniformity*; absent it (per-phase gap only) the unique-vacuum half reverts to its price `W1₀∧W1₁`. So **given uniform G2, OS4 is genuinely free; given only a fixed-volume/per-phase gap, exactly one step — sector-uniform clustering `W1₀∧W1₁` — must be re-bought.**
