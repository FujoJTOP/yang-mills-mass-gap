# PROOF-COMPLEX-BETA-2D — a 2D complex continuation is circumventable but never reduces the problem: any path that carries `f₁²` to `2.5` is, by Kato, the gap on `[1/48,2.5]` restated

> 2026-11-23 · PROOF-MODE test of escaping the Pringsheim wall by analytic continuation in complex `β`, a `(β, complex L)` plane, or `(coupling+λ)` plane, avoiding the positive-real singularity and the Savvidy point. **Verdict: no such continuation is proved, and none is a relaxation — it *is* the gap.** (1) An off-axis path along which `f₁²` is analytic exists **iff** gap + isolation hold at every point *including the endpoint* `2.5`, where all are open; continuation being path-independent, the real interval is the shortest such path, so no detour beats it. (2) `Z_O²` and the KL pole are real-axis statements (a covariance under (CP), and reflection positivity) and supply no path. (3) No off-axis structure gives a *provable* gap to `2.5`: unitary (imaginary) `β` gives oscillation not decay; complex `β` breaks RP; volume/anisotropy shift only finite-size, not the `|β|<1/48` radius. Even granted, analyticity transports the *value*, never the *lower bound*. **The 2D continuation reduces exactly to gap + isolation + complex zero-freeness, all open at 2.5.**

**1. Circumventing Pringsheim is possible but empty.** The positive-activity expansion converges for small `|activity|(|β|)` — an *absolute* condition — in a phase-independent `|β|`-disk of radius `1/48`; on the positive axis Pringsheim places *a* singularity at `1/48`, fixing neither its type (pole / branch / natural boundary) nor that `f∞` even has one (`1/48` is the *series'* radius; no-transition says `f∞` has no wall there). If isolated, an off-axis loop around it is *defined*; if a natural boundary (dense Fisher zeros), no continuation exists. Either way nothing is gained: an isolated singularity is dodged without enlarging the `|β|<1/48` region any controlled representation occupies, and no resummation of the divergent positive-coefficient series selects a value at `|β|=2.5` — the Borel ambiguity `∼(Λ/μ)^{2n}` *is* the missing datum (CONTINUUM-ASYMPTOTIC-BEYOND).

**2. Kato: every carried path is the gap.** On `T⁴_L` the Wilson kernel is entire, so `f₁²_L=|a(β)|²` is analytic wherever `λ₁` is simple and isolated (PROOF-TRANSPORT-ANALYTIC). Continuation is path-independent on the domain `Ω`: a complex path `γ:β₀↦2.5` carrying `f₁²` exists **iff** `Ω∋γ`, i.e. gap + isolation at every `γ(t)`, crucially at `γ(1)=2.5`. As `Ω` is open and connected, containing `β₀,2.5` forces it to contain their real segment — the detour needs a *superset* of the real interval's hypotheses. The provably gapped real set is `|Re β|<1/48`; reaching `2.5` puts `2.5∈Ω`, the open (upper) gap. No path has a *provable* gap and reaches `2.5`.

**3. Off-axis candidates buy no positivity.** *Unitary `β=is`:* `T_{is}` is unitary, not a contraction — correlations oscillate, `|⟨O(t)O(0)⟩|` does not decay; a real spectrum but no OS clustering, hence **no gap**. *Complex `β`:* RP needs the real weight `e^{-βA}`, `β>0` (OS); complex `β` makes it a phase and `∫θ\bar{Θθ}\,e^{-S}` is no longer real-`≥0` — there is no reflection-positive complex region to appeal to. *2D `(β,L)` / anisotropy:* these move finite-size corrections and the phase line only; the `1/48` radius is `V`-independent and the `T=0` anisotropic gap is the *same* dynamical gap.

**4. `Z_O²` and the KL pole are renamings, not routes.** `f₁²(μ)=Z_O²f₁²(μ₀)` (`Z_O²>0`, a *square*) is *exact on the real coupling line* **given (CP)** (INVENT-TRANSPORT-INEQ) — a covariance of the value, not a `β`-continuation. Continuing `Z_O` off-axis needs the perturbative β-function, whose IR renormalon at `u=2/β₀` *is* the `O(Λ²)` ambiguity; and (CP) = single phase = the gap/continuum wall. KL `ν≥0`, `f₁²=ν({m₁²})≥0` is RP at real `β`; at complex `β`, `ν` need not be positive, so the modulus-square — the sole reason the residue cannot go negative — is lost.

**Sharpest line.** Pringsheim's singularity is on the positive real axis and *is* avoidable in `ℂ`; avoiding it is worthless because the controlled region is a `|β|`-disk, not a half-plane, so complex phase reaches no larger `|β|` — and the physical `f₁²` may not even have the wall. The obstacle was never a singularity to dodge but a *missing representation* at `2.5`; Kato makes it exact — a complex path carrying `f₁²` exists iff gap + isolation hold into `2.5`, and path-independence means the real interval already demands, and is the least of, those hypotheses. **The 2D continuation is not an escape from the gap; it is the gap (+ isolation, + complex zero-freeness) written in `ℂ`** — and even then analyticity transports the *value*, never the *lower bound*.
