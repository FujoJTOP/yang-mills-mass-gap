# TRANSMUTE-STRING-SPECTRUM — can the flux-tube *excitation* spectrum force a `0⁺⁺` mass `≥ c√σ`, non-circularly?

> 2026-11-23 · Flux-tube / string-excitation angle on the `0⁺⁺` channel. **判词 (verdict).** The closed flux tube *does* carry a `0⁺⁺` quantum-number mode — its ground state and its breathing/dilaton (trace) mode are `0⁺⁺`, so **(a) "contains a `0⁺⁺` mode" is true in form**. But that mode *is* the loop's **shrink modulus**, and the Nambu–Goto energy `E(L)=σL−(D−2)π/(6L)` has **no interior minimum**: monotone, cutoff-set, complex below `L_c≈1.45/√σ` (PROOF-EFFECTIVE-STRING). The `√σ`-*stabilized* string is the **charged** torelon/endpoint sector, N-ality≠0, J=1, provably invisible to `tr F²` (PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY); the **neutral** closed string is exactly the shrinkable one — the two desiderata are **mutually exclusive by 1-form superselection**. **(b) fails:** `√σ` is the string's **energy per unit length**, not a gap; the Lüscher excitation spacing is `∼2π/L→0` (gapless transverse phonons), `O(√σ)` only at `L∼1/√σ` where `1/(σL²)∼1` breaks the EFT, and Lüscher's correction is *negative*. String breaking above `2m_g` makes the excitation a **resonance**, and that threshold is `2m_g` — circular. **Score 2/5; Clay 0%. Exact stuck point = a uniform lower bound on the mass of a *neutral, non-shrinkable* closed flux loop = its stabilizing core thickness, a UV datum the EFT lacks = H1/(MS).**

## §1 · What is actually in the spectrum
An open flux tube (static sources) has transverse modes `ω_k=πk/R`; a closed tube (loop length `L`) has `ω_k=2πk/L`. The ground-state Casimir is the Lüscher term `−(D−2)π/(24R)` (open) / `−(D−2)π/(6L)` (closed), universal only as a large-`R,L` asymptotic coefficient (Aharony–Komargodski: universal through `1/L⁵`). The transverse oscillators carry the little-group `SO(D−2)` (`D=4`: helicity `±1` about the axis). A closed loop has no preferred axis, so its ground state and the uniform (trace) oscillator combination are `0⁺⁺` — the stringy "dilaton."

## §2 · Steelman (a) + the one honest bridge (i)
The `0⁺⁺` mode's existence is genuine: a symmetric closed loop's breathing direction is a scalar, and every closed-string tower contains it. There is even a rigorous *dictionary*: tool A3 (Fredholm) reads `f₁²` off `D_O(z)=det(1−zT_O)`, whose "periodic orbits" `Tr T_O^k=Z_{S¹_k×T³}` are exactly **closed time-like flux worldsheets** — the atom and the closed loop share one transfer operator. So the sector question (i) is **not** a quantum-number obstruction: OS reconstruction embeds line-operator states in the *same* `H_phys`, and a state's `J^{PC}` is independent of which operator creates it.

## §3 · Attack (a)+(i): the sector split is *superselection*, not labeling
The bridge that matters is N-ality, and it is conserved. A **contractible** loop is N-ality 0 (neutral, `tr F²`-visible) but **shrinkable**: NG leaves its size unstabilized, so its mass is the core/cutoff, not `√σ`. A **non-contractible** wrap (torelon) is *stabilized* by `σL` but is N-ality `k≠0` — a different superselection sector the neutral local algebra cannot reach (PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY; GKSW: area law = electric `Z_N^{(1)}` unbroken), and its lightest state is `1⁻⁻`, not `0⁺⁺`. Thus **"`√σ`-pinned" and "neutral `0⁺⁺`" cannot be simultaneously attained**: the lightest stabilized string is charged; the lightest neutral string is shrinkable. The mismatch is center-charge superselection — sharper than "1-form vs 0-form operator."

## §4 · Attack (b)+(ii): `√σ` is a density, the Lüscher gap is gapless
`σ` enters as `E≈σL` — energy **per length** — so a *mass* needs a pinned `L`. On a long string (`σL²≫1`) the excitation gap is `2π/L→0`: the transverse modes are **Goldstone phonons** of broken transverse translation, so the excitation sector is *gapless* in the IR. A `√σ`-sized excitation exists only at `L∼1/√σ`, precisely where `1/(σL²)∼1` and the EFT's higher terms are unknowable — no theorem, and the Lüscher correction is *negative* (the wrong sign for a lower bound). The NG closed-string energy has **no interior minimum** and `→0` at `L_c` (PROOF-EFFECTIVE-STRING §c): nothing to bound below.

## §5 · Attack (iii): string breaking makes it a resonance, circularly
Above the two-glueball scale the string decays into glueballs; the excitation becomes a **resonance in the multi-glueball continuum**, not an atom (BS-VARIATIONAL-LOWER; PROOF-DISSOLUTION-SPECTRAL-DENSITY). Below it, it is a gapless phonon. The crossover is the EFT-breakdown window — there is no stable isolated `0⁺⁺` atom in the spectrum. And the breaking threshold is `2m_g`: using it to lower-bound the glueball *assumes* `m_g` — the same circularity as the BS threshold.

## §6 · Score, sharpest line, exact stuck step
**1 nonperturbative PASS · 2 sign-correct lower bound FAIL (gap `∼1/L→0`; `E(L)` unbounded) · 3 non-circular FAIL (`σ_phys>0` continuum open; `2m_g` and core scale both `=H1`) · 4 fails-free-field PASS (free Maxwell: perimeter law, no string) · 5 uniform `Δ≥λ`,`a→0` FAIL (EFT breakdown at `L∼1/√σ`). 2/5.**

**Sharpest line.** The transmutation `m_{0⁺⁺}=c√σ` is measured (Morningstar–Peardon, `c≈3.9`), not derived, because the string *cannot supply its own stabilizing length*: the neutral `0⁺⁺` breathing mode is the shrink modulus, and the `√σ`-stabilized string is charged. The missing object is the closed string's **core thickness** — the scale that pins `L` at `∼1/√σ` — which is exactly the IR scale `Λ`, i.e. the gap itself.

**Exact stuck step.** A uniform-in-`(a,L)` lower bound on the mass of a **neutral, non-shrinkable** closed flux loop — equivalently a *non-EFT* string-core coercivity that fixes `L~1/√σ` — the same `Λ`-datum as `H1`/(MS). Until then the flux tube is a *discriminating* (free-field-failing) object that nevertheless **stops at `√σ` as a density** and never yields a 0-form mass lower bound.
