# ORBIFOLD-KUY — can the Kovtun–Ünsal–Yaffe orbifold/quiver equivalence transfer a gap?

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh-framework probe (not in the 10-method map; Eguchi–Kawai appears only as a negative aside in H1-T8, volume independence in R2/R1).
> **Verdict: new as a route and structurally distinct — it would transfer the gap *from a parent*, not tame the strong IR — but it cannot deliver one, for a crisp reason: nowhere in the chain is there a gapped side to transfer *from*. The only constructive use, Eguchi–Kawai volume independence, re-collapses to the large-N gap plus the volume-uniform continuity theorem (G2 + R2/R4), and additionally restricts to N=∞. New statement, same wall.**

**(a) The equivalence, and what is proven.** Two large-N gauge theories related by a Z_k orbifold projection have identical planar ('t Hooft) expansions — same free energy and same *neutral* single-trace correlators, order by order in perturbation theory (Bershadsky–Johansen 1998; Lawrence–Nekrasov–Vafa). This is "planar equivalence," a perturbative statement. KUY elevated it nonperturbatively *in lattice regularization, at N=∞*: hep-th/0305058 proves it by matching large-N loop (Migdal–Makeenko) equations, **only in the strong-coupling/large-mass phase**; hep-th/0411177 gives necessary-and-sufficient conditions in *any* phase — (1) the projection's discrete symmetry unbroken in the parent, (2) the theory-space permutation unbroken in the daughter. Applying this to SYM (hep-th/0505075): Z_k with k>2 **fails** (chiral symmetry is spontaneously broken in the parent N=1 SYM); only Z_2 survives, with delicate θ/energy mappings.

**(b) Does the projected sector have a gap? Can N=4 + projection give it?** No. "Projection removes the CFT" is false: the vacuum, the stress tensor T_μν (spin-2, exactly conserved, dim 4) and the R-currents of N=4 are all color/flavor singlets, hence Z_k-invariant, hence *survive* the projection. An invariant subspace of a gapless Hamiltonian is still gapless, so the projected sector always contains the massless T_μν — the orbifold daughter of N=4 is another SCFT. Projection can only delete states that transform nontrivially; the massless CFT states are precisely those that do not. The consistency check confirms it: were the equivalence a spectral isomorphism, the confining daughter would inherit the parent's T_μν and be gapless, contradicting its expected gap. Therefore the equivalence is *not* an isomorphism — it equates planar neutral correlators, which the IR gap does not determine. It is simply too weak to see a gap.

**(c) Is a nonperturbative proof possible?** KUY *gave* one (lattice 't Hooft / center symmetry; hep-th/0411177, hep-th/0702021) — but at N=∞ and conditional on unbroken symmetries. Three blocks: (i) it is planar/leading-N; the Clay problem is SU(2)/SU(3), and nothing links m(N=∞) to m(3) (H1-T8, gluing §2.6). (ii) The condition is exactly the center symmetry, which for pure YM *breaks* at small volume — pure-YM volume independence fails (hep-th/0702021); it holds only for QCD(Adj) with periodic adjoint fermions, i.e. not pure YM. (iii) Even when valid it transfers *equivalence at fixed coupling*, not a gap; the gapped side is always the target, never the input.

**(d) What blocks rigor — and does it re-collapse?** The only gap-relevant reading is volume independence: "small weakly-coupled torus is gapped, and the gap is volume-independent at N=∞, hence ℝ⁴ is gapped." This needs the center unbroken at *all* volumes (false for pure YM) **and** a volume-uniform nonperturbative RG/continuity theorem — which R2/R4 identify as *indistinguishable from the mass gap itself*. So it **re-collapses to the large-N gap + the volume-limit theorem (= G2 + R2/R4)**, and adds an N=∞ restriction the direct route does not carry. It is strictly not-better than G2.

**Genuinely new?** As a statement, yes — transfer-from-a-parent is absent from the tried routes, and it cleanly isolates *why* it fails (projection is not a spectral map; the center-symmetry condition is the 1-form channel, so — as in H1-T10 — the rigorous handle is channel-mismatched with the 0⁺⁺ gap). But it moves no wall.
