# PROOF-FLOW-CONVEXITY-TRANSFER — the flow does not convexify the measure: the pushed-forward Hessian keeps the Savvidy sign, and un-smoothing restores it

> **Verdict (判词).** **No.** The Yang–Mills gradient flow does **not** make the flowed measure `μ_t=(Φ_t)_*μ` log-concave in any flow window, and a gap at `t>0` does **not** transfer to `t→0`. One sign fact and one scale fact close the route. **(Sign.)** Pushing the measure forward by the contracting flow re-weights the log-density by the inverse-map Jacobian; to Gaussian order the flowed Hessian eigenvalues transport as `λ↦λe^{2tλ}`. A Savvidy direction `λ_S<0` is *attenuated* to `λ_S e^{−2t|λ_S|}` (toward `0⁻`) but **never flipped**, and at `t→0` returns to the full `λ_S`: smoothing suppresses the Savvidy *magnitude*, not its sign, and **un-smoothing necessarily re-concaves**. **(Scale.)** On the gap-relevant `k→0` marginal the multiplier is `→1` — the flow is the *identity* where the gap lives; its UV attenuation is sign-preserving and irrelevant. **(Transfer.)** Backward Poincaré carries `‖DΦ_t⁻¹‖²=e^{2tλ_UV}~e^{ct/a²}`; the deconvolution symbol `e^{+t|ξ|²}` is unbounded, so un-smoothing costs most exactly on the Savvidy modes. The flow's real theorem is the operator `E(t)` at `t>0` (CONTINUUM-GRADIENT-FLOW-DEFINE), a different object; defining `f₁²(t)` neither needs nor yields log-concavity of `μ_t`.

**1. The flowed action.** `Φ_t` is the time-`t` map of `∂_tB=−∇S_W`, a diffeomorphism of the compact `G^E` for every `t`. With `dμ=Z⁻¹e^{−S_W}dU`, the pushforward density is `Z⁻¹e^{−S_W(Φ_t⁻¹B)}|det DΦ_t⁻¹(B)|`, so the flowed action — whose Hessian sign is the whole question — is `S_t(B)=S_W(Φ_t⁻¹B)−log|det DΦ_t⁻¹(B)|+log Z`.

**2. Linear-transport lemma — sign inviolate.** Freeze a background, linearize: `S_W≈½⟨a,La⟩`, `L=Hess S_W=−D²+2 ad F₀` (Savvidy-indefinite). Then `DΦ_t=e^{−tL}`, `DΦ_t⁻¹=e^{tL}`, the Jacobian is the constant `−t tr L`, and
`Hess S_t=e^{tL}Le^{tL}=e^{2tL}L ⇒ λᵢ(t)=λᵢ e^{2tλᵢ}`
(exact for quadratic `S_W`, one-loop at fixed background). Rigid consequences: `λ>0` stays positive; `λ_S<0` gives `λ_S e^{−2t|λ_S|}<0` for **every finite `t`** — attenuation, never a sign change; `t→0` gives `λᵢ(t)→λᵢ`, restoring the full magnitude. The interaction's correction to this sign sits in the same indefinite `Hess S_W`; controlling it is *exactly* `(★)` (PROOF-G1-BPHZ), so no nonperturbative flip is on offer. **No `t` turns a concave direction convex.**

**3. The gap mode is flow-invariant.** The multiplier `e^{−tλ(p)}→1` on `k₁=2π/L` (PROOF-GRADIENT-FLOW §4), i.e. `e^{2tλ(k→0)}→1`: the coarse Hessian `S_t″≡S_W″`, so the marginal `G″(k→0)` that *is* the gap (PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES, PROOF-COARSE-POINCARE) is untouched. The flow acts only at `k≳1/√t`, attenuating a concavity that no longer controls the IR — neither helping nor hurting where the gap must be proved.

**4. Transfer fails, costing most where concavity lives.** Pushforward keeps Poincaré forward (`μ` gapped `⇒μ_t` gapped, constant improved by `‖DΦ_t‖²≤1`); but the converse `μ_t⇒μ` carries `C_μ≤‖DΦ_t⁻¹‖²C_{μ_t}`, `‖DΦ_t⁻¹‖=e^{tλ_UV}~e^{ct/a²}` — un-flowing is exponential expansion on gradients, equivalently the unbounded heat-deconvolution symbol `1/ĝ_t=e^{+t|ξ|²}` (INVENT-CONVEXIFICATION (a); Tweedie floor `−1/t`). A bound at flow scale `√t` deconvolves to scale `0` at cost `e^{ct/a²}` on the high-frequency modes carrying `−2g|F₀|`. **Un-smoothing re-concaves, and loses most precisely where the non-convexity is.** Since §2 gives no finite-`t` gap to start from, the route fails twice.

**5. Why this is not the operator theorem.** The flow's genuine achievement (CONTINUUM-GRADIENT-FLOW-DEFINE) is the *renormalized operator* `E(t)=¼G²(t)` at `t>0` — a `√t`-smeared interpolator with finite continuum correlator. That is about *one operator's* matrix elements, not the Hessian of `μ_t`; `f₁²(t)=|⟨0|E(t)|G⟩|²` is defined with `μ_t` never log-concave. Conflating the two is the error the "convexity window" rests on. Transfer would need `μ_t` gapped uniformly for small `t>0` with backward cost `o(1)`; the cost is `e^{ct/a²}→∞` and the concavity returns as `t→0`. The block stays the **isolation wall** (`Λ>R̄`, PROOF-ISOLATION).

**Sharpest line.** The flow truly suppresses the Savvidy region — but only its *magnitude* `e^{−2t|λ_S|}`, never its *sign*, on the high-frequency modes while the `k→0` gap mode is an identity. The physical residue is the `t→0` limit, where attenuation vanishes and the sharp concave Hessian returns; to transfer a finite-`t` gap backward one applies `Φ_t⁻¹`, whose expansion `e^{ct/a²}` is largest exactly on the Savvidy modes. The flow gives a continuum *operator*; it does not give a convex *measure* — and un-smoothing necessarily gives the concavity back.
