# CONTINUUM-VARIANCE-DEFECT — is the Savvidy defect ratio `D/S` RG-invariant? Covariant, not invariant; the denominator is H1

> **Verdict.** **No — `a→0` does not bound `D/S`.** `D/S` is not an RG-invariant but an RG-*covariant* ratio: a dimensionless function of the running coupling, `→0` at the Gaussian point and `O(1)` at the gap-relevant IR mode. **(1)** It need not stay at the measured 2%: that number is *one coarse-lattice point* (`β=2.5`, `ξ/a≲0.3`, `k=π/2,π/3`, single `β`) — outside the scaling window, with no `a`-scan. **(2)** `D` *does* renormalize: its leading (Gaussian) divergence cancels exactly since `D|_Gauss≡0`, the residue is a marginal dim-4 insertion of the BRST class `{Tr F², Tr F F̃}` with anomalous dimension `γ_O=−d ln(β/2g)/d ln μ`, so `D` has a well-defined continuum limit (BPHZ core closes) — existence, not sign. **(3)** The UV-numerator/IR-denominator hope is **half true and fails on the denominator**: `D`'s fiber/UV content is AF-suppressed as `a→0`, but `S` is the *IR* object (coarse inverse propagator), and `S≥λ` uniformly **is** `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` **is** H1 **is** the gap. **(4)** The trace anomaly `θ^μ_μ=(β/2g)Tr F²` controls `D`'s *scale* (a positive square `Z_O²`, as for `f₁²` in INVENT-TRANSPORT) but is RG-invariant *existence/aggregate* data: it fixes neither size nor sign of `D<S`. `a→0` bounds nothing; `D<S` at `k→0` is H1 renamed.

## (1) `D/S` is RG-covariant, not invariant
`S` and `D` are both built from the same marginal operator (`Tr F²`, exactly marginal in 4D), so their ratio is a dimensionless RG-covariant number. `D|_Gauss=0`, and near the Gaussian point `D/S=O(g²)→0`; nothing makes it small in the IR. The sole measurement (`D/S≈2%`, `G″≈1/Var` to 2%) is at `β=2.5` where `ξ/a≲0.3` — within one lattice spacing, i.e. *outside* the scaling window — at `k=π/2,π/3`. A genuine `a→0` extrapolation (`β→∞`, `ξ/a→∞`) does not exist and is out of reach (`ξ/a≈7` needs `β≈7.6`, `~10⁴–10⁵` CPU-h, STATUS).

## (2) Renormalization: `D` has a continuum limit
`D=Var(f|0)−E[⟨K^{−1}c,c⟩|0]` cancels the free-field value identically, so the leading `1/a⁴,1/a²` divergences (pure Gaussian) drop out; the residue is `∝ g²(a)·`(marginal log), a dim-4 BRST insertion. `{Tr F², Tr F F̃}` is the complete dim-4 class (PROOF-G1-BPHZ §1) and the scale sum `Σg_j⁴<∞` closes, so `D` transports multiplicatively through the anomaly, `D(μ)=Z_O²D(μ₀)`, `Z_O=(β/2g)(μ₀)/(β/2g)(μ)>0` (INVENT-TRANSPORT). But BPHZ gives existence and `a`-uniformity of the moments, not their sign: the missing ingredient is exactly `(★)` (per-shell positivity, PROOF-G1-BPHZ §3).

## (3) Why the UV/IR split does not decouple the ratio
`D` is fiber-dominated (90–97% of Savvidy negatives in the fluctuation block, W6-BE) and its deepest-UV content dies by `g²(π/a)→0` (asymptotic freedom) — the "UV numerator" is real. But `D<S` is decided at the scale where `S` lives. For a Gaussian marginal `S=1/Var(a)=Γ^{(2)}_T(k)`: gapless ⇒ `Var→∞`, `S→0`; gapped ⇒ `S→m_eff²>0`; hence `S≥λ` uniformly *is* `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` (PROVE-Gpp §5). The conditioned fiber `μ₀=μ(·|a=0)` is the effective measure *at the coarse scale k*, and its Hessian `K` is Savvidy-indefinite at *that* scale — the fast-mode-cannot-gap-the-slow-mode argument of PROOF-IR-H1 §4. A UV bound on `D` therefore cannot bound `D/S` without an independent IR lower bound on `S`. (The provable `S^Φ≥N/12` is the *isolated* Jacobian fiber; coupling in the Wilson Hessian makes `K` indefinite and lets `S` fall to the tracking `1/Var→0`.)

## (4) The trace anomaly controls the scale, not the sign
`θ^μ_μ=(β/2g)Tr F²`, `β(g)=−b₀g³/(16π²)`, `b₀=11N/3`, gives `D` the anomalous dimension `γ_O=−d ln(β/2g)/d ln μ=(11N/24π²)g²+O(g⁴)`. So `D∝g²(π/a)→0` as `a→0`: the exact UV running of the condensate `⟨Tr F²⟩`. But (i) the anomaly acts identically on `S`'s Gaussian part (same marginal class), so `Z_O²` cancels in `D/S`; (ii) it is RG-invariant *aggregate* data — "an anomaly forces a state to *exist*, not to be *heavy*" (GAP-IN11 §4), "RG-invariance buys constancy, not nonvanishing-at-the-edge" (INEQ-R7). It fixes `D`'s running, never the `O(1)` strong-coupling competition `D<S`.

**Sharpest line.** `D/S` is a ratio of same-class marginal insertions, hence RG-*covariant*: `→0` in the UV (`a→0` at fixed high scale, AF) and `→O(1)` at the IR mode `k→0` where the gap lives. The numerator `D` is UV and physically bounded; the denominator `S` is IR and its positivity *is* H1. Taking `a→0` suppresses the UV numerator and proves nothing about the IR competition; `D<S` at `k→0` is H1 renamed, not evaded.
