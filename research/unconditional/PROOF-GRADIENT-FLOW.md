# PROOF-GRADIENT-FLOW — Lüscher flow: a rigorous UV operator, the wrong half for the gap

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict. The 4D Yang–Mills gradient flow is a genuine, rigorous UV regularization: it *defines* the `0⁺⁺` interpolator `E(t)=¼G^a_{μν}(t)G^a_{μν}(t)` as a renormalized local operator with finite separated-point correlators at `t>0` (Lüscher–Weisz). It gives no gap.** The correlator's exponential decay in Euclidean *separation* **is** the spectral representation of the gap, and its rate is `t`-independent for every `t>0` — the flow moves only the overlaps `c_n(t)=⟨0|E(t)|n⟩`, never the masses `m_n` (spectral theorem on `T=e^{−aH}`). So "decay at `t>0` ⇒ gap" restates the gap, and "transfer `t→0`" is vacuous. As a flowed LSI it splits on the exact fiber/coarse decomposition into a *provable* high-band part (H2/L3, banked) and an *unchanged* `k→0` part that **is** H1. **Wrong half, sharply — its linearized multiplier `e^{−tλ_a(p)}` is the identity on the mode that sets the mass (P1-L0); the exact missing estimate is H1, on the IR side.**

**1. The genuine theorem (UV).** For finite `t>0` the flow `∂_tB=−D^*F(B)` is a well-posed deterministic smoothing map (quasilinear parabolic; on the lattice an ODE with global solutions by compactness of `G` — continuum 4D bubbling is irrelevant at finite `t` and fixed measure). The flowed field carries smoothing radius `√t`; short-distance singularities are regulated at scale `√t`. After the `t`-dependent vacuum/contact subtraction `⟨E(t)⟩` is finite — its small-`t` limit is the renormalized `⟨tr F²⟩` (small-flow-time expansion) — and `⟨E(t,x)E(s,y)⟩_c` is finite for `x≠y`. Rigorous at all orders in perturbation theory (Lüscher–Weisz): the one clean way to define a renormalized `0⁺⁺` operator — the operator half G1/OS reconstruction and CONDITIONAL-LATTICE-GAP take as given.

**2. Decay ≠ a gap — the rate is `t`-independent.** On any RP theory (OS'78), `⟨E(t,x)E(t,0)⟩_c=∫_{[m₁²,∞)}ρ_t(ds)\,e^{−√s\,|x|}`, `ρ_t(ds)=Σ_n|c_n(t)|²δ_{m_n²}(ds)`: support on the *same* spectrum `{m_n}` for every `t>0`, the weights `c_n(t)` the only `t`-dependence. Hence (i) a one-`t` decay bound pins the `0⁺⁺` threshold `m_1`, not the full gap (all sectors); and (ii) — decisive — the rate cannot be *produced* by choosing `t`; it is already fixed or never there. The true implication `[G1 ∧ OS ∧ gap m] ⇒ |⟨E(t,x)E(t,0)⟩_c|≤C(t)e^{−m|x|}\ ∀t>0` runs the wrong way: a *consequence* of the gap, not a route. Only unconditional cases: `β<1/48` (SZZ), where the measure already has LSI; and finite box, where `Δ_L≈1/a` (LATTICE-TM-GAP) is an *upper* bound on `Δ_{ℝ⁴}` (P4 §2).

**3. Flow-LSI is circular and splits wrong.** `μ_t=(Φ_t)_*μ`. LSI(μ) ⇒ LSI(μ_t) (pushforward through the Lipschitz flow), but LSI(μ_t) ⇏ uniform LSI(μ): `Φ_t^{−1}` expands high frequencies at rate `~e^{ct/a²}`. And LSI(μ) *is* the gap (H1-T1/OB-1 — circular). The positive half is again the fiber: the Dirichlet multiplier `e^{−tλ_a(p)}` conditions `k≳1/√t` — that is `Λ_UV`/H2, already banked. The coarse half `k→0` is untouched; no flow manufactures it.

**4. Which half — quantitative.** With `λ_a(p)=(4/a²)Σ_μsin²(p_μa/2)`, linearization multiplies each mode by `e^{−tλ_a(p)}`: `→0` for `k≳1/√t` (smoothed), but for the gap mode `k_1=2π/L`, with `√t` fixed and `L→∞`, `e^{−t(2π/L)²}→1`. The flow acts on scales `≳1/√t` and is the **identity** on `k_1`, where H1/W6's `λ_min=(4/a²)sin²(πa/L)` lives. It is the *same* semigroup as the Polchinski/OU evolution `P_t=e^{−tL_0}`, whose volume-uniform gap P1-L0 already proved fails — exactly by masslessness, the G2 IR sector. The flow is the gradient twin of W6/BE (H1-T6: rate `=−Hess S`, Savvidy sign) and the zero-noise limit of stochastic quantization (H1-T1: fictitious-time gap ≠ mass gap): strictly further from the measure.

**5. What it buys.** A rigorous renormalized `0⁺⁺` interpolator with `t`-independent mass; a clean estimator for the H1 sign test (`G″(0)≈−0.165`) and for `m_{0⁺⁺}` (lattice APE smearing *is* the flow — LATTICE-TM-GAP's `Δ`). It improves the *measurement* of `f₁²=ν({m²})`, not its proof — that needs binding/isolation `Λ>R̄` (EDGE-f₁²), not regularization.

**Exact missing estimate.** Uniform in `(a,L)`: `⟨G″(0)v,v⟩≥λ>0` on coarse links — equivalently `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ`, the blocked-SLC H1 / uniform coarse Poincaré. IR/coarse half; flow-invariant.

**Sharpest line.** The gradient flow is the cleanest UV statement in the corpus — it builds the operator and renormalizes the contact terms — and the empty IR one: a spectral measure whose support is fixed, and a multiplier that is the identity on the single mode that sets the mass. It hands you `E(t)` and withholds every number you would feed it; the decay rate is H1, and H1 is exactly what the flow cannot move.
