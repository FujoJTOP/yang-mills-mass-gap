# ABELIAN-PROJECTION — abelian projection / dual superconductivity made rigorous

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh **construction** probe, set against `H1-T10-dual` (which judged "seek a dual"). **Verdict: this is the most *concrete* candidate in the dual family — it names the gauge, the \(U(1)^{N-1}\) fields, the projected monopoles, the condensate — but it is not a *rigorous* construction and it does not reach the same-channel \(0^{++}\) gap.** Every step bottoms out in three missing pieces: a Gribov-safe gauge-invariant definition (whose only shadow is the 't Hooft-loop perimeter law \(=\) `GAP-G2-magnetic`, open); a rigorous gauge-invariant monopole-condensate order parameter in 4D non-Abelian (nonexistent); and the UV\(\to\)IR RG theorem. Even granting all three, the dual-Meissner mass is a spin-1 / \(\sqrt\sigma\) scale in the 1-form *confinement* channel — cross-channel, exactly as T10/B1.

## 1. The construction, stated
't Hooft (1981) abelian projection. Fix a gauge by diagonalizing an adjoint color operator \(\mathcal X(x)\) — e.g. maximal-abelian gauge (MAG; Kronfeld–Laursen–Schierholz–Wiese 1987), \([\mathcal D_\mu,\mathcal X]=0\) — leaving the maximal torus \(U(1)^{N-1}\subset SU(N)\) as residual symmetry. Off-diagonal gluons become \(U(1)^{N-1}\) charged matter; where two eigenvalues of \(\mathcal X\) coincide the projection is singular and hosts a **color-magnetic monopole** worldline, Dirac-quantized by \(\pi_1(U(1)^{N-1})\). 't Hooft's hypothesis: these monopoles are the IR variables, and their **condensation** (Mandelstam 1979 dual-superconductor picture) confines — a dual Meissner effect squeezes electric flux into a tube.

## 2. (a) Rigorous projection despite Gribov?
No single global gauge does it: by Singer (1978) the non-Abelian quotient has no global continuous section. 't Hooft's projection is local/topological — monopole positions move with the gauge choice — and "abelian-projection independence" is **conjectured, unproven**. A gauge-invariant reformulation does exist *in statement*: dual Meissner \(\iff\) magnetic flux confined to a tube \(\iff\) **'t Hooft-loop perimeter law** \(V(C)\sim e^{-m\,\mathrm{Perim}(C)}\). But `GAP-G2-magnetic` proved this equivalence and showed the perimeter law *is* the open 4D magnetic-screening theorem. So (a) folds into (d).

## 3. (b) Is monopole condensation provable — a rigorous order parameter?
Not in 4D. A condensate \(\langle\phi_{\rm mon}\rangle\neq0\) breaks magnetic \(U(1)\); by Elitzur a *local* order parameter vanishes, and non-Abelian monopoles carry color, so they are not gauge-invariant objects outside a fixed gauge — the naive order parameter is ill-defined. The one **rigorous** instance is 3D compact \(U(1)\) (Polyakov 1977; Göpfert–Mack 1982: monopole plasma \(\Rightarrow\) area law + gap). But 4D compact \(U(1)\) has a *Coulomb* phase at weak coupling (Guth 1980; Fröhlich–Spencer), so condensation is not even generic in the Abelian 4D case. Abelian/monopole dominance (Ezawa–Iwazaki 1982; Suzuki; Greensite) is lattice evidence, not a theorem.

## 4. (c) Same-channel \(0^{++}\) gap, or confinement-only?
**Confinement-only, cross-channel.** Dual Meissner masses the *dual* field: in 4D a massive 2-form \(\equiv\) Proca vector (spin-1, 3 dof), mass \(\sim\sqrt\sigma\) — structurally not the \(0^{++}\) scalar (T10 (ii)). It lives in the 1-form centre channel (`B1`; confinement §2.1), which does not imply the neutral gap \(W1_0\). Lattice \(m_{0^{++}}/\sqrt\sigma\approx3.5\)–\(4\) (Morningstar–Peardon; GL7) shows two scales. The only same-channel dual is 3D compact \(U(1)\) (photon \(=\) lightest state) — wrong dimension.

## 5. (d) Exact missing step
A **gauge-invariant, Gribov-safe monopole-condensate order parameter for 4D non-Abelian YM, plus a theorem that its nonvanishing persists to the continuum** — equivalently uniform control of \(V(C)\le K^{|C|}e^{-m\,\mathrm{Perim}}\) at \(\beta\to\infty\). Same G2 UV\(\to\)IR wall (Savvidy \(\beta\approx2.47\)); SW \(N{=}2\) (Seiberg–Witten 1994) is non-OS and SUSY.

## Verdict — construction vs T10's "seek a dual"?
**More constructive in spirit, not in rigor.** T10 said "seek a dual"; this route *builds* a candidate — definite fields, monopoles, condensate — the closest thing to a mechanism one can write down. But it terminates at the identical wall: Gribov forces the gauge-invariant statement back to the open 't Hooft-loop theorem, condensation lacks a 4D order parameter, and success pays out in \(\sqrt\sigma\) (confinement), never in the \(0^{++}\) residue \(f_1^2\). It constructs a *confinement mechanism*, not the Clay gap.
