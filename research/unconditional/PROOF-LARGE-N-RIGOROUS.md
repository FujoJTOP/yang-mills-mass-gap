# PROOF — Rigorous large-N: master field, loop equations, and the gap

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh route probe, **distinct from the Eguchi–Kawai volume-reduction verdict** (no one-site reduction, no center requirement — the limit is built on the lattice by loop equations + concentration). **Verdict: rigorous large-N does give a mass gap — but the *strong-coupling* one (cluster expansion: Osterwalder–Seiler; Nissim adds U(N), known for SU(N)/SO(N)) — the easy finite-N gap. It does NOT give the continuum gap: the master-field / loop-equation construction (Chatterjee; Jafarov) lives at small fixed β, proves an area-law bound and the closed Makeenko–Migdal equation but claims no gap, and the equation + positivity do not force decay at physical coupling. Transfer N=∞→3 has no theorem. Missing: a weak-coupling (continuum 't Hooft) large-N construction with loop-equation decay, stable at N=3.**

**(a) What rigorous large-N proves.** Chatterjee (CMP 366 (2019) 203; arXiv:1502.07719), any d: ∃β₀(d)>0 with |β|≤β₀ ⇒ Wilson loops ⟨W(C)⟩_N converge, jointly with Λ↑ℤ^d, to an absolutely convergent sum over lattice string trajectories — a proven gauge/string duality; SU(N) is Jafarov arXiv:1610.03821; the 1/N **asymptotic** expansion is Chatterjee–Jafarov arXiv:1604.04777. Corollaries: factorization (deterministic master field, by concentration of loop observables), an **area-law upper bound** in the 't Hooft limit, and the finite-N **master loop (Makeenko–Migdal) equation**, which at N=∞ closes. β is *fixed small* — a 't Hooft-scaling limit at **large λ (strong coupling)**, where the character/string expansion converges. The **strong-coupling mass gap** is separate and standard (Nissim arXiv:2510.22788 for U(N), pre-existing for SU(N)/SO(N)). The continuum needs λ(1/a)→0, β→∞ — the opposite end, uncovered. (2D is exactly solved but spectrally gapless; Cao–Chatterjee arXiv:2111.12813 is 3D.)

**(b) Do the loop equations + positivity force the gap?** No. "Exact *linear* equations" holds only at finite N, where the linear tower never closes (2-pt sourced by 3-pt, …). At N=∞ it closes but becomes **quadratic** (factorization: ⟨W(C₁)W(C₂)⟩_c → W(C₁)W(C₂)) — a nonlinear PDE on loop space with boundary W(∅)=1. Obstructions: (i) no 4D weak-coupling existence/uniqueness (the strong-coupling series *defines* the solution; elsewhere it is the unbuilt master field); (ii) an area-law *upper* bound + reflection positivity does not lower-bound a Källén–Lehmann atom — the free massless field is reflection-positive and gapless, so "universal structure + positivity ⇒ decay" is false; (iii) clustering is a dynamical property of the solution, not of the equation. The proven logic runs *backwards*: center + exponential decay ⇒ confinement (Chatterjee arXiv:2006.16229) — gap is hypothesis, not output.

**(c) Transfer N=∞→3.** No theorem. m(N)=m_∞+c/N²+… is an **asymptotic** genus series — for each k a remainder O(N^{−k−1}), *no* bound at N=3 (correction c/9, uncontrolled). Nothing forbids a gap to vanish at ∞ and reappear at 3; the N=∞ value is not a rigorous limit at physical coupling. At strong coupling both ends have gaps — agreement only in the easy regime. Empirically masses are O(1) in N at fixed λ, but that is data, not a bound (H1-T8 §4, SURVEY NP-6: physical λ_G lies outside the strong-coupling radius).

**Sharpest line.** Rigorous large-N solves the *master field at strong coupling*, and its gap is the cluster expansion's, not the loop equation's; the equation + positivity do not force decay at physical coupling, and the asymptotic 1/N series plus disjoint regimes block transfer to SU(3). The exact missing theorem is the **weak-coupling (continuum 't Hooft) large-N construction together with exponential decay of the reflection-positive solution of the 4D Makeenko–Migdal equation, uniform in a,L and stable at N=3** — the unbuilt master field plus the gap, in loop language.
