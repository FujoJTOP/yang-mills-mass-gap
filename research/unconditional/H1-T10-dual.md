# H1-T10-dual — higher-form / magnetic dual: is the 4D gap a manifest Meissner mass?

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh-framework probe on H1. **Verdict: no rigorous same-channel 4D dual exists; the rigorous duals that make a gap manifest are channel-mismatched.** The dual-superconductor Meissner mass is the magnetic image of the electric string tension √σ (cross-channel, = B1), not the 0⁺⁺ glueball mass — m_{0⁺⁺}/√σ ≈ 3.5–4 ≠ 1 (GL7). The only rigorous same-channel computable dual — dual-photon Debye mass = gap — is 3D compact U(1) (Polyakov). Its 4D non-Abelian lift is exactly the missing theorem.

**Higher-form duals, rigorously.** 4D SU(N) has a 1-form Z_N center symmetry (GKSW 2015); the 't Hooft algebra makes confined/deconfined ↔ perimeter/area law of the 't Hooft loop, rigorous on the lattice (Osterwalder–Seiler 1978; SZZ 2204.12737). But that is the 1-form (confinement) channel — B1's cross-channel object, logically independent of the 0⁺⁺ gap (confinement §2.1). Higher-group/2-group with θ constrains *phases*; SymTFT (Freed–Moore–Teleman; Kaidi–Ohmori–Zheng) gives phase rigidity, never a mass.

**BF, BF+Higgs, dual superconductor.** Pure BF, S=(i/2π)∫B∧F, is a TQFT: no propagating d.o.f.; E=0 with topological degeneracy — not a Wightman gap, cannot manifest a dynamical 0⁺⁺. Higgsing (monopole condensate) gives the dual 2-form a Meissner mass. Three failures: (i) the 4D YM ↔ BF+Higgs map is unproven (no rigorous 4D interacting construction, G1; SW is N=2, non-OS, conjectural — W3-adjacent-4D); (ii) **a massive 2-form in 4D is dual to a Proca vector — spin-1, three d.o.f. — so its Meissner mass is structurally ≠ the 0⁺⁺ scalar**; (iii) that mass is ~√σ, the string scale. So "dual superconductor made rigorous" would prove confinement (σ>0), not the gap — cross-channel, not the intended same-channel rescue.

**The only rigorous same-channel dual.** 3D compact U(1): monopole plasma → dual-photon Debye mass = the gap; the photon *is* the lightest state (Polyakov 1977; Göpfert–Mack 1982). Same-channel, computable, rigorous — wrong dimension/symmetry. 3D YM–Higgs reaches only local time (CCHS); pure 3D open.

**Why "exact duality ⇒ manifest mass" is circular.** An exact duality is an isomorphism, so it preserves the full spectrum and hence every mass. It makes m_{0⁺⁺} computable only if the dual sits in a controlled (weak) regime; proving the dual controlled *is* constructing/controlling the strongly-coupled theory = G1+G2. A same-channel dual mass that is g-continuous also hits SCHUR-CONVEX (returns an upper bound); the Meissner mass evades that but must first exist as a theorem.

**Sharpest line / absence.** I do not claim a global no-go for all conceivable duals. I prove the available ones fail by channel (BF/dual-superconductor → √σ, a spin-1 scale), by construction (SW/BF+Higgs → unproven 4D), or by dimension (Polyakov → 3D Abelian). The structural reason: 4D magnetic duality maps the electric *string*, not the electric *scalar* — so the manifest mass is the confinement scale, and the same-channel computable gap remains H*.
