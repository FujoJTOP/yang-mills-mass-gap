# FORGE5-FPACTION — the Hasenfratz–Niedermayer perfect action: scale-invariance transports the gap, and "classical perfectness" *reproduces* the Savvidy negative mode

> 2026-11-23 · FORGE5 probe: a **renormalisation-group fixed point / perfect action** whose scale-invariant spectrum makes the coarse-mode Hessian provably `⪰λ`. **判词: cannot satisfy all 5.** Passes **#1**; **#4 only in letter**; **#2 fails structurally**, **#3 and #5 fail**. The decisive irony: the FP action's defining virtue — an *exact* quadratic spectrum (classical perfectness) — is precisely what forces it to reproduce the continuum Savvidy concavity, so "perfect" is the wrong instrument for the *sign*.

## The forge attempt
Let `S_FP` be the block-spin fixed point, `e^{−S_FP[V]}=∫dU e^{−S_FP[U]−T[U,V]}` (HN `hep-lat/9308004`; SU(3) `hep-lat/9506030`). By the fixed-point property the coarse effective action is `S_eff=S_FP` at **every** scale, so the coarse Hessian is `G″=Hess S_FP[V]`, **scale-invariant**. The hoped logic: prove `Hess S_FP⪰λ` at *one* controlled scale; scale invariance transports it to `k→0`, uniform in `(L,a)`. This is the single most elegant possible transport.

## Five-point verdict
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS.** The min/integral block transform and the FP solution are defined without a loop expansion; HN advertise cut-off-independent, nonperturbative perfectness.
2. **Controls SIGN — FAIL (structural).** "Classically perfect" means the *exact quadratic spectrum* equals the continuum operator. On the Savvidy cone that operator is `−D²+2ad F_B` with bottom `λ_min=−g|B|<0` (`PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN`); the FP action reproduces it *exactly*. The FP action's known positives are **(a)** action/reflection positivity `S≥0` (a *value* bound), **(b)** BPS/instanton positivity — SD/ASD are global minima with PSD Hessian — and **(c)** blocking stability (unique min ⇒ stable RG map). None is a Hessian lower bound, and none touches the cone: the Savvidy background is the **non-BPS, `Q=0`, maximally unstable** mixture where the exact spectrum is negative. Any lattice action with a *positive* cone Hessian is an `O(a²)` artifact the FP action is built to **remove**. Perfectness guarantees the wrong sign on the cone.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL.** `λ>0` needs positivity at one scale. The **UV anchor** is the Gaussian fixed point (4D YM is asymptotically free; the only UV fixed point is free): `Hess=−∂²`, zero mode at `k=0` ⇒ `G″=0`, `λ=0`. The **IR anchor** `G″(0)>0` *is* `m_eff²(k→0)>0`, the gap (`PROOF-COARSE-POINCARE`; `PROOF-IR-H1`). Scale invariance gives a *self-consistency relation*, not a number: evaluating it at `k→0` is the gap. There is no interacting fixed point to anchor a mass.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS IN LETTER ONLY.** Pure SU(N), no SUSY ✓; the free-field FP is Gaussian (`f1²=0`, `G″=0`) and returns `λ=0` ✓. But the *same* UV transport returns `λ=0` for YM too — the mechanism is blind to `f1²`, so the pass is vacuous.
5. **Uniform `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL.** No `λ>0` from #3, and the cone sign is negative (#2).

## Exact failure
**#2 is structural, not merely open:** classical perfectness *is* exact agreement with the continuum, and the continuum cone Hessian is indefinite — the FP action cannot improve a genuine continuum instability away. **#3/#5** reduce to a clean dichotomy: the only provable anchor is the Gaussian UV where `λ=0`; the only scale with `λ>0` is the IR `k→0`, which *is* the gap. The FP action converts "prove at one scale" into "prove at all scales" but leaves the only provable scale at `λ=0` and the only positive scale at the gap.

## Sharpest line
The perfect action is defined by having no `O(a^n)` artifacts and the *exact* quadratic spectrum — so it carries the Savvidy negative rung exactly, and its celebrated positivity is BPS positivity (SD/ASD minima), absent on the `Q=0` cone; its stability is the blocking map's, not the coarse Hessian's. `S≥0` is a value statement, and the wall is precisely that the action is *concave where the field is large*. Scale invariance transports a sign it never supplies. **Passes #1; #4 by letter; fails #2 (continuum concavity reproduced by construction), #3 (anchor = Gaussian `λ=0` or the gap), #5 (no uniform `λ>0`).**
