# G2D-ISOLATION-I2 — attacking I2 from kinematic + vNW + RP: the circularity *is* broken for the **pointwise** statement (vNW), but for the **uniform** statement it is not tool-limited — it is **single-scale covariance**, and reduces I2 to `gap ∧ (scale-invariant ratio)`, whose ratio is the same `O(u)` wall as I1

> 2026-11-23 · Head-on attack on isolation `I2`. Target: lightest `0⁺⁺` strictly below the continuum, `Λ>m₁²`. Inputs tried: kinematic no-dissolution, same-channel von Neumann–Wigner, reflection positivity, and the `2m₁` vs `1.5m₁` threshold split. **判词：`2/5`, NOT achieved.** SUB2's "uniform `I2` ⟺ gap" survives — but its *reason* is upgraded from "no tool" to a **structural theorem**: in a one-scale theory every isolation margin is a fixed multiple of `m₁²`, so uniform isolation and the gap are the same inequality up to a universal constant. The **pointwise** statement is genuinely non-circular via vNW (a real partial), and the only scale-**invariant** escape is the ratio `m_{0⁺⁺*}/m₁`, which lands back on the `O(u)`-decoration sign wall = `H*`, no new species.

## 1 · `I2` splits at the two thresholds, both margins `∝ m₁²`
`I2` names two cuts (PROOF-ISOLATION §4.4): **I2a** below the two-glueball cut `2m₁` (margin `θ−m₁=m₁`, the no-dissolution pin), and **I2b** below the *excited* `0⁺⁺` at `m_{0⁺⁺*}≈1.5m₁` (margin `≈0.5m₁`), which is the **operative** threshold — `0⁺⁺` is C/P-blind, so the excited tower (not `2m₁`) fills down under the two-body cut. Both margins scale with `m₁` (`m₁` and `(ρ−1)m₁`, `ρ≈1.5`).

## 2 · Single-Scale Covariance (SSC) — why uniform `I2` ≡ gap, structurally
Pure YM has one dynamical scale: every mass `= c·Λ_QCD`, so `m_{0⁺⁺*} = ρ m₁` with `ρ` β-independent, and every isolation margin `Λ−m₁² = (ρ²−1)m₁²` is a **fixed multiple of `m₁²`**. Hence the candidate theorem `Λ−m₁²≥κ>0` (uniform) is **equivalent**, via `m₁≥√(κ/(ρ²−1))`, to `m₁≥μ` — **the gap**. The circularity is not that our instruments are blind; it is that a one-scale theory has no way, dimensionally or kinematically, to make a margin that is *not* a multiple of the atom mass. The `2m₁` vs `1.5m₁` split confirms it: `2m₁` is universal (kinematic), `1.5m₁` dynamical, but **both** `∝m₁`. To escape, one needs a threshold set by a **different scale** (topological, order-parameter) — absent in pure YM. **Precise correction to SUB2:** without the scale-pin, uniform `I2` is *strictly stronger* than the gap, being `gap ∧ [ρ≥1+c]`; the extra factor is the scale-**invariant** ratio, not a restatement of the gap.

## 3 · The genuine partial — same-channel vNW gives *pointwise* isolation, non-circularly
Ground and excited `0⁺⁺` are **both A1g**: same sector ⇒ von Neumann–Wigner **forbids crossing**. Since strong-coupling cost grading puts `m₁<m_{0⁺⁺*}` (ground lighter), and the level difference is sign-constant on connected `β` (no zero-T transition — confinement is a crossover), `m₁<m_{0⁺⁺*}` holds **at every `β` while both are bound**. This is genuine pointwise I2b **that does not presuppose the gap** — it presupposes only the strong-coupling ordering (a *different* input). It **breaks the circularity for the pointwise statement.** It does **not** reach Clay: (i) the excited `0⁺⁺` dissolves into the continuum at large `β` (leaves the discrete spectrum; Kato isolation fails *there*), so vNW protects only the strong-coupling side; (ii) it gives strict inequality, never a uniform `κ`; (iii) `ρ−1` is set by the unresolved `O(u)` (SUB2), not by vNW.

## 4 · Reflection positivity's actual role — a hypothesis, not a discriminator
RP gives `ν≥0`, hence `C(t)=∫e^{−√st}dν` and log-convexity ⇒ `m_eff(t)↓m₁` **from above** (PROOF-EDGE-E2). This pins the *approach direction* and the atom `f₁²=ν({m₁²})≥0`, but supplies **no threshold lower bound** beyond kinematic. As an isolation tool it is **vacuous**: the free field also has log-convex `C` and satisfies every RP consequence — it fails the **acid test** (satisfied by `f₁²=0`, the free field). RP is an admissible hypothesis, never a lever on `Λ`.

## 5 · The only non-circular residue is the scale-invariant ratio → same wall
`ρ=m_{0⁺⁺*}/m₁≥1+c` is β-invariant, acid-test-clean (free field has no tower to order), and would give uniform I2 from the **already-proven** strong-coupling gap (SZZ) without touching `a→0`. But the cost grading gives only a **weight** ratio `(p'−p)ln(1/u)/a`; resolving it against the `O(u)` self-energy of `λ₁` is verbatim the SUB2 failure (`Cu` vs `Be^{−δt}`) and the I1 residue sign wall (`H*`). **Candidate new species — Landau-ladder rigidity (LLR):** if the `0⁺⁺` tower is Landau-like, `m_n²=(2n+1)m₁²` ⇒ `ρ=√3`, a *symmetry-fixed* ratio. Acid-test-passing (vacuous on free field) but **UNPROVEN** — recorded as conjecture, not theorem.

## 6 · Five-point scorecard (target = uniform I2; NOT 5/5)
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS** (kinematic, vNW, RP: all β, no loops).
2. **Sign-controlling residue/`m_eff²` lower bound — FAIL/hollow.** vNW orders levels; it never bounds the `trF²` residue sign.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL *for uniform* (the target), PARTIAL for pointwise.** vNW genuinely breaks it pointwise; uniform `I2` = `gap ∧ ρ` is circular through the gap half and wall-bound through `ρ`.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS.** Pure YM; free field has no tower (`f₁²=0`) and no ratio to order.
5. **Uniform `≥λ` beyond `1/48` — FAIL.** `O(u)` resolution and `a→0` both untouched.

**Exact missing step.** A β-,`L`-uniform ratio `m_{0⁺⁺*}/m₁≥1+c`, equivalently `λ₂/λ₁≤λ₁^{c}` — the scale-invariant form of SUB2's `Be^{−δt}`. With it + the proven SZZ gap, uniform `I2` closes at strong coupling; without it, `I2` stays the `O(u)`-decoration sign problem = `H*`.

**Sharpest line.** vNW plus kinematics honestly breaks the *pointwise* circularity — two same-sector `0⁺⁺` levels cannot cross, so the atom *is* below the excited cut wherever both are bound, with no gap assumed. But the *uniform* statement Clay needs is not blocked by missing tools; it is blocked by **dimensional analysis**: a one-scale theory makes every isolation margin a multiple of `m₁²`, so "isolated by `κ`" and "gapped by `μ`" are one sentence. The only quantity that escapes scale covariance is the **ratio** `m_{0⁺⁺*}/m₁` — and proving `ρ>1` rigorously is not a new species but the same `O(u)`-decoration sign estimate that is `I1`. **`2/5`; the circularity is refined, not broken.**
