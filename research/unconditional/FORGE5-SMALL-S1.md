# FORGE5-SMALL-S1 — small-S¹ continuity (reflection-positivity + no-level-crossing): can a proven small-L bound survive to ℝ⁴?

> 2026-11-23 · Fresh attack on the dead small-S¹ route. Forge a rigorous adiabatic/continuity machine that carries a sign-controlled lower bound at small S¹ through the RP-decompactification S¹→ℝ⁴, testing the NEW ingredient (reflection positivity + no-level-crossing) rather than the old semiclassics. **判词: 不过。** #4 is the only genuine pass; #1 is heuristic-only; **#2, #3, #5 fail, and they are one object: the decompactification inequality runs backward.** A positive small-L bound is an *upper* bound on the ℝ⁴ gap (min–max / RP–KMS), "no level crossing" cannot keep a gap open, and the controlled centre-symmetric small-L theory is *deformed* YM, not pure YM.

**The forge.** Work on ℝ³×S¹_L, centre-stabilised (double-trace / 't Hooft flux) so monopole-instantons + magnetic bions generate a dual-photon mass `m_σ(L)>0` and a 3D area law. Granting R1's open step, suppose `m_σ(L)≥λ` is *proven* at small L. Transport: (i) RP gives `ν≥0`, `0≤T≤1`; (ii) "no-level-crossing" is invoked so `m(L)` cannot reach 0 — hence λ survives. This is the strongest form of the proposed new continuity.

**Five-point verdict.**
1. **Nonperturbative — method ✓, theorem ✗ (空过).** The bion gas is genuinely nonperturbative (`e^{−S₀}`, `S₀=8π²/g²N≫1`), but *no rigorous small-L SU(N) gap exists*: diluteness is a small parameter, determinants are computed not bounded, the bion is marginal `1/r`, and the only rigorous "monopole gas ⇒ area law" is abelian (Göpfert–Mack; R1 §). The premise "proven at small S¹" is itself unproven — nothing to transport.
2. **Controls SIGN (Savvidy cone) — FAIL.** The sign fixed at small L is the 3D dual-photon (monopole-gas) sign, a confinement-scale object; it never touches the 4D Savvidy-cone Hessian `−D²+2adF_B` (`λ_min=−2g|F|<0`) whose sign is the wall (PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN). Wrong object — and (§ below) wrong direction.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL.** *Undeformed* pure YM on small S¹ is centre-*broken* (GPY one-loop; Gross–Pisarski–Yaffe, RMP **53** (1981) 43): deconfined, `⟨trΩ⟩≠0`. Centre symmetry must be *imposed* by a double-trace/'t Hooft deformation ⇒ the controlled theory is deformed YM / QCD(adj), **not pure YM**. Identifying its large-L limit with pure YM is *adiabatic continuity* — the conjecture, i.e. the gap. Circular.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails for free field — PASS (the sole genuine pass).** Pure-YM monopoles (non-SUSY, not Seiberg–Witten); the free/abelian field on ℝ³×S¹ has no monopole-instanton ⇒ no dual-photon mass ⇒ `m=0`, `f₁²=0`. Acid-clean. Caveat: the controlled object is the confining/dual-photon mass, not the neutral `0⁺⁺` residue — a channel mismatch, on a heuristic seed.
5. **Uniform lower bound `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL, structurally.** See below.

**Exact failure — the inequality runs backward.** By min–max the compactified gap dominates the decompactified one, `m(S¹_L) ≥ m(ℝ⁴)`, because trial states on ℝ³×S¹_L embed as ℝ⁴ trial states; equivalently the RP/KMS spectral representation gives a thermal screening mass `m_scr(β) ≥ m_gap` that is non-increasing as the circle `L=β` grows. So a positive small-L bound certifies only `m(ℝ⁴) ≤ m(L)` — an **upper** bound (P4 §2–3). To floor the ℝ⁴ gap you would need `m(L) ≤ m_gap` *and* a uniform lower bound on `m(L)`: the first is false (min–max), the second *is* the gap (P4 §5, `L*`).

**Why RP + no-level-crossing cannot repair it.** (a) The min–max/RP direction is fixed by structure, not technique. (b) "No-level-crossing" is vacuous for a floor: the bottom state closes the gap without crossing *any* level — it rises into its threshold or the theory goes gapless continuously (second-order, Clay's ¬(iii)); von Neumann–Wigner protects only same-irrep *reordering*, never the bottom remainder (PROOF-TRANSPORT-CONTINUITY §3). (c) The real deconfinement transition *is* a centre-symmetry level crossing (GPY + lattice), which RP permits — Elitzur binds only gauge-*variant* order parameters — so "no crossing" is false on the pure-YM path; deleting the transition requires the #3 deformation. And RP is satisfied by the *free field* with `f₁²=0` (INEQ-R9 §4): the transporting half (RP) is free-field-blind, while the #4-passing half (monopole gas) is exactly the piece that does **not** exist off ℝ³×S¹.

**Sharpest line.** Small S¹ is an upper-bound machine: min–max and reflection positivity both force `m(S¹_L) ≥ m_gap(ℝ⁴)`, decreasing in L — the better you control small L, the more you *cap* the gap, never floor it. No RP/adibatic gesture reverses the direction; the floor is H1, the gap.

**Fails #2, #3, #5 (one object: backward decompactification); #1 heuristic-only; #4 the only pass.**
