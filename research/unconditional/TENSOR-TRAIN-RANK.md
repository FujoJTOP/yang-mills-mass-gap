## §1 The forged object (precise)
Order the `V=4L⁴` link groups into a path (TT) or tree (HT). Unfold `ρ(X)=e^{−S_W−Φ}/Z` after site `k`; the minimal TT rank `r_k` = the unfolding matrix rank = the **Schmidt rank** of `{1..k}|{k+1..V}`. A *certified bound* is `∃R_k` with `r_k≤R_k` for all `k`,`L`. Since `S_ent≤log r_k≤S_ent+1`, a rank bound is an entropy bound controlling the **number** of Schmidt coefficients — nothing more.

## §2 The one true theorem runs the wrong way; and rank⊥gap
The only rigorous rank–gap implication is **Hastings' area law** (JSTAT 2007 P08024): a unique gapped (`Δ>0`) 1D ground state has `S_ent≤c`, hence `r≤e^{c}` **bounded in `L`** — one direction. Its contrapositive, *unbounded rank ⇒ gapless*, is an **upper** bound (certifies `Δ=0`, never `Δ≥λ`). The reverse, *bounded rank ⇒ gap*, is **false**: gapless low-EE states exist (Rokhsar–Kivelson; Eisert–Cramer–Plenio RMP 82,277), and the one `rank⇒gapped` theorem (Fannes–Nachtergaele–Werner) is **parent-Hamiltonian, 1D only** — YM is neither. Even there the parent gap is not monotone in `D`: correlations decay as `(λ₂/λ₁)^{x}` (`λ₂,λ₁` the top eigenvalues of the `D²×D²` transfer matrix) and `λ₂/λ₁→1` is reachable at fixed `D`, so **no `D→λ` bound**. Concretely: a product measure is **rank 1** yet has arbitrary `Var(a₀)`; a rank-2 TT can be infinitely stiff or delocalized. **Rank (a count) and stiffness (a ratio) are independent — the forged relation has no sign.**
