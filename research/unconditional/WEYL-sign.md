# WEYL-sign — numerical sign test of the Weyl-modulus marginal curvature

**Verdict: the sign test PASSES for the radial modulus — the Vandermonde makes $V_{\rm eff}''>0$ everywhere the raw modulus curvature is negative — but the marginal is a *radial* object, and the gauge-invariant negativity that the gap proof actually needs (full transverse Hessian, ~7%, min $-0.9\beta$) lives in directions the Jacobian does not touch. Discriminant does NOT dominate at $\beta=2.5$ raw field strength. Net: positive literal sign, not a gap.** SU(2), $\beta=2.5$, L=4/6, `weyl_sign.py`.

**Exact single-plaquette marginal.** $\mu(\theta)\propto\sin^2\!\theta\,e^{-\beta(1-\cos\theta)}$, $\theta\in[0,\pi]$, Vandermonde $J=|\lambda_1-\lambda_2|^2=4\sin^2\theta$. Raw action curvature $S''=\beta\cos\theta$: $-2.5$ at $\theta=\pi$, negative for $\theta>90°$. Marginal curvature
$$V_{\rm eff}''=\beta\cos\theta+2\csc^2\theta .$$
At $\beta=2.5$: **global min $=+1.378$** (at $\theta=115°$, spread $s=2\sin\theta=1.81$); **$>0$ on the entire Savvidy branch** ($S''<0$). At the VAR-FINAL proxy $S''=-0.165$: $V_{\rm eff}''=+1.84$. $\mu$ peaks at $\theta^*=0.827$ ($s^*=1.47$), $V_{\rm eff}''=+5.39$. Single-plaquette *raw link Hessian* along the modulus is negative on the Savvidy branch (e.g. $-0.62$ at $\Theta=3.04$, exactly $\tfrac{\beta}{4}\cos\Theta$), while the link-coordinate marginal stays $+0.34\!\dots\!+0.9>0$. So the centrifugal term dominates by a factor.

**Lattice ($\beta=2.5$).** Sampled thermalized SU(2) (mean plaquette 0.65, $\theta_{\rm peak}\!\approx\!0.7$). Raw per-link Hessian: **0% negative**, min $+0.08\beta$ (reproduces 04/W4). Raw *modulus* curvature $\beta\cos\Theta$ over sampled plaquettes reaches **$-2.29$** (3.2% on the Savvidy branch); at those plaquettes $V_{\rm eff}''=+1.378$ — **the Jacobian makes $V_{\rm eff}''>0$ exactly where the raw modulus Hessian is negative.** Empirical marginal (KDE on plaquette angles) is **log-concave in bulk** (band curvature $+5$ to $+55$); log-concavity fails only in sparse, low-density tails.

**The catch.** On the *same* configs the full gauge-projected **transverse Hessian is indefinite**: 7.5% negative, min $-0.9\beta$ ($-2.28$) — reproduces W4 ($7.1\%,-0.82$) and MARGINAL. The Vandermonde is the measure on *eigenvalue differences* (the radial modulus); the transverse negatives sit in flag / high-momentum directions it does not convexify. This is precisely IDEA-new's flagged failure. The sign test passes on the modulus but does not give positivity of the SLC object.

**Discriminant locus.** At $\beta=2.5$ raw field strength, measure weight near $\lambda_i\!=\!\lambda_j$ is small: spread $s<0.3$ only **0.7%**, $s<0.5$ **3.3%**, median $s=1.40$, peak $\theta\approx0.7$ — away from the discriminant where $+J$ is strong. So the flagged "discriminant dominates" failure is **not** triggered at this scale. Under strong APE blocking the field relaxes toward the trivial vacuum: APE4 gives 47% within $s<0.3$ — but that is zero field strength ($\theta\!\to\!0$), not a nontrivial abelianized Cartan; the $+J$ weight vanishes there because there is no spread to stabilize.

**Boundary.** Single-plaquette = toy modulus; the full radial marginal is high-dimensional and not a product; 1D log-concavity $\ne$ multi-D Hessian positivity; L=4/6, one $\beta$, sampled. **Confirms the Vandermonde convexifies the modulus direction; does not deliver the gap** — the transverse/flag negativity is untouched.
