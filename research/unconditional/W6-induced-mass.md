# W6 — Induced mass of the k→0 transverse gluon

**Verdict. The mechanism is physically settled; a uniform proof is the gap in different clothes. Collective-Hessian positivity (P3) does not imply it, and the background-field effective potential provably cannot see it.**

**Object.** In P1's variables the free marginal eigenvalue is λ_min=(4/a²)sin²(πa/L)→0. Dynamical mass generation means the *interacting* transverse inverse propagator m_eff²(k) obeys lim_{k→0}m_eff²(k)=m²>0 uniformly in L,a — exactly P1's c=m² and P4's L*.

**(i) Background-field curvature: protected, identically zero.** Γ[A_bar] depends on F_bar alone (Abbott, NPB 185 (1981) 189; PT–BFM correspondence, Binosi–Papavassiliou, Phys. Rept. 479 (2009) 1). Its Hessian at A_bar=0 is the transverse k²; the k=0 curvature vanishes at every loop order, and gauge/BRST invariance forbids a local m²A² term. The one-loop ∝F²ln F² piece is wave-function renormalization. A gluon mass is therefore *nonlocal*: a pole in the vertex feeding the fluctuation self-energy (Schwinger mechanism), not a curvature of Γ. Route (i) is closed.

**(ii) SD/gap equation: a bootstrap, not a theorem.** Cornwall (PRD 26 (1982) 1453): m²(p²)=g²N∫K/(q²+m²(q²)); a nonzero solution needs the kernel IR-divergent as m→0, giving m²~Λ²e^{−c/g²}. Lattice DSE are exact integration-by-parts identities but an infinite hierarchy; a volume-uniform closure does not exist in 4D. PT–BFM supplies the vertex Ward identity and a massless-pole ansatz (Aguilar–Binosi–Papavassiliou, PRD 78 (2008) 025010; Aguilar–Ferreira–Papavassiliou, PRD 105 (2022) 014030), and lattice shows the IR plateau (Cucchieri–Mendes, PRL 100 (2008) 241601; Bogolubsky et al., PLB 676 (2009) 69). This yields a *conditional* theorem — pole residue c>0 ⇒ m²>0 — plus strong evidence, not a proof.

**(iii) Hessian positivity ⇏ mass.** (a) P3/W4 measure the pointwise PᵀHP, not the marginal G″=E[PᵀHP|A′]−Var(Pᵀ∇S|A′); variance only subtracts (MARGINAL.md), so pointwise positivity is compatible with G″<0 (P3's −0.165 is itself only a lower bound). (b) A positive blocked Hessian at b~2a is Laplacian-dominated; a mass at 1/b says nothing about k=0 without an RG step. (c) The k→0 transverse mode is exactly the unprobed one. Seed and mass are logically independent. Foothold: the sign of G″(0) is a binary, computable object via constrained sampling.

**What a proof needs.** (L1) a gauge-invariant m_eff²(0) (spectral measure of a transverse current); (L2) an exact identity bounding the marginal curvature below, uniformly in L,a; (L3) a controlled closure surviving a→0. L2–L3 are the wall. Within this programme m_eff²(0)>0 uniformly *is* the β=2.5 gap (P1, SZZ), so the obstruction is fundamental — yet not vacuous: the sign test is a real experiment, and the 2D, small-S¹, and strong-coupling corners make the mechanism rigorous there.
