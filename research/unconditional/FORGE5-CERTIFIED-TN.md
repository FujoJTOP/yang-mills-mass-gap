# FORGE5-CERTIFIED-TN — certified PEPS / transfer-matrix truncation: the one true-lower-bound mechanism, dead on operator-norm

> 2026-11-23 · FORGE5 target: a PEPS/MPS with **rigorous** (non-statistical) bond-dimension error `‖T−T_D‖≤ε(D)` and a certified `gap(T_D)`, yielding `gap(T)≥gap(T_D)−2ε(D)>0` **uniform in volume**. **VERDICT: cannot satisfy all 5.** The Weyl-truncation mechanism is the *one* place a genuine operator-norm **lower** bound can arise — but it needs (i) a rank-D operator-norm truncation of the transition operator, (ii) `ε` volume-uniform, (iii) a D-independent truncated gap. All three are false for 4D gauge. **Passes (1); fails (2),(3),(4),(5).**

## The attempt
Fix D. *If* `T_D` were a certified operator-norm approximation of the Euclidean transfer matrix `T=e^{−aH}` with `‖T−T_D‖≤ε(D)`, Weyl interlacing gives `gap(T)≥gap(T_D)−2ε(D)`. One D with `gap(T_D)>2ε(D)` then lower-bounds the **exact** gap, no sampling — a true theorem. This is the sole rigorous route to the requested lower bound, and worth recording because its collapse is structural, not numerical.

## Five-point verdict
**(1) Nonperturbative — PASS.** TN contraction carries no `g`/`1/N` expansion; the error bound holds at all β.
**(2) Sign — FAIL.** `T_D` bounds eigenvalues of a *positive* operator built from `Z`; it is sign-blind. OS positivity/PF makes every contraction-derived constraint Schur-concave (`Var≤E|G|²`, Efron–Stein, FORGE5-CONE-VARIANCE), so it upper-bounds — never lower-bounds — `Var(G|0)` or `sign G″`. The Savvidy negative mode (`λ_min=−g|B|`, PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN) is invisible to a positive contraction. No `+δ`.
**(3) Non-circular — FAIL.** The input that would make `ε(D)` useful — a boundary transfer-matrix gap uniform in transverse size — *is* the mass gap re-expressed (H1-T3; KLPG CMP 366,895). And no generic theorem can exist: the gap is undecidable for PEPS parent Hamiltonians (Cubitt–Pérez-García–Wolf, Nature 528,207). The terminal lemma is the gap in disguise.
**(4) Free field — FAIL.** A Gaussian theory is the *easiest* to contract: `gap=m>0` is certified at tiny D. The method therefore succeeds **most cleanly exactly where it must fail**. It also emits no residue — `f₁²=ν({m₁²})` (the edge atom) is not a spectral-energy object, so it cannot enforce `f₁²=0` (EDGE-f1sq-attack; GAP-G2-edge: every PSD/moment functional bounds the edge *above*). Non-SUSY ✓, acid test ✗.
**(5) Uniform lower bound — FAIL, three independent ways.** *(a) Direction:* a finite-volume bound is `gap(T_L)≥λ`; but `Δ_∞=inf_L Δ_L≤Δ_L`, so a lower bound on `Δ_L` is an **upper** bound on `Δ_∞` (PROOF-FSS §1,4). *(b) Rate:* the gap is the exponential **rate** of clustering; certified correlators carry error `ε`, which swamps the `e^{−m|x|}` tail for `|x|≳1/m`, so the rate cannot be lower-bounded without already knowing it. *(c) Volume:* in a 4D contraction per-bond error compounds, so `ε(D)` is **volume-exponential**; D must grow with the transverse area to hold `ε` fixed.

## Why the Weyl mechanism has no legal ε
The physical slice space `L²(G^{E_V})` is infinite-dimensional (Peter–Weyl); a PEPS is a *state*, not an operator-norm truncation of `T`, so no `T_D` with `‖T−T_D‖≤ε(D)` exists. In 1D the MPS transfer matrix *is* finite-D with a controlled spectral gap (Fannes–Nachtergaele–Werner CMP 144,443); in 2D+ injective PEPS gives a unique ground state but **no gap** (KLPG 2019) — injectivity does not bound `ε` — and the 3D+ area law is open (2D frustration-free theorems, Anshu–Arad–Gosset STOC 2022, exclude frustrated gauge theory). The only other certified-lower-bound device, Temple/MacDonald, needs a lower bound on `E_0` (variational supplies only the upper) and is *still* finite-volume.

## Sharpest line
Certified-TN is the one place Weyl turns a finite-D computation into a true operator-norm lower bound — which is exactly why it dies: it needs an operator-norm truncation of an infinite-dimensional transition operator, with a volume-uniform `ε` and a D-independent gap, and **all three are the mass gap in another dress**. **Fails 2,3,4,5; passes 1.**
