# W2 — Can a positive-measure expansion reach β≈2.47?

**Verdict: the crossover obstruction is relocated, not avoided.** Positive bare measure removes the sign problem only at small β; at β≈2.47 the activity is O(0.1–0.4), and the only description valid there (the RG-blocked action) is signed. The "sign problem" and the "small-activity wall" are one obstruction at one coupling, seen from two sides.

**What positivity buys.** The bare Wilson measure is positive, so its character/polymer expansion has non-negative surface weights — a genuine, sign-free convergent cluster expansion at small activity (Osterwalder–Seiler, Ann. Phys. 110 (1978) 440; Seiler, LNP 159 (1982); Kotecký–Preiss, CMP 103 (1986)).

**Double-edged — no cancellation loophole.** Because weights are non-negative, there is no sign cancellation to exploit: the convergence boundary is a genuine real-activity barrier (Pringsheim-type), and Borel/naive resummation meets a positive-axis singularity (the renormalon mechanism, cf. T4). Crossing that barrier by reorganization requires sign-changing weights — i.e. the blocked/Savvidy action.

**The wall is far below 2.47.** Best rigorous radii: |β|<1/48≈0.021 for SU(N), d=4 (Shen–Zhu–Zhu, arXiv:2304.13917); Borgs gives an equally small explicit β_c (Nucl. Phys. B261 (1985) 455; J. Stat. Phys. 47 (1987) 867). Reaching β=2.47 (λ≈7.3) needs activity O(0.1–0.4) — ~90–120× beyond; refinements gain only ≈1.3×. Strong-coupling series are known to have finite radius below the crossover (Drouffe–Zuber, Phys. Rep. 102 (1983)).

**Direct gauge-invariant expansions do not help.** Surface sums are exact for every β>0 (Cao–Park–Sheffield, arXiv:2307.06790) but give no decay control at β≈2.47, where the surface sum is outside its convergence region. Rigorous decay also exists at *weak* coupling (Adhikari–Cao, arXiv:2202.10375), so the intermediate β≈2.47 regime is uncovered from both sides. No rigorous bare↔blocked β bound exists at the crossover (Balaban-type RG, itself open).

**Sharpest statement.** A positive scheme at β≈2.47 would have to prove activity/surface-expansion convergence with a radius ~10²× the proven one — controlling gauge-surface entropy at O(1) activity. No known technique does; positivity blocks resummation across the radius, and the sole route past it — blocking to a weaker effective action — re-imports the Savvidy negativity. Plausibility: very low.
