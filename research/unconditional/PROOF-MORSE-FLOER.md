# PROOF-MORSE-FLOER — S_W as a Morse–Bott functional on 𝒜/G: topology, not the gap

> 2026-11-23 · Sixth-species probe on request (topology / instanton count). **判词 / Verdict: TOPOLOGY, not the gap.** `S_W=(1/2g²)∫Tr F²` on `𝒜/G` is **Morse–Bott**, not Morse: its finite-action critical set is the vacuum plus the non-compact instanton moduli, **all with PSD Hessian** (BPS). Its Morse/Floer invariants bound **critical-point counts by topology** (Morse numbers ≥ Betti numbers of `𝒜/G`; Donaldson/Floer/Seiberg–Witten classes of the 4-manifold), **metric- and coupling-independent**. The gap is a **coupling-dependent spectral** object of the measure `e^{−βS_W}`. They decouple: the critical equation `F=±*F` carries no coupling, and the Hessian at `F=0` is the **free** Maxwell operator for every `g`. The route fails the acid test at the vacuum and is **blind to the Savvidy wall, which is not on the critical locus.**

**1. The functional, the critical manifold — and why "saddle" is wrong.** Critical points solve `D_A^*F=0`, `DF=0` ⇒ `F=±*F` plus `F=0` [BPST; Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer]. These are **manifolds**, non-compact and scale-flat on `ℝ⁴` (the dilatation mode is a zero eigenvalue), so `S_W` is Morse–**Bott**; the gradient flow is the Yang–Mills flow, whose **bubbling** [Uhlenbeck] forbids a finite-dimensional Morse complex, and on `ℝ⁴` the instanton number `Q=∫TrF∧F̃/8π²` is *continuous*, not a discrete label. The rigorous theories are 3-manifold Floer [Floer 1988] and compact-4-manifold Donaldson theory [Donaldson 1983]. The finite-action critical points are exactly the **PSD locus** — vacuum (`Hess=−Δ⪰0`) and BPS instantons (`Hess⪰0`, kernel = moduli) — local **valleys**, not saddles. The **Savvidy negative** modes sit at *non-critical* backgrounds (`D_A^*F≠0`), **off** the Morse-critical set: Morse/Floer cannot see the wall at all.

**2. What the invariants bound.** Morse inequalities `c_k≥b_k` bound a **count** by a **Betti number**, and `Σ(−1)^k c_k=χ`. Floer/Donaldson/Seiberg–Witten are invariants of the 4-manifold, unchanged under metric and (for `g>0`) coupling [Witten 1994]. A "Floer spectrum" is the spectrum of a **topological** operator; relating it to the Hamiltonian needs a supersymmetric **topological twist** [Witten 1988] or self-duality, neither of which pure non-SUSY SU(N) has (PROOF-NONINVERTIBLE-SYM). Absent a duality the spectra are unrelated: no Floer eigenvalue bounds `m_{0⁺⁺}` or `f₁²`.

**3. Acid test — fails.** (a) At the vacuum `F=0`, `Hess S_W=−D²=−Δ`, *identical* to free Maxwell for every `g`, with `λ_min(k→0)=0`; the Morse data at the minimum returns the **free, gapless** spectrum for **both** free and interacting theory. (b) The only distinguishing data — the instanton manifold — is coupling-free topological, so no order parameter for the coupling-dependent gap. The tool is **zero for all**: it does not exclude `f₁²=0`, `G″=0`; it returns them.

**What it buys.** A rigorous *derivation* of the existing obstruction: Morse–Bott makes the BPS scale modulus an exact `Hess S_W=0` direction, so `E[H]=0` there and `G″(0)|_moduli=W−Var(G)` with `Var(G)=O(1)` (flat action, wandering force) — the collective-mode localization of GAP-G2-mass (c). It *locates* the wall (off the critical locus, in the Savvidy-indefinite region and along the BPS moduli) and gives it an index; it supplies no inequality across it, and the crossing is H1 (`W>Var−E[H]`, PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES).

**Sharpest line.** Morse/Floer is the right theory of the wrong object: it computes the topology of `𝒜/G` and of the 4-manifold from a *coupling-free* critical equation, while the gap is the *coupling-dependent* measure. At the vacuum its Hessian is the free operator (gap 0 for everyone); at the moduli it certifies flatness (the localization wall); and the Savvidy concavity that *is* the wall is not critical, so it lies wholly outside the Morse complex. It derives the obstruction exactly and crosses it by zero distance — the same H1 wall, one categorical level up.
