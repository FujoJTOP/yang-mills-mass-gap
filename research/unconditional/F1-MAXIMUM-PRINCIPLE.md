## §1 The device — value comparison ⇒ curvature floor (contact)
Let `V_eff=−log p` and `ψ` be `C²`, with `V_eff(a)≥ψ(a)` on a neighborhood of `0`, `V_eff(0)=ψ(0)`, and `V_eff′(0)=ψ′(0)=0` (the latter is symmetry of the reflection-symmetric mode, not an assumption). Then `f=V_eff−ψ≥0`, `f(0)=0` ⇒ `0` is a minimum ⇒ `f″(0)≥0` ⇒ **`V_eff″(0)≥ψ″(0)`**. So one comparison on *values* yields the curvature floor — no `V_eff⁗`, no derivative tower. This is the one real advance over E1-FRG §4: the open "curvature not closed" is bypassable at the value level.

## §2 Why the barrier is *exactly* H1
`V_eff≥ψ ⟺ p≤e^{−ψ}`. With `ψ(0)=V_eff(0)`, `ψ′(0)=0`, `ψ″(0)=λ>0`, the statement is: the coherent marginal is dominated by a Gaussian of variance `≤1/λ` on the zero mode — i.e. `Var(a₀)≤1/λ`, i.e. `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ`. The comparison input **is** the variance bound. E2-INFORMATION says the same from the entropy side (`Gaussian extremality ⇒ free field is the maximizer; no Gaussian ceiling separates gapped from gapless without inserting `Var` — circular`). So "construct a positive-curvature supersolution" and "bound `Var(a₀)` above" are one inequality written twice.

## §3 The Savvidy "subsolution" is not a function
The instruction "treat the Savvidy cone as a subsolution" is not well-posed: the cone is a *region of the fiber* where `H_ff=−D²+2adF_B` is indefinite, not a function `ψ(a)` solving the marginal PDE. The natural Gaussian subsolution (one-loop `V_eff`) is a **Schur complement over an indefinite Hessian** — undefined on the negative direction that sits *on* the `k→0` slice (PROOF-BORN-OPPENHEIMER; PROOF-COARSE-POINCARE (a)(iii)). Worse, in the exact flow the diffusion `σ_k` is a conditional supertrace that is **largest** precisely where `H_ff` is most indefinite (E1-FRG §1) — the cone *inflates* `σ_k`, dispersing `p`; a `λ>0` barrier is flattened by the very region it must dominate. The cone is a **repeller** for positive-curvature barriers. This is the marginal-side image of PROOF-OPTIMAL-CONTROL-HJB's spectral-side fact: comparison (Crandall–Lions) rigorously pins the ground state `E₀`, but the **non-convex** gap-HJB has no comparison, its optimizer is `ψ₁` (circular), and the convexifying control has **no finite value function** on the Savvidy ladder.
