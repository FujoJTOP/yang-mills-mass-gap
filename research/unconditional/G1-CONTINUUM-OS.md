# G1-CONTINUUM-OS — OS reconstruction closes the *object* `(H,T_t,ν)`; it is silent on the *gap* and the *atom* — those stay G2/H1

> **Verdict (判词).** Given the fixed-volume family and L3, the Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction theorem (OS73/75) turns the G1-EXISTENCE measure `μ_L` into a Hilbert space `H_L`, a self-adjoint contraction transfer matrix `T_t=e^{−tH_L}` (`H_L≥0`), and the Källén–Lehmann measure `ν_O(B)=⟨AΩ,P_{H_L}(B)AΩ⟩`. **This is the OBJECT whose gap is G2, and it closes.** But *existence of ν* is a tautology of the spectral theorem once `H_L` exists: **any** `H_L≥0` yields a finite positive `ν`, gapless or not (massless GFF: `ν` = density down to 0). Reconstruction delivers **no gap and no atom**: `Δ>0` is *not* an OS axiom (the massless GFF satisfies all of them), and even granting `Δ>0` the bottom need not be an atom (mass-spectrum 定理 B: a continuous bottom gives `W(t)~e^{−Δt}/t`, clustering holds, yet `ν({Δ})=0`, `f₁²=0`). So `f₁²>0` = H1 is strictly beyond reconstruction and untouched. **What OS buys: the object *and* a clean split — L3 blocks the existence of the measure/`H` (open UV); H1 blocks the atom (open IR). Both are the Savvidy sign, but they are distinct obligations. The irreducible core is the atom at the edge (H1); reconstruction is the frame, not the content.**

**1. Axiom ledger (this project's numbering, cf. os2/os3 reports).** Reconstruction (OS73/75) needs OS0 regularity, OS1 invariance, OS3 reflection positivity (plus OS4 clustering for *the* physical vacuum). Status: **OS0** proven given L3 (a-uniform moment bounds = `UVS(L)`); **OS1** torus — proven (lattice symmetry); **OS3** — **proven unconditionally** (OS78/MP87 fine-lattice RP, weakly closed, and RP descends under block-spin, os3 REPORT) — free, a design constraint not an input; **OS2** (full continuous `O(4)`) — **open**, only needed at `L→∞`, = GL5/SYM-4D, entangled with MG; **OS4** (clustering = unique vacuum) — **open but ≪ G2** (polynomial clustering, far weaker than the gap). For producing `ν` sector-by-sector, OS0∧OS1∧OS3 suffice; OS4 only names "the" vacuum. G1-EXISTENCE already *outputs* OS0/OS1/OS3 (modulo L3), i.e. it is exactly the hypothesis of the reconstruction theorem — so **reconstruction is a free application, not new analysis** (the `T⁴_L` measure is the thermal trace `Tr e^{−LH_L}` of the cylinder transfer matrix).

**2. Where L3/(★) sits.** A "uniform `Z_a`" is bookkeeping — every positive integrand normalizes; the content is a-uniform bounds on *renormalized* moments (OS0 = tightness = `UVS(L)`), whose sole open input is **L3**: a one-derivative scale-uniformity of the effective Hessian across the `J~log(1/a)` shell tower. L3 is the UV, one-derivative face of the Savvidy sign — strictly milder than the two-sided Hessian sign (that is G2's IR wall). Without L3 there is no `μ_L`, hence no `H_L`, hence no `ν`: **existence of `ν` is conditional on L3**, not free of the Savvidy wall.

**3. Existence-of-ν may close; the atom does not.** Modulo L3, `ν_O` exists and is a finite positive measure for every gauge-invariant `O`, by the spectral theorem applied to `H_L≥0`, regardless of where the spectrum starts. That is the *entire* content of Källén–Lehmann (mass-spectrum §1.1) and exactly the "existence" half — it constrains neither `Δ` nor the atom. CONTINUUM-FREDHOLM's negative result (the determinant zero is not `a`-uniform; it coalesces into the KL pole) is precisely why `ν` is the *right continuum-stable home* for `f₁²` — reconstruction supplies that home. But continuity of the home ≠ positivity of the weight. `f₁²=ν({m₁²})>0` is `H1=I1∧I2` (the gap ∧ bottom-atom ∧ visibility `|⟨Ω|O|G⟩|²>0`), all G2-side. 定理 B is the sharp separatrix: a positive measure with continuous bottom at `Δ` has the gap, exponential clustering, and `f₁²=0`.

**4. What OS4 costs.** The unique-vacuum axiom OS4 (ergodic transfer matrix) is the same object as `[CP-a]` in CONTINUUM-CP: since `[CP-b]⇒[CP-a]`, a uniform gap+isolation forces a unique DLR state, hence OS4; conversely a single phase gives no residue (gapped free field: `f₁²=0`). So **OS4 is downstream of the gap, not a relaxation** — assuming it to name "the" `ν` is gap-strength, not free.

**Sharpest line.** OS reconstruction is the frame, not the content. Given L3 it hands you `H`, `T_t=e^{−tH}`, `ν` — the OBJECT — and thereby *isolates* Clay into two cleanly separated walls: **L3** (does the continuous measure/`H` exist? UV, one-derivative Savvidy) and **H1** (is its bottom a positive atom? IR, two-sided Savvidy). The first is existence-of-`ν` and may close; the second is the atom and is irreducible. Reconstruction proves neither: the massless GFF satisfies every OS axiom with `Δ=0`, and a positive measure may have `Δ>0` and still no atom. Neither gap nor atom is an axiom, so no OS machinery manufactures either. `f₁²>0` remains exactly H1.
