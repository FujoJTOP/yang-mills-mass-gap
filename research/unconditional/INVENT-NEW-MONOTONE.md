# INVENT-NEW-MONOTONE — the gauge W-entropy: Perelman's W transfers to Yang–Mills only where there is no gap, and is sign-obstructed at the Savvidy point

> 2026-11-23 · Invented object. I construct a Perelman-type coupled functional — the **gauge W-entropy** $\mathcal F_W[\mu_t,f_t]$ — prove the monotone that holds (Fisher/de Bruijn, unconditional) and the Perelman strength it lacks (the $\Gamma_2$-refinement), and locate the failure: $\Gamma_2=\mathrm{Ric}_{\mathcal A}+\mathrm{Hess}_{\mathcal A}S_W$ is Savvidy-indefinite (min $\approx-2.0$ at $\beta=2.5$, W6). So $\mathcal F_W$ is monotone on the **free/weak-coupling** side — where the "gap" it certifies is the *free* lattice gap $\lambda_{\min}\to0$ — and **fails sign** at the physical coupling where the gap lives. **Acid test passes sharply:** for free Maxwell $\mathcal F_W$ is genuinely monotone yet forces only $\lambda_{\min}(a,L)\to0$ as $L\to\infty$ — no thermodynamic gap — so it is non-vacuous; but that is the *mirror* of what is needed. It forces the SZZ gap ($\beta<1/48$), nothing at $\beta=2.5$, and never lower-bounds $f_1^2$ (Schur-concave). No fabricated theorem.

**1. Why Perelman's architecture is the right thing to imitate — and why pure YM lacks its engine.** Perelman's $W$-entropy is monotone because *one* object — the metric $g$ — is both the diffusion geometry and itself flowing ($\partial_tg=-2\mathrm{Ric}$); the coupled scale equation $\partial_tf=-\Delta f+|\nabla f|^2-R$ then turns the entropy into the perfect square $2\int|\mathrm{Ric}+\mathrm{Hess}f|^2e^{-f}dV\ge0$. Pure 4D YM on flat $\mathbb R^4$ has a **fixed** base metric ($\partial_tg=0$) and no base curvature; the flow is on *field space* $\mathcal A=\mathfrak g^{E}$ with a **fixed** DeWitt metric $G$. So Perelman must be rebuilt on $(\mathcal A,G)$.

**2. The construction.** Let $\mu=Z^{-1}e^{-S_W}d\mathrm{vol}_G$ be the lattice gauge measure, $\rho_t=e^{-f_t}$ its density along the pushed-forward flow $\mu_t=(\Phi_t)_*\mu$, and $\nabla_{\mathcal A},\Delta_{\mathcal A}$ the field-space gradient/Laplacian. The **conjugate YM flow** is $\partial_tA=-\nabla_{\mathcal A}S_W(A)$ together with $\partial_tf=-\Delta_{\mathcal A}f+|\nabla_{\mathcal A}f|_G^2-S_W$. Define

```
  F_W[μ_t] = ∫_A ( S_W(A) + |∇_A f_t(A)|²_G ) e^{−f_t(A)} d vol_G          (gauge W-entropy)
```

**3. The monotone that holds, and the one that does not.** Along the flow the entropy production is exactly the Fisher square,
$$\frac{dH}{dt}=-\int_{\mathcal A}\big|\nabla_{\mathcal A}\log(\rho_t/\rho_\mu)\big|_G^2\,\rho_t\,d\mathrm{vol}_G\le0 ,$$
unconditionally monotone (de Bruijn) — but this *is* the relative entropy to $\mu$: circular (targets the measure whose gap is the question) and, on the Källén–Lehmann measure $\nu$, **Schur-concave** (EDGE-f₁², INEQ-T3). The Perelman *strength* — a scale-invariant monotone whose decay certifies a spectral gap — needs the $\Gamma_2$-refinement: the certified gap is $\ge\rho>0$ **iff** $\Gamma_2=\mathrm{Ric}_{\mathcal A}+\mathrm{Hess}_{\mathcal A}S_W\ge\rho$ on $T\mathcal A$, exactly the Bakry–Émery criterion W6 tested. With $G$ fixed there is **no scale leg** independent of $S_W$; the only candidate, the trace anomaly $\beta(g)\mathrm{tr}F^2$, is a thermodynamic *total* (PROOF-TRANSPORT-MONOTONE). So $\mathcal F_W$ collapses leg by leg onto objects the corpus has excluded.

**4. Acid test — and the sign inversion.** *(i) Free Maxwell ($g=0$).* $S_W$ quadratic ⇒ $\mathrm{Ric}_{\mathcal A}=0$, $\mathrm{Hess}_{\mathcal A}S_0=-\Delta_{\mathcal A}\ge0$ on transverse modes ⇒ $\Gamma_2\ge0$, so $\mathcal F_W$ **is** monotone; BE/LSI certifies $\lambda_{\min}(a,L)=\tfrac4{a^2}\sin^2\tfrac{\pi a}{L}\to\tfrac{4\pi^2}{L^2}\to0$ (P1-L0). It forces **no thermodynamic gap** for the free massless field — non-vacuous; acid test **passed**. *(ii) Physical YM ($\beta=2.5$).* $\mathrm{Hess}_{\mathcal A}S_W$ is Savvidy-negative, $\min\Gamma_2\approx-2.0<0$ (W6: $21\%$ of directions negative, $\le10\%$ in the collective subspace), so the $\Gamma_2$-refinement — the *only* gap-certifying monotone — is **sign-indefinite** exactly where the gap must be proved (the plain Fisher leg survives but forces nothing on the gap). *(iii)* Where $\Gamma_2\ge0$ at strong coupling, $\beta<1/48$, it reproduces the SZZ gap; nowhere else. What it forces, exactly: $\mathrm{gap}\ge\rho$ on $[0,\beta_{\mathrm SZZ}]$, the free $\lambda_{\min}\to0$ at $g=0$, and **nothing** on $f_1^2$.

**5. Why it cannot reach the edge even where monotone.** $\mathcal F_W$ is concave in the spreading order of $\nu$, so it controls totals and bounds the edge from **above**; the free field is the lower end, $f_1^2=0$, and the residue is upper-semicontinuous (INEQ-T2) — invisible to any Schur-concave monotone. The edge needs the reversed sign (`total ≤ atom`), which no leg of the architecture supplies (PROOF-TRANSPORT-MONOTONE: monotone and atom-coercive objects lie on opposite sides of the Schur order).

**Sharpest line.** Perelman's $W$ needs one object to be *both* geometry and flow; pure YM on flat space splits them — geometry is the fixed field-space metric (constant $\Gamma_2^{\mathrm{free}}\ge0$), flow is the interacting $S_W$ (Savvidy $\Gamma_2<0$). The gauge W-entropy is monotone on the **gapless** side of the phase diagram and sign-obstructed on the **gapped** side; the object that would cross the Savvidy point is the missing nonperturbative flow itself, not a new functional.
