# FORGE-DIRAC-ETA — Dirac / eta / spectral action as a LOWER bound on m₁: it survives Savvidy *because* it is free-vacuous

> 2026-11-23 · 4D pure SU(N) YM, β=2N/g². **Verdict: not 5/5 — a class no-go with an exact stuck step (MS-Dirac).** The Dirac operator genuinely survives the Savvidy indefinite Hessian: `D̸_A²=−D_A²+spin·F ⪰ 0` on *every* background, where the bosonic `H=−D²+2ad F` is not PSD. But that positivity *is* free-field positivity — `D̸²⪰0` is a **square**, hence it holds identically at `g=0` and carries no gap. The one datum that differs free↔interacting, the Dirac gap `δ=min|spec D̸_A|`, is **zero on the topological sectors** dominating β=2.5 (index theorem ⇒ exact zero modes — wrong direction), and at finite `(L,a)` is positive for free *and* interacting (box mode `2π/L`), distinguishing only in the continuum limit — the TDA wall, so `(L,a)`-uniform ≡ the gap. The eta is worse: **4D `η≡0`** by γ₅ symmetry; the 3D `η`'s only gauge-invariant content is `e^{iπη}` = instanton phase = the **index** — rigidity (`INVENT-NEW-INDEX`) drags it to 0, leaving `⟨n²⟩=χ_t` (the `0⁻⁺⁺` susceptibility: wrong channel, *upper* bound). Every Dirac edge is a **fermionic** probe; pure YM has no SUSY tying it to the `0⁺⁺` glueball. The exact missing step is a fermion→boson spectral bridge that does not exist.

**1. The appeal is real — and is exactly the trap.** `D̸_A = iγ^μ(∂_μ+A_μ)` is self-adjoint, so `D̸_A²≥0` always; its spectrum is γ₅-symmetric (`{D̸,γ₅}=0 ⇒ λ↔−λ`), and the Savvidy negativity (`λ_min=−g|B|`) never appears. So the Dirac cone *does* survive the indefinite Hessian. But `D̸_A² = −D_A² + (i/2)Σ_{μν}F^{μν}` equals `−∂²` at `g=0` and is PSD there too: survival and free-field vacuity are the **same fact** — `D̸²≥0` is the trivial `(self-adjoint)²≥0`, not a constraint on `F`. The bosonic `H` is indefinite precisely because `2ad F` is *not* a square; that non-squareness carries the dynamics.

**2. The Dirac gap is zero where it matters — wrong direction.** On a background of instanton number `Q≠0` the index theorem gives `n_+−n_−=Q` exact zero modes (`'t Hooft`). The β=2.5 measure is topology-rich, so `min|spec D̸_A|=0` on the dominant sectors: the interacting Dirac gap is *smaller* than free (`δ_YM=0 ≤ δ_free`), the opposite of a lower bound.

**3. Eta: the caveat resolves against the tool.** 4D massless `D̸` has `η≡0` identically (γ₅ symmetry; only the *index* is nonzero). Asymmetry survives only on a 3D slice `D₃(A_τ)`, where `η(D₃)` varies — but under a large gauge transformation `η(A^g)−η(A)=2·Ind` (even), so **only `e^{iπη}` is gauge-invariant**, and that is the instanton phase (Witten's global anomaly). Its quantized content is the **index** `n(A)=(1/8π²)∫tr F∧F̃`, locally constant on the connected β-family (rigidity), forced to `<n>=0` (CP), leaving only `⟨n²⟩=χ_t=Σf_n²m_n²≥f₁²m₁²` — `m_{0⁻⁺⁺}²≤χ_t/f₁²`: an **upper** bound in the wrong channel. The continuous part is gauge-dependent (a determinant-line section), not observable. **Rigidity does kill eta: there is no gauge-invariant β-varying continuous eta to carry a gap.**

**4. Spectral action and the cone.** `Tr f(D̸²/Λ²)` Seeley–DeWitt-expands to the classical `∫tr F²` plus higher operators (`H1-T5-NCG`) — `g`-continuous, defined at `g=0`, hence free-valued and Schur-convex: `atom ≤ total`, the wrong cone. The IR Dirac density `ρ_D(λ)=⟨Σδ(λ−λ_i(A))⟩_YM` is the only genuinely nonperturbative Dirac datum (Banks–Casher ties `ρ_D(0)` to a *chiral* condensate — wrong channel, a density not a gap), but its `⟨·⟩_YM` **is the path integral**: circular.

**5. Fermion→boson: the exact missing step.** Even granting a clean Dirac bound `λ₁² ≥ Φ(A)>0`, mapping it to `m_{0⁺⁺}` needs `m_{0⁺⁺}=λ₁` — a degeneracy true only under SUSY (`{Q,Q̄}∼H`). Pure YM has only nilpotent BRST; a gaugino gives N=1 SYM and decoupling is uncontrolled (`PROOF-WITTEN-SPINOR`, `BPS-SUSY-PROTECTION`). Casher's bound `λ₁≲√σ` is an *upper* bound, the wrong cone for `m₁≥δ`.

**Acid test.** `D̸²≥0`: PSD free *and* interacting → fails "fails free field." Dirac gap: `0` on instantons, `2π/L` box mode free — positive-vacuous at any regulator. `η`: `0` (4D) / index (3D) → rigidity → free value. Spectral action: `g=0` value. **None passes.**

**Exact stuck point (MS-Dirac).** The missing object is an inequality `m_{0⁺⁺} ≥ Φ[spec D̸_A]` with `Φ` gauge-invariant, non-circular, distinguishing free, `(L,a)`-uniform. Every candidate is (i) the square positivity `D̸²≥0` → free-vacuous; (ii) the Dirac gap → zero on topology, box-mode-vacuous, continuum-limit-only; (iii) 4D `η` → `≡0`; (iv) 3D `η` → index → rigid → wrong channel/upper; (v) spectral action → `g=0`/Schur-convex. The bridge from a **fermionic** spectral edge to the **bosonic** `0⁺⁺` atom (a SUSY-type degeneracy pure YM lacks) is the precise missing step. **Dirac/eta/spectral-action gives no lower bound on m₁.**

**Sharpest line.** Savvidy-survival and free-field-failure are *antagonistic* for a spectral-square probe: `D̸²⪰0` is the only reason it survives the indefinite Hessian, and that same reason makes it `⪰0` at `g=0`, where `f₁²=0`.
