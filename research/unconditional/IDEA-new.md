# IDEA-new — Weyl-modulus marginalization: the Vandermonde Jacobian as a positive convexifier of the Savvidy region

**The mechanism.** Work in gauge-invariant **Weyl-chamber coordinates**: diagonalize the blocked field-strength / connection into eigenvalues λ₁,…,λ_N (Cartan + weights) times flag-manifold angles. The measure splits with a **Vandermonde Jacobian** ∏_{i<j}(λ_i−λ_j)². The gap-relevant object is the *marginal* density of the radial eigenvalues,
  μ(λ) ∝ ∏_{i<j}(λ_i−λ_j)² · e^{−S(λ)},
not the Cartesian Hessian. Its log-curvature is V_eff'' = S'' + (curvature of −ln∏(λ_i−λ_j)²). That second term is **positive and λ→0-dominant** — the exact analogue of the centrifugal barrier that gives a Mexican-hat measure a positive radial mass despite S''<0 at the origin. This object is never computed by the background-field/Savvidy method, which fixes a background, reads off the −g|F| Cartesian eigenvalue, and discards the angular measure that supplies the Jacobian.

**Why it might evade the one obstruction.** "Large-field non-concavity at every scale" is a statement about S'' in *link* coordinates; curvature is not reparametrization-invariant, and the mass a gap proof needs is the curvature of the *gauge-invariant modulus marginal* V_eff = S − ln J, where J is the Vandermonde. The directions Savvidy discards are exactly the ones whose measure contributes +J. This matches the campaign's own data (bare measure positive; collective Hessian positive in P3/W4): the positivity lives in the angular Jacobian, the negativity in the radial S''.

**First proof obligation.** A *Weyl-chamber curvature lemma*: away from the discriminant locus λ_i=λ_j, show V_eff''(λ_min) ≥ c₀>0 uniformly in volume and cutoff at β≈2.5 — concretely, that the Vandermonde curvature dominates g|F|·|negative-mode coefficient|, and that the discriminant locus carries vanishing weight so it does not spoil uniformity. Computable as a sign test on the *exact modulus marginal*, not the pointwise proxy G″≈−0.165 of VAR-FINAL.

**Most likely failure.** The Vandermonde vanishes on the discriminant locus λ_i=λ_j (eigenvalue collisions), which is *generic in the IR* — confining/abelianizing configurations pile onto the Cartan. There the +J stabilization switches off and the marginal can be non-log-concave exactly where the IR mass is decided, collapsing to the same marginal-curvature wall (G″<0). Sharper: the coupled flag-manifold/B-deformed S-curvature may stay indefinite in directions the Jacobian does not cancel.

**Honest status.** Distinct from `IDEA-positivity-blocked` (same link coordinates, no Jacobian) and from refuted `IDEA-wasserstein`. It shares their fate *if* the sign test returns negative; its entire new content is the bet that the Vandermonde Jacobian is a sign-definite convexifier the other routes structurally omit. Past this, the space looks genuinely near-exhausted: every contraction/series/positivity/convexity route reconverges on the same marginal sign.
