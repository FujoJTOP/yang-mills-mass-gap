# PROOF-GMT-CURRENTS — can geometric measure theory (minimal currents / varifolds) bind the glueball mass or the area law's rigidity?

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict: a genuine *conditional structure theorem*, not the missing machine.** GMT is the exact language for the *geometry* of the confining thin-string limit. If the flux-tube worldsheet is a mass-minimizing 2-current, Almgren-type regularity (smooth off a codim-2 singular set) plus calibration fix its area equal to the Plateau mass — **the area law's rigidity is a real GMT theorem-let, conditional on σ>0**. But GMT carries **no tension σ and no gap**: every bound it gives is a metric/topological datum, nonzero already for the free field, and the glueball's own scale is the *fat*-string regime outside the asymptotic theory. So GMT reaches the long-string/surface limit; it cannot bound the short glueball mass and does not produce σ>0. The Savvidy wall is untouched.

**(1) The formulation.** The Wilson loop feeds a *Plateau problem*: the strong-coupling/random-surface expansion writes ⟨W(C)⟩ as a sum over surfaces with ∂ = C, dominated by the mass-minimizing integral 2-current spanning C (Federer–Fleming). The flux-tube worldsheet is that minimizer; the glueball is the lightest *closed* string — a stationary 1-current at fixed time with E = σ·(length) + Δ, the Nambu–Goto/effective-string picture whose worldsheet is a (branched) minimal surface. Honest only as an *effective* description: the physical flux tube is fat (transverse width ~Λ_QCD⁻¹ ~ 1/√σ), so the 2-current is the thin-string centerline, faithful only for ℓ ≫ 1/√σ.

**(2) What regularity/compactness buys — and the mass bound.** For a mass-minimizing 2-current in ℝ⁴: Federer–Fleming plus Allard compactness extract the minimizer; Almgren's big regularity theorem, made quantitative by De Lellis–Spadaro and — for stationary (rectifiable, froth-free) varifolds — Naber–Valtorta, proves it is C^{1,α} off a singular set of dimension ≤ 0 (isolated branch points for 2-currents in ℝ⁴). The area is then rigid by Federer's calibration: a closed comass-1 form calibrates the minimizer, so Area = Plateau mass exactly, and a surface at constant tension is CMC — **the area-law rigidity, a real theorem-let.** A genuine *mass lower bound* exists too, but only from *topology*: a nontrivial homology class or a min–max width (Almgren–Pitts; isoperimetric filling) forces mass ≥ filling/systolic volume. Both are rigorous — and both are where it dies for YM.

**(3) Acid test — and it fails.** Free massless field: σ = 0, f₁² = 0, no flux tube. Every pure-GMT bound is a *metric datum*: the Plateau mass of a spanning surface for C, or the filling volume of a cycle, is strictly positive at σ = 0 and *unchanged* when the YM coupling is switched off. So the GMT-only bound does **not** vanish for the free field ⇒ vacuous as a gap statement. To pass it must read m ≥ σ·(GMT geometric factor) + Δ, where **σ and the intercept Δ are external dynamical inputs**; GMT supplies only the geometric factor and its rigidity. This is not the Schur-concave sign trap of EDGE-f₁² — GMT's bounds have the right (convex) sign — it is *dynamical blindness*. The closed-string minimization is moreover *scale-free*: unconstrained it collapses (ℓ→0 regardless of σ>0), so a positive glueball mass needs either the topology constraint (metric ⇒ acid-test fail) or the quantum intercept Δ (outside GMT). The glueball sits at ℓ ~ 1/√σ — precisely the fat-string boundary where the thin minimal surface is unfaithful. So GMT gives no short-glueball lower bound.

**The one unconditional meeting.** In 4D the Kähler/ASD 2-forms are Harvey–Lawson calibrations (calibrated sets = holomorphic curves), and the twistor correspondence (Atiyah–Hitchin–Singer; Ward) makes ASD instantons holomorphic bundles on ℂP³ — a rigorous calibrated/holomorphic bridge. It controls *topological soliton moduli*, not the light glueball spectrum, and does not touch Savvidy concavity.

**Sharpest line.** GMT is the exact geometry of the confining string in its thin limit — conditional regularity, calibration rigidity of the area, CMC at constant tension — but it is *tension-blind and gap-blind*: every purely geometric bound survives σ=0, so the acid test fails unless a dynamical σ (the very object under attack) is inserted by hand. It models the long string; the short glueball is outside its regime, and the Savvidy wall is not a geometric-measure-theoretic wall.
