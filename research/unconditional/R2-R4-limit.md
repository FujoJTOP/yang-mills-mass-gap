# R2 — The ℝ³×S¹ → ℝ⁴ limit

**Verdict.** **Open "needs the target."** Not known-impossible (no theorem forbids a gap), but not achievable by any known technique. The limit is conjectural at every link.

**Adiabatic continuity is a conjecture, not a theorem.** Ünsal–Yaffe 0803.0344 propose the 't Hooft-flux / magnetic-bion construction on ℝ³×S¹ and *conjecture* that the small-L confined phase connects smoothly to large-L pure YM ("adiabatic continuity"). Semiclassics controls only NΛL ≪ 1. Review: Poppitz 2111.10423; Poppitz–Schäfer–Ünsal 1205.0290. Crucially, *undeformed* pure YM on small S¹ is center-*broken* (GPY one-loop potential; Gross–Pisarski–Yaffe, Rev. Mod. Phys. 53, 43, 1981) — so the controlled theory is deformed YM / QCD(adj), not pure YM.

**A real phase transition blocks naive continuation.** Pure YM on ℝ³×S¹ has the deconfinement transition at L_c = 1/T_c (GPY; lattice). The weak-coupling bion expansion cannot cross L_c. Deformations postpone the transition, but the lattice "reconfinement" evidence (Bonati et al 2012.13246; 1912.02662) is numerical, not a perturbative proof of continuity.

**No torus↔ℝ⁴ or lattice↔continuum gap theorem exists.** Osterwalder–Seiler (1978) give OS + 2d continuum; Balaban (CMP 109/116/119/122) gives UV stability but no continuum measure and no gap; cluster expansions give gaps only where they converge (strong coupling). Sharp mathematical obstacle: in a reflection-positive theory the Matsubara gap is non-increasing as the time-circle grows, so any finite-L gap only *upper-bounds* gap(ℝ⁴). A positive ℝ⁴ gap needs a volume-uniform **lower** bound — exactly the missing theorem.

**Bridges do not close it.** Large N: the deformation is O(1/N²) (0803.0344), but volume independence holds only in the center-symmetric phase at N=∞, and the large-N ℝ⁴ gap is equally open. SUSY: N=1 SYM at small L is controlled (1205.0290; Seiberg–Witten hep-th/9407087), but no controlled gaugino-mass flow reaches pure YM.

**Sharpest true statement.** The ℝ⁴ limit is an open step requiring a volume-uniform nonperturbative RG/continuity theorem indistinguishable from the mass gap itself.
