# k0-HOLONOMY-EFFPOT — the holonomy effective potential's curvature as the k→0 mass

> 2026-11-23 · Forge-test. The k→0 coarse mode, read as the temporal Polyakov holonomy Ω with effective potential V(Ω) (GPY one-loop + corrections); curvature `V''(Ω)` = the temporal zero-mode "mass". Does a rigorous/bounded V give a *positive* curvature = the k→0 mass, passing all 5? **判词: 不过 — 0 crisp passes (#1 nominal, unrigorous).** `V''(Ω)` is a *finite-L, one-loop, free-gluon* electric mass ∝ 1/L². Positive, but **nonzero for the free Gaussian theory** (not an order parameter for the interaction-induced gap), it lives in the center/1-form channel (blind to the neutral 0⁺⁺), cannot control the *magnetic* Savvidy sign, and identifying small-L with ℝ⁴ is adiabatic continuity = the gap. The decompactification limit is *not* controllable; min–max makes it an **upper-bound** machine. Exact failure: `V''=c/L²→0` with the backward inequality `m(S¹_L)≥m(ℝ⁴)`.

**1. Nonperturbative — FAIL (nominal only).** V(Ω) is the one-loop determinant of *free* gluon/ghost winding modes — a Gaussian path integral, i.e. perturbative. Nonperturbative corrections are the small-L magnetic-bion gas: unrigorous semiclassics (`e^{−S₀}`, determinants computed not bounded; R1; Ünsal 0709.3269). Here "rigorous" and "nonperturbative" are mutually exclusive.

**2. Sign-controlling — FAIL (wrong operator).** V''(Ω) is the second variation w.r.t. the *constant temporal* `A₀` at `F=0`. The Savvidy wall is the Hessian `H=−D²+2ad F_B` in a *large magnetic* background, bottom `λ_min=−g|B|<0`, negative count `∝(gB)²V` (PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN). A positive static curvature does not bound the sign of a magnetic-background Hessian; transport needs an exact 4D electric–magnetic duality absent in nonabelian YM (GAP-G2-magnetic §4; FORGE5-CONFINING-MONOPOLE #2).

**3. Non-circular — FAIL.** Undeformed pure YM on ℝ³×S¹_L is center-*broken*/deconfined at small L (GPY; Gross–Pisarski–Yaffe), not the confined vacuum; keeping `Z_N` requires a double-trace/'t Hooft deformation ⇒ *deformed* YM, not pure YM. That its L→∞ limit is the confined ℝ⁴ vacuum is *adiabatic continuity* — the gap conjecture (R1; EGUCHI-KAWAI (b); FORGE5-SMALL-S1 #3). Input = answer.

**4. Non-SUSY + fails free field — FAIL on the acid test.** The non-SUSY half is trivial, but V''(Ω) is built from free-gluon determinants — one loop *is* the Gaussian theory — so it is **nonzero at every finite L with the interaction off**. The mechanism does *not* return `m=0` for the free massless gluon; it fabricates a curvature `~1/L²`, unable to distinguish interacting YM from free gluons (contrast the monopole routes, whose free value is correctly 0).

**5. Uniform lower bound — FAIL, structurally.** `V''(Ω)=m_el²(L)=c/L²→0` as `L→∞` (T→0); at T=0 there is no holonomy at all. Worse, min–max/reflection positivity force the compactified gap `m(S¹_L) ≥ m(ℝ⁴)`, *decreasing* in L: a controlled small-L curvature is an **upper** bound on the 4D gap, never a floor (P4 §2–3; FORGE5-SMALL-S1). The decompactification limit *is* the gap, and the inequality runs backward. Exact failing quantity: `λ(L)=c/L²` and the direction of `m(S¹_L)≥m(ℝ⁴)`.

**Sharpest line.** The holonomy potential is the one honest loophole in W6's "Γ depends on F only" — a constant `A₀` has `F=0` but a nontrivial Wilson line — yet that global, F-free modulus is intrinsically *finite-L*: its curvature is a free-gluon electric mass `∝1/L²`, it sits in the center/1-form channel the neutral `tr F²` is provably blind to (PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY; cf. FORGE5-CONFINING-MONOPOLE §5), and it vanishes exactly where the target lives (`L→∞`). Controlling the decompactification is the Clay gap, and its inequality points the wrong way for a lower bound. The direction is real and physical; it is not a proof.
