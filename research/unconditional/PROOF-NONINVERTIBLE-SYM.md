# PROOF-NONINVERTIBLE-SYM — do non-invertible / higher symmetries constrain the 0⁺⁺ gap?

> **Verdict: No — the wall does not move; it is the same cross-sector wall, one categorical level up.** The modern non-invertible structures of 4D pure SU(N) YM are, without exception, **built by gauging/condensing the 1-form (center) symmetry**: they are codim-1 condensation defects localized on 2-surfaces, whose action on **local** operators is **trivial** (`tr F²` is invisible, exactly as it is to the 1-form itself); their non-invertibility is carried by the **line/'t Hooft-line** sector. The one genuinely non-invertible *time reversal* lives in the **center-gauged PSU(N)** theory, not pure SU(N), and reduces to an ordinary phase on local operators. Pure SU(N) YM at θ=π keeps an **invertible** T; its mixed anomaly is an **invertible** 0-form×1-form anomaly that forces at most a *vacuum* (TQFT/SSB/CFT) statement — never a 0⁺⁺ mass or `f₁²`.

**Inventory — what actually exists.** (i) The electric & magnetic `Z_N^{(1)}` center (GKSW 1412.5148); unbroken in confinement. (ii) Invertible **2-groupings**: θ-periodicity × center (Cordova–Freed–Lam–Seiberg 1905.13361), and at θ=π the time reversal × center anomaly (Gaiotto–Kapustin–Komargodski–Seiberg 1703.00501). (iii) **Non-invertible**: the *condensation and duality/triality* defects obtained by higher-gauging (or half-space gauging) the discrete 1-form symmetry (Choi–Córdova–Hsin–Lam–Shao 2204.09025, CMP 402, 489; 2111.01139, PRD 105, 125016); the *continuous 2-category* around the UV gauged `U(1)^{N−1}⋊S_N` description, which RG-breaks to the center (Antinucci–Galati–Rizi 2206.05646, JHEP 12 (2022) 061); and a *non-invertible time reversal* at rational θ=πp/N in Maxwell/QED and — after S-gauging the center — in PSU(N) at θ=π (Choi–Lam–Shao 2208.04331, PRL 130, 131602). Note: genuine **duality/triality** defects require a **self-duality** the theory has only with extended SUSY (N=4, self-dual τ) or as free Maxwell; **pure non-SUSY SU(N) is not self-dual**, and the explicit realizations exclude it. No non-invertible symmetry of pure SU(N) YM acting nontrivially on local operators is established.

**Why it cannot touch `tr F²`.** A condensation defect from gauging a q-form symmetry is a sum over symmetry operators on codim-≥2 submanifolds; a 2-surface can always be separated from a point, so the defect commutes with every **local** operator and its non-invertible fusion (e.g. `𝒟†𝒟 = 𝒞 ≠ 1`) is read only on **lines** (the 't Hooft-line quantum symmetry). For the non-invertible time reversal the antilinear part acts on local fields through the naive `T` (a `P`-parity phase); the "no inverse" is again a line-sector phenomenon. The AGR 2-category acts on Gukov–Witten **surfaces**. So the local, N-ality-0 `tr F²`, the residue `f₁²`, and the 0⁺⁺ edge remain untouched — the codim-2/3 blindness of the 1-form, restated.

**Mixed anomalies force a vacuum, not a mass.** At θ=π the T×center anomaly (nonzero for even N) and the center×θ anomaly forbid a *trivially gapped, symmetric* vacuum, forcing one of {TQFT, 0-form SSB, gapless CFT}; on ℝ⁴ uniqueness excludes SSB, leaving **gapped** TQFT or CFT — no local-channel mass fixed (cf. PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY, THETA-PI). At θ=0 the electric anomaly is trivial; no non-invertible anomaly touches local operators. Nothing here bounds `f₁²` or `m_{0⁺⁺}`.

**Sharpest line.** *Every non-invertible symmetry pure YM possesses is a descendant of its 1-form center — codim-1 but built on 2-surfaces — so it is provably blind to the local `tr F²`; and every anomaly it carries is either invertible (θ=π) or a line-sector statement, so it constrains phases and defect spectra, never the 0⁺⁺ gap. The non-invertible wall is the 1-form wall.*
