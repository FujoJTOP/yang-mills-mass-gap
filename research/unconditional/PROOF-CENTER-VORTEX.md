# PROOF-CENTER-VORTEX — center-vortex percolation / vortex free energy at β=2.5: criterion vs theorem

> 2026-11-23 · Center-vortex ('t Hooft) route to σ>0 at the crossover. **Verdict: the vortex criterion is a *proven sufficient condition* — Tomboulis–Yaffe (1985) bound the Wilson loop by the vortex free energy, so a condensing/percolating vortex ensemble forces an area law, σ>0 — but its premise is open at β=2.5.** Rigorous only one-directional: (criterion ⇒ σ>0). The input — the vortex free-energy density at the confining threshold at β=2.5 — is proven only at strong coupling (β<1/48 SZZ; β<12/17≈0.706 Borgs–Seiler) and is gauge-fixed numerics at the crossover. 't Hooft's commutation gives only the area/perimeter **dichotomy**, not the selection; no 1-form anomaly forces σ>0; and σ>0 is the 1-form (confinement) channel — the 0⁺⁺ gap follows only via the measured scaling m∝√σ. So no confinement+gap theorem; the missing bound is a volume-uniform vortex-condensation inequality at 2.5.

## 1. The criterion.
The k-ality Wilson loop is a center-vortex average ⟨W_k(C)⟩=⟨ω_k^{n(C)}⟩, ω_k=e^{2πik/N}, n(C) the signed linking number of vortex worldsheets with a surface Σ spanning C (Engelhardt et al.; Greensite). Two equivalent forms:
- **Percolation:** ⟨W_k⟩~e^{−σ_k Area} **iff** vortices percolate — n(C) has variance ∝ Area (random flux through Σ); finite clusters ⇒ perimeter law.
- **Vortex free energy:** the 't Hooft loop ⟨V(C)⟩=Z_C/Z=e^{−F_v} *is* the vortex free energy. The confined phase has F_v~perimeter (a vortex costs only its boundary — the condensate; hep-lat/0007034), so the vortex free-energy density ρ̂ sits at the confining threshold. Condensation/percolation ⇒ σ>0.

## 2. What is rigorous.
- **'t Hooft commutation** (NPB 138 (1978) 1; NPB 153 (1979) 141): V(C)W(C')=ω^{link}W(C')V(C) — a rigorous operator identity. It forbids W and V both area-law *and* both perimeter-law (N≤3): the **complementarity/dichotomy**. It does **not** select which is area — that is dynamical.
- **Tomboulis–Yaffe inequality** (CMP 100 (1985) 313): ⟨W(C)⟩ ≤ 2{½(1−Z⁻_Λ/Z_Λ)}^{A_c/L₁L₂}, Z⁻ the vortex-twisted partition function. This turns 't Hooft's criterion into a *sufficient* condition: a vortex free-energy density of the confining (condensing) sign ⇒ ⟨W⟩ area law, σ ≥ c·ρ̂>0, c>0 a lattice constant. **This is the one rigorous "criterion ⇒ σ>0" implication in the vortex family.**
- **Strong coupling:** at β<1/48 (SZZ arXiv:2204.12737) and β<12/17 (Borgs–Seiler) the ensemble condenses and ⟨W⟩ is area-law by cluster expansion — the criterion is *realized*, unconditionally, far from 2.5.

## 3. What is NOT rigorous at β=2.5.
- **ρ̂(2.5) at threshold** (vortex condensation/percolation at the crossover): unproven. Center vortices are identified after **maximal-center-gauge projection** — gauge-fixed, finite-volume Monte Carlo (Del Debbio–Faber–Giedt–Greensite–Olejník; Engelhardt–Langfeld–Reinhardt–Tennert, PRD 61 (2000) 054504). The underlying surface/map expansion is **signed** (Weingarten; PROOF-RANDOM-CLUSTER), so no FK/positive percolation theorem exists non-abelian.
- **The all-β program is disputed.** Tomboulis (arXiv:0707.2179) claimed σ>0 at all β via TY + Migdal–Kadanoff; Seiler (arXiv:0901.4246) argues the interpolating/comparison inequality is not established. So even the intended route is not accepted as proof.

## 4. 1-form symmetry / anomaly.
Center vortices are exactly the objects charged under the magnetic Z_N^{(1)} center 1-form symmetry; the 't Hooft loop is its symmetry defect (GKSW 1412.5148). Rigorous — but it yields the **order parameter, not its value**: at θ=0 the electric Z_N^{(1)} carries no 't Hooft anomaly, so nothing forces an area law; the θ=π mixed anomaly forces at most a gapped TQFT (PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY). The symmetry is provably blind to the local tr F² (a point carries no 1-form charge); σ>0 ⇒ m_{0⁺⁺}>0 only through the measured m_{0⁺⁺}≈(3.5–4)√σ (Morningstar–Peardon), area law ⇏ gap (PROOF-STRING-TENSION-STABILIZER).

**Exact missing bound.** A volume-uniform vortex-condensation inequality at β=2.5: Z⁻_Λ/Z_Λ ≥ 1−ε(L) with ε(L)→0 (vortices cost vanishing free energy ⇒ percolation), equivalently ρ̂(2.5) at the confining threshold, uniform in L. Granting it, TY gives σ(2.5)≥c·ρ̂>0 — **fixed-β confinement**, a 1-form/flux-tube statement; the Clay 0⁺⁺ gap still needs σ_phys>0 (a→0 transport) **and** m_{0⁺⁺}∝√σ.

**Sharpest line.** *The center-vortex picture is rigorous exactly one way: Tomboulis–Yaffe turn "vortices condense" into a sufficient inequality that forces σ>0. But "vortices condense at β=2.5" is the confinement problem re-expressed — proven at β<1/48, gauge-fixed numerics at 2.5, its all-β proof disputed (Tomboulis 2007 vs Seiler 2009). And even a vortex proof of σ>0 lands in the 1-form channel, blind to tr F²: the 0⁺⁺ gap stays behind the same RG wall, reachable only through the unproven m∝√σ.*
