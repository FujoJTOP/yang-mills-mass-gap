# G1-L3-VIA-FLOW — flow-based renormalization is valid for the coupling and the flowed operator, invalid for the sharp moment bound; L3 is not closed, the wrong half again

> **判词 (verdict).** **No.** The gradient flow renormalizes two objects `a`-uniformly and rigorously — the **coupling** (`α_GF`, Lüscher 2010) and the **flowed operator** `E(t)=¼G²(t)` at `t>0` (Lüscher–Weisz). It does **not** renormalize the `a`-uniform **moment bound** `UVS(L)` that *is* L3. Three quantitative facts close it, all the same "wrong half." **(i) Forward-only.** Flow bounds the *flowed* measure `μ_t=(Φ_t)_{*}μ`; pulling back to the sharp `μ` costs `‖DΦ_t^{−1}‖=e^{ct/a²}`, largest on Savvidy modes — the one-derivative generator transports as `H=e^{−2tL}H_t`, inflated by `e^{+2t|λ_S|}` there. **(ii) Wrong scale profile.** A fixed `√t` damps `k≳1/√t`, is the **identity** on the gap mode `k→0`, over-damps deep shells and leaves the shallow shells `j∼j_*` (where Savvidy concavity is typical) untouched — can't track the `β_j`-tower without becoming a multiscale flow, i.e. Balaban, which stops at large field `=(★)`. **(iii) `t→0` un-smooths.** Physical moments are the `t→0` limit; transferring flowed bounds there is exactly the `a⁻⁴` renormalon, non-resummable (G1-L3 §2). SQ-RG adds nothing (§5). L3 stays open; `(★)` — the sharp one-sided variance upper bound — is untouched.

## 1 · L3 needs the *sharp* measure's moment bound
`UVS(L)`: `|S^{(n)}_{L,a}(f_1,…,f_n)|≤C_n(L)∏‖f_i‖` uniform in `a` plus factorial growth — OS0 of the **sharp** `μ_{L,a}`. Moments scale as `N_□∼L⁴/a⁴→∞`, so *finiteness* is not `a`-uniform; only log-concavity (★: per-shell `Var_μ(⟨Pᵀ∇S_j,v⟩)≤E[H]+W−δ`) gives uniform tails (PROOF-L3 §1). The flow never touches this object — it acts *after* sampling and returns `μ_t`.

## 2 · Forward–backward asymmetry, quantitatively
For the linearized flow `DΦ_t=e^{−tL}`, pushforward contracts variance: `Var_{μ_t}(v)=e^{−tL}Var_μ(v)e^{−tL}`, damping high modes — the tempting "a-uniform bound." But the identity `Var_μ(f)=Var_{μ_t}(f∘Φ_t^{−1})` carries `Lip(Φ_t^{−1})=e^{tλ_UV}~e^{ct/a²}`; the bound diverges. Concretely `H=e^{−2tL}H_t`, and on Savvidy `λ_S<0` the factor `e^{2t|λ_S|}>1` *inflates* the sharp generator, so `sup_a Z_a^{−1}‖H_a‖_D` is not read off any flowed norm. Choosing `√t∝a` (flow by one lattice spacing) makes the backward cost `e^{O(1)}` `a`-uniform — but `μ_t` is still the UV-divergent 4D measure, `Var∼β_j→∞`: a finite reparametrisation, not a bound. Forward-only is structural: a smoothing map certifies the *smoothed* remnant, never the UV it removed.

## 3 · The multiplier's scale profile is wrong for the tower
L3 needs a per-shell `(★)` across `j=j_*,…,J`, `β_j→∞`. The flow damps shell `j` by `e^{−t/a_j²}`: enormous for deep shells (`a_j→a`), `≈1` for shallow ones. The shallow shells — where the Savvidy region is *typical* and `H` indefinite — are undamped; the gap mode `k_1=2π/L` is an identity (`e^{−t(2π/L)²}→1`, PROOF-GRADIENT-FLOW §4). One `t` cannot match a `β_j`-growing tower; matching shell-by-shell *is* a multiscale flow, whose `a`-uniform large-field bound is Balaban's open step, `=(★)` (G1-L3 §3).

## 4 · Flow-based renormalization is real — for the other object
`α_GF` renormalizes the **coupling** `a`-uniformly (Lüscher 2010; Fritzsch–Ramos 2013) — L2's object (running/matching), already closed. `E(t)` is a renormalized **operator** (Lüscher–Weisz) — definitional (CONTINUUM-GRADIENT-FLOW-DEFINE). Neither bounds the sharp *moment*. The flow renormalizes by **smoothing**, not subtraction: it suppresses the large-field Savvidy region (`e^{−|F|√t}`) where `(★)` is concave, and `t→0` returns the sharp operator with the `a⁻⁴` renormalon blocking transfer — the same wall as L3 §2.

## 5 · Stochastic quantization: marginal and circular
Parisi–Wu's invariant measure is `μ` itself; bounding `μ`'s moments from the SPDE is L3 verbatim (circular). Nonperturbatively, 4D is **marginal** (`[g]=0`): Hairer subcriticality fails, no gauge-covariant BPHZ, no well-posedness (H1-T1 §1). Order-by-order only — that is L2, not L3.

## 6 · Honest boundary
Flow does **not** close L3. It buys a rigorous *flowed* QFT (`E(t)`, OS0 of `μ_t`) — banked, real — and withholds OS0 of the *sharp* `μ`. The exact missing estimate is unchanged: **(★)**, the sharp one-sided force-variance upper bound, equivalently the `a`-uniform coarse Poincaré `G″(k→0)≥λ`. G1 remains conditional on L3 alone; a flow-based route reproduces every other ledger line and adds no residue.

**Sharpest line.** The flow rigorously renormalizes the *coupling* and *flowed operators* — and precisely non-renormalizes the *moment bound*. It smooths exactly the UV it would have to certify, at a scale profile that is the identity on the gap mode and mismatched to the tower; reading a sharp-moment bound off it inverts `Φ_t`, whose `e^{ct/a²}` blow-up lands precisely on the Savvidy modes. Wrong half, confirmed — not for lack of rigor, but because a smoothing map bounds what it keeps, never what it removes.
