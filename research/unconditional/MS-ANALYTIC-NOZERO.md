## 4 · Why max/min-modulus and Rouché cannot be the instrument
*Minimum modulus* forbids an interior minimum of `|a|` only if `a` is **already zero-free** (circular); *maximum modulus* is upper-only; `log|a|` is subharmonic and permits `−∞` at isolated zeros. *Rouché* needs a zero-free comparator (the target) **and** control of `a` off the real axis — exactly Kato isolation `Λ−m₁²>0` (the gap). *Monotonicity*: no `β`-monotone atom-coercive functional exists — atom-coercive ⇒ Schur-convex, while `β`-monotone objects (free energy, `a`-charge, `F_T`) are totals/per-state ⇒ Schur-concave/off-edge; opposite Schur sides (PROOF-TRANSPORT-MONOTONE).

The structural reason: Herglotz/Stieltjes/passivity positivity lives in **energy** (`λ=e^{−aE}`; INEQ-T1, PROOF-NEVANLINNA), **not in `β`**. In `β` the only positivity is Perron, anchored at the vacuum; the first excited eigenvector is ⊥ the positive Perron functional and necessarily sign-changing (INEQ-R1), so `a(β)` — an indefinite functional on a positive vector — carries **no sign-protection**. The trace anomaly is blind: the pole Ward is homogeneous in `f₁²` (it cancels), an identity for `g_GGG`, not an inequality on `a` (G2B).
