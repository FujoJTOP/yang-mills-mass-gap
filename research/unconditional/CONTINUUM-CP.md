# CONTINUUM-CP — splitting (CP): [CP-a] single-phase is *entailed by* the isolation, not a separable relaxation; [CP-b] is the whole of (CP)

> 2026-11-23 · PROOF-MODE attack on (CP) of INVENT-TRANSPORT-INEQ, split into **[CP-a]** (single `T=0` confining phase, no transition on `[1/48,2.5]`) and **[CP-b]** (`0⁺⁺` edge Kato-preserved + uniform isolation). **Verdict: the split is logically valid but yields NO partial theorem, because the implication runs the wrong way. `[CP-b] ⇒ [CP-a]`: a uniform gap-plus-isolation forces the infinite-volume state unique (exponential clustering ⇒ no coexistence) and forbids a critical point (bounded correlation length ⇒ no Fisher zeros on `ℝ`), so the spectrum hypothesis the residue needs already delivers single-phase. Conversely `[CP-a] ⇏ [CP-b]`: a single phase may be gapless or dissolve the atom continuously (PROOF-NO-MELT-PHASE), giving no residue. All three routes to `[CP-a]` fail: no `T=0` Polyakov/center order parameter; no Lee–Yang–Fisher theorem for continuous SU(N) (signed Weingarten); complex-β cluster analyticity reaches only the Pringsheim-maximal disk `β≲1/48`, never 2.5. `[CP-a]` is downstream of the gap, not a relaxation of it; the transport's open content is exactly `[CP-b]` = isolation = the upper gap (`I1∧I2`), one rung above the mass gap.**

**1. What `[CP-a]` asks; what is proved.** `[CP-a]` = `f=−(1/V)log Z` real-analytic on `[1/48,2.5]` at infinite volume. *Proved:* finite-`Λ`, `Z_Λ(β)>0` for every real `β` (positive integrand) — no real-axis Fisher zero, any coupling/`V`; `β<1/48` is a single gapped phase (OS78; SZZ23); area law to `β<12/17≈0.71` (Borgs–Seiler). *Not proved, any `β≳0.71`:* nothing — no single-phase theorem, and area law ≠ phase uniqueness. Uniqueness is weaker than the gap and than confinement (uniqueness/STATUS); but no-transition on a closed interval is stronger — it needs the correlation length bounded.

**2. `[CP-b] ⇒ [CP-a]` (load-bearing).** `[CP-b]` gives, uniformly, `m₁≥μ>0` and `Λ−m₁²≥κ>0`. A uniform gap is uniform exponential clustering; clustering ⇒ a unique (extremal) physical DLR state at each `β` ⇒ no first-order coexistence (the `MG⇒limit-unique` step, uniqueness/STATUS); bounded correlation length forbids a second-order point, where the rate `→0` and Fisher zeros pinch the axis. Hence `f` is analytic on the interval: `[CP-a]`.

**3. The three routes — all dead.** *(a) Polyakov/center.* At `T=0` the theory is on `ℝ⁴`: no Polyakov loop, no center order parameter. `Z_N` classifies only finite-`T` deconfinement (`β_c(N_t)→∞`); no bulk transition is bounded. *(b) Lee–Yang–Fisher.* Needs a positive/ferromagnetic representation — available for `Z_N`/`U(1)` by duality, absent for continuous SU(N) (signed Weingarten, PROOF-RANDOM-CLUSTER). *(c) Complex-β cluster `1/48→2.5`.* Radius `1/48`; by Pringsheim the positive-activity disk is maximal, ending on the positive real axis; RG blocking re-imports the Savvidy sign — no domain reaches 2.5 (PROOF-FISHER-ZERO-FREE). Meurice's SU(2) Fisher zeros stay off the axis: evidence, not theorem.

**4. `[CP-b]` is the whole of (CP).** `[CP-b]=I1` (residue `f₁²≥r₀`) `∧ I2` (isolation `Λ−m₁²≥κ`); by the residue–level-gap lemma (GAP-G2-edge) `I1⟺I2` given moments, and `I2` is the upper gap, strictly above `m_g` (PROOF-EDGE-E2, PROOF-ISOLATION). Only `I1` closes, at `β<1/48` (P3); extending it to 2.5 is Clay-adjacent.

**Sharpest line.** The split is a true decomposition but a null relaxation: `[CP-b]⇒[CP-a]`, so the residue's hypothesis entitles single-phase for free, while `[CP-a]` alone gives no residue (a gapped free field has `f₁²=0`) and forbids no continuous dissolution. The three routes stop at the strong-coupling disk. The open content is exactly `[CP-b]=isolation=I1∧I2`; `[CP-a]` is entailed by it, unreachable without it. NET: no partial theorem.
