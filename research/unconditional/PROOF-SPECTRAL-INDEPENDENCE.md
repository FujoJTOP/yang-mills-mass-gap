# PROOF-SPECTRAL-INDEPENDENCE — the one sign-free criterion, and the exact step where the sign returns

> **Verdict. Spectral independence (SI) is the strongest *positivity-free* criterion here. Unlike FKG, random-cluster, and the surface/Weingarten expansions — all sign-blocked because each needs a positive *representation* (PROOF-COMPARISON-FKG, PROOF-RANDOM-CLUSTER, W2) — SI is stated on the original *positive* Wilson measure and needs only bounded-oscillation marginals (PROVE-H2), so its *definition and constant survive* at β≈2.5. It still cannot prove the mass gap: its two consumers split. (T1) η-SI ⇒ Glauber mixing is a theorem, but bounds the *dynamical* generator, not the OS transfer matrix (PROOF-Y3). (T2) η-SI ⇒ zero-free ⇒ SSM ⇒ gap needs a *positive-weight contraction of the signed influence Jacobian* — the non-abelian stand-in for FKG — which fails in the adjoint (Savvidy) sector, where the influence eigenvalue is ≈ −1.1β < 0 (W6). The sign is outsourced, then re-imported at exactly the step that produces a gap. No gap.**

## The criterion, precisely
Links e carry U_e∈G=SU(N); μ_Λ ∝ e^{−S_W}∏dU_e is a probability measure on the compact product G^E — no expansion is taken. Fix a **pinning** τ (a set S of links frozen to group elements) and write μ_τ for the conditional. The **pairwise influence** of link u on link v is the change in v's marginal when u is pinned, μ_v^{τ,u←b}(c)−μ_v^{τ}(c) — an operator on L²_0(G) (on a δ-net, the colour change of Chen–Liu–Mani–Moitra Def. 5.2; δ→0 uniformly by compactness). The **influence matrix** Ψ_τ collects these, and μ is **η-spectrally independent** if λ_max(Ψ_τ) ≤ η for *every* pinning τ (Def. 5.3; Anari–Liu–Oveis Gharan arXiv:1906.01665; AJKPV arXiv:2011.02075). Three advertised outputs have *distinct* hypotheses:
- **(T1) mixing.** η-SI ⇒ O(n log n) Glauber mixing (AJKPV; Chen–Liu–Vigoda arXiv:2001.00303). This is the gap of the *Glauber/down-up* generator.
- **(T2) decay.** η-SI + a **weighted Jacobian contraction** ‖diag(w)Ψ diag(w)⁻¹‖ ≤ 1−δ, w>0 (Chen–Liu–Mani–Moitra §8, Condition 8.2 / Thm 8.5) ⇒ total-influence decay ⇒ strong spatial mixing; equivalently η-SI *in a sector* ⇒ Z_Λ(ζ) zero-free near β ⇒ SSM ⇒ gap.
- The contraction in (T2) is a **Perron–Frobenius positivity** of Ψ: automatic when the influence is entrywise ≥0, i.e. for **attractive / FKG** systems. That is the omitted hypothesis.

## Verification at β≈2.5: the constant survives
**Marginal boundedness holds.** As in PROVE-H2: each link marginal sees 6 plaquettes, each weight ∈[0,2] for every field, so μ_τ is within e^{±2β·O(1)} of Haar *uniformly in the pinning*; every influence entry is a bounded holomorphic function of the pinning and **C_SI<∞** — SI is not vacuous at β=2.5. Because Ψ is *local* (support ≤ plaquette-adjacency), C_SI is set by *local* row sums, not the e^{cV} block constant of H2-globalization: finite and volume-uniform at fixed β, though large (∝ a power of ξ(β) as β↗β_c). **Genuine advance over FKG.**

## The exact failing step
(T2)'s contraction needs a positive Perron vector of Ψ_∂B. Ψ acts on ⊕L²(G), block-diagonalised by irreps; the **adjoint sector**'s eigenvalue is fixed by the action Hessian. With min spec(Hess S_W) ≈ −1.1β (−2.81 at β=2.5; 90–97% of negatives in the fluctuation block — W6), the adjoint influence is
$$\lambda\big(\Psi_{\partial B}^{(\mathrm{adj})}\big)=c\,\min\mathrm{spec}\,(\mathrm{Hess}\,S_W)+o(1)\approx -1.1c\,\beta<0,$$
so the influence is **anti-attractive** in the Savvidy directions: no w>0 makes it contract, and the Perron/positive-mapping argument behind the η-SI zero-free bound has no positive test vector there. The blocked +0.81β (W4) is a 204-dim low-pass slice — necessary, not the full boundary influence; VAR-FINAL's G″(0)≈−0.165<0 carries the same sign. **Exact failing inequality: ∃ δ>0, w>0 with ‖diag(w)Ψ_∂B diag(w)⁻¹‖ ≤ 1−δ — false; the adjoint-sector eigenvalue ≈−1.1cβ<0 breaks every positive weight.**

## Sharpest line
**SI outsources the sign problem: it needs no FKG merely to be *stated* — the positive Wilson measure and bounded-oscillation marginals (PROVE-H2) suffice, so it is the one modern criterion the non-abelian measure does not sign-block outright. But the single step that turns it into a *mass gap* — a positive-weight contraction of the influence Jacobian — is FKG in disguise, and it fails at the adjoint-sector Savvidy directions (λ≈−1.1β<0) exactly where FKG and the Weingarten surface weights already failed.** Even a completed η-SI at β=2.5 yields (T1) rapid mixing, not the transfer-matrix gap; the gap needs (T2), i.e. the non-abelian FKG that does not exist.
