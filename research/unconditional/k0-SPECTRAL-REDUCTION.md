# k0-SPECTRAL-REDUCTION — a certified finite-dimensional spectral reduction for the `k→0` coarse mode?

> 2026-11-23 · 4D pure SU(N) YM. Target: obtain the gap as the zero-momentum coarse mode's covariance norm, `Var_μ(a_0)≤1/λ ⟺ m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` (PROVE-Gpp §5; PROOF-COARSE-POINCARE), by projecting `T=e^{−aH_K}` on `ℋ=L²(G^{E_V},Haar)` onto the collective low-`k` sector (constant mode + a few harmonics) and bounding the tail with Kato/Weyl. **Verdict: the reduction is a legitimate finite-`(V,a)` instrument, nonperturbative, and acid-clean — but it cannot certify the physical gap.** #1, #4 pass narrowly; **#2, #3, #5 fail and are one object: the discarded tail's spectral separation is the transverse mass, i.e. the gap.** The infinite-dimensional tail and `a`-uniformity are not technical nuisances — they *are* the wall.

**The object.** `T` is infinite-dimensional (`E²` diagonal in the rep label with unbounded Casimir, `V_mag` a bounded one-step-fusion multiplication; PROOF-COMPUTER-ASSISTED). Let `P` project on the constant mode plus harmonics `|k|≤2πm/L`, `Q=1−P`; enclose `PTP` (Rayleigh–Ritz + Temple/Kato–Temple) with `Q`-tail correction `η=‖PHQ‖²/(γ−E_max)`.

**Five-point verdict.**
1. **Nonperturbative — PASS.** Rayleigh–Ritz / Temple / Weyl are `β`-uniform, no `g`/`1/N` expansion. Genuine.
2. **Sign-controlling — FAIL.** `T` is a *positive* operator; its compressed spectrum is isospectral-type data, sign-blind to the signed curvature `G″=E[H|a_0]−Var(f|a_0)+W`. The Savvidy-negative directions (90–97%, W6-BE) are high-momentum/fiber — in `Q`; the sign is set by the *conditioned* force variance `−Var(f|a_0)`, built from exactly the discarded fast modes. Dropping them turns `T` positive by fiat — the "inner product/truncation is a free coordinate" vacuity of FORGE5-WILSON-ALGEBRA-POSITIVITY; positivity is no lower bound on a signed Hessian (CERTIFIED-TN #2).
3. **Non-circular — FAIL.** Kato/Weyl needs the retained–discarded separation `γ=dist(spec(PTP),spec(QHQ))`; that transverse mass *is* `m_eff²(k→0)` *is* H1 (PROOF-IR-H1 §4; IDEA-finite-size), and the coupling `‖PHQ‖` is the magnetic one-step fusion, `O(1)` per boundary plaquette, not small. Both tail hypotheses are the gap.
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS (narrow, target-vacuous).** Pure YM non-SUSY; massless Maxwell's `k=0` mode is the exact zero mode, correctly returned gapless, error identically zero in the Gaussian sector. But that corner is the *only* regime of controlled error — the reduction is rigorous precisely where the answer is `0`, so the pass buys nothing toward YM ("succeeds most cleanly where it must fail", CERTIFIED-TN #4).
5. **Uniform `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL, three ways.** *(a) Direction:* a certified `(V,a)` enclosure is an *upper* bound on `Δ_∞=inf_LΔ_L` (PROOF-COMPUTER-ASSISTED; FORGE5-SOLVABLE-CORNER). *(b) Dimension:* soft-mode density `~Vk³`, spacing `(2π/L)²→0`, so "a few harmonics" is `D~V` as `L→∞` — no uniform finite `D`. *(c) `a`-uniformity:* in `z=e^{−aH}`, `z₁=e^{−am}→1`, `z₂−z₁≈a(E₂−m₁)→0`; every enclosure error `∝1/(a·gap)` diverges while the target `λ` is itself `O(a)` — error ≫ target (CONTINUUM-FREDHOLM's coalescence).

**Exact failure — the missing estimate.** Uniform `D₀<∞`, `γ>0` with `dist(spec(P_{D₀}HP_{D₀}),spec(Q_{D₀}HQ_{D₀}))≥γ` and `‖P_{D₀}HQ_{D₀}‖²≤(γ−Λ)(λ−δ)` **for all `(V,a)`**. No such pair exists: `D₀~V`, `γ=m_eff²(k→0)=H1`, and `‖PHQ‖` is the `O(1)`-per-plaquette magnetic coupling. The enclosure cannot be made uniform without already knowing the gap.

**Sharpest line.** The reduction is a finite-dimensional microscope aimed at an object that *is* the retained–discarded separation; as `a→0` the eigenvalue gap collapses to `1` and the soft-mode density grows as `Va⁻³`, so the Kato/Weyl error is always comparable to the target. The tail stones the answer into the estimate: #2, #3, #5 are one wall — **the tail's gap is the gap.**
