# LOC-DETERMINANT — continuing `D_O` past `z₁`: the crude Hadamard wall is a *bad bound*, but factoring the atom leaves exactly uniform eigenvalue-counting above `m₁` (= isolation), and residues are the wrong handle

> 2026-11-23 · Target: analytic-continue `D_O(z)=det(1−zT_O)` across `|z|∈(z₁,z₁u^{−1/2})` using the rigorous `H` from OS/constructive existence, and read off the support datum `Σ_{n≥2}λ_n=O(u²)`. **判词 (verdict, up front): the determinant route does NOT deliver the support datum; it *is* the analytic-continuation wall — but the honest wall is narrower than G2F's exponential objection. G2F bounded `∏_{all}(1+|z|λ_n)=exp(O(u^{−1/2}))`; factoring the atom gives the remainder `D_rest=∏_{n≥2}(1−zλ_n)` with `log|D_rest|≤R·S₂`, `S₂:=Σ_{n≥2}λ_n`, and Rouché then closes on the whole annulus iff the single condition `S₂=o(λ₁)=o(u)` holds. That condition *is* isolation in determinant coordinates. At fixed `L` it is free (discreteness); the only content is `(L,a)`-uniformity = the continuum state density. Residues cannot be bounded uniformly and, being point-values while the obstruction is multiplicity, control the wrong quantity. Scorecard 2/5.**

**1 · What the rigorous `H` actually hands you.** OS/constructive reconstruction gives a self-adjoint `0≤T=e^{−aH}≤1` with vacuum `1`, purely discrete spectrum at finite `T³_L`; hence `T_O=P_OTP_O` is a finite-rank positive contraction, `D_O` a polynomial. `H` delivers *(i)* the zero location `z₁=1/λ₁=e^{am₁}`, *(ii)* the total `Tr T_O=a₀u+O(u²)`. It does **not** deliver the split of `Tr T_O` among eigenvalues — that split is the state density, and a self-adjoint operator constrains nothing at second order beyond its trace. This is the precise sense in which "newly-available rigorous `H`" is necessary but not sufficient: existence of the transfer operator fixes its bottom and its compact trace, not its spectral counting.

**2 · Steelman: the factored Rouché, and why G2F's exponential wall is an artifact.** Write `D_O=(1−zλ₁)·D_rest`, `D_rest=det_{O⊥}(1−zT_O)=∏_{n≥2}(1−zλ_n)`, `D_rest(0)=1`. On `|z|=R`, `log|D_rest|≤Σ_{n≥2}log(1+Rλ_n)≤R·S₂`. Rouché compares `D_lead=1−zλ₁` (one zero at `z₁`) against `D_rest−1`; it certifies *exactly one* zero in `|z|<R` iff `|D_rest−1|<|D_lead|`, i.e. `R S₂<|1−Rλ₁|`. Take `R=u^{−5/4}` (between `z₁=u^{−1}` and `z₂=u^{−p₂}≥u^{−3/2}`): then `|1−Rλ₁|≈u^{−1/4}` and the condition is **`S₂=o(u)=o(λ₁)`** — strict one-state dominance of the compact trace. This is *strictly weaker* than the corpus target `Σ_{n≥2}λ_n=O(u²)` (since `O(u²)⊂o(u)`), and — crucially — it is **not** the `exp(O(u^{−1/2}))` wall: the factor `z₁∼u^{−1}` by which the all-eigenvalue Hadamard over-counts is exactly cancelled by factoring the atom, landing the bound at the Rouché margin. So the steelman is real: the determinant *does* isolate the atom across the entire annulus, with z-plane margin `z₂/z₁∼u^{−1/2}` robust, provided the eigenvalue trace above `m₁` is `o(λ₁)`.

**3 · Attack: that condition *is* the support datum, and residues hit the wrong variable.** `S₂=∫_{(m₁,∞)}e^{−aE}dN(E)`, `N` the degeneracy-weighted eigenvalue-counting function. Because the zeros of `D_O` are `e^{aE_n}` with **multiplicity = deg `E_n`, independent of the overlap** `f_n²`, the annulus counts *every* eigenvalue in `(m₁,Λ)` — a tiny-weight atom at `ε∈(m₁,Λ)` is still a zero and Rouché sees it (the determinant is strictly stronger than any weighted trace here). Hence `S₂=o(λ₁)` is literally "no eigenvalue cluster in `(m₁,Λ)` of trace-weight `≳λ₁`," i.e. `Λ−m₁²≥κ` *with multiplicity control* — verbatim `I2`. It is not weaker than isolation; it **is** isolation in trace coordinates. At fixed `L` this is automatic (discrete spectrum, `λ₁` separated by a finite gap): the determinant is a *perfect* probe per box, and G2F's "no Rouché" is only the lossy all-eigenvalue bound. The whole content is uniformity in `(L,a)`: as `L→∞` the density above `m₁` grows, `S₂/λ₁→O(1)`, the Rouché margin `→0`, and `z₂` coalesces (CONTINUUM-FREDHOLM). KL positivity `ν≥0` cannot bound multiplicities — a positive measure puts a cluster anywhere (G2B §1). And the **residues** `N_O(z_k)=f_k²·cofactor` cannot help: overlap point-values (`f_k²≥0` is a *lower* bound only) while Rouché must control zero *multiplicities*, a property of `T_O`'s spectrum unrelated to the residues. A uniform residue bound is thus neither available (positivity is Schur-concave, upper-only on totals) nor sufficient — it controls the wrong quantity.
