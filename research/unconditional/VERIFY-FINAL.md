## (1) Block-local Holley–Stroock — CORRECT
`dμ(·|U′) ∝ e^{−βH_fib}dHaar(ξ)` on `G^{n_B}`. Product Haar satisfies LSI: SU(N) compact with bi-invariant metric has Ric>0, so each factor has LSI (Bakry–Émery; Gross tensorization gives the product constant `C_G`). Holley–Stroock (J. Stat. Phys. 46 (1987) 1159) gives LSI constant `≤ C_G·e^{osc V}` for a bounded-oscillation tilt. Each plaquette term `1−½Re tr U_p ∈[0,2]`, so `osc(βH_fib) ≤ 2βN_p(B)`, uniform in `U′`. LSI ⇒ Poincaré on the same constant (safe direction: the true Poincaré is `C/2`). Hence `Λ_UV=e^{−2βN_p}/C_G>0`. **Qualification:** the exact conditional is `e^{−βH_fib}·J·dHaar` where `J` is the blocking Jacobian (block-averaging does not factor Haar). `J` is smooth and bounded above/below on compact G, so it merely shifts the exponent by `osc(log J)`; positivity is unaffected. Block-local only; global fiber and uniformity-in-`b` remain open, as stated.
