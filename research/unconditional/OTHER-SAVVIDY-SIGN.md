## 0. Which wall — microscopic or coarse?
The "10× short" compares the **microscopic** Hessian `min=−1.1β=−2.8` (W6, all of `TM`) with `W_geo≈N/12≈0.2–0.34`; correct, and it kills every **pointwise-Loewner** tool (PROOF-SAVVIDY-WGEO-DOMINATION). ## 1. Lane (a) — self-dual excess: true locally, dead under parity

No instanton gas (dilute or not) is SD-dominated at `θ=0` — the same parity wall as k0-TOPOLOGICAL-COUPLING §III. SD-domination needs `θ≠0` or fixed `Q` — a *different theory* (#3).
