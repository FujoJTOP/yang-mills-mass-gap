# C-BBS-MARGINALLOG — porting BBS's marginal-log resummation onto the capacity trap: the log ports as a theorem, the "dominating" large-field bound does not, and the residue is (★)=H1

> **判词 (verdict).** BBS's marginal-log resummation has **three separable mechanisms; one and a half port.** **(A)** The *log itself* (the "`Σλ_j²~log J`" wall) is not a wall in gauge theory: asymptotic freedom resums it into the running coupling `1/g_j²=2β₀j`, and every residual power is absolutely summable `Σ_j g_j⁴=ζ(4)/(16β₀⁴)<∞`, `δg_J=O(J^{−3/2}log J)→0` — a **theorem given L2** (AF-analogue of BBS's log-resummation). **(B)** The *method* — do **not** absolute-sum the BPHZ trees (false by the renormalon), prove T_uni as a **flow trapping region** — **ports exactly**: it is BBS's deepest structural insight and it is `a,L`-uniform. **(C)** The *large-field bound* that makes BBS's trap contract on the large-field half **breaks**: φ⁴'s large field is *more* convex (`−Δ+m²+12λφ²⪰m²`); YM's is *less* convex (`−D²+2ad F_B⪰−2g|F_B|`). WSTC substitutes for BBS's polynomial LF estimate **only on the value tail**, not on the Hessian sign. On top of the capacity/Dirichlet trap the two machines (UV log-flow, IR capacity) are each correct on their half and hand back the **same** one-signed inequality at the crossover, which at `k→0` is H1.

**§1 · The log is the easy half — and it ports.** BBS track the exact marginal trajectory because `Σλ_j²` diverges; the gauge marginal `Tr F²` is the *same dim-4 class* and the divergence is *exactly* the one-loop running: on the RT `β(a)=2N/g²(a)`, `g_j²=1/(2β₀j)+O(j⁻²)`, so `Σ_j g_j²∼(2β₀)⁻¹log J` is absorbed identically and `Σ_j g_j⁴` converges (PROOF-G1-BPHZ §2b; PROOF-MULTISCALE §1). The operator space is finite — BRST (ghost-#0, dim 4) leaves `{Tr F², Tr F F̃}`, `θ=Tr F F̃` a total derivative that drops — so there is **no infinite counterterm tower**, one coupling and `Z_j`, not the scalar `λ_j` (Tr F² is kinetic+cubic+quartic). On the Borel plane this is the *UV* branch `u=−2n/β₀` on the negative axis, where no obstruction sits and whose resummation is the summable log. **Ports: (A), cleanly.**

**§2 · The method ports; the absolute sum does not.** BBS never bound the tree sum absolutely — they *are* the trap. Gauge inherits this precisely: the literal `Σ_{trees}|weight|` is **false** (the asymptotic renormalon), so T_uni must be the flow trapping region `‖R_{j+1}‖_X≤e^{−c}‖R_j‖_X+Cg_j⁴`, `c,C` independent of `j,a,L` (PROOF-G1-BPHZ §2c; W4 T1–T3). This structural answer to the renormalon — *resum by tracking the flow, not by summing the series* — **ports (B) exactly**, and it is the genuine BBS transplant.

**§3 · The large-field positivity breaks on the indefinite Hessian.** BBS's one-step contraction survives into the large field because polynomial convexity makes the LF Hessian **positive** (`+12λφ²`), so `e^{−c}` contracts on *both* halves. YM's LF Hessian is `−D²+2ad F_B`, `λ_min=−2g_j|F|<−μ_j²` on the Savvidy cone `|k|<√(2g_j|F_B|)`, so `e^{−c}` becomes **amplification** there. A **scaling no-go** seals it: marginal closure *pins* `‖R_j‖_X≲g_j²` (absorbability by the `Cg_j⁴` contraction, PROOF-TRAPPING §1), while the cone depth is `≳μ_j²`; along the UV tower `g_j²/μ_j²→0`, so no jet/Hessian norm built from the remainder can sign the cone (FORGE5 §1). Large field makes it *worse* — depth `∝g|F|`, negative-mode count `∝∫|F|³` both grow with `|F|` (PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN). BBS's coercivity `∫Tr F²≥0` has no gauge analogue that bounds the *second variation*.

**§4 · Does WSTC substitute for the polynomial LF bound?** **Partially.** WSTC (Ward–Stein transport cone bound) is the correctly-signed, non-positivity, volume-uniform large-field estimate BBS gets from `λφ⁴≥0`: `Var_{μ0}(f·1_{C_κ})≤osc²μ0(C_κ)≤(cβκ_max)²e^{−cβ(κ²−κ_typ²)N_□}→0` (ABC-NEW-INEQ §2). That bounds the **variance mass** of the high-field cone — the value-tail `(◇)` role. It does **not** supply the Hessian sign: the cone's `δ²(g_j^{−2}∫TrF²)=−2g_j|F|<0` is untouched, and — decisively — WSTC's exponent vanishes at `‖F‖≈κ_typ`, *exactly where the negative variance lives* (W6-BE: 21–42 % negatives, `−1.12β`, 90–97 % in the UV block). So WSTC fills BBS's LF *value* slot on the high-field tail, not the LF *convexity* slot on the typical-action high-momentum cell that decides the sign.

**§5 · Capacity trap ⊥ log-flow; same lock.** The capacity/Dirichlet trap (POTENTIAL-THEORY-CAPACITY) acts at the **IR** `k→0` zero mode (convex, positive Dirichlet min; sign free but vacuous in finite volume; uniform floor = H1's tail-coercivity). The BBS log resummation acts on the **UV** tower (uniform in `a`, independent of the IR box gap `2π/L`). They do not fight — they are a **two-ended** construction, both rigorous where rigorous. But by the exact Ward identity `G″·Var(a)=1` (iff the coarse marginal is Gaussian), *both* reduce to `Var(a)≤1/λ` at typical action. The log-flow discharges existence (`L3` minus (★)); the capacity trap discharges the free-field discriminator; neither discharges the sign. The IR renormalon `u=+2n/β₀` ambiguity *is* `⟨Tr F²⟩`, and the trap's convergence edge coincides with the ambiguity's maximum at `k→0` (CONTINUUM-ASYMPTOTIC-BEYOND).

**Sharpest line.** The marginal **log** is the renormalon's UV face and it resums to a theorem; the marginal **sign** is its IR face and it does not. BBS ports its *resummation* and its *method*, but not its *convex large field* — and the capacity trap cannot lend it, because capacity makes the sign free where it is vacuous and the Arrhenius floor where it is not is H1. What remains is one inequality on the typical-action high-momentum cell.
