# FORGE5-DUAL-SUPERCONDUCTOR — can a rigorous, non-circular monopole-gas / abelian-projection computation lower-bound the 0⁺⁺ gap?

> 2026-11-23 · Fifth-constraint forge of the **constructive** 't Hooft–Mandelstam dual superconductor — the *direct monopole-gas* route, **distinct** from `FORGE5-DUALITY` (the killed *exact-duality/isomorphism* route). Does a direct plasma computation escape the isomorphism-circularity, or is the channel fatal?
> **Verdict (判词): 2 of 5 — passes 1, 4; fails 2, 3, 5.** Fatal and coupled: **#3 (circularity)**, **#5 (lower bound in the wrong channel)**; **#2** fails on the wrong object. **The failure is circularity + channel — *not* the free field**, which passes.

**The forged tool (strongest form).** Abelian-project to `U(1)^{N−1}`; the monopoles form a gas whose IR is a Debye–Meissner plasma with `m_D>0`. Since `tr F² = ±tr(F_D)²` exactly (ε-duality is a linear identity on quadratic invariants), the neutral interpolator is a **two-dual-photon** object, so a massive dual Proca gives a connected `(F_D)²` correlator decaying at threshold `2m_D`. A cluster bound `|⟨trF²(x)trF²(0)⟩_c| ≤ A e^{−2m_D|x|}` would make the 0⁺⁺ decay rate `≥ 2m_D`: a genuine **lower** bound (correct direction), uniform if `m_D` is. Free field: no monopoles, `m_D=0` → "≥0", acid test passed. It breaks at three joints.

**1. Nonperturbative — PASS.** A monopole plasma resums an infinite IR topological-defect class; no finite-order UV loop expansion. (Contrast `FORGE5-DUALITY` #1, where the exact *dual object* does not exist; here the nonperturbative object does.)

**2. Sign — FAIL.** The natural positivity `m_D²>0` is the *magnetic / dual-photon screening* mass, not the electric Savvidy-cone IR Hessian `G″=S−D`. Transporting one to the other needs an exact 4D electric–magnetic duality, absent in non-abelian YM (`GAP-G2-magnetic` §4); and the pointwise Savvidy bottom `−g|B|` is unbounded (`PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN`), which no IR collective mass caps. Wrong object.

**3. Non-circular — FAIL (fatal).** No 4D gauge-invariant monopole order parameter exists (Elitzur; Singer/Gribov — the projection is gauge-dependent), and the rigorous dilute-monopole gas converges *only* in the **Coulomb** phase (4D compact `U(1)`: Guth; Fröhlich–Spencer) or in **3D** — never in the confining phase it must describe. Where `m_D>0` the expansion is non-rigorous, and "the monopoles condense" **is** dual Meissner ⟺ 't Hooft-loop perimeter law ⟺ `W1_1` ⟺ electric area law = confinement (`GAP-G2-magnetic` §3). Deriving condensation = deriving the uniform connected-decay = the 4D dual-Meissner theorem = the gap. Input = answer.

**4. Non-SUSY + fails free field — PASS.** No Seiberg–Witten; free `U(1)` Coulomb has zero monopole density, `m_D=0` → `f₁²=0`, correctly no positive gap. Stronger than `FORGE5-DUALITY` #4 (whose dual is *undefined* for the free field); here the tool is defined and does vanish. Caveat: the pass does not transfer — `m_D>0` needs the (circular) condensation.

**5. Lower bound `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` uniform — FAIL (fatal).** Beyond (3): even granting the operator identity, the free-dual-Proca threshold `2m_D` is an *upper* bound on the correlator, violated *downward* by attractive bound states; and the physical 0⁺⁺ sits at `≈(3.5–4)√σ`, a distinct, heavier composite (Morningstar–Peardon) tied to the dual scale only by measurement. `tr F²` is N-ality-0 and **provably blind** to the 1-form center symmetry carrying the dual string (`PROOF-1FORM-ANOMALY`), so a dual-channel bound never becomes a 0⁺⁺ bound.

**Constructive ≠ non-circular.** A *direct* plasma free-energy computation, unlike an exact isomorphism, was hoped to sidestep "duality = gap." It does not: the only convergent monopole-gas regime is the *wrong* phase (massless, no gap), and the right phase is exactly the strong-coupling wall. The route is non-circular *only* at `m_D=0`.

**Sharpest line.** A rigorous monopole gas exists only where it confines nothing (Coulomb) or in the wrong dimension; where `m_D>0` it is non-rigorous *and* its positivity is the dual string's (1-form, `√σ`), blind to the neutral `tr F²`. The dual superconductor survives the free-field acid test — the free field being the one case with no monopoles and a correct zero — yet cannot turn that zero into a uniform positive 0⁺⁺ lower bound. **5 条过不齐：确切断于 #3（单极凝聚=禁闭，循环）与 #5（错道下界，非 `0⁺⁺`），并 #2（磁屏蔽质量≠Savvidy 电 Hessian 号）；通过的 #1、#4（方法非微扰、自由场正确返零）都不触及正界。**
