# FORGE5-CONSTRUCTIVE — the trap cannot sign the Savvidy cone: the remainder is `O(g²)`, the cone depth is `O(μ²)`, so the sign is the measure's (★)=H1

> **Verdict (判词).** Two of the five hold, one holds only on its UV half, and **#2 (sign) and #5 (uniform lower bound) fail — and they are a single object: (★)=H1.** A completed BBS/Balaban-style construction (trap + AF log-resummation + cone-emptying + Lemma B + fixed-volume OS) is nonperturbative (#1), non-SUSY and acid-test-clean (#4), and non-circular on its existence half (#3). Making the trap's Hessian-norm *sign-controlling on the cone* — the one step that would cross the Savvidy sign **inside** the construction — is impossible **as a matter of scaling, not of effort**: marginal closure *forces* `‖R_j‖_X ≲ g_j²`, while the cone's leading second variation has depth `≳ μ_j²`; on the UV tower `g_j²/μ_j² → 0`, so no jet norm built from the large-field remainder can flip the sign. The cone sign is the *measure's*, `Var ≤ E[H]+W−δ` with `Var≥0` subtracting — exactly (★), and at `k→0` exactly H1.

**The construction attempt (forge).** Use the LARGE-FIELD structure to sign the cone: weight the trap norm by the coercive action `Q=∫TrF²` and by the positive Hessian part `H_+`, so the large field supplies a coercive mass and penalizes the negative spectral directions; the one-step map would be `‖R_{j+1}‖ ≤ e^{−c}‖R_j‖ + Cg_j⁴` with `e^{−c}` carrying the cone sign, i.e. require the trapped second variation `δ²K_j = g_j^{−2}(−D²+2ad F_B) + δ²R_j ⪰ δ>0` on `{2g_j|F|>μ_j²}`.

**Exact failure — a scaling no-go.** The trap `𝔗={0<g_j≤g*, ‖R_j‖_X≤rg_j², 2g_j|F|>μ_j²}` fixes `‖R‖ ≤ rg²` *not by choice but by the marginal BBS balance*: the quadratic `R²` must be absorbable by the `Cg_j⁴` contraction, which pins `‖R‖≲g²` (PROOF-TRAPPING-REGION §1). The jet norm dominates the second variation, `|δ²R_j| ≤ ‖R_j‖_X e^{−Q/4g_j²}/ℓ = O(g_j²)` — the coercive weight makes it *smaller* on the large-field cone. Meanwhile the leading term has `λ_min = −2g_j|F| < −μ_j²` on the cone. On the UV tower `μ_j=Λe^j` grows, `g_j²~1/(2β₀j)` shrinks, so
`δ²R_j / |λ_min(cone)| = O(g_j²/μ_j²) → 0.`
The remainder is powerless: **no jet/Hessian norm built from `R_j` and the large field signs the cone in the UV.** The large field makes it *worse*: depth `∝g_j|F|` and negative-mode count `∝∫|F|³` both grow with `|F|` (PROOF-SAVVIDY-EXACT-SIGN); the only large-field controls are the value-tail `e^{−cμ⁴/g⁴}` (◇) and trace-half-positivity `Tr H_−≤Tr H_+` (aggregate, measured to saturation `0.94`, no gap). Neither bounds `Var(G)`. At the IR end `μ_j→2π/L` the remainder *is* comparable — but there the cone half is the `k→0` mode, where `G″→E[H]=` bare IR curvature, i.e. H1=G2. Either way the cone sign lives in the measure, not the construction: the missing inequality is `Var_μ(⟨v,∇S⟩|0) − E_μ[H_vv|0] < W_full` (◆), `⟺ Var(a)≤1/δ ⟺ m_eff²(k→0)≥δ`.

**Five-point verdict.**
1. **Nonperturbative: PASS.** Trap + AF resummation `Σg_j⁴=ζ(4)/16β₀⁴` is a genuine RG; the sign is BPHZ-invisible (PROOF-G1-BPHZ §4).
2. **Controls SIGN: FAIL.** `δ²R_j=O(g²) ≪ λ_min(cone)=O(μ²)`; depth/count grow with field; large field gives only a value-tail + aggregate trace, never an upper bound on `Var`. This is (★).
3. **Non-circular: PASS on UV/existence** ((★) strictly stronger, `⊥` gap); **but #5's `k→0` step is H1=the gap** (rename, zero slack, PROOF-IR-H1).
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field: PASS.** Pure YM; the only provable positivity `W_geo≥0` (`Φ″≥N/12`, nonabelian Haar) vanishes for the free field ⇒ `G″=0`, `f₁²=0` (acid-clean).
5. **Lower bound `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ`: FAIL.** At the soft mode the required positivity is exactly H1.

**Sharpest line.** The trap is small-field-complete and its only margin is the Haar curvature; every estimate but one is banked. The one it cannot win by construction is the cone sign: marginal closure forces `‖R‖≲g²`, Savvidy depth is `μ²≫g²`, so the remainder can no more bend the cone than a value weight can — the bending on the cone is the measure's, and that is (★)=H1. The construction crosses the marginal **log** (it closes) but hits the marginal **sign** exactly where the measure, not the action, decides it.
