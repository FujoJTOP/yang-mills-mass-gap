# H1-T2-AQFT — operator-algebraic / algebraic QFT as a route to the gap

> 2026-11-23 · **Verdict: AQFT is the sharpest *statement layer* for the gap, not an independent lever.** Its modular structure (Tomita–Takesaki, Bisognano–Wichmann, KMS) is *kinematic and mass-blind* — true of gapless free fields — so it cannot lower-bound the transverse residue \(f_1^2\ge r_0\) in 4D. Where AQFT "proves a gap," the mass is exactly solvable or an **input**; every quantitative modular tool lands on the same sign / edge / localization wall. No fabrication.

**1. Modular structure is kinematic, mass-blind.** A Haag–Kastler net with a vacuum gives, by Tomita–Takesaki, a modular group. Bisognano–Wichmann (1975/76) identify the *wedge* modular flow with the Lorentz boost, \(J=\mathrm{CPT}\cdot R\), so the vacuum is KMS at \(\beta=2\pi\) (Araki; Haag–Hugenholtz–Winnink); local algebras are generically type III₁ (Fredenhagen), spectrum \(\Delta=[0,\infty)\) — all true of the **free massless** field. The modular generator is the boost, continuous even when gapped: the "modular gap" is **not** the mass gap.

**2. What AQFT proves — the mass is input.** P(φ)₂: Glimm–Jaffe–Spencer prove the Wightman axioms and an isolated one-particle mass (weak coupling) — constructive/OS, not modular. YM₂: exact (Migdal; Witten) with rigorous measure (Driver; Sengupta); gap explicit, but 2D. 1+1D integrable models: Lechner's factorizing-S-matrix construction uses modular localization + nuclearity (Buchholz–d'Antoni–Longo; Buchholz–Lechner), but supplies the **massive spectrum as input** — nuclearity buys locality/Reeh–Schlieder, *not* the gap. The free massless field is gapless yet satisfies split/nuclearity/BW. In every gapped case the mass is **solvable or assumed**.

**3. Where it stops for 4D YM — re-encode, not a new door.** No constructed 4D YM net exists (the G1 wall); modular theory has nothing to act on. Split/nuclearity is a **UV** condition (finite local degrees of freedom) — wrong energy end. Particle/sector theorems run the opposite way: Haag–Ruelle / Haag–Swieca need a mass shell; Buchholz–Fredenhagen derive particle structure *from* a gap. DHR / soliton sectors are nonlocal sector/charge data (`χ_t`), no edge mass; for pure 4D YM the physical sector is trivial under confinement — the B1 centre / 't Hooft objection. The one different handle, BGL modular localization, *reconstructs a free field of prescribed mass*: \(m\) is a parameter, never selected, no residue produced. The wall is relabelled: transmutation becomes "an atom / mass shell exists" \(=H^*\).

**4. Can any modular statement lower-bound \(f_1^2\) in 4D? No known one.** **Reeh–Schlieder** gives qualitative cyclicity only — no quantitative overlap, blind to atom-vs-continuum. **KMS/BW** are mass-blind (\(m=0\), spectrum \([0,\infty)\)). **Split/nuclearity** give *upper* bounds on local level density. **QNEC** (strongest modular-derived inequality — Balakrishnan et al. 2019; Ceyhan–Faulkner 2019) lower-bounds **local** \(\langle T_{kk}\rangle\) in gapless CFTs; the edge is a volume-delocalized \(k\to0\) mode (GAP-G2-mass), so it hits the *localization* wall — wrong channel. And \(\nu\ge0\) (reflection positivity) makes every modular-positivity total an **upper** bound on \(f_1^2\) (LEVER §0B; GAP-IN11 moment lemma).

The missing object is unchanged: a **same-channel nonperturbative coercion** \(T\le C\,f_1^2\), i.e. \(H^*(r_0)\). AQFT gives the sharpest *definition* — gap ⇔ an atom in the KL measure ⇔ a mass shell exists (Haag–Swieca; Buchholz–Fredenhagen) — and no lower bound. **Correct language, same wall.**
