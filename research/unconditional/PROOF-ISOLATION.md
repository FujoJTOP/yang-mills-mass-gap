# PROOF-ISOLATION — attempting L1 (= I1∧I2) for 4D lattice SU(N): the GJS mechanism reaches the atom, not its residue

> 2026-11-23 · Attempted GJS/Bałaban–Jaffe proof of the isolation lemma for lattice SU(N), SZZ regime (`β<1/48`, λ>96), fixed `a`. **Verdict: no theorem.** L1 = `I1∧I2` (Theorem A) splits into **L1a** (`I2`: spectral gap `Λ−m₁²≥δ` above the lightest atom) and **L1b** (`I1`: `f₁²≥r₀>0`, volume-uniform). The strong-coupling **energy grading proves the physics of both** — the lightest glueball sits `~0.5 m_g` below the first 0⁺⁺ excitation, `m_g~c ln(1/u)→∞` — but the rigorous volume-uniform two-exponential bound is **not closed**. The sole missing input (P3) is the *resummed positive residue of the composite `tr F²`*, where 4D non-abelian leaves 2D P(φ)₂ at four sharp points. Sharpening: the operative `Λ` is the **excited glueball `~1.5 m_g`** (physically `1.56 m_{0⁺⁺}`), not the two-glueball `2m_{0⁺⁺}` — the C/P-blind tower fills up to `2m_g`.

**Setup.** `T⁴_L`, spacing `a`, Wilson action, character variable `u→0` at strong coupling, `T=e^{−aH}` (`0≤T≤1`). SZZ give `m₁=m_{0⁺⁺}≥m(β)>0` uniform in `L` — the **gap only**. `O` = the `0⁺⁺`(A1g,C=+)-projected single plaquette `N⁻¹Re tr U_p` on one time slice; `C(t)=⟨O(t)O(0)⟩_c`, KL measure `ν≥0`, `f₁²=ν({m₁²})`.

**1. L1, precisely.** ∃`β₁,r₀,δ>0` (`δ≥c m_g²`) s.t. ∀`β<β₁`, all `L`: (i) *atom* `ν({m₁²})=f₁²≥r₀`; (ii) *isolation* `Λ:=inf supp(ν−f₁²δ_{m₁²})`, `Λ−m₁²≥δ`; (iii) equivalently `C(t)=f₁² e^{−m₁t}(1+η(t))`, `|η(t)|≤B e^{−δ't}`. This is `I1∧I2`; L1 ⇒ `f₁²>0`, and (ii) buys the residue–level–gap resale.

**2. Polymer expansion; leading overlap.** The strong-coupling expansion grades configurations by a **cost** (a power of `u`; mass additive in loop size). `tr F²` creates a **single** fundamental flux loop, mass `a m_g~c₁ ln(1/u)→∞`. `C(t)` is a sum over polymers (flux tubes spanning `[0,t]`); the **leading single-polymer** term is the one-tube amplitude — **positive at leading order** (a real overlap `⟨G|O|0⟩=O(1)` squared times a positive group weight). A `2×1` loop rides at `~1.5 m_g`; two-plaquette configurations cost `u^{2c₁}`, support `s≥(2m_g)²`.

**3. GJS argument.** Cluster-expand `C(t)`, grade by cost: the minimal spanning family is the one-glueball tube; every other family has support `≥m₁+c m_g` (excited loop) or `≥2m_g` (two-particle). The gap is a *whole power of u*, so `δ~m_g→∞` — isolation holds at every order in `u`; the leading grading is unambiguous. **Conditional skeleton.** Grant (P1) a convergent expansion of the *connected composite* correlator with this grading, and (P2) the two-polymer remainder bounded with support `≥(2m_g)²` (d=4 gives `poly(t)e^{−2m_g t}`, harmless). Then L1 ⟺ (P3); grant (P3) ⇒ **Theorem (conditional):** `f₁²≥r₀>0`, uniform in `L`, `β<β₁`, fixed `a`.

**4. (P3) — the exact obstruction.** (P3) is the volume-uniform positivity/size of the *resummed* one-particle cluster weight. 4D non-abelian leaves 2D P(φ)₂ at four points:
1. **Composite, no free-field anchor.** GJS's φ is elementary, free residue `Z_free>0`, continuously deformed; `tr F²` has free residue **0** (a two-gluon cut) and is, at `g=0`, the analogue of `:φ²:`. The residue exists *only* after confinement — "binding manufactures it," no continuity shortcut.
2. **Binding, not dressing.** The 2D particle is φ-dressed; the glueball is a *bound* flux loop; L1b is its stability.
3. **Non-abelian sign mixing.** SU(N) character coefficients alternate, so terms are not positive (unlike positive-coupling φ⁴); RP gives only `ν≥0`, and `f₁²>0` needs a nonvanishing computation, not a sign.
4. **Non-protected threshold, C/P blind.** In 0⁺⁺, C and P cannot separate `gg` from `(gg)(gg)` (both C+P+), and the excited-glueball tower is likewise 0⁺⁺ — so the operative `Λ` is the **excited loop `~1.5 m_g`**, not `2m_{0⁺⁺}`; the coarser target "no 0⁺⁺ until `2m_g`" is **false** (support resumes at the excited loop), leaving only the correct I2 — a gap to that excitation, `δ≈0.5 m₁` — fixed by power-counting, not symmetry.

**Exact missing estimate (P3).** `f₁² = lim_{t→∞} e^{m₁t} C(t) ≥ r₀ > 0` uniformly in `L` (then `a`): the volume-uniform **lower** bound on the connected `tr F²` correlator — the direction SZZ clustering (an upper bound) does not supply; equivalently the resummed one-tube weight `≥r₀` across the SU(N) character sum.

**Sharpest line.** The strong-coupling energy grading proves isolation *kinematically* (`δ~m_g²→∞`); what fails is the volume-uniform positivity of the **composite** operator's residue — no free-field anchor, no termwise sign — so it is not a corollary of SZZ and is not obtained here. The sharpening lands: `Λ` is the C/P-blind excited glueball at `~1.5 m_g`, and the F1SQ target `2m_{0⁺⁺}` overshoots the actual gap.
