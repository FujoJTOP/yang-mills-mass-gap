# PROOF-TOPOLOGICAL-DATA — persistence of the transfer spectrum: a stable barcode of the very gap it cannot bound

> 2026-11-23 · TDA / persistent-homology forge on the gap. **Verdict: not a new tool — an exact, stable re-expression.** Under the heat/energy filtration the persistence module of the transfer operator $T=e^{-aH}$ is canonically the Källén–Lehmann measure $\nu_\Omega$: the lowest bar's birth is the gap $m_1$, its atom is $f_1^2$. Because $\sigma(H)\subseteq[0,\infty)$ is **0-dimensional**, the barcode has only $H_0$, every bar $[E_i,\infty)$ never dies, so "persistence" is vacuous as a finiteness statement, and the data it carries **is** $\nu_\Omega$ — inheriting GAP-G2-edge's non-determination molecule-for-molecule. Bottleneck stability is a *continuity* bound, sign-blind: it cannot raise a 0-bar to a $\delta$-bar without a $\ge\delta$ input, and uniformity-in-$(L,a)$ is the gap. **Acid test: at the only regulator a barcode is computable, the free massless lattice field has $E_1(L)=\omega(2\pi/L)>0$ — a positive, "persistent" bar — yet $f_1^2=0$; it reaches 0 only in the $L\to\infty$ limit the tool cannot take.** Persistence visualizes the gap; it does not lower-bound it.

## 1. Filtration and module — the canonical identity
Fix $\beta,L,a$; $0\le T=e^{-aH}\le1$. The heat filtration is $P_t=\mathbb 1_{[0,t]}(H)$, monotone $0\le P_s\le P_t\le1$, with inclusions. For a gauge-invariant interpolator $\Omega$ the rank function is $t\mapsto\|\mathbb 1_{[0,t]}(H)\Omega\|^2=\int_0^t d\nu_\Omega(E)$, so the **module is $\nu_\Omega$**: under the decomposition theorem its barcode is $\{[E,\infty):E\in\operatorname{supp}\nu_\Omega\}$ in degree 0, empty in degree $\ge1$; the lowest positive birth is $m_1=\inf(\operatorname{supp}\nu_\Omega\setminus\{0\})$, its atom is $f_1^2$. (RG-scale variant: graph eigenvalue branches $E_i(b)$ over block scale $b$; bars track branches; a branch crossing 0 $=$ gap along the RG trajectory $=$ IN-9 control.)

## 2. A "persistent bar" is not a lower bound
$\sigma(H)\subseteq[0,\infty)$ is totally disconnected, so $H_k=0$ for $k\ge1$ and *every* $H_0$ bar is a half-line: nothing dies. Hence "a bar surviving all scales" holds for **every** eigenvalue — including the uncountable bars of a gapless continuum. Finite-length bars require an auxiliary space (a Rips/density filtration), but that space is a deterministic function of $\nu_\Omega$, carrying no data beyond it: the barcode $\equiv$ the spectrum. Reading the lowest birth requires $\sigma(H)$; and $\nu_\Omega$ alone cannot pin its edge (GAP-G2-edge moment lemma). A finite barcode threshold is a moment-like truncation — the same wall.

## 3. Stability is continuity, not positivity
Bottleneck stability $d_B\le\|f-g\|_\infty$ moves bars by at most the perturbation; it cannot make a $\delta$-bar from a 0-bar unless the theory itself moves by $\ge\delta$ — and the free$\to$YM distance is $O(1)$ (the whole nonperturbative gap). Regulator-independence of the barcode is uniform finite-size scaling $+$ $a\to0$ at $\beta=2.5$: the gap and G1. Total persistence $\sum\ell_i^p$ is spreading-monotone (concave) — the Schur-convex sign obstruction of EDGE-$f_1^2$. As a *stable* invariant it is locally constant in spirit: like INVENT-NEW-INDEX's rigidity no-go it cannot "switch on" at finite $\beta$; the gap slides continuously off the free value.

**Acid-test result.** The free massless lattice theory has explicit transfer spectrum with lightest momentum mode $E_1(L)=\omega(2\pi/L)>0$ (e.g. $2\sin(\pi/L)\sim2\pi/L$): its barcode's lowest bar is **positive for the free field at every finite $L$**, while the continuum free field has $f_1^2=0$, $\nu$ absolutely continuous, $E_1\to0$. The construction therefore does **not** fail for the free field at any computable regulator; it distinguishes YM only through a limit equivalent to the theorem — exactly as the fixed-$a$ determinant zero coalesces into the KL edge (CONTINUUM-FREDHOLM). It passes the acid test *only* in the vacuous reading "bar $=$ gap." The $G''(0)$ version is identical: the barcode is the curvature spectrum, giving no bound.

**Sharpest line.** The transfer operator's persistence module is the Källén–Lehmann measure in a barcode; the lowest bar *is* the gap and the atom *is* $f_1^2$, so persistence can no more lower-bound the gap than $\nu$ can pin its edge. A 1-D spectrum has 0-dimensional persistence — only $H_0$, all bars infinite — so "a bar surviving all scales" is every eigenvalue, free or interacting; the sole non-vacuous reading needs uniform-in-$(L,a)$ control, which is the gap. Honest: a faithful, stable *visualization*; not a lever. What survives is a structural no-go — the spectrum is 0-dimensional, so its persistence is counting.
