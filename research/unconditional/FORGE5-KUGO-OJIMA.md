# FORGE5-KUGO-OJIMA — the confinement criterion is the wrong channel, and the honest surrogate is the gap renamed

> 2026-11-23 · FORGE5 target: a **non-circular theorem** "`u(0)=−1` (or a gauge-invariant surrogate) ⟹ `0⁺⁺` gap", via BRST/quartet completeness, with `u(0)=−1` established **independently** (strong coupling, or a Ward identity), so that the confinement→gap step is the theorem and not the hypothesis. **VERDICT: cannot satisfy all 5.** The transfer is a **channel error**: `u(0)=−1` is the Kugo–Ojima *color-confinement* witness — it certifies that *charged* (colored) states are removed from `H_phys=ker Q/im Q`. The lightest `0⁺⁺` glueball is already a color singlet, already in `ker Q/im Q`; the quartet mechanism has nothing to remove from it and no scale to weigh it. Making the surrogate honest (the OS-positive `tr F²` edge) is *literally the gap renamed*. And `u(0)=−1` is itself unproven — empirically violated by the very branch that carries the mass.

## The attempt
Grant a nonperturbative BRST charge `Q` (`Q²=0`) and a quartet-complete asymptotic space in Lorenz gauge. Kugo–Ojima: `u(p²)` is the transverse ghost–gluon KO function; `u(0)=−1` (the *scaling* branch) ⇒ every colored mode — the transverse gluon included — pairs into a BRST doublet with the global-color Nambu–Goldstone field ⇒ all colored states are unphysical. Claim: "therefore the physical spectrum is gapped, `m_{0⁺⁺}≥λ`." The hope: establish `u(0)=−1` first, so the step `u(0)=−1 ⟹ gap` is the theorem, and non-circular.

## Exact failure
**(a) Channel error.** Confinement ≠ gap, already recorded: area law / color screening ⇏ `0⁺⁺` gap (`PROOF-CONFINEMENT-RANDOM-SURFACE §Q3`; `PROOF-STRING-TENSION-STABILIZER`; `FORGE5-DUALITY §S3`). `u(0)=−1` is a filter on *which asymptotic states exist* (cohomology **membership**); the gap is *where the surviving singlet spectrum starts*. BRST cohomology carries no scale. The quartet removes longitudinal/temporal gluons and ghosts; it says nothing about the neutral glueball **atom**.

**(b) Hypothesis unproven — and false on the mass-bearing branch.** In 4D continuum, `Q` and its cohomology are not rigorously constructed (this is part of the Clay construction itself). The lattice/DSE/FRG physical solution is **decoupling**, `u(0)≈−0.7…−0.8` (Fischer 2008), which **violates** `u(0)=−1` — and is exactly the branch carrying the IR mass (`PROOF-LANDAU-GAUGE-IR`; `INVENT-GAUGE-INVARIANT-MASS`). Strong coupling rigorously gives the OS/KP polymer gap `f₁²≥r₀(β)>0` (β<β₁=O(1/N), fixed `a`) **bypassing KO entirely** (`PROOF-P3-UNCONDITIONAL`); it does *not* yield `u(0)=−1`.

**(c) The substitute is circular.** The honest gauge-invariant surrogate — the edge `m_GI=inf supp ρ_s` of the OS-positive `tr F²` correlator — is, by Källén–Lehmann + factorization, the **edge atom itself**: the "gauge-invariant Schwinger mechanism" collapses onto it (`PROOF-INDUCED-MASS-GAUGE-INVARIANT`). OS positivity is Schur-concave: it bounds moments, never the edge atom (`GOLDS-POS-MASS`; `REDTEAM-SPEC`); free Maxwell is the witness `m_GI=0`. So surrogate ⟹ gap is the gap renamed — that is #3.

## Five-point verdict
1. **Nonperturbative — FAIL (usability).** No rigorous nonperturbative `u(0)=−1` for 4D pure YM; the deployable rigorous object (strong-coupling gap) does not use KO.
2. **Controls SIGN — FAIL (structural).** KO is the charged-sector cohomology witness; it is orthogonal to the gauge-invariant singlet Savvidy-cone curvature `G″=Δ_dyn+W_geo+X` and to `Var(G|0)`=H1 (`PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES`). It touches neither sign nor variance.
3. **Non-circular — FAIL.** `u(0)=−1 ⟹ singlet gap` is a non-sequitur; the gauge-invariant surrogate is the edge atom = the gap (renamed).
4. **Non-SUSY + fails free field — non-SUSY PASS; free-field letter-only.** At `g=0` the ghost–gluon vertex vanishes ⇒ `u(0)≡0`, so "`u(0)=−1`" is *vacuously* unmet — not `f₁²`-sensitive (the genuine `m_GI=0` acid test belongs to the circular surrogate). FORGE5-DUALITY "vacuous-pass" precedent.
5. **Uniform `m_eff²(k→0)≥λ` — FAIL.** No singlet lower bound follows; even `u(0)=−1` is a color statement, not a mass. The strong-coupling `r₀` is regime-limited (no `a→0`, no β=2.5).

## Sharpest line
`u(0)=−1` answers "are colored states confined?" — a yes/no about *existence*; the gap asks "how heavy is the lightest singlet?" — a number about *where the spectrum starts*. BRST cohomology is a **filter, not a scale**; the `0⁺⁺` glueball is already a singlet the filter cannot touch. Confinement ⇏ gap is already on record, and making the link honest turns `u(0)` into the `tr F²` edge, which *is* the gap. **Fails #1 (unproven hypothesis), #2 (wrong channel, no sign), #3 (non-sequitur / renamed gap), #5 (no `λ`); #4 only vacuously.**
