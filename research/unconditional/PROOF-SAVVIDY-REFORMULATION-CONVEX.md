# PROOF-SAVVIDY-REFORMULATION-CONVEX — does any gauge-covariant change of variables convexify large-field YM?

> **Verdict (判词).** **No — and the reason is a theorem, not a failed search.** The Savvidy concavity `−D²+2ad F_B` is **gauge-invariant Hessian data**: the inertia (the negative eigenvalue on the cone `|k|<√(2g|F_B|)`) of the *physical, gauge-transverse* second variation at a genuine saddle `A_B`. It is **irremovable by any reparametrisation**: at a critical point the Hessian transforms by an invertible congruence, so by Sylvester's law its signature is chart-independent; away from it, the chart's correction is exactly the Jacobian/Christoffel curvature, bounded (`≲N/12`) against a floor that grows `∝β|F|`. The residue `f₁²` is chart-**invariant** (the Jacobian cancels — on-shell physics is fixed); positivity of the *estimate* is chart-**dependent** (the `A→F` Jacobian is a signed, vanishing ghost determinant). Every candidate relocates the burden, none removes it; the sole genuine positive object remains the bounded Haar term `W_geo≥0`.

**The invariant-inertia theorem (central).** For any chart `y=φ(x)`, `S̃=S∘φ⁻¹` obeys
`Hess_y S̃ = (Dφ⁻¹)ᵀ Hess_x S (Dφ⁻¹) + (∂_a S)(x)·Hess_y(φ⁻¹)^a.`
The first term is a **congruence** (inertia-preserving); at `∇S=0` the second vanishes, so `Hess_y S̃` has the **same signature** as `Hess_x S`. The Savvidy background — a constant Cartan chromomagnetic field (`DF=0`, so a critical point of `S`, where (★) is evaluated) — has `Hess_x S ⪰ −2g|F_B| < 0` on the transverse cone, and this negativity is **not pure gauge** (7% survives projection, W4). **Every chart therefore sees a negative eigenvalue there.** Off the saddle the correction is the chart's Jacobian curvature (≤N/12), which cannot uniformly beat the `∝β|F|` floor — a factor `≳10` short (PROOF-SELFDUAL). Both regimes fail.

**(1) The candidates.** *(a) Curvature variable:* `S(F)=(1/4g²)∫Tr F²` is convex (`Hess=(1/2g²)·id`) — the "Cole–Hopf linearisation" — but physical `F=F(A)` is constrained by Bianchi `D_AF=0`; an admissible `δF=d_AδA` returns `δ²S=∫Tr δA(−D²+2ad F_B)δA`, the *same* indefinite form. The map is **degenerate at `A_B`**: a constant Cartan (flat/Wilson-line) shift has `d_AδA=0`, so `det d_A=0`. *(b) Chordal/Cartesian/Cayley charts of SU(N):* plain chart changes — the matrix action `βΣ(1−Re Tr U_□)` is degree-4 and indefinite near `U≈−1`, and no nonconstant convex potential exists on the closed group (periodic no-go, INVENT-CONVEXIFICATION); the wrap `|x|>π/2` is intrinsic to Savvidy.

**(2) Convexity on large fields?** Only the *unconstrained* `F`-quadratic is convex; its feasible set — the Bianchi surface — is nonconvex and is exactly where the Savvidy cone lives. A chart may call `S` convex along *its own* straight lines, but the physical negative direction images to a curve whose second variation is pinned (the inertia theorem). Convexity-in-the-chart is vacuous for (★).

**(3) Jacobian and residue.** The `A→F` Jacobian `det(d_A)` (a ghost-type determinant) is sign-indefinite and vanishes at `A_B`, so the pushed measure is **signed** — convex-but-signed, no positivity gain. Yet the path integral is exact under change of variables, so `f₁²` is **invariant**: positivity is a chart-dependent *tool*, the residue chart-independent *physics*. The one provably positive chart term is `W_geo=E[Φ″]−Var(Φ′)≥0` (Φ convex ⇒ Prékopa), real but (PROOF-JACOBIAN-SAVES) one variance bound short at β=2.5.

**Sharpest line.** The Savvidy sign is not a choice of coordinates: it is the inertia of the physical Hessian at a saddle, invariant under every chart by Sylvester, and its only candidate cure — the Jacobian/Christoffel curvature — is precisely the bounded Haar term that provably exists, is provably too small, and provably cannot carry the residue. The obstruction is **gauge-invariant data**; a reparametrisation relocates it into a signed determinant and no further.
