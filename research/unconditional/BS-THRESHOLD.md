# BS-THRESHOLD — in the confining phase the "two-gluon threshold" is not a physical object: the exact measure's edge is the multi-glueball threshold `θ_c ≥ 4m₁²` (needs `m_g>0`), the free 2-gluon cut at `s=0` is OPE/UV data blind to it, and the only non-circular lift — the 1-form string tension — fails a sector match

> 2026-11-23 · Threshold/support angle on the `0⁺⁺` two-gluon (`tr F²`) channel. 判词 (verdict, up front): **The question conflates two thresholds.** (T1) the *perturbative/OPE* 2-gluon cut, starting at `s=0` (free `tr F²`: `m_GI=0`, soft-photon continuum) — UV/aggregate data, Schur-blind to the edge; and (T2) the *exact physical* threshold `inf supp ν_{trF²}` — in confinement there is no asymptotic gluon, so there is **no** 2-gluon cut; the only physical multiparticle states are multi-glueball with pair masses `≥2m_g`, i.e. threshold `θ_c=(2m_g)²≥4m_g²` in the `s=m²` variable. The exact measure is `supp ν = {m₁²,(1.5m₁)²,…} ⊔ [θ_c,∞)`, `θ_c≥(2m₁)²` when `0⁺⁺` is lightest. `ν((0,m₀²))=0` is therefore *literally* Clay's support statement (A), identical to `m_g>0`; the "lift by confinement" is a **relabeling**, not a dynamical lifting of a would-be zero. The only non-circular candidate (string tension) is **1-form**; the volume channel's edge is **0-form**, and the transmutation `m_{0⁺⁺}=c√σ` is measured, not a theorem. **2/5; exact stuck step = a uniform physical 0-form mass lower bound = `(MS)/H1`.**

**§1 · Two thresholds, not one.** On `H_phys=ker Q_BRST`, `s=(β/2g)tr F²`, the exact KL measure `ν_{trF²}≥0` (OS). (T1) is the two-gluon cut of the *free* correlator — what makes the OPE `O_S·O_S∝1/x⁸` non-empty and what INVENT-GAUGE-INVARIANT-MASS shows gives `m_GI=0` for free Maxwell. It is `s→∞`-anchored and aggregate; by GAP-G2-edge it fixes only *moments*, never `inf supp`. (T2) is the bottom of the *exact* measure. Confinement = absence of the single-gluon pole from `H_phys`; hence `ν_{trF²}` has **no (T1) cut**, and its continuum is two-or-more glueballs (pair masses `≥2m_g`, so `θ_c≥4m_g²`). This is the whole content of the task's (a): the two-gluon threshold is not a clean kinematic object because the two gluons are not asymptotic; the lightest `0⁺⁺` *is* the glueball, the atom at `m₁²`, and its nearest neighbour is the C/P-blind excited atom at `(1.5m₁)²` (PROOF-ISOLATION §4.4), not the cut.

**§2 · The naive worry was never physical.** "Massless gluons ⇒ threshold `0`" is a claim about (T1). Since (T2) never contained a (T1) cut, there is nothing to lift: `ν((0,m₁²))=0` is *the definition* of `m₁` being the lightest physical state in the channel, i.e. Clay's support reading (A) `E₁−E₀≥Δ` — which SUPPORT-NOT-ATOM shows is *strictly weaker* than the atom `f₁²>0` and drops the visibility `(M1)`. The genuine gain of the threshold frame is exactly this separation: **Clay needs a support gap, not an atom.** The cost is that support is still not easier.

**§3 · BS + confinement + no-dissolution does not close (b).** The BS kernel `K^{1/2}VK^{1/2}` is sign-indefinite at the Savvidy background (BS-BLIND (a); D-BBS-LOWRANK-KATO), so the bound-state mass is a variational *upper* estimate — the wrong direction for a lower bound. No-dissolution (PROOF-NO-DISSOLUTION-KINEMATIC, PROOF-DISSOLUTION-SPECTRAL-DENSITY) is conditional: *if* `m₁<2m_lightest` and `m_g>0`, the atom is pinned below the cut — but that antecedent *is* (A). So the trio *reduces* (b) to "the atom exists at `m₁>0`." It is unconditional only at `β<1/48` (P3 + cost-grading give `f₁²≥r₀` and a two-mass structure); the entire content is its transport to `2.5`.

**§4 · The string-tension lift: steelman and attack.** *Steelman:* area law `σ>0` ⇒ flux tube ⇒ line-sector gap `m_1=√σ`; the glueball is a closed flux loop, so one might lift the 0-form support from the 1-form input **without** assuming the 0-form gap — a genuinely different species. *Attack, four failures:* (i) **sector mismatch** — `√σ` is the 1-form ('t Hooft) mass; `tr F²` is a volume (0-form) operator; `m_{0⁺⁺}=c√σ` (`c≈3.9`) is a *measured* transmutation (Morningstar–Peardon), not a theorem, and area law ⇏ gap (PROOF-STRING-TENSION-STABILIZER Prop 6, PROOF-CONFINEMENT-RANDOM-SURFACE). (ii) `σ>0` rigorous only `β<1/48` / `<0.706`; `σ_phys>0` (continuum) is the `a→0` RG wall = the gap. (iii) the "dynamical gluon mass" is gauge-fixed (gluon ∉ `H_phys`) and *is* the confinement gap. (iv) no first-principles lower bound on a crumpleable closed-string mass. So the lift is circular in the coin that matters: the number raising the 0-form edge *is* the gap.

**§5 · Verdict and exact missing step.** 2/5. The threshold/support frame is cleaner than the atom frame — it pins exactly Clay's (A) and dissolves the vacuous (T1) worry — but opens no lower-bound route: the BS kernel is sign-indefinite, no-dissolution presupposes the atom, and the string tension is the wrong sector. Every road lands on the same unresolved datum: a uniform-in-`(a,L)` lower bound on the physical **0-form** glueball mass, `inf supp ν_{trF²} ≥ m₀²>0` — `(MS)/H1`. Sharper, the one genuinely different-species target is the **string-tension→glueball transmutation as a theorem** (1-form `√σ` ⇒ 0-form `m_{0⁺⁺}`), and it is nowhere proven.
