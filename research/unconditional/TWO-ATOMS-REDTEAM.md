# TWO-ATOMS-REDTEAM — G1⊥G2 is true for fixed-volume existence, false for the YM construction

**Verdict.** Not one atom, not two orthogonal atoms. "G1⊥G2" is TRUE for (a) existence of *some* fixed-volume continuum measure; FALSE for (b) the YM construction with volume uniformity + OS + clustering. The red-team proved (a) and asserted (b).

## What the OS witness really is

The explicit OS model (IN-0, prior-work §3.4) satisfies all 16 construction inputs + clustering yet is gapless. That is genuine: a massless free (Gaussian) field is OS0–4-complete, clustering (connected correlators decay by power law, →0 in d=4), and has spectral support down to 0. So

  G1_ex = ∃ fixed-volume measure, OS + gauge-invariant  ⊥  G2 = Δ>0.

Existence alone does not entail a gap. This kills the literal "one atom = existence⟺gap."

## Why that witness is not YM

The witness is Gaussian. It therefore fails IN-7 (non-triviality ⟺ non-Gaussian ⟺ Λ>0 = a dimensional IR scale), which the audit itself routes into G2. IN-0 ⟹ "an OS theory," not YM. So the witness is not YM *for exactly the reason that it omits the G2 ingredient* — it proves independence by being the trivial (free) theory. Lifting it to a claim about the YM object is the ∀-known ⇒ ∀-possible error the red-team's own §(d) warns against.

## Why (b) is entangled

Three independent results put the gap inside the YM construction:

- **P1:** the volume-uniform Polchinski contraction constant is exactly `c=m²`; a gap-free, volume-uniform L0 is impossible. The failure is masslessness of the transverse propagator — G2, not UV.
- **GAP-G1 §3:** the irreducible construction residue at `k→0` is the constant mode, and its positivity requires an induced gluon mass `m²>0` — "that is G2, not Savvidy."
- **IN-7:** non-Gaussianity ⟺ `Λ>0` = IR scale = G2.

So (b) needs low-lying-spectrum control. It is not orthogonal to G2.

## Precise statement

Let `U` = volume uniformity (single infinite-volume measure; IN-6, PF-circular, weaker than G2, unproven) and `NT` = non-triviality (IN-7). Then:

- `G1_ex ⊥ G2` — TRUE (massless-free witness).
- `G1_ex ∧ U ∧ NT` — the YM object — does **not** close without G2: `U` needs `c=m²` (P1), the residue is the induced mass (GAP-G1), `NT` is `Λ>0` (IN-7). This is *constructive dependence*, not strict logical entailment (no theorem forces a gap from non-Gaussianity alone), but it is exactly the dependence the campaign cannot evade.

**Verdict.** Two atoms with a nonzero cross-term: `G1_ex` (fixed-volume existence) is independent of the gap; the YM construction (volume-uniform + clustering + non-trivial) is entangled with it. The red-team's ⊥ is true of the abstraction YM does not satisfy; the audit's closing "由 P1/P2 知二者纠缠" is the correct verdict. "One atom" is too strong; "two orthogonal atoms" is false.
