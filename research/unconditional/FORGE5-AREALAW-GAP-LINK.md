## The most serious attempt — the mixed correlator, and why it dies

- **Charge grading blocks it.** Area law ⟺ electric `Z_N^{(1)}` **unbroken** (GKSW). A point (0-manifold) **cannot carry a 1-form charge**, so every local conserved current built from `F` — `*F`, `T^{μν}` — is 1-form-**neutral**, and `O_S` is neutral. A Ward identity of an unbroken symmetry is an **equality diagonal in the 1-form charge**. The *stable* confining string (area law) carries nonzero 1-form charge (N-ality 0 strings break, perimeter law). - **The only charged option self-defeats.** A mixed correlator `⟨O_S V(C)⟩≠0` that *did* carry the σ-scale into the neutral channel is an order parameter *breaking* the 1-form symmetry — contradicting the area-law phase it is meant to use. Non-circularity requires the very quantity the phase forbids.
- **Current coupling is topological.** Contracting the conserved `*F` with the local `O_S` yields a total derivative (`∫θ`), a *forward / topological* charge — not the pole residue. Exactly INEQ-T4: the anomaly fixes `g_GGG`, the forward trace charge, and `f₁²` **factors out** (homogeneous identity).
