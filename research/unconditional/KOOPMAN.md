## 1. The operator and the exact reduction
`U_t f(x)=E[f(X_t)|X_0=x]=(P_t f)(x)` is the adjoint of the Fokker–Planck/transfer operator; for a deterministic flow it is `f∘Φ_t`. On `H_phys=L²(𝒜/G,μ̄)` the Wilson transfer `T=e^{−aH}` (`H=(g²/2)ΣE²+`magnetic, both Hermitian w.r.t. Haar `dU`) is **self-adjoint** ⇒ `μ̄`-reversible ⇒ `U_t=P_t=e^{tL}`, and the Koopman spectrum equals the semigroup spectrum. The gap over constants is `λ_1=−λ_1^{(L)}`, the **L²-Poincaré constant**. Koopman eigenfunctions obey `E[φ_λ(X_t)|X_0]=e^{λt}φ_λ(X_0)` — multiplicative martingales — but the top is `φ_0≡1` (vacuum) and the sharp modulus is the variational `λ_1`, so no new constant. On the slow amplitude `a=⟨v,X⟩`, `λ_1^{(a)}=−b′(0)=G″` (D2); hence `G″≥λ ⟺ λ_1^{Koop}(a)≥λ` — the narrowed target is H1 in operator clothing.

## 3. No slack — the reversibility no-go (new)
Koopman and transfer spectra can differ **only** for non-reversible dynamics. Euclidean YM SQ is reversible ⇒ no difference; the gap is the symmetric Dirichlet gap = H1. The one classical escape is **hypocoercivity** (Villani): a degenerate symmetric part plus a *genuine antisymmetric/rotational* drift still yields a gap (kinetic Fokker–Planck). The YM SQ drift `−∇S` is **pure gradient** — zero antisymmetric part — so hypocoercivity adds nothing. The only antisymmetric candidate is `iH`, whose Koopman spectrum `{e^{iω_nt}}` is the energy ladder: reading its gap **is** reading `m_{0⁺⁺}` (circular; equivalently A3: Koopman modes = determinant zeros = masses).
