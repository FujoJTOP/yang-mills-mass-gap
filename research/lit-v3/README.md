each producing `LIT-ERA-XX.md` with 20–35 entries, verification status per reference, the
campaign's labels, a 14-era longitudinal table from its own angle, and a "gaps and attack

## Requirements imposed on every report

1. 20–35 entries, each with authors, title, venue, year, volume/pages, DOI or arXiv.
2. Every load-bearing entry checked against publisher / arXiv / zbMATH / INSPIRE and marked
   `VERIFIED` (with the source checked) or `UNVERIFIED`. No invented bibliographic data.
3. Labels: `[T]` theorem, `[C]` conditional theorem, `[NG]` no-go for a named class,
   `[O]` exact open/missing step, `[V]` numerical evidence.
4. What was established vs what was hoped, per cluster.
5. A "Position in 14 eras" table: one line per era from the report's angle.
6. "Gaps and attack surfaces for v3".
7. A verification ledger at the end.

## Honesty rules (carried from CLAUDE.md)

- No claim that the mass gap is proved; Clay remains open.
- An announcement is not a theorem; a preprint is not peer review.
- Where a citation cannot be checked, it is flagged, not guessed.
