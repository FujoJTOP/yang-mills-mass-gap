## Correction

A monodromy / twist (`U(1)` Aharonov–Bohm) defect is **codim-2**: it needs
`π₁(transverse)=Z`, i.e. a line in 3D or a **surface** in 4D (transverse `R²`). The bulk–defect
block is **codim-3** (line in 4D, transverse `R³`, `π₁(R³∖line)=0`). So the textbook
monodromy-defect 2-point (Billò–Gonçalves–Lauria–Meineri, 1601.02883) is a **different-codimension
analogue**, not the benchmark.

## Codim-2 monodromy 2-point

One cross-ratio `ξ₂`; a hypergeometric with monodromy exponent `(α, 1−α)`; the bulk scalar is
neutral so there is **no `SO(3)`/Legendre factor**. It therefore **cannot** reproduce
`ξ^{Δ_j} P_j(cos θ)`.

## Correct codim-3 free benchmark

The free-scalar 2-point with a conformal **line** defect has the defect-OPE structure
`ξ^{Δ_j}·(SO(3) harmonic)` — *consistent* with `PDE-SOLUTION.md` in form — but its closed form
needs the defect spectrum `Δ_j` and the inversion/boundary construction, which was **not
verified**.

## Honest boundary

No verified codim-3 free benchmark was produced; the monodromy 2-point is the wrong codimension
and cannot serve as the check. The right benchmark is the free-scalar line-defect 2-point (open).
