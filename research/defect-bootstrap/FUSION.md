# Fusion rules of the `Z_N`-charged Wilson/'t Hooft lines

> CPB-attack probe (sub-task 8). Verdict: fusion + anomaly fix the crossing **structure**
> (grading, phase), **not** the dynamical spectrum `{Δ_i}`.

## Fusion rules

Lines are labelled by center charge `q ∈ Z_N` (the N-ality). Fusion is additive:
`W_q × W_{q'} = ⊕_{q''∈q+q'} W_{q''}` — group multiplication in `Z_N`. For representations,
`R⊗R'` decomposes as usual and the center charge adds mod `N`; non-abelian reps give a
fusion category with multiplicities, but the 1-form symmetry sees only `q`. 't Hooft lines
carry magnetic charge; a Wilson and an 't Hooft line have **projective mutual braiding** (the
't Hooft algebra), whose phase shifts with `θ` (Witten effect). The mixed `Z_N–θ` anomaly
(5d BF symmetry TQFT) makes the crossing kernel carry an unremovable phase `e^{iθ/N}`.

## Allowed defect content

Defect operators `O_i(τ)`, **graded by `q`** (superselection), with transverse spin `s_i` and
dimension `Δ_i`; the defect OPE respects the grading.

## Constraint on crossing

Crossing decomposes into `q`-sectors; the anomalous phase is **fixed by the anomaly**, not
free. Fusion + anomaly fix the crossing *structure* (grading, phase), **not** the spectrum
`{Δ_i}`.

## Honest boundary

Fusion fixes superselection and the anomaly phase but **not** the dynamical defect data
(`Δ_i`, OPE coefficients), which the bootstrap must supply; the full rep fusion
(multiplicities) exceeds what the `Z_N` center charge encodes; non-SUSY 4D defect CFT data are
unknown.

(fusion categories).
