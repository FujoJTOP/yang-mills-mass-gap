# Defect bootstrap: the `Z_N^{(1)}` anomaly and its role

> CPB-attack probe. Verdict: the anomaly is a **selection rule**, **not an exclusion**; no
> known anomaly-based exclusion of an interacting pure-gauge CFT.

## The anomaly

4D SU(N) YM at θ-angle has an electric `Z_N^{(1)}` 1-form symmetry. Its 't Hooft anomaly is
the **mixed anomaly with θ** (equivalently the electric–magnetic `Z_N × Z_N` mixed anomaly,
the "Witten effect"): a `Z_N^{(1)}` transformation shifts `θ → θ + 2π/N`. It is encoded by a
5d **symmetry TQFT** (BF-type, `Z_N` level) with anomaly inflow; equivalently `Z_N^{(1)}`
and the θ-periodicity combine into a **2-group** (GKSW 2015; Cordova–Tachikawa;
Cordova–Dumitrescu–Intriligator).

## Effect on the defect spectrum

A **defect anomaly**: Wilson/'t Hooft lines carry a projective **'t Hooft algebra**; the
line-defect **crossing kernel** acquires an anomalous phase `e^{iθ/N}` that is **not**
removable by defect phase redefinitions; the defect Hilbert space is `Z_N`-graded. So the
defect bootstrap must include this phase and grading — a **selection rule**, not a free
parameter. (Concrete entry point: the anomalous crossing phase is fixed by the anomaly.)

## Anomaly-based exclusion?

**No known exclusion of an interacting pure-gauge CFT.** The anomaly's constraining power is
**`θ`-dependent**: the electric–magnetic (BF-type) mixed anomaly is nontrivial at generic `θ`,
most sharply at `θ=π` (CP), where it **forbids a trivially gapped symmetric phase** — forcing a
TQFT (`Z_N` topological order — gapped), a CFT (gapless), or SSB. On `R⁴` the vacuum is unique
⟹ SSB is excluded, but the **TQFT/CFT dichotomy remains** at `θ=π`.

**Caveat (`θ=0`, the pure-YM point relevant here).** At `θ=0` only the electric `Z_N^{(1)}` is a
symmetry, with **no purely-electric 't Hooft anomaly**, so the "forbids trivially gapped" step
**does not apply**: a trivially gapped confining vacuum at `θ=0` is allowed by the anomaly. Hence
at `θ=0` the anomaly **constrains but does not exclude** a gapless phase (nor a gapped one) — it
supplies no gap proof. (This makes the no-go for an anomaly-based gap *stronger*, not weaker.)

## Honest boundary

The anomaly is a constraint / selection rule for the defect bootstrap, **not** a gap proof.
"Anomaly ⟹ gap" would additionally need to rule out a CFT matching the anomaly — that is
exactly the open classification step (iii), which the anomaly does not supply.

Cordova–Tachikawa, *Anomalies of 1-form symmetries* / symmetry TQFT;
Cordova–Dumitrescu–Intriligator, *2-Group Global Symmetries*.
