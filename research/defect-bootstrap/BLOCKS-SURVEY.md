# Survey: known defect conformal blocks

> CPB-attack probe (sub-task 1). Verdict: **no closed-form non-SUSY 1D-defect-in-4D block
> exists**; the closest (codim-2) is structurally different and not directly adaptable.

## Inventory

**(a) 2D defect in 4D (codim-2).** Well developed: Billò–Gonçalves–Lauria–Meineri
(arXiv:1601.02883) derive codim-2 defect blocks via the bulk–defect OPE; the defect-bootstrap
program (Lauria–Liendo–?; Gimenez-Grau–?, 2020s) computes 2D-defect blocks in 4D. These are
**codim-2**, not a line.

**(b) Line (1D) defect in 4D.** **The missing case.** Known line-defect bootstraps are
almost entirely **SUSY**: `N=4` SYM Wilson/'t Hooft lines (Liendo–?; Giombi–Komatsu;
2020–2025), where superconformal defect blocks are fixed by SUSY. For **non-SUSY** 1D
defects in 4D there is **no closed-form defect block** verifiable in the literature. 3D
line-defect / boundary blocks exist (Liendo et al.), but not the non-SUSY 4D line.

**(c) Boundary in 4D.** Known (Liendo–Rastelli–?; Gliozzi–?; boundary bootstrap), but a
boundary is **codim-1**, structurally different from a codim-3 line.

## Closest existing block

The **codim-2 defect block** (1601.02883) — same "defect OPE + bulk OPE crossing" structure
as the line case — but codim-2 ≠ codim-3. The line's preserved group is `SO(2,1)×SO(2)`
(1D conformal × transverse rotation); the codim-2 case preserves `SO(2,2)×SO(2)`. The
transverse-rotation symmetry and the cross-ratio structure differ.

## Adaptability

**Not directly adaptable.** The codim-2 block cannot be specialized to codim-3; the
transverse-space dimension enters the blocks essentially. One would need to **build** the
`SO(2,1)×SO(2)` (codim-3) block from the defect OPE of a bulk scalar — a genuine
construction, not a lookup.

## Honest boundary

No closed-form non-SUSY 1D-defect-in-4D conformal block exists to our knowledge; the closest
(codim-2, 1601.02883) is structurally different. A 2023–2025 non-SUSY line-defect block
could not be verified. Reach/survey assessment, not a construction.

Sources: [Billò–Gonçalves–Lauria–Meineri, *Defects in CFT* (arXiv:1601.02883)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.02883);
boundary bootstrap (Liendo et al.); N=4 line-defect bootstrap (2020–2025).
