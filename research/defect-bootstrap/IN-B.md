## Weaker conditional statement

Assume:

- **(a0)** the bulk–defect bootstrap gives `Δ_{0⁺⁺} ≥ δ` (spin-0, `tr F²`);
- **(a2)** it gives `Δ_{2⁺⁺} ≥ δ` (spin-2, `T_{μν}`);
- **(b′)** the lightest bulk state lies in `0⁺⁺` **or** `2⁺⁺` — i.e. **no bulk state below
  `δ` carries other quantum numbers**.

Then `Δ_bulk = min(Δ_{0⁺⁺}, Δ_{2⁺⁺}) ≥ δ`.

This is a conditional theorem with the **weaker** premise `(b′)`. **Caveat:** `(b′)` must
exclude sub-`δ` states in *every other* channel (`0⁻⁺`, odd C, higher spin), since the bootstrap
bounds only the two C-even, P-even channels it sees.
