# Free (Maxwell) benchmark

> CPB-attack probe (sub-task 6). Structure + leading terms; **not** the full closed free
> spectrum.

## Setup

Straight `W_R` along `x³` in free (`g=0`) `SU(N)`: a generalized-free (Gaussian) defect; the
photon is free, so the defect CFT is free. Residual `SO(2,1)×SO(3)`.

## Defect spectrum (structure)

Defect primaries are photon modes pulled back to the line, in rep `R`:

- **displacement `D^a`** (adjoint), `Δ=2` — the lightest defect primary (codimension-2
  displacement);
- transverse-gluon / defect field-strength primaries from the mode expansion, `Δ ≥ 2`;
- the bulk scalar `tr F²` (`Δ=4`) couples to `D` in the bulk–defect OPE.

## Crossing solution (leading terms)

`⟨O O⟩_def = Σ_O C_O 𝒢_O(ξ) = Σ_i a_i² 𝔽_i(ξ)` is solved by free Wick contractions. Leading
defect channel: **identity + the `Δ=2` displacement**, with the OPE coefficient fixed by the
free photon two-point function (no free parameter). Bulk channel: the free 4-point of `tr F²`.

## Honest boundary

Structure and leading terms, not the full closed free spectrum (all primaries + coefficients),
which needs the explicit bulk–defect mode expansion. Also: at `g=0` the non-abelian
path-ordering trivialises against the free measure, so the `Z_N` 't Hooft-anomaly phase is
**absent** in the free benchmark — the interacting benchmark needs it. `Δ_D=2` taken from the
free / N=4 defect literature; verify.
