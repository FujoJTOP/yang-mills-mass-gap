# L2 — `a₀=0` for pure Yang–Mills

> CPB-attack probe. Verdict: **L2-closed (structural)** — conditional on L3.

## Argument for `a₀=0`

In 4D pure YM the only field is `A_μ` (dim 1, **not** gauge-invariant). Gauge-invariant
operators are built from `F_μν` (dim 2); the gauge-invariant **scalars** are even powers —
`tr F²`, `tr F F̃` (dim 4), `tr F⁴` (dim 8), …. `tr F = 0`; no fundamental scalar;
`tr(A_μA^μ)` is not gauge-invariant. So **the lowest gauge-invariant scalar has dimension
4**; there is no dimension-2 scalar, hence `a₀=0`. The `a₀>0` branch is empty: **L2 is
closable for pure YM by operator content.**

## But it does not close ESI (4.1)

The other half — no dim-4 scalar (`2b₁+b₂=0`) — is **not** forced: `L = tr F²` is itself
dim 4 and appears in the `L·L` OPE (`⟨LLL⟩≠0` for non-abelian). That is **L3**, the
decisive lemma. L1 (`2n`-point ⟹ `a′=0`) remains.

## Caveat

ESI's `a₀` is a coefficient in their 4-point projective space; identifying it with a local
dim-2 operator presupposes the L3 reduction, so L2's closure is **conditional** on the ESI
setup applying to the gauge theory at all.

## Verdict

**L2-closed** (structural `a₀=0` for pure YM) — but the route still needs L1 and L3;
**L3 is decisive**. Honest boundary: a standard operator-content argument, not a theorem in
the ESI normalization; assumes no anomalously-low-dimension scalar composite.
