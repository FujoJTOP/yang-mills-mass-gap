# Method 6b — anomaly classification: the refined step (iii)

> CPB-attack probe. Verdict: **needs-X** — and the **only route that does not consume
> the area law**.

## Status of (iii): no theorem, no counterexample, strong partial evidence

- **Continuous 1-form symmetry:** a 4D CFT ⟹ free Maxwell (Hofman–Iqbal 1802.09512,
  theorem-level). But pure YM has a **discrete** `Z_N` 1-form symmetry, not continuous —
  this does not transfer.
- **Pure gauge, non-SUSY:** the ESI result (`esi1335` = arXiv hep-th/0305200,
  Nikolov–Stanev–Todorov 2003, **published**) gives a **conditional** 4-point no-go:
  under an `L L` OPE assumption, `a′=0` forces the free Maxwell 4-point function. But it
  **does not close** — it needs (L1) the `2n`-point positivity to yield `a′=0`, (L2) to
  exclude the `a₀>0` scalar branch, (L3) the single-`L` reduction. See `ESI-detail.md`.
  The earlier "strongest evidence" framing overstated it. All known interacting 4D CFTs
  are superconformal or **with matter** (JHEP 11(2024)010; Lawrence–Nekrasov–Vafa
  orbifolds carry bifundamental matter); pure YM is outside the conformal window.

## Emergent SUSY

All known "non-SUSY UV → SUSY IR" flows (Magnetic Fixed Points; "Supersymmetry without
Supersymmetry"; Prakash–Sinha JHEP 2024) are **with matter** — none pure gauge. For pure
YM this step is open, but **moot if (iii) holds**.

## "Unbroken" is automatic

Spontaneous breaking of a 1-form symmetry needs **degenerate vacua**; a CFT on `R⁴` has a
unique vacuum, so `Z_N(1)` is unbroken **without the area law** (2408.14532; 2310.03786).
**Confirms Method 6's structural point: the route does not consume IN-4.**
