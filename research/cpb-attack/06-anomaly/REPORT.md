# Method 6 — generalized symmetry / 't Hooft anomaly matching

> CPB-attack probe. Verdict: **needs-X** (not dead, not provable).

## Refined step (iii)

**Proposition.** A 4D CFT with only gauge fields (no matter), no SUSY, and an unbroken
`Z_N` 1-form center symmetry must be **free**.

- Naive version is **false**: adjoint QCD (Dirac adjoint fermions, conformal window) is
  an interacting 4D CFT with a `Z_N` 1-form symmetry — but has **matter**; N=4 SYM
  likewise — but has **SUSY**.
- Refined version (no matter, no SUSY): **no theorem, no counterexample.**
  Kang–Lawrie–Lee–Song (arXiv:2408.14532) classify N=1 SUSY and non-SUSY *with matter*
  (Banks–Zaks); **pure-gauge, no-matter, non-SUSY CFTs are not classified.** Known
  pure-gauge theories are either confining-gapped (GKSW; GKKS) or SUSY.

## Logical skeleton

| step | content | status |
|---|---|---|
| (i) | gapless IR ⟹ 4D CFT | standard |
| (ii) | CFT unique vacuum ⟹ `Z_N` 1-form **automatically unbroken** | standard; **does NOT need the area law** |
| (iii) | pure gauge + no matter + no SUSY CFT ⟹ free | **OPEN (key)** |
| (iv) | IN-7 non-Gaussian ⟹ gap | project input |

**Extra gap:** (iii) also needs "the IR cannot *emerge* SUSY" — the UV has none, but if
the IR emerges N=4-type SUSY, (iii) fails. A second open step.

## Verdict

**needs-X:** X = (a) classification of non-SUSY pure-gauge 4D CFTs (⟹ free);
(b) no emergent SUSY.

**Positive point:** step (ii)'s "unbroken" holds automatically in a CFT (unique vacuum),
so the anomaly route **does not consume the area law (IN-4)** — this differs from the
older judgment that it "must prove the area law first". That is the one structural piece
of good news of this route.

## Honest boundary

A decision input, not a proof; moduli cases need separate treatment. "No counterexample"
≠ theorem; this step needs a classification-level result, not an analytic estimate.
