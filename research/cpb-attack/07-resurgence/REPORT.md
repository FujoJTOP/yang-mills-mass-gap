# Method 7 — resurgence / Borel / IR renormalon

> CPB-attack probe. Verdict: **DEAD** (for *giving the gap*).

## 2D calibration (partial)

Dunne–Ünsal (CP^{N-1}, large N) resurgence does reproduce the mass gap and
θ-dependence. But Bajnok et al. (O(3)) report resurgence **fails** at leading
nonperturbative order (instanton and renormalon mismatch). So "resurgence gives the
gap" is **not** a general fact.

## 4D pure YM (decisive)

- IR renormalons sit at `z = −2n/β₁` (`β₁<0` ⟹ positive real axis); ordinary
  Borel–Laplace fails.
- Maiezza–Vasquez 2024 Borel–Écalle gives a **one-parameter transseries, with the
  nonperturbative constant undetermined** — "renormalons have no semiclassical limit".
- Bion ambiguity resolution holds **only on ℝ³×S¹** (monopoles); **ℝ⁴ has no such object**.
- The renormalon–OPE relation `t₀=−a_IR/β₀` is proved only in 2D large N; in 4D it is
  an assumption.

## Can it supply BL-6's sign? No

The resurgence sign (Stokes / renormalon alternation) is the sign of a **transseries
ambiguity**, not of the neutral-sector defect activity `κ_c`; bions live on ℝ³×S¹.
No intersection.

## Needed to become viable

A **nonperturbative definition on ℝ⁴** (lateral Borel-sum ambiguity fixed by a 4D
nonperturbative sector) — which is the original problem. Vacuous.

## Honest boundary

A verdict that "resurgence does not give the 4D gap" (structural: ℝ⁴ has no
semiclassical sector), **not** "the gap is false".
