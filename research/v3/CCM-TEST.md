## 1 · Setup — the projection identity (why CCM is kinematic)

Write `(X, μ)` for a probability space (here: a Gaussian / Gibbs measure on a field) and `A_i ⊆ B(X)`
four sub-σ-algebras. The conditional expectation `E_i = E[·|A_i]` is the **orthogonal projection** of
`L²(μ)` onto the closed subspace `L²(X,A_i,μ)` (self-adjoint, idempotent). With
`A := P₄P₃P₂P₁` and `B := P₁P₂P₃P₄ = A*` (each `P_i` self-adjoint),
```
H□ = A − A*        ⇒   H□* = −H□  (skew-adjoint),      tr H□ = 0 .                        (1.1)
```
Consequently:
```
H□ = 0  ⟺  A = A*  ⟺  P₄P₃P₂P₁ = P₁P₂P₃P₄  ⟺  the P_i commute in cyclic order.            (1.2)
```
**The defect is a commutator of projections.** It is fixed by the **geometry of the four subspaces** and
the inner product `⟨f,g⟩ = ∫ f ḡ dμ`; the Hamiltonian / transfer operator enters only through the state `μ`,
*and only through the overlap structure of `μ` with the partition into regions*. There is **no eigenvalue,
no decay rate, no spectral gap** in (1.1)–(1.2). Any detected "memory" is the **non-closure of a cycle of
projections** — the quantum analogue of a nonzero "holonomy" of a connection built from the `P_i`.

*Scope note.* This is the reading the task specifies ("conditional expectations onto overlapping subspaces
are generally non-commuting projections"). If `E_i` were **not** state-preserving (non-orthogonal
expectations), `H□` would not be skew-adjoint and (1.1)–(1.2) would fail — but then `E_i` would not be a
`μ`-conditional expectation, and the probabilistic content (`L²(μ)` projection, the very meaning of "memory"
along a cycle) is lost. The orthogonal reading is the only one under which CCM is a genuine function of the
state.

## 2 · Test 1 — free massless Gaussian field: `H□ ≠ 0` (explicit) `[T]`+`[V]`

For a zero-mean **Gaussian** field with covariance `C` (e.g. `C = L⁺` for the massless GFF, or the
field-strength covariance of the free Maxwell field), the conditional expectation on the **first Wiener
chaos** is the `C`-orthogonal projection onto the span of the local coordinates: with selector `A_S`
(columns = the site/plaquette coordinates of region `S`),
```
Π_S = A_S (A_Sᵀ C A_S)⁻¹ A_Sᵀ C ,      Π_S² = Π_S ,   Π_Sᵀ C = C Π_S .                    (2.1)
```
(Verified to `‖Π²−Π‖ ≤ 3e-15`, `‖ΠᵀC−CΠ‖ ≤ 7e-15`.) The defect `H□ = Π₄Π₃Π₂Π₁ − Π₁Π₂Π₃Π₄` is then a
plain matrix and is nonzero iff the four `C`-orthogonal projections fail to commute.

**Results `[V]`** (overlapping 4-cycle of blocks; `‖·‖_C` = operator norm in the covariance metric):

| model | gap | `‖H□‖_C` | meter fires? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **free massless scalar GFF**, `C₁₂`, periodic | **0** (thermo. limit) | **`6.29e-1`** | **YES** |
| control: **nested** blocks on same field | — | `2.3e-15` | no (correct) |
| control: **disjoint-but-correlated** blocks | — | `2.17e-1` | yes (correlation, not disjointness) |
| **free massless `U(1)` Maxwell**, `d=4`, `L=2` | **0** (photons) | **`6.54e-1`** | **YES** |
| **free massless `U(1)` Maxwell**, `d=2`, `L=8` | **0** (noncompact) | **`1.02e-1`** | **YES** |
| free **massive** scalar, `m²=0.01 / 0.1 / 1 / 4` | `>0` (gapped) | `3.7e-1 / 2.7e-1 / 6.2e-3 / 5.1e-5` | yes (gapped!) |

Two structural facts fall out:
- **The free massless field gives `H□ ≠ 0`.** The gap is `0`, the meter is positive ⇒ **CCM is not a gap
  witness** and fails the acid test (a gap tool must vanish on the massless free field — `ch09` §acid, the
  weak-\* discontinuity test). The nonzero value is a pure overlap-geometry effect of the correlated
  Gaussian (even **disjoint** correlated blocks fail to commute, `0.217`): conditional expectations onto
  overlapping *correlated* Gaussian subspaces are generically non-commuting projections, exactly as the
  task anticipated.
- **The gapped massive free field also gives `H□ ≠ 0`, non-monotonically in `m²`** (`0.629→0.057→0.367→
  …→5e-5`). `H□` is **not** a monotone order parameter; it is set by the correlation/overlap geometry, not
  by the spectral gap.

The 4D free photon is a **genuine free massless Gaussian gauge field**: on the `L=2` torus,
`F = dA ∈ im(d₁)` (a `45`-dimensional exact-2-form subspace of the `96` plaquette coordinates), where `F`
is white Gaussian — the same projection identity (2.1) applies, and `H□ ≠ 0`.

## 3 · Test 2 — 2D Yang–Mills: the holonomy/cycle-constraint misread `[C]`+`[T]`+`[V]`

2D YM has **no local propagating degrees of freedom**: `F` is a top form, and the only gauge-invariant
local data are **holonomy/Wilson** variables `U_p` around plaquettes (cycles). Its transfer matrix is
**gapped**, `Δ₂D = (e²/2)·min_{R≠1} C₂(R)` (`3e²/8` for `SU(2)`, `2e²/3` for `SU(3)`; X2 §2.2).

The **free** 2D gauge field already has `H□ ≠ 0` (`0.102`, §2). Its field strength `F=dA` is white Gaussian
on `im(d₁) = {∑_p F_p = 0}` (one global **torus-flux constraint**, `dim im(d₁)=63` of `64` plaquettes), and
**that single global constraint alone** is enough to make the plaquette-block conditional expectations
fail to commute. The gapped compact 2D YM shares the **same local holonomy algebra** (`U_p` on cycles).

⇒ A nonzero CCM in 2D YM is a **misread of the cycle constraint**: the plaquette holonomy is *literally*
the constraint `∏_{links∈∂p} U = holonomy` around the cycle, and the non-commutativity of the `U`-matrices
that compose it is **kinematic**, present at **every** coupling (and already in the gapless free limit).
CCM cannot separate the gapped compact theory from the gapless free one, because both have the same local
cycle structure — the number it reads is the constraint's noncommutativity, not the Casimir gap.

## 4 · Test 3 — a model with CCM but no gap

The **free massless field** is exactly that model: **gapless** (continuous spectrum down to `0` in the
thermodynamic limit; `0` mass) yet `H□ ≠ 0` (scalar `0.629`, 4D photon `0.654`, 2D photon `0.102`).
So "CCM `≠ 0`" does **not** imply a gap. (Test 3 and the positive half of Test 1 are the same exact
computation; both are the free massless field.)

## 5 · Test 4 — a gapped product model with CCM `= 0`

Take independent massive degrees of freedom, one per site: `C = v·𝟙` (product measure), gapped (gap `=`
the site mass `> 0`). The local algebra of a region is the coordinate subspace; the conditional
expectations are the `C`-orthogonal projections onto **coordinate blocks**, and for `C = v·𝟙` these are
ordinary Euclidean block-projections, which **commute** (the per-site tensor factors commute). Hence
```
H□ = Π₄Π₃Π₂Π₁ − Π₁Π₂Π₃Π₄ = 0     exactly   (witness 0.0e+00) .                            (5.1)
```
So a **gapped** model can have **`H□ = 0`**: "CCM `≠ 0`" is **not necessary** for a gap either. Combined
with §4, CCM is **neither necessary nor sufficient**.

## 6 · Structural diagnosis and the tier

| reading of CCM | what it is | gap content |
|---|---|---|
| `H□ = P₄P₃P₂P₁ − P₁P₂P₃P₄` | commutator of four conditional-expectation projections | **none** — spectral data absent |
| `‖H□‖` | norm of the cycle non-closure | **none** — depends on overlap geometry, varies non-monotonically with mass |
| `tr H□` | `≡ 0` identically (skew-adjoint) | **none** — tracial readings are blind by `[T]` (1.1) |

`H□` is a proxy for **non-factorization of `μ` over the region quadruple** (a "non-productness" or
"cycle frustration" functional), i.e. it sits at the **kinematic / algebraic** tier. The mass gap is a
**spectral** property of the global transfer operator. The two are **independent axes** — the same split
X2 established for factorization-algebra local constancy (2D YM: LC + gapped; free: non-LC + gapless).
CCM renames the *non-commutativity of conditional expectations*, not the transfer matrix; it is **not** a
sixth species, and it does not reach `W` (atom weight) or `I` (isolation).

## 8 · Sharpest line

**CCM is the commutator of four conditional-expectation projections and nothing more.** By definition each
`E_i` is an orthogonal projection, so `H□ = P₄P₃P₂P₁ − P₁P₂P₃P₄ = A − A*` is **skew-adjoint with trace
zero** and is nonzero **iff** the four projections fail to commute in cyclic order — a statement about
**four subspaces and one inner product**, with **no eigenvalue, no decay rate, no gap** anywhere in it. The
**exact free massless field** — scalar GFF and `U(1)` Maxwell in `d=4` and `d=2` — has `H□ ≠ 0`
(`0.63, 0.65, 0.10`) while its gap is `0`; and a **gapped product model** has `H□ = 0` exactly, because its
conditional expectations commute. Hence CCM is **neither necessary** (it fires without a gap) **nor
sufficient** (it is silent with one) for a mass gap: it measures the **kinematic non-factorization** of the
state over the region quadruple, an axis **orthogonal** to the spectral gap (the same axis on which 2D YM is
"locally constant *and* gapped"). In 2D YM the nonzero value is a **misread of the cycle constraint**
(`∏U` around a plaquette), present already in the gapless free field. The wall is unchanged and this is
**not** the machine. **No QED. Clay 0%.**
