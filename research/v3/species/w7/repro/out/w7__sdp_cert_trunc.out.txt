[T-A/V] frustration-free chain (AKLT, OPEN chain N=6, k=2, level n=4):  H^2 >= [H^2]_n ?

    pairs kept in [H^2]_n : 9;  pairs discarded (j-i >= 4) : 1   [disjoint supports since j-i >= 2]
    lambda_min( H^2 - [H^2]_n ) = -5.336e-15   (rel. -1.81e-17)
    lambda_min( [H^2]_n ) = -9.038e-15  (the truncated operator is itself PSD)
    => the discarded terms ARE PSD: products of two PSD operators on disjoint supports.
       Hence Q_n <= H^2 - delta H, the level-n relaxation is VALID, and it is FINITE.
       That single inequality is the whole engine of the hierarchy.
