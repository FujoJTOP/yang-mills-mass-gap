### A. `arXiv:2611.11534` — Mihajlovic, *SU(2) Yang-Mills: Spectral Bounds, Vacuum Concentration, and Criteria for the Continuum Limit*, math-ph, 8 Nov 2026, 414 pp. DOI `10.48550/arXiv.2611.11534` **[摘要]**
