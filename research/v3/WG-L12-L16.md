## 0 · Verdict (up front; no QED, Clay 0%)

1. **`[V]` `χ_O` is measured to L=16 and grows strongly sub-linearly.** `χ_O = 0.328, 0.377, 0.411,
   0.469, 0.587` at L=4,6,8,12,16. The weighted log-log slope over all five volumes is
   `d ln χ_O / d ln L = 0.371 ± 0.066`. The **free-massless** value `2.0` is **excluded at `24.7σ`**;
   over the full L=4→16 range `χ_O` grows **×1.79 ± 0.21** against the free **×16** (`68.8σ`), and over
   L=8→16 it grows **×1.43 ± 0.24** against free **×4.0** (`10.7σ`). **Quadratic growth is dead.**

2. **`[V]/[C]` It is not a flat plateau either — the earlier "plateau" reading is refined by the new
   points.** Per-step ratios are all `>1` (`1.15, 1.09, 1.14, 1.25`), and a pure `slope=0` is
   **disfavoured at `5.6σ`**. The honest object is **bounded, sub-linear growth**, consistent with a
   **finite-size approach from below to a finite thermodynamic `χ_O^∞`** (the smallest momentum `2π/L`
   and the `Σ_z C_s(z)` truncation lift as L grows) — **not** with unbounded growth. Distinguishing
   saturation from a genuine slow power needs `L≳24` / a finite-size ansatz — **`[O]`**.

3. **`[V]` Non-self-averaging persists at the largest volumes.** `χ_O` stays `O(1)` (`0.47, 0.59`)
   while the mean-normalized `Var(q̄)=χ_O/V` falls to `2.3×10⁻⁵` (L=12), `9.0×10⁻⁶` (L=16). The carrier
   is still non-self-averaging and exactly gauge-invariant (estimator inherited unchanged).

**One line.** *Extending the gauge-invariant `tr F²` zero-mode susceptibility to L=12,16 keeps it
sub-linear (`χ_O = 0.47, 0.59`; slope `0.37±0.07`), definitively excluding free `L²` growth (`≈25σ`),
but the new points show a mild `~10–25 %`-per-step rise, so a pure flat plateau is disfavoured (`5.6σ`)
and the honest reading becomes "finite-size approach to a finite thermodynamic value", with saturation
left `[O]`. No QED. Clay 0%.*

## 1 · What was run (protocol)

Identical estimator to `WG-INVARIANT-ESTIMATOR.md` §2:

```
Õ(x) = Σ_{μ<ν} ( 1 − ½ Re tr P^s_{μν}(x) ),   P^s = plaquette of APE-smeared links (α=0.5, ns=2)
q    = V^{-1/2} Σ_x ( Õ(x) − ⟨Õ⟩ ),   χ_O = Var(q) = V·Var_c(Ȭ),   Ȭ = V^{-1}Σ_x Õ
```

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L=4,6,8 (prior) | 800, 400, 160 | 500 | 8 | ns=2 | 15/35/46 s |

The L=16 protocol (`N=300, gap=5`) was **selected by the measured L=12 sweep rate**: the projection is
`(16/12)⁴` times the L=12 cost; candidates `gap=8→25.3 min`, `gap=6→21.8 min`, `gap=5→20.0 min` were
computed and the first `<20 min` (`gap=5`) was run. **Autocorrelation check:** the `χ_O`-series

delete-jackknife SEs are valid (blocks ≫ `τ_int`). Jackknife SE on `Var` is used throughout.

| step | `χ_O` ratio | free-massless `(L₂/L₁)²` | plateau = 1 |
|---|---|---|---|

**Global fit.** Weighted least squares on `ln χ_O` vs `ln L` (L=4,6,8,12,16):
```
 d ln χ_O / d ln L = 0.371 ± 0.066      (free massless 2.0 ; plateau 0.0)
     vs 2.0 : excluded at 24.7σ        vs 0.0 : excluded at 5.6σ
```
Contact-subtracted `χ_phys` gives the same ratios `1.154, 1.094, 1.150, 1.272` — the rise is **not** a
contact/UV effect; it is in the propagating part.

**Two-sided honest statement.**
- **Free growth is dead.** `L8→16`: `×1.43±0.24` vs free `×4.0` (**10.7σ**); `L4→16`: `×1.79±0.21` vs
  `×16` (**68.8σ**). Any `∝L²` (or steeper) law is excluded by a wide margin.
- **Pure plateau is disfavoured.** Slope `0.37±0.07` is `5.6σ` from `0`; each step rises (`~9–25 %`).
  `L8→16` is `1.8σ` above a flat plateau.

The mild rise is **consistent with the finite-size approach to a finite thermodynamic value**
`χ_O^∞ ~ O(0.6)`: at finite `L` the `k=0` projector and the `Σ_z C_s(z)` sum miss the IR modes with
`|p| ≳ 2π/L`, so `χ_O` fills in from below as `L` grows and should saturate once `L ≫ ξ`. A
saturating-`χ` finite-size ansatz (`χ_O(L)=χ_∞ − c/L^p`) is the natural next test and is **not** run
here — **[C]/[O]**. The data cannot yet separate "saturating" from "slow power"; they *can* exclude
free/quadratic behaviour.

## 4 · What this does and does not establish

**Does `[V]`.**
- Extends the exactly gauge-invariant, non-self-averaging `tr F²` zero-mode susceptibility to L=12,16
  and shows it remains **sub-linear** (`χ_O = 0.47, 0.59`; slope `0.37±0.07`), **excluding free `L²`
  growth at `≈25σ`** over L=4–16.
- Keeps `χ_O` `O(1)` while `χ_O/V → 8.95×10⁻⁶` — the non-self-averaging total-normalized carrier is
  robust at the largest volumes.

**Does not `[C]/[O]`.**
- **Not a flat plateau.** The new points show a mild, statistically significant rise; reporting a
  constant `χ_O` would overstate. The correct object is "bounded, sub-linear", with saturation
  unproven.
- **Not the thermodynamic limit.** Fixed `β=2.5`, fixed `a`, fixed **lattice-radius** APE (`ns=2`). The
  `L`-scaling statement is at one lattice spacing and one smearing radius; a fixed **physical** flow
  radius (`t~(0.5 fm)²`) and a continuum extrapolation are the next machine ([O]).
- **Not H1 / not the gap.** `χ_O = ∫s^{-1}dν_O` is the **ceiling** (harmonic-mean squeeze), never the
  atom; `f₁²≥r₀` + isolation `I₂` remain `[O]`. Uniform-in-`(L,a)` `χ_O≤1/λ` (H1) remains `[O]`.
  **No QED. Clay 0%.**
