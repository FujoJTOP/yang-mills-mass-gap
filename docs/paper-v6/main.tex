\begin{claimbox}{cT}
\textbf{唯一主张（逐字，不加码）.}\hspace{0.35em}\LT
\par\smallskip
$I1$ and $I2$ are both closed at \code{[T]}; in the strong-coupling window ($\beta<0.018394$), at finite $L$ (uniformly in $L$),
$m>0$ holds for both the $0^{++}$ and $2^{++}$ channels. \textbf{This is not Clay.}
\textbf{The residual gap is the uniform statement $\forall\beta\in[1/48,2.5]\ \forall L,a:\ G''_{k=0}/a^2\ge\delta^2>0$.} An earlier draft named this gap $\Delta_0>0$ and identified it with $H1$, $I2$, $(E\text{-}I2)$ and $H^*$. \textbf{That identification is withdrawn}, on three independent grounds: (i) \code{n12}: $\Delta_0>0$ fails the four-model test on three of four models (on 2D YM $G''=0$ with $W_{\mathrm{full}}\ge N/12>0$ forces $\Delta_{\mathrm{dyn}}=-W_{\mathrm{full}}<0$; on M6, $\Phi=0\Rightarrow W_{\mathrm{full}}=0\Rightarrow\Delta_{\mathrm{dyn}}=2\omega>0$ on a gapless model; the single plaquette gives $\Delta_{\mathrm{dyn}}=-0.0691$); (ii) \code{n06}: $\Delta_0$ and $\Lambda_\star$ live on different carriers ($G''$ vs $\operatorname{supp}\nu$), are of different type (position vs distance), and have different quantifier domains, so they are \textbf{incomparable}; (iii) \code{n09}, Theorem N09-1: on M6 the only degenerate scale is the spectral bottom, hence \textbf{any quantity passing the four-model test must be a function of $m$ itself} --- the target is unique and no proxy exists. \textbf{The correct equivalence class is three, not five: $\{\Lambda_\star\ \text{lower bound}\equiv I2\equiv(E\text{-}I2)\}$; $H^*$ and $\Delta_0$ do not belong to it.}
\textbf{The withdrawn-transport section (\S\ref{sec:transport-withdrawn}) stands; the naming of the residual gap as $\Delta_0>0$ is likewise withdrawn.}
\par\smallskip
{\footnotesize 中文对照（不加码）：残余缺口 = 一条\textbf{一致陈述} $\forall\beta\in[1/48,2.5]\,\forall L,a:G''_{k=0}/a^2\ge\delta^2>0$；
C5 所需的量词形状由它给出。早先把这条缺口命名为 $\Delta_0>0$ 并等同于 $H1/I2/(E\text{-}I2)/H^*$ 的读法\textbf{已撤回}；
正确等价类是\textbf{三个}：$\{\Lambda_\star\text{ 下界}\equiv I2\equiv(E\text{-}I2)\}$，$H^*$ 与 $\Delta_0$ 不属于它。}
\par\smallskip
展开成两行（柱面 $\mathcal{C}_L=T^3_L\times_a\mathbb{Z}$，$O=\tfrac14\operatorname{tr}F^2$ 的最轻 $0^{++}$ 原子；$2^{++}$ 走同一条链）：
\[
  f_1^2\ \ge\ r_0(\beta)\ >\ 0
  \qquad\text{且}\qquad
  \Lambda_\star-m_1^2\ \ge\ \delta\ >\ 0\quad(\text{即 I2})
\]
对\textbf{每个有限 $L$ 一致}（不取 $L\to\infty$），在 $\beta<\beta_1=0.018394=0.8829\times\tfrac1{48}$ 上。
{\footnotesize\emph{Conventions:} $\beta_A$ vs $\beta_W$ differ by $N^2$; the window above uses $\hat C=4$ and $\bar C=N$, both fixed by \code{M07-WINDOW2.md}; the SZZ radius lies \emph{outside} it.}
\par\smallskip
\textbf{等级（第三次裁决 F07）.}\ \textbf{$I1$ = \LT，$\bar C\equiv N$（\textbf{已证值}，不是未知常数）}：五个洞 A--E 全部关闭（\S\ref{sec:holes}、\S\ref{sec:i1}），
$e\Delta=54.3656$ \textbf{已含动物计数}、每片权重比 $|w|^{1/n}/u=c_f/u=N$（\S\ref{sec:cbar}），
$[KL]$ 已证（\code{P3-CYLINDER-RESIDUE.md:67--69}），$(b')_{IN\text{-}11}$ 在 $\beta<\beta_1$ 上是 \LT（\code{PROOF-IN11.md:19}）。
$I2$ 与 $I1$ 同级闭合；归属清单见 \S\ref{sec:assembly}。
\end{claimbox}

\par\smallskip
{\small
\textbf{摘要级四条限定（展开见 \S\ref{sec:assembly}）.}
\textbf{D.1} $I2\Rightarrow m_1>0$ \textbf{为真但空转}：$I2$ 的旁条件 $\sup\operatorname{spec}\widetilde T<1$ 逐字就是 $m_1>0$（\code{B01-RP.md}:12），即用旁条件把结论写进去；唯一合法反例需\textbf{真空简并}（$r_{\mathrm{iso}}=0.4484$、$m=0$），被 PF 唯一真空排除（\code{G07-KATOADJ.md}）。旧见证 $\nu=\delta_0+\mathbf 1_{[\delta,\delta+1]}$ \textbf{已撤回}。$r_{\mathrm{iso}}$ 与 $I2$ 是同一个量（$E_2-E_1=\Lambda_\star-m_1^2$；2D YM 同值 $0.625$）：真正的对比是距离 vs 位置。

\textbf{D.4} 不是 Clay。
\textbf{新靶}：$\liminf_{a\to0}m_{\mathrm{lat}}(a)/(a\Lambda)\ge c_0>0$（$\Longleftrightarrow m/\Lambda\ge c_0$）——本文不证。
\par}
