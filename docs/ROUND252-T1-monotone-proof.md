## A. 证明（`[T]`，一行）

**Bessel 三项递推：**

$$\boxed{\ I_{\nu-1}(z)-I_{\nu+1}(z)\ =\ \frac{2\nu}{z}I_\nu(z)\ }$$

**`\mathcal D_k` 的重组：**

$$\mathcal D_k\ =\ I_k(I_{k+1}-I_{k+3})+I_{k+2}(I_{k+1}-I_{k-1})$$

**代入递推（`\nu=k+2$` 与 `\nu=k$`）：**

$$I_{k+1}-I_{k+3}\ =\ \frac{2(k+2)}{z}I_{k+2},\qquad I_{k+1}-I_{k-1}\ =\ -\frac{2k}{z}I_k$$

$$\mathcal D_k\ =\ \frac{2(k+2)}{z}I_kI_{k+2}-\frac{2k}{z}I_kI_{k+2}\ =\ \frac{4}{z}I_kI_{k+2}$$

$$\boxed{\ \mathcal D_k(z)\ =\ \frac{4}{z}I_k(z)I_{k+2}(z)\ }$$

**而 `I_k,I_{k+2}\gt0$`（`z\gt0$`）⇒ `\mathcal D_k\gt0$`。**

**⇒ `D'=\frac12\mathcal D_1=\frac{2}{z}I_1I_3\gt0$。**
