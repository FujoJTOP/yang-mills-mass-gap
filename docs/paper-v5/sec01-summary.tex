\section{Summary}\label{sec:summary}

\begin{keybox}
\textbf{The verdict, in one sentence.} The coarse-mode curvature condition
H1 ($\Gpp\ge\delta$) is \emph{not} the road to the mass gap --- there are now two independent
obstructions, both at label \LT{} --- and the correct object is $\fone$, the pole residue of
$O=\tfrac14\tr F^{2}$, which is exactly one step from $m>0$, the step being
$\Lstar>m_1^{2}$ (condition I2).
\end{keybox}

\noindent
\textbf{Why each obstruction is fatal.} \emph{(i)} $\Gpp=\Dyn+\Wg+X$ is exact, and
$\Wg\ge N/12>0$ holds for every $\beta$, every measure and every conditioning point, so a positive
$\Wg$ masks a negative $\Dyn$: at one plaquette, $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, $\beta=2.5$, $\theta=\pi$ one
has $\Gpp=+0.135994711\ldots$ with $\Dyn=-0.069120586\ldots$ (\S\ref{sec:obs1}). \LT{}
\emph{(ii)} In the exactly solvable ``harmonic $\otimes$ free'' model $\Gpp=2\omega>0$ while
$m=0$, by the identity $\Gpp=4(E_0-V(0))$, which asks whether $a=0$ lies above the ground-state
energy and says nothing about the spectrum (\S\ref{sec:obs2}). \LT{} The two are independent, so
repairing one does not revive the route.

\noindent
\textbf{Two warnings.} \emph{On the certificate} (\S\ref{sec:cert}): it is the first strict,
non-Monte-Carlo positivity result in this project --- three points decided by $800$-bit directed
rounding over exact rational Taylor coefficients, enclosures of width $\le3\times10^{-21}$ (down to
$1.1\times10^{-117}$ at $\theta=\pi$), margin $1.36\times10^{-1}$ --- and it certifies a
$0$-dimensional integral at fixed $\beta$ in a window $\beta<\beta^{*}=4.3066$, in lattice units
($G''_a=a^{2}m^{2}_{\mathrm{phys}}$): true, and not a statement about the theory.
\emph{On scope}: no step of Clay is closed and no claim of progress on Clay is made below; the
count remains $0\%$.
