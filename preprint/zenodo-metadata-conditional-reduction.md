## Description (abstract)

```
We report the outcome of an AI-assisted campaign - Lean 4 formalization plus lattice numerics -
aimed at the 4D pure SU(N) Yang-Mills mass gap. We do not prove the mass gap; we prove a
reduction and a ledger. (i) The gap is rewritten onto the Kallen-Lehmann atom
f1^2 = nu({m1^2}) of tr F^2, together with the isolation of the lightest 0++. (ii) An exact
spectral squeeze, f1^2/chi <= inf supp nu <= M0/chi, shows that a lower bound on the mass needs
both a residue floor and a susceptibility ceiling; the commonly quoted condition H1
(chi <= 1/lambda) is necessary but not sufficient, because a thin atom satisfies it while
remaining gapless. (iii) On the order of ninety routes reduce, without loss, to one inequality:
a single-sided, nonperturbative, non-circular, cutoff-uniform upper bound on a conditioned
variance, appearing once in the ultraviolet (the L3 node of existence theory) and once in the
infrared (H1). Positively, we state and prove an unconditional strong-coupling residue theorem
for beta < 1/48, a kinematic no-dissolution theorem for the lightest 0++, non-negativity of the
Haar geometric curvature, gauge-invariant Perron-Frobenius, a strong-coupling coarse Poincare
lemma and an abstract Feshbach-Bogoliubov two-block gap lemma, and we machine-check the
reduction in Lean 4. Negatively, we prove no-gos showing that positivity, convexity, spectral
theory, Perron-Frobenius and renormalization-group routes cannot supply the missing bound.
Clay remains open. The contribution is to locate and instrument the wall, not to cross it.
```
