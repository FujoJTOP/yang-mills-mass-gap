logZ0 = -1/2 sum_p log(lambda_p+m^2); quartic via the O(lambda) term -(lambda/8)L^2 G0(L)^2.
Delta(n) is strictly DECREASING; it crosses 1 at n* ~ 1.9/m (see summary). For lambda>0 the O(lambda) correction is NEGATIVE, pushing n* slightly up; for genuinely small lambda (0.001-0.005) the curve stays positive and monotone.
HONESTY: at lambda=0.01 the O(lambda) shift already drives Delta<0 at n=2 and large n -- i.e. the linear-in-lambda truncation has broken down (O(lambda^2) matters), so 0.01 is NOT a valid 'small' quartic here.  The quartic column is a schematic perturbation, not a precision result.
