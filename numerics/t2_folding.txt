For c<0 the map has a maximum: g'^2 grows to 1/(4|c|) at g^2=1/(2|c|), then decreases.  A scheme-2 integral across the fold is ambiguous and does not match scheme 1.  Restricting to g^2 < 1/(2|c|) restores flow preservation to ~1e-12 (the corrected t2_scheme_shift.txt). The earlier c=-0.1 Lambda-invariance deviation of 1.94 was the fold, not physics.  The 3-loop Landau breakdown (g^2 >~ 4*pi) is a separate, higher-g^2 effect and is never reached in these checks.
