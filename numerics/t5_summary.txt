Fit ln Delta = ln C + slope*n over 0.03<Delta<5.  Empirically slope = -m (to a few %) and C ~ 6.5-6.6: Delta(n) ~ C exp(-m n).  So the log-Delta slope directly equals the mass gap, and Delta crosses O(1) at n* ~ 1.9/m, i.e. same order as the correlation length 1/Delta=1/m (factor ~1.9).  This is the cleanest confirmation of the template's design intent: Delta(n) is monotone and gap-sensitive.
Caveat: free massive toy only; no interacting/gauge-theory claim is made.
