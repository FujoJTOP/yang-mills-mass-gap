(W) g^2=1 is at beta=6 exactly (trivial).  (S) a*Delta=1 (q~1, strong side) is at beta=5.7 (Delta=4Lam) .. 6.3 (Delta=8Lam).  So on the beta axis the two windows COINCIDE near beta~6 -- an apparent overlap.  This is the lattice statement that the strong-weak crossover / scaling region is beta~5.7-6.0.
BUT at beta~6 the lattice spacing is a~0.1-0.15 fm (UV), 2-loop scaling is violated at O(30-50%), and Delta/Lam is a literature input -- so this is a plausibility indication, not a proof of q0>=q1.
MS-bar g^2(mu=Delta): at the PHYSICAL handoff scale mu=Delta=(4..8)Lam the coupling is g^2~1.8-2.2 (2/3-loop), NOT 1.  Meanwhile g^2=1 sits at mu=158*Lam ~ 35 GeV (with the illustrative Lam_MS=0.22 GeV).  Hence the 'g^2=1' threshold is NOT invariant: it is ~20-40x above the physical handoff scale.
