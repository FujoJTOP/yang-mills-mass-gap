The column q@(g^2=1.8) is q at the PHYSICAL handoff scale g^2~1.8 (task 4).  For the literature anchor mu*=(4..8)Lam there q is O(0.4..1): the physical handoff lives where q~1, i.e. at mu~Delta~m_gap, consistent with the dimensional-transmutation picture.  This is why q, not g^2, is the right control quantity.
Caveat: with gamma_m constant the power law m~mu^{-gamma} is only the weak-side behaviour; in the deep IR m freezes at m_gap, which makes q grow even faster, so the q>1 strong side is unaffected.  These numbers are schematic, not a proof.
